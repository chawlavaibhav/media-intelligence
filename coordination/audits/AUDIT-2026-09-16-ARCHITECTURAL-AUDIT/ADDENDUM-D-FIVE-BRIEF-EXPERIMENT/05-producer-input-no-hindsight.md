# Producer input — two briefs, five-stage method, no project history

You are the producer. You have never seen this project, its previous jobs, or any of its earlier plans. Using only this file, write a production plan for each of the two customer briefs below by working through the five stages and the question template. Answer every template question; where the answer must come from the customer, write the question you would ask and the default you would proceed with. Where the answer is a fact you cannot verify from this file, write "fetch: <what and from where>" rather than asserting it. Cite a knowledge claim by its id when you use it. Do not invent statistics, platform rules, product facts or brand assets. Output each plan as prose under the headings **Intent contract / Structure / Creative (with a numbered board) / Selection / Verification / Unresolved**, about 500–700 words per brief.

## The five stages and their reusable questions

**Stage 1 Intent** — deliverable; what it is for and what the viewer should do or feel; product/brand/person that must appear and which features must be exact; mandatory events in the customer's words, each with a verification method; forbidden claims/content; tone with an example; language per surface; where it runs; cap, deadline, approver; 3–6 acceptance statements decidable from the file; contradictions (a contradiction blocks the creative stage); which decisions are yours and the cost of being wrong.

**Stage 2 Structure** — exact dimensions and duration limits (fetch from the platform page); safe zones (fetch); if an ad: hook window, brand-early, ask placement, close-on-product per the knowledge claims below; which claims may be made and against which source; which assets exist and which are missing; sound (muted-first? captions? loudness target); whether each delivered format must be composed separately; is the information sufficient.

**Stage 3 Creative** — one-sentence proposition; the hero and the numbered frame that sells it without copy; the opening frame and its event; brand timeline (first mark, in-context presence, spoken mention); the ask, words, surface, timing; the last frame; every mandatory event on a numbered board frame; on-screen lines equal spoken lines; copy zone per frame per format off hands/faces/product; voice register, who casts it, on which line; composition/light/finish per key object; cuts and holds.

**Stage 4 Selection** — the highest-risk element (hands, objects in contact, falling objects, faces speaking, legible screens or text in motion, identity across shots, chained extensions, non-English speech) made and judged first; route per asset with evidence or a test; known failures on that route; nothing textual generated (composite all text, logos, UI); voice from exactly one generation; VO durations measured before shot timings; identity references per shot (≤ 3–4); balances read; spend vs cap; order of work: voice → riskiest shot → plates per format → board with measured VO → remaining clips → assembly.

**Stage 5 Verification** — exact strings; bounds/contrast/disjoint/fit; no text over the subject mask in any format; no VO overlap, last line ends before the film, no internal silence > 0.8 s; loudness; duration and safe zones; no generated lettering; every mandatory event on a frame, hero first and last, brand timeline; identity and mouths; which stage each failure belongs to; human release.
