**Verdict on the core hypothesis** ("the project has accumulated enough useful expert knowledge to materially improve many productions, but its consumption mechanism does not reliably convert relevant knowledge into timely decisions"): **half right.** Consumption failure is OBSERVED at rungs 2, 3 and 5 for seven of nine failure classes (council 15 §1.10). But "enough useful expert knowledge" overstates: for structure the knowledge is redundant with the frontier prior on the coarse spine and additive only on specific rules; for obstruction and voice it is absent; for everything else it lives in MF prose and case files, not Canon. And "consumption mechanism" understates: the load-bearing failure is that no mechanism converts *any* knowledge — Canon, prior, case, or the model's own — into a **challenged** decision before spend. Fixing consumption without challenge reproduces PA-D7.

## 4. The twenty red-team questions

1. **Why did Cumin become a tutorial although the repository contained advertising knowledge?** OBSERVED: the brief described a tutorial; the operator was told "proceed on the brief" and "do not paraphrase a missing domain"; `commercial_communication` fired and was uncompiled by ruling C-10; the one compiled advertising check (PA-D7) was self-marked "pass"; no reviewer existed (`reviews: []`, personas absent); the human approved five strings and a cap, never the proposition. K2-by-policy + K7 + K14. (Part 6 §3.4 row 1.)
2. **Why could text still cover subjects after months of Canon and composition work?** OBSERVED: Canon has no rule for copy-over-subject (0 hits); the knowledge existed as an MF prompt discipline and a 002 candidate whose promotion condition ("an obstruction check exists in the compositor") nobody owned; the only barrier was a human checklist row (CF2) the author self-marked PASS; the flagged 4:5/1:1 files were presented three times because "flagged ≠ deliverable" is not a rule. K0(Canon)/K13/K7/K11/K14.
3. **Why did voice consume multiple iterations despite prior experiments and case 001?** OBSERVED: MF recorded "raw Sarvam robotic" and "ElevenLabs Sarah rejected by ear" in July; the router reads the taint register only; RR-12 made Sarvam the default on ≤ 70-char lines; RO-05 is `routing_authority: none` by rule; the job cited RO-05 and rationalised it away; voice ran after plates and during clips; the human redirected the search mid-way; no house default exists; the route that worked has no cell. K1/K3/K4/K5/K6/K8/K13/K14/K15. (Part 6 §3.4 row 5.)
4. **Why are known failures repeatedly rediscovered?** OBSERVED: learning propagates as prose read once per session or as library code with no mandatory caller; `check_case.py` checks schema only; candidates are re-derived under new names; conditions are never evaluated; memory is keyed by CASE-ID, not defect class; the taxonomy cannot name K7/K13/K14; MF priors have no reader. (Part 6 §4; cause 4.)
5. **Does Canon fail because its knowledge is bad or its consumption is bad?** Consumption, for the seven classes that have knowledge; absent knowledge for two (obstruction, voice); and — the part the dichotomy misses — no *challenge* step for any of the nine. (§2 verdict.)
6. **Is the two-pack strategy making most of Canon inert?** Yes, OBSERVED and quantified: 8.0 % of SK, 0.63 % of bytes, 10/37 sources reachable; the compiled two are the lowest-demand packs; the gate and compiler hard-code two; the runtime continues on a gap by design; the skill defers gap-recording until after human rejection. (Council 06 §7.)
7. **Are Q&A pairs a superior interface for LLM consumption?** As a form (worked negative example with scope discipline), yes for ≤ 15 items; as a corpus, no (Part 7 §7).
8. **Does the operator receive too much process and too little expert context?** Yes, OBSERVED: 48 % process / 25 % QA / ≤ 12 % expert (hand-strict 4.5 %); 60–80 k tokens before the brief, PROFILE.md the largest block; craft = one 14-row table at stage 5 of 14. (Council 09 §Q1.)
9. **Is the operator spending its reasoning budget on bookkeeping?** Yes, OBSERVED: 15 roles; a 450-line dispatcher and 414-line compositor written per job; 10 k-word job record; 21 check lines rendered; 1,153 frame PNGs committed; while the one structural question went unasked. (Council 09 §Q2; council 04 §C(11).)
10. **Has the Lab optimised what is easy to measure?** Yes, by construction and by its own admission: five deterministic properties in 575 rows; 31/36 capabilities 0 rows; zero contract lines on naturalness, hero, structure; 0/3 acceptance-turning routes covered. (Council 08 §2.6.)
11. **Is one executor playing too many incompatible roles?** Yes, OBSERVED: 15 roles in one session incl. author and QA of the same artefact, and (in a second session) author of the case that judged its own job. No independent challenge ran in any of the three cases. (Council 09 §Q2.)
12. **What could a strong LLM have inferred from the Cumin brief without Canon?** INFERENCE (three councils, converging): bowl as hero; open on a close-up with brand from frame 1; three macro beats with supers on the wall; payoff; packshot end card with name + URL; one calm Indian-English narrator cast by ear first; VO before cut; supers never over hands; per-geometry compositions; the six questions to ask. Caveat OBSERVED: the same model family, under the actual brief and instruction set, did not — because the brief framed a tutorial and nobody asked. (Councils 04 §F, 11 §4, 14 §2.1, 15 §2.)
13. **What did Canon add beyond that?** OBSERVED: lighting/finish vocabulary, one window source, no lettering, hold lengths — all held on the plates (cause unproven; co-owned with the Lab's mechanism B). Nothing structural.
14. **What should Canon have added but failed to?** The ABCD/Ogilvy/Hopkins structure (brand early and throughout; open in action; CTA more direct as you go; end on the product; see and say) as a *required question* before the deck freeze — and the LSM packshot rule for the end card.
15. **Where exactly did that failure occur?** Between 13:17 and 13:28Z on 15 Sep, in the blueprint step: retrieval blocked at 13:23Z by policy; `cta: none` decided at 13:27Z by the operator alone; five strings approved at 13:28Z by the human; no challenge between. Everything after was competent execution of a wrong plan, patched three times.
16. **What would a real agency have done before generating the first asset?** OBSERVED practice (Part 8 §G, §I): extracted the twelve brief decisions; written a one-sentence proposition; named the hero product and its distinctive assets; held a tissue/PPM review with someone other than the author; locked script and storyboard as the contractual baseline; recorded and measured the VO before fixing shot durations; cast the voice on the actual script; put the safe-zone overlay on the boards.

Under 3 % of bytes; every accepted asset was produced with exactly this subset. (Council 11 §3.)
20. **What evidence would convince us to simplify?** It exists: 43/46 defects non-model; EVAL-038 0/6; Cumin's "canon had this knowledge" answered by `missing_domains: 8 of 10` and a frontier prior; the bypassed job had the best TTAO while the conformant one failed; state docs regrew despite a migration; 84 % of spend in a Lab that no production route decision cites. What is missing and would settle the Canon/Registry question: the 28-Aug head review's blinded A / B′ / E round on real briefs, never run — Part 16 E1–E7. (Council 11 §9.)

## 5. Disconfirming findings the ranking must survive (OBSERVED unless marked)

1. Knowledge *was* absent for two of six Cumin failures; no consumption fix touches them.
2. The pilot's own contrast doctrine was in context before V1 and V3 drew over the dal photo — context presence did not bind a compositor; a pause-point question would not have either. Only a gate did (Cumin: no contrast complaint).
3. Sonnet without Canon was competitive or leading in both experiments; "Canon helps" has no committed judging artefact.
4. Case 002, which bypassed the workflow, had the best TTAO and lowest CpAO; conformance is uncorrelated with acceptance at n = 3 — a warning against adding process to fix process.
5. The Cumin operator applied ABCD correctly in one pass at USD 0 once told — so the cheapest fix is a sentence, not an architecture (INFERENCE); but the same operator had 001's CANON-BRIEF in context by id and still had its opening rejected twice — so "in context" is not "applied" without a challenge.
6. The human's own taste is not stable across days ("why not Veo with voice" vs 002 RO-02; "I don't agree voice is an issue" after rejecting 14 takes); E6 must precede any experiment whose outcome is his verdict.
