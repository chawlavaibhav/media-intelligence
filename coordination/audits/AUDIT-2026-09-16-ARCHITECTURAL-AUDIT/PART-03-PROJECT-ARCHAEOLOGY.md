## 2. Patterns the chronology exposes

1. **Design → supersede without execution.** V1 bank → macro reset (1 day); T2–T8 v1 → v2 (1 day); reset → Lab direction (7 days, "SUPERSEDED, stated plainly"); Lab → stop-widening (6 days); Alpha-1 → three non-Alpha productions (1 day). INFERENCE: the programme repeatedly chose to design the next layer rather than run the previous one.
2. **Every accepted outcome bypassed the system built to produce it** (E10, E11); the first conformant job failed (E12). Conformance and acceptance are uncorrelated at n = 3.
3. **The central Canon question has been reserved since 1 Sep** and no experiment since can answer it (E5, E6). The "cheapest decisive measurement" named in the EVAL-038 disposition was never commissioned.
4. **The 28-Aug independent reviews were adopted in form and defeated by regrowth:** PROJECT-MEMORY 105 KB → 18.6 KB → 38.4 KB; CONTROL-STATE 12.9 KB → 30.1 KB; the recommended USD 30–40 need-driven screen became a USD 122 Lab; the blinded A/B′/E architecture test was never run (`11` §10).
5. **Evidence hygiene is the strongest asset**: sealed bytes re-hash, validators pass, cap crossings recorded not annulled, negative results kept. This audit could be computed because of it.
6. **What survived into production from ten weeks of work** (INFERENCE from what the three jobs actually imported or cited): the ledger discipline, the NR vocabulary, code-set exact text, the two packs' check lines, the 61-cell map summary and price pins, the six compositor gates, the case files as directional notes, and the operator skill.
