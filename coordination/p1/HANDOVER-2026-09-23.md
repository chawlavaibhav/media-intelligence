## 4. What was proven, what failed

**Image: accepted.** Mokobara Transit Backpack launch poster (`job_20260923_6720871c`), 1:1 and 4:5, USD 2.28. The founder picked
the takes, asked for colour, clearance and a logo-led layout, and accepted. The downloads are byte-identical to the accepted files.

**Film: REJECTED** (`job_20260923_7d36a5b6`, USD 13.59), a 30-s hands-only packing Reel. The founder's words:
> "the video is fucked up. two random hands, too many AI slops. video is robotic." / "the yellow colour is also changing in the
> bag. it was blue and then turned yellow."

The builder's diagnosis — **hypotheses, not verified causes** (the observations inside them are real; the causal claims have not
been tested):
1. **The plan asked the video model for its weakest thing.** Eight 3–4-s beats of hand choreography on zips and flaps. Veo
   produced jerky one-handed motion, morphing zip hardware and changing bag construction. The atlas already names this mode
   (`route_cannot_meet_the_demand`, MOKO7 DF-2). The director needs route limits: few shots, camera moves on a stable product,
   at most one simple hand action.
2. **Every beat still was generated independently.** Background, light, wardrobe (white cuff vs grey jacket) and bag geometry
   drift between shots. The yellow lining shows where the plan has the bag closed, hence the "blue then yellow" jumps. Beat stills
   should be generated from the previous beat's approved still (or one master scene) as a reference, not only from product photos.
3. **Three beats fell back to code push-ins on stills.** b3 and b4 were refused twice by Veo's safety filter as false
   positives ("celebrity likeness", "audio issue"). b6 morphed the bag in both takes. Static inserts between moving shots add
   to the "robotic" feel.
4. **Director latency: 4–10 minutes per direction round** (gpt-5.6-sol at high reasoning effort, about 25k tokens of Canon).
   This is the main obstacle to the 10–15 minute target.
