## Clarification of the checker boundary

A checker/instrument is any mechanism that converts an output into an observation for a requirement:
deterministic code, OCR, computer vision, VLM/LLM, specialised model, or a human.

Qualification tests the checker itself against material where the truth is already known before trusting its automated scores.

That is required before Registry-grade automated measurement. It is not required before a bounded product-learning pilot where human review is explicit and results are not promoted into the Capability Registry.
