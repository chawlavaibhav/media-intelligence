## Programme sequence
The working sequence is now:

1. pre-pilot substrate + experimental feasibility;
2. one real vertical-slice pilot;
3. demand-driven model/workflow screen;
4. outcome-level architecture experiment;
5. architecture keep/kill decision;
6. targeted evaluator qualification + Stage B / Capability Registry;
7. runtime v0;
8. untouched holdout / Stage C;
9. ship / simplify / stop.

## Architecture experiment direction
The intended development experiment remains outcome-level, not prose-plan-level:

A. raw prompt;
B. strong LLM reasoning;
C. Creative IR;
D. Creative IR + Canon;
E. Creative IR + production intelligence;
F. Creative IR + Canon + production intelligence.

Actual media is the object judged. The exact arm implementation, route controls, reviewer protocol and engineering decision thresholds will be frozen only after CANON-013 and the pilot expose feasibility constraints.

## Scientific posture
- Human media review can be used for product-learning if blinded and governed by frozen acceptance criteria.
- Automated evaluator qualification is required when a decision depends on that automated evaluator or when Registry-grade evidence is being claimed.
- Plan-level Canon testing may be diagnostic but cannot by itself retain or kill Canon's product role.
- Production IR should be extracted from successful real recipes, not designed in the abstract.
