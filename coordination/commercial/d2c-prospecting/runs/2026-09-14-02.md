### 3.3 Typsy Beauty — 84 → **73**. A material, evidence-driven downgrade

This is the finding that most changes the pipeline, and it is a downgrade.

**What changed (OBSERVED 2026-09-14):**

- **YouTube: 544 videos against 2,880 subscribers.** That ratio is the signature of a channel used as
  an **ad-asset repository**, not an audience. A brand with genuine organic pull does not sit at
  5 subscribers per video. (Compare Cumin Co. below: 29,600 subs on 95 videos.)
- Combined with the Controller's hand-check — **~990 active Meta ads, almost all creator-whitelisted
  reels, 100% lip SKUs, and the Spritz fragrance SKU in none of them.**

**Why this is a downgrade and not an upgrade.** The first run read "990 active ads" as enormous
creative appetite. Read against `runtime/ALPHA-1.md`, it is the opposite signal:

1. **Their creative engine is precisely what Alpha 1 cannot make.** Creator-whitelisted reels are
   faces, speech and application-on-skin. Ruling C-7 excludes talking heads, lip-sync and native
   speech outright. We would not be improving their creative; we would be offering a different
   product they have not asked for.
2. **Lip colour on skin is the weakest fidelity case we have.** Shade accuracy on skin is exactly
   where generation risk is highest.
3. **They are saturated, not starved.** ~990 active ads means established creative vendors. There is
   no volume gap to fill, and reply probability to unsolicited work is correspondingly low.
4. **The run-01 pitch SKU was wrong.** Spritz fragrance appears in none of the sampled ads. The
   first run's hypothesis — "fragrance is whitespace we could own" — is the optimistic reading; the
   Controller flagged the pessimistic one (lips are the business, fragrance is a side bet nobody will
   fund creative for). **This run adopts the pessimistic reading as the base case**, as instructed.

**Score movement:** AI CREATIVE POTENTIAL 16 → **11** · REPEAT-WORK 13 → **11** · CURRENT CREATIVE
GAP 11 → **8** · REACHABILITY 9 → **7**. **84 → 73.** Typsy Beauty **leaves the Top 3** and is
retained at "worth retaining" only. It is not discarded — a brand spending at that level is worth
revisiting if it ever opens a non-creator-led category — but it should not receive spec production.

## 11. Production-capability dependency — unchanged and worth repeating

This run selected brands. It still cannot make the ad, and neither can anything else today. Per
`runtime/ALPHA-1.md` and `coordination/CONTROL-STATE.md` §9: the runtime has **never called a
provider**, `spend_authority.status: none` on every profile, and `dispatch_mode: live` refuses
outright ("live dispatch is not wired in this tranche"). A first paid run needs three things that do
not exist: a decision on whether the plan comes from a recorded fixture or a paid reasoning pass;
live transport behind `ExecutionBridge.run()`; and a signed runtime spend record.

**The prospecting loop is now two runs ahead of the production loop.** The pipeline holds twelve
qualified brands and can produce for none of them. That is not a defect in either loop, but it is now
the binding constraint on this workstream's value, and every further prospecting run widens the gap.

Two constraints any spec ad from this pipeline must respect, both reflected in the hypotheses above:
exact copy is **code-composed, never generated in-scene** (C-7), and no concept may depend on a
celebrity likeness (which is why Que joined MetaMan and Phitku in the negative-filter column).
