## 1. Brand overview

Delhi-based D2C fashion accessories and jewellery brand, founded 2022 by **Sonaal Goel, Twisha Gupta
and Kanishka Garg**. Originally a fashion-jewellery brand, now deliberately repositioned as a broad
everyday-accessories brand — press describes the ambition as "India's Claire's, but saltier".

Commercial shape (INFERRED, 2026-09-14):
- **₹4–4.5 Cr monthly revenue** (≈ ₹50 Cr run-rate), targeting **₹100 Cr this financial year** with an
  internal stretch of ₹150 Cr.
- **₹30.1 Cr Series A on 9 Jan 2026**, led by MG Investment, with Anicut Capital, All In Capital, the
  JK Group and angels. An earlier ₹5.4 Cr round precedes it.
- Channel mix: **40–45% own D2C website and app · 30% quick commerce (Blinkit the key channel) ·
  25% marketplaces (Amazon, Myntra)**.
- Funds earmarked for: watches, sunglasses, scarves, belts and bag charms; a **new "Salty Bags"
  vertical**; core team; wider ecommerce and quick-commerce reach; a **flagship offline store**.

**Why this is the right size.** Big enough to have a real creative budget; small enough that three
founders are plausibly still the decision layer. This is the band the prospecting brief asks for.

## 3. Current positioning

Affordable, trend-led, everyday accessories for young Indian women (and increasingly men) — volume and
newness rather than preciousness. A new category or drop is the product, not a one-off hero SKU.

## 4. Target consumer

Young, urban and increasingly Tier-2, phone-first, price-aware, discovering through Instagram and
buying on impulse via quick commerce or the app. Responds to newness, price clarity and outfit context.

## 11. Creative whitespace

A brand spending the majority of its budget on performance marketing, across five new categories at
once, plus a new bags vertical, plus quick commerce, needs an enormous and constantly refreshing volume
of SKU-level creative — and micro-creator UGC does not produce *premium product-led* work. The likely
gap is a **repeatable, premium, product-first visual system** that scales per SKU and per price point
without a shoot for every drop.

This is also the best physical fit in the entire run for what we can actually build: accessories are
**hard-surface, non-skin, reflective, small and shootable in isolation** — no human performance, no
skin-tone fidelity problem, no claims minefield.

## 12. Creative territories (high level — the production process chooses)

1. **Product-as-object, elevated.** A single accessory treated with the material seriousness of a
   luxury still — light, metal, glass, shadow — at a ₹600–1,500 price point, with the exact price
   composed by code. Deliberately contradicts the category's flat-lay norm.
2. **The impossible material moment.** The accessory forms, assembles or transforms out of its own
   material (metal flowing into a bag charm, light collapsing into a lens). Uses generative visuals for
   something a shoot genuinely cannot do — then resolves to the real pack and exact price.
3. **One system, many SKUs.** A single deterministic visual grammar (one light setup, one surface, one
   typographic slot) demonstrated across three products at three price points — pitched as a *system*
   for the whole catalogue rather than one clever ad. This is the territory most likely to convert into
   recurring work, because it shows the repeatability rather than describing it.

## 13. AI production risks

- **Fine detail fidelity.** Chains, clasps, engraving and lens curvature are exactly where generation
  degrades. Mitigation: favour bolder forms (bag charms, sunglasses, scarves) over fine jewellery.
- **Brand-exact product identity.** A generated accessory that is *almost* their SKU is worse than
  useless in a spec ad. A supplied-photo route would solve it, but `IMG-EDIT`/`IMG-REF` evidence is
  directional only and **routes to a person today**; a static ad with a supplied product photo demands
  `image_generation` + `reference_fidelity` from one call, **which no route currently answers**
  (`runtime/ALPHA-1.md`, DEF-8). This is the central production risk and must be resolved before
  production, not during it.
- **Exact copy:** must be **code-composed onto a textless plate**, never generated in-scene (C-7).
  This is the mechanism with 4/4 clean evidence (RR-1) and is exact by construction.
- **Motion:** only one short silent clip, derived only from the accepted still. No voiceover, no
  multi-shot.
- **Unverified assets.** Every source in §8 is unconfirmed. Production cannot start on this dossier
  alone.
- **Salty Bags may not have launched.** The Jan-2026 press states it as a plan. Verify before using it
  as the "why now".
