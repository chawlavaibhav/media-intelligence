The Comfort Collection is crafted from MITTI stoneware, made with premium clay and finished with a high-quality, non-porous glass glaze. High-fired at 1200–1300°C, MITTI ceramic achieves very low porosity and premium strength, making it durable enough for everyday use while being less prone to absorbing oils, stains or odours.

The high-fired ceramic also retains heat beautifully, keeping your coffee warm, your soup comforting and your food at the table for longer. The non-porous glaze makes it easy to clean and resistant to the colours and flavours of everyday cooking.

Non-toxic, easy to clean and made for everyday meals, MITTI stoneware brings lasting durability and peace of mind to the moments you make time for.
