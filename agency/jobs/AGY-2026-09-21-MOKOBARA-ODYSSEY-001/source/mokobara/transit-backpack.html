    Designed for everyday use and 1-2 day trips. Works for laptops upto 16 inches.

      • Incredible functionality with a sleek minimalist design.
      • Crafted with reverse-coated polyester, and vegan leather trims.
      • Front compartment houses a retractable key holder, cardholder, pen pockets, slip pockets and a zipper pocket.
      • Separate tech compartment with 5 pockets at the back to hold your laptop, ipad, books and chargers securely.
      • Padded laptop compartment that fits upto 16 inches of laptop with a strap lock & a 180 ° flat opening for easy access.
      • Has two hidden water bottle/umbrella compartments. Can fit up-to 1L water bottle easily.
      • Has a silicon grab handle & nicely padded shoulder straps for extra comfort. Straps features easy length adjustment buckles.
      • Body blended magnetic pocket for passport, boarding pass and cellphone. 
      • Luggage sleeve secures to any Mokobara suitcase for a smooth ride.

      Specifications:

      • Splendidly sized: 48 x 31 x 16 cms
      • Laptop Pocket Dimension: 41 x 27 x 3 cms
      • Capacity: 30L
      • Fits upto 16 inches of laptop & a large tablet/ipad.

      Care Instructions:

      • Use a wet cloth to wipe the bag. Do not dry clean.
      • When not in use, keep the bag in a well ventilated space.
