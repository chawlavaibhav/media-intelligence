## GENERATION_PROMPTS

Dispatched prompt (motion, from the accepted still):
"Continuous single take, handheld cinematic camera walking backwards slightly ahead of her, keeping her full body in frame the whole time; no cuts. Seconds 0 to 4: she walks toward the camera at a relaxed pace, sipping from the paper coffee cup once, phone and keys in her other hand, the street behind her drifting past. Seconds 4 to 5: a young man on a skateboard rolls in fast from frame-right and clips her shoulder as he passes; she is knocked half a step sideways, her arms fly up, and the coffee cup, the phone and the keys leave her hands. Seconds 5 to 10: time slows to extreme cinematic slow motion — the cup tumbling with an arc of coffee spilling out of it, the phone turning end over end, the keys spinning and catching the sun, all hanging in the air around her at head height while her hair and jacket lift; her face turns toward the flying cup, mouth open in surprise; the skateboarder blurs out of frame. The camera holds steady and slowly pushes in during the slow motion. Everything stays in the same sunlit street; same clothes, same face, same light. No text, no lettering, no logos."
