# Canon Coverage Map v0

**Date:** 23 Aug 2026 · **Question:** *what bodies of knowledge would a strong creative and media
intelligence need?* — not *how good is our current six-chapter Canon?*

Coverage is rated against the **library we own** (40 titles), not against what has been processed.
Six partial chapters have been processed; that is noted separately and carries no weight in the
rating.

**Importance** is to the first commercial product: short-form commercial media for Indian
businesses — static ads and 6–20 second product videos.

**Owner** answers *where should this knowledge come from?* Zero book coverage is only a defect
where books are the right owner.

## Summary

```
Domains mapped                      52
critical importance                 22
strong or medium coverage           37   (71%)
weak coverage                       10
absent                               5

Domains where books are the right owner        41
Domains needing expert / platform / empirical  11
```

**The library is strong where craft is old and stable** — composition, typography, lighting, shot
grammar, advertising strategy. **It is weakest exactly where our first product lives:** short-form
feed video, Devanagari, Indian cultural context, product photography, hooks.

Two structural observations:

**Trade-off reasoning and principle interaction are weak across the board.** No single source
teaches them; they emerge from comparing sources. That is a cross-source synthesis job, and it is
the one thing a Canon can do that no individual book can.

**Judgement-quality knowledge is strong and is pointed at us, not at the customer.** *Noise*,
*Thinking Fast and Slow* and *Superforecasting* are about how our own evaluator should be built
and calibrated — a legitimate Canon domain aimed at Evaluation, not at creative output.
