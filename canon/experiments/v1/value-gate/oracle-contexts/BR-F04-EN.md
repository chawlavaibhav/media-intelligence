# Canon context

## preparation sequence before writing
_Source: ogilvy-ch2-advertising-that-sells · scs_ogl_c003_001_

The chapter opens with an ordered preparation routine: study the product, then study competitors' advertising and how well it worked, then research consumers, then decide the position, then decide the image. Each step is presented as feeding the next, and the whole sequence is presented as preceding any creative work.

Advertising begins with an ordered preparation routine, not with invention. Product study feeds competitor study feeds consumer research feeds positioning feeds image, and the big idea is said to arise only from an unconscious already loaded by that work.

## values held against style
_Source: vignelli-canon-intangibles · scs_vig_c003_002_

A cluster of the source's values defined by what they are not. Discipline is not style; appropriateness transcends style; design is a discipline and emphatically not a style; intellectual elegance is not a design style; visual power is not visual impact; timelessness is against fashion. Each is asserted by exclusion.

The book's central move in Part One is to separate a permanent, transferable discipline from transient style, and to place every value it holds on the discipline side. The values are defined largely by what they exclude rather than by tests they satisfy.

## structure read as signification
_Source: samara-making-breaking-grid-ch1 · scs_sam_c003_005_

The claims in which a structure is chosen or judged for what it will be taken to mean rather than for what it organises: the manuscript grid's classical and authoritative reading, the modular grid's rationalist reading used as an interpretive overlay, symmetry as classical and asymmetry as modern, and the general statement that structure itself can be a message read culturally, historically and associationally.

Running alongside the functional argument is a second, unmeasured one: every structural choice already carries an inherited reading, and that reading can override the functional match. The source states the general principle only in the last of these four places, three sections after it has twice relied on it.

## brand image is a personality built by consistency
_Source: ogilvy-ch2-advertising-that-sells · sk_ogl_c003_0007_

A brand's image is its personality, and that personality is an amalgam of its name, packaging, price, the style of its advertising and above all the product itself. Every advertisement contributes to it, so advertising must project the same image year after year.
