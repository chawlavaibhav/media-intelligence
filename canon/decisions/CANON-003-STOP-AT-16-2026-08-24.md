# CANON-003 Controller decision — stop extraction at 16 usable books

**Date:** 24 Aug 2026  
**Decision:** Stop CANON-003 source extraction at **16 Controller-accepted usable books** and proceed to final integration/revalidation/synthesis.

## Basis

CANON-003 defined:

- minimum success: **15 usable distinct books**;
- target: **18**;
- maximum without further approval: 20.

The batch has reached **16 Controller-accepted usable books** under the frozen method. This exceeds the task's minimum success threshold and already spans the intended heterogeneous source shapes/domains. The remaining two preselected books are not required to establish task success.

The Controller therefore judges the marginal value of spending further execution time on Books 11–12 lower than the value of moving now to cross-book synthesis and method consolidation.

## Deferred books

The following are **deferred, not failed or blocked**:

- Book 11 — Christopher Kenworthy, *Master Shots* (`work/canon-003-rebalance-d`)
- Book 12 — Michael Ondaatje, *The Conversations* (`work/canon-003-b`)

Do not count either as a usable-book failure, source-integrity block, or negative result. Their existing partial/uncommitted worker state is outside the accepted 16-book evidence set.

If a worker later returns additional material for either book, do not silently add it to CANON-003. Treat it as optional reserve evidence requiring a separate Controller decision if we ever want to expand the batch beyond the frozen 16-book synthesis set.

## Consequences

- **CANON-003 extraction phase is closed at 16 usable books.**
- No more book extraction is required for task success.
- The accepted evidence set is the 16 books already Controller-audited.
- Accepted worker branches remain untouched until one fresh integration session reconciles them.
- Final integration must mechanically revalidate every accepted book; earlier lane validators were ephemeral and are not independent completion evidence.
- The final synthesis must answer the existing CANON-003 end-of-batch questions using **16 books**, and explicitly state that the batch stopped above the minimum but below the 18-book target by Controller choice.
- The frozen method remains unchanged until synthesis completes.
- No Canon-consumption/RAG/training experiment begins merely because extraction stopped.

## Next gate

Run one fresh CANON-003 integration/revalidation/synthesis session over the 16 accepted books. Only after that synthesis may the Controller decide whether to open one consolidated Canon-method revision task.
