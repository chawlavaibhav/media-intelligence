## 1. The primary result is negative, and it is clean

> **Weak model + the two compiled packs did not match a strong model working alone on any
> brief. 0 of 6. The three Sonnet NO_CANON repetitions took all 18 top-3 slots, judged blind.**

The decision rule was fixed before execution (win or tie on ≥3 of 6 briefs). It was not met,

The blind notes show *why*: the treatment packages lost on creative substance — thin copy, no

schema with zero structural defects. **The packs made weak models disciplined. They did not make
them good.**

## 2. The economics refute the thesis independently of quality

| Arm | Cost per package | Blind rank |
|---|---|---|
| Sonnet NO_CANON (baseline) | **USD 0.063** | 1st–3rd, all six briefs |
| Haiku + packs | **USD 0.072** | never above 4th |
| Gemma + packs | USD 0.000 (pinned free tier) | never above 4th |

The "cheap" arm cost **more** than the "expensive" arm: injecting ~5–6K tokens of doctrine into
Haiku exceeds the 2× price gap at these package sizes. The substitution trade had no economic
case at this pair even before quality — a fact the break-even algebra (C = B + 5·O) obscured
because it budgeted headroom, not advantage.

More fundamentally: one 8-second video draw (USD 0.40–0.47) costs as much as **6–7 complete
Sonnet reasoning packages**. Reasoning is 4–15% of a single media draw. **The reasoning model
was never the cost lever. Media draws per accepted outcome is the lever** — which is what CpAO
said all along.

Together: **Canon's demonstrated value is not making a weak model write like a strong one. It
is knowing what to reject and how to execute** — the acceptance gate and the execution
discipline, which sit exactly where the money is (media draws per accepted outcome).

1. **Close the substitution question as answered for this configuration:** two packs,
   Haiku/Gemma-class consumers, forced injection — refuted. Do not rerun to reconfirm.
2. **Stop optimising reasoning cost.** Use the strong reasoning model; at USD 0.063/package it

   mechanical gate → cheapest generation route → cheap redraws until the gate passes → human
   acceptance. Blueprints amortise; draws are variance; the gate is Canon.

   whatever the *gate* needs (colour/register and commercial-communication check lines), not
