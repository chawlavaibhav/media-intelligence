# Finding 10 — Source knowledge vs product binding: re-audit of six probes

**Date:** 23 Aug 2026 · **Mode:** re-audit only. No book processed. No Pass 2. SPEC-01 untouched.
SPEC-02 and all six probe outputs retained unmodified as experimental evidence.

## The assumption that failed

SPEC-02 admitted knowledge by asking whether it informs a field in today's Creative IR.

> **Current product consumption is not a valid admission criterion for durable source knowledge.**

It failed in both directions, and the six probes contain evidence of each.

**It excluded durable knowledge.** *Light: Science & Magic*'s family of angles — the organising
concept of the book — was filed as a note because it describes where to place a physical light and
Production IR does not exist. Nineteen items were discarded this way across the batch.

**It admitted distortion.** An extractor required to find a binding will find one. Molly Bang's
"a larger element reads as stronger" was recorded as informing `entities.role`, our schema's
hero/supporting slot. Her colour-grouping principle was recorded as informing `relationships`,
which in SPEC-01 means entity-to-entity relations like *person holding product*. Neither mapping is
her claim; both were stored as though they were.

**The decisive evidence is internal.** SPEC-02's own worked counter-example was
`pointed_shapes_read_as_threatening`, presented as knowledge that informs nothing and belongs in
human notes. The Molly Bang extraction produced `mb_011` — the same knowledge, informing
`creative.visual_language`. Same rule, opposite verdicts, three days apart. `informs` is elastic
and cannot carry an admission decision.

## What the systems layer recovered

Ten systems, and three of them carry knowledge that no member states alone.

**Lupton's cue economy** is the strongest case. `lu_005` says inline emphasis takes one signal;
`lu_006` says redundancy is recommended. **They contradict each other when retrieved separately.**
They cohere only alongside `lu_007`'s three-cue ceiling and `lu_003`'s consistency requirement.
SPEC-02 handled this by writing cross-references into each atom's `exceptions` field — which
worked only because a human noticed. Nothing structural required them to travel together.

**Grammar of the Shot's continuity geometry** is a dependency chain, not a set. Frame edges give
directions; sight lines create the axis; the axis constrains camera placement; screen direction is
what that preserves. Retrieved alone, "stay within 180 degrees" refers to a line defined two
objects upstream.

**Light: Science & Magic's reflection management** produced the cleanest recovery. Its orphaned
note *"lighting is primarily an exercise in reflection management"* was never an atom — it is what
the system as a whole asserts, and SPEC-03 has a field for that.

Direct evidence of the loss: Molly Bang's `mb_008` contains a cross-principle relationship inside
its own principle text — *"a horizontal placed across verticals restores order"* — because there
was nowhere else to put it. A system leaking into an atom.

## Molly Bang's 18/18, resolved

FINDINGS-09 flagged the zero-rejection rate as the batch's most likely over-fitting. The re-audit
says **the count was not the problem.**

The "Principles" section is a numbered sequence of compositional claims. A numbered claim is
genuinely a knowledge object, and all 18 survive as SourceKnowledge. What was wrong is that SPEC-02
offered exactly two outcomes — bind it or discard it — so the extractor's real choice was to bind
everything or throw away Molly Bang.

The distortion was never in the count. It was in **what the atoms claimed**: two manufactured
bindings, and product vocabulary written inside fields that purported to record what Bang teaches.
`mb_002`'s diagnostic read *"Is the rank-1 element at or near centre."* `rank-1` is SPEC-01's word,
not hers.

## Evidence handling changed materially

Nine uncalibrated decimals were dropped from the Ogilvy probe alone. They ranged 0.55–0.85, were
never calibrated against anything, and were doing authority work they had not earned — the
clearest case being `og_009`, which reached `operational` at confidence 0.55 while `og_001`
(`established`, 0.85) sat pending, purely because of vocabulary novelty.

Under SPEC-03 there is no `operational` status to misassign and no decimal to over-read. `og_009`
now carries `practitioner_assertion` and `source_concedes_difficulty` as facts about its support.

One consequence worth naming: `advertising_can_reduce_sales`, discarded as a note, is now
knowledge. It is Ogilvy's argument that professionally admired advertising routinely fails to
sell — **direct counter-evidence to assumption 13 in the falsification register**, on which our
entire human-acceptance evaluation design rests. SPEC-02 threw it away.

## Assumptions: survived, weakened, falsified

**Falsified**
- *Current product consumption is a valid admission criterion for durable knowledge.* Failed in
  both directions, with internal evidence from SPEC-02's own counter-example.
- *A single shared string vocabulary can be seeded from early sources.* Zero cross-source reuse
  across six sources.

**Weakened**
- *Atoms are sufficient to represent creative expertise.* Three systems carry claims no member
  states; one atom contains a relationship in its own principle text.
- *The current corpus is representative enough to bootstrap the Canon.* Three fields absorbed most
  bindings; `delivery`, `acceptance`, `creative.hook`, `copy.cta`, `brand.logo`,
  `brand.mandatories`, `brand.prohibitions` and `video.dialogue_intent` still have none.
- *Decimal confidence is a useful authority signal.* Never calibrated; actively misleading.

**Survived**
- *Creative and production knowledge must be separated.* Reinforced from two directions — four
  production candidates with nowhere to go, and Lupton's independent statement that ranking belongs
  to content while cue choice is a separate decision.
- *`entities` and `relationships` were correctly specified.* Independently confirmed by sources
  from 1983 and 2009. Molly Bang's `relationships` binding was wrong; the field is not.
- *Two-pass discipline is necessary.* Every physical-production repair remains untranslated. The
  temptation was strongest in the source with the least Creative IR yield, which is the point.
- *Provenance must be page-and-figure precise.* The Molly Bang visual pass revised four objects and
  found two confounds; chapter-level provenance could not have supported it.

**Unresolved**
- Whether the Canon improves front-model reasoning at all. Untested; everything is downstream.
- Whether book-derived terms and real model failures can actually be joined — assumption 4, the
  load-bearing one, and still zero empirical terms exist to test it.
- Whether systems are necessary or reconstructible from intra-source relations alone.
- Whether retrieval is the right consumption mechanism. Never examined against alternatives.
- Naming convention for the ontology. Must be decided before seeding.

## Can the six-source material now be represented without loss or forced fitting?

**Yes, with one qualification.**

Everything discarded has a home: 16 of 19 notes are knowledge, 3 are correctly human-learning only.
Everything manufactured has been dropped: 2 bindings. Everything systems-level has a container: 10
systems. Everything untranslatable stays untranslated: 4 production candidates, all marked. Every
source term is preserved: 89 terms, zero merges. All evidence is now categorical and factual.

The qualification is **Lupton**. All nine objects carry `extraction_uncertainty:
column_interleaving`. The architecture records the uncertainty faithfully, but recording is not
resolving — those claims still rest on de-interleaving decisions an extractor made, and need
checking against the printed page before anything depends on them.

## Smallest next experiment

**Not more books. Test assumption 4 — the join.**

Take twenty real generation failures from existing material: the `spike/` outputs, the Devanagari
frames from Finding 01, whatever the live WhatsApp product has logged. For each, write the failure
in the observer's own words as an `origin: empirical` term. Then attempt to relate it to any of
the 42 book-derived terms using SPEC-05 relationships.

Three outcomes, all informative:
- **Most map cleanly** — the Canon/Empirical join exists and the ontology is worth building.
- **Most map to nothing** — books predict problems that generative models do not have, and the
  Canon's value is not where we assumed.
- **Most map to several equally well** — the terms are too coarse to route a repair.

Cost is roughly Finding 01: a sitting, no generation spend, existing material. It tests the
assumption everything else rests on, and it is the only experiment in the register that can be run
today without building anything.

The A/B/C experiment stays queued behind it. It costs real money and answers assumption 6; this
one is free and answers whether the architecture we just wrote has a purpose.
