# Canon — definition of done (v0, 2026-09-22)

Status: PROPOSED by the Controller on the founder's instruction ("I would rather have complete
canon once than spending time and money again and again to fix it"). Written before any pack is
built. Nothing here compiles, ingests or adopts anything; the amendment to C-10 is a separate
DRAFT decision (`coordination/decisions/CONTROLLER-CANON-COMPLETION-2026-09-22.md`).

## Why a definition first

Two of ten packs were compiled in September, marked PROPOSED, never adopted, and the rest were
frozen by C-10 pending "a real runtime failure" that could never occur because no production job
ran through the runtime. Nobody had written down what a finished pack had to deliver, so nobody
could say the two were finished — or that the eight were needed. This document is that test. It
is code (`canon_done.py`) where it can be, and a protocol where it cannot.

## The test — `python3 canon/validation/canon_done.py`

| # | Condition | How it is checked |
|---|---|---|
| A | On every recorded brief the lookup selects the packs the trigger table names, **injects every one of them, records 0 gaps**; every injected pack carries an adoption status (not PROPOSED); each pack ≤ 3,000 tokens, the prefix ≤ 30,000; `validate_compiled_pack.py` green | `runtime.canon.normalize` + `lookup` on the Mokobara, RentOK A, RentOK V2 and Cumin briefs read from their branches |
| B | **Coverage:** every claim id a producer retrieved by hand on a recorded job is delivered for that brief — cited by an injected pack, or returned by the fetcher | `HAND-RETRIEVED-CLAIMS.yaml` (generated by `hand_retrieved_claims.py` from the job branches: 87 distinct claims, 23 sources, 0 unknown ids) ∩ pack citations ∪ fetcher output |
| C | **Blind comparison** (protocol, §C below): compiled packs do not make the board worse than the hand-read claims did, on the same briefs, judged by a non-author | recorded once, before adoption |
| D | A Controller decision adopts each compiled pack by name | `coordination/decisions/CONTROLLER-*.md` |

Exit 0 only when A, B and D pass and C is recorded.

## The fetcher — measured, and it is not the mechanism (OBSERVED)

The plan of 22 Sep proposed reusing the CANON-015 retrieval code (PR #84, draft, 31 Aug: fixed
question catalogue → BM25 within object kind → bounded accepted-only bundle) as the code path for
the eight uncompiled packs. Measured with `fetcher_adapter.py` on the 37-source corpus (its tests
fail only on the pinned 24-source fingerprint):

| Query | Budget | Items | Hand-retrieved claims delivered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mokobara brief text | default (~7k tokens) | 7 | **0 of 22** |
| Mokobara brief text | 3× (~17k tokens) | 22 | 0 of 22 |
| RentOK Lane A brief | default / 3× | 8 / 22 | 2 of 32 / 2 of 32 |
| RentOK V2 brief | default / 3× | 8 / 22 | 1 of 25 / 1 of 25 |
| Mokobara **board prose** (feeling / framing / impact of all 7 beats) | 3× | 22 | 1 of 22 |
| Mokobara producer's own justification words, ids stripped | 3× | 21 | 2 of 22 |

A lexical retriever cannot get from "he packs food and stuff and goes back" to *screen direction
must be maintained across cuts* or *humour is an accent, not a language*. The producer got there
by reading with judgement. So: **the fetcher does not replace hand retrieval, at any budget, with
any query we have.** The earlier assumption is withdrawn.

Consequences for "done":

1. **Coverage (B) must come from the compiled packs.** Each pack's decisions must carry, by id,
   the hand-retrieved claims from its sources (the table above is the seed list), plus whatever
   the domain's critical claims add. A pack that cites its seed list is a pack a machine-run
   producer can use the way the hand-run producer did.
2. **Job-specific retrieval is a model step, logged.** What worked on six jobs was a frontier
   model choosing claims against the board. In the product that step stays — as a bounded,
   recorded call (index of accepted claims in, cited ids out, every id logged with
   `retrieved_by: model` in the Stage 3 form) — and it is measured by the same coverage test
   before it is trusted. CANON-SHAPE §4's "the model never chooses" is retired for this step,
   on evidence: the lexical alternative delivers 0–2 of 22.
3. **The fetcher stays unadopted.** It may return later as a candidate generator for that model
   step; it is not on the P1 path.

## How a pack is built and checked (per pack; USD 0 in media)

1. **Seed list:** the pack's rows from `HAND-RETRIEVED-CLAIMS.yaml` plus the source's claims
   tagged to the pack's critical domains.
2. **Builder session** (one per pack, in the cloud, not on the 8 GB Mac): reads the contributor
   sources' `source-knowledge.yaml`, writes 8–12 decisions with check lines in the format of the
   two existing packs (`decision → check → cited ids`), ≤ 3,000 tokens terse text, corpus digest
   pinned. Every decision cites ids; no decision without a citation.
3. **Checker session** (did not build it): opens every cited claim and confirms the decision
   says what the claim says; runs `validate_compiled_pack.py`; runs `canon_done.py` and reports
   the pack's seed coverage; a pack under 100 % seed coverage goes back with the missing ids
   named.
4. **Order** (P1 dependence first): commercial_communication → editing_pacing_and_short_form →
   typography_and_copy → camera_and_spatial_grammar → colour_and_visual_register →
   concept_and_distinctiveness → indian_indic_context → critique_and_effectiveness. Two builders
   in parallel; the largest pack (critique, 28 sources, 63 seed claims) last.
5. **Re-run `canon_done.py` after each pack.** Done is when A, B and D pass and C is recorded.

Estimated effort: 8–12 builder-days plus checker time, ≈ 2 weeks with two builders.

## C — the blind comparison (protocol)

Purpose: know whether compiled packs help the board before we depend on them. Text only, USD 0.

- Briefs: Mokobara, RentOK V2, Cumin (three classes, three accepted/rejected outcomes on record).
- Arms, same reasoning model, same Stage 3 form: (i) ten compiled packs injected; (ii) the
  hand-retrieved claim texts for that brief injected verbatim; (iii) nothing beyond the brief.
- Output: `03-creative.json` per arm, passing `check_stage.py --through 3`.
- Judge: a session that wrote none of the boards, blind to arm labels (sha-mapped packet, as the
  RentOK experiments did), scores each board against the recorded customer verdict and the
  checker's failure list for that job (does the board prevent D1, D5, D6?), and picks a rank.
- Record: verdicts verbatim before unsealing; the result goes into the adoption decision. If (ii)
  beats (i), the packs are still adopted for coverage but the model retrieval step is ranked as
  the primary mechanism; if (iii) ties both, the honest record says so and we stop compiling.

## What this does not do

- No new sources are ingested; the 37 accepted sources are the corpus.
- The audio gap (no accepted source) and the animation/game gap (no domain) stay written down in
  the trigger table; P1 excludes speech and code-rendered animation.
- The two existing packs are not rewritten; they are extended to their seed lists and adopted
  under the same test.
