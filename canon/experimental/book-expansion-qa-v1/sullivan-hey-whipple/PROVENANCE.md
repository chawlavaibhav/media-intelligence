### Correction on authorship

The lane brief names the source as "Luke Sullivan (with Edward Boches)". **The copyright page and
Library of Congress record of this 6th edition name Luke Sullivan as sole author.** Edward Boches
was a co-author of an earlier edition and is quoted repeatedly in this one — Sullivan attributes the
branded-content checklist (Ch. 11) and the Do > Invite > Capture > Share process (Ch. 11) to him and
introduces one passage with "In a previous edition, author Edward Boches offered…". Every claim in
this extraction that originates with Boches is attributed to him inside the object rather than to
Sullivan. This is recorded rather than silently normalised.

## 7. Extraction stance for this source

Sullivan writes in a comic, profane, digressive register. **The reasoning was extracted, not the
voice.** Where the tone carries an actual claim — that a certain kind of advertising insults or
irritates its reader, that an exclamation point makes a brand sound desperate — the claim is
extracted and the joke is not.

Refused wholesale: war stories with no reusable principle, agency-life colour, industry gossip,
career and portfolio advice, motivational passages, and the long runs of award-show examples that
carry no mechanism. See `EXTRACTION-NOTES.md` §"What was deliberately not extracted".
