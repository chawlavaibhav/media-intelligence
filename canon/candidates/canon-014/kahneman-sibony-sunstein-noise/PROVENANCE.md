## Why this book is here — and how tightly it was scoped

This is **not** a book about creative craft and this extraction does not pretend it is. It earns a
place for one reason: this project's acceptance model depends on **human judgement of media
outcomes** and it has no source about how judgement behaves.

Extraction was restricted to what bears on **judging, evaluating, rating and deciding**: the
noise/bias distinction and its vocabulary; the mechanisms by which independent judges of the same
case diverge and why the divergence is invisible to them; the noise audit and how it is constructed;
decision hygiene; failure conditions and diagnostic cues; the boundary conditions and the authors'
own objections; and where mechanical aggregation beats holistic judgement.

## The extraction's central caution, restated

*Noise* is about **human professional judgement in organisations**. It says nothing whatever about
machine or model evaluators, and no SourceKnowledge object in this lane claims or implies anything
about them. Applying any of this to an automated media-evaluation pipeline is **our** extrapolation.
It appears only inside OperationalBindings, with honest `applicability.limits`, and the bindings that
cross that gap carry `evidence_basis: extractor_inference`. Where the extrapolation could not be made
honestly at all, the thought is recorded as an explicitly-labelled open question in
`EXTRACTION-NOTES.md` and nowhere else.
