## What was compared

Six real customer briefs, the same six EVAL-037 used, in three columns.

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **observed EVAL-037** | What the Sonnet CONTROLLED_CANON lane actually received, read out of the committed transcripts on `work/eval-037-sonnet-controlled-canon`. Real behaviour and real byte counts — the only column that is evidence about a model rather than about code. |
| **replayed EVAL-037** | The same 53 recorded queries re-issued through EVAL-037's own unmodified `canon_tools.py` against today's corpus. A control: it separates the interface change from any corpus change. |
| **CANON-015** | One `canon_context` call per brief, default budgets. |

The accepted-Canon bytes are identical in both eras — the fingerprint above recomputes to
the value the corpus index committed — so the size and composition comparison is fair.
EVAL-037 could additionally reach HOLD candidates and Q&A; CANON-015 cannot, and that
difference in what is *reachable* is itself one of the findings.

The accepted share before ranges from **26%** (B01, the
Indian-market video brief) to **88%** (B05, the café dialogue scene). The pattern is
readable: briefs whose subject matter is well covered by *accepted* Canon — shot grammar and
continuity — drew mostly accepted material, while briefs about Indian consumer context and
product photography drew mostly HOLD material, because that is where the held candidates
sit. A model working the Indian-market brief was reading knowledge the project has not
admitted roughly three times out of four.

B04 is the weakest spread after the change at 4 distinct sources against a cap of 8. That is a
corpus fact, not a retriever fault: accepted Canon has comparatively little on creator-style
performance video.

## Why an unbounded default is not an option

Quoted from the committed lane results, not re-run:

- **sonnet_full_canon_repair_001** (claude-sonnet-5, 18 trials): `{'failed_execution': 16, 'complete': 2}`

- **gemma_required_canon** (gemma-4-31b-it, 18 trials): `{'failed_technical': 18}`

The repaired Sonnet lane completed 2 of 18 with 16 context overflows. The mandatory-unbounded
Gemma lane exposed roughly 1.13M tokens against a far smaller provider quota and failed all
18 on technical grounds. Neither is evidence against Canon knowledge; both are evidence
against handing it over without a bound.

## What this evaluation does NOT measure
Relevance, usefulness, and any effect on an accepted customer outcome. No item in the corpus is labelled relevant to any brief, so no precision or recall number is computed. Use HUMAN-REVIEW-RUBRIC.md for relevance and a controlled model experiment for outcome effect.

One further number is reported per brief in the JSON and should be read with care.
`declared_need_term_coverage` asks how many of the discriminative words the EVAL-037 model
itself wrote when stating its knowledge needs appear anywhere in the CANON-015 bundle. It is
a **lexical proxy**. A high value does not mean the need was answered and a low value does
not mean it was missed. It is reported because it is checkable and labelled because it is
weak. Do not promote it to a relevance score.

For relevance, use `HUMAN-REVIEW-RUBRIC.md` and the six bundles under `bundles/`. For
outcome effect — whether any of this raises the accepted-outcome rate or lowers Cost per
Accepted Outcome — only a controlled model experiment can answer, and CANON-015 neither ran
one nor is authorised to.

## Reproducing

```bash
git fetch origin work/eval-037-sonnet-controlled-canon work/eval-037-gemma-controlled-canon \
                 work/eval-037-gemma-required-canon work/eval-037-sonnet-full-canon-repair
python3 canon/retrieval/evaluation/build_eval_set.py --out canon/retrieval/evaluation/EVAL-SET-v0.1.yaml
python3 canon/retrieval/evaluation/run_offline_eval.py
```

Both steps are read-only over `eval/` and write only inside `canon/retrieval/evaluation/`.
Note that the committed bundles under `bundles/` are pretty-printed for reading, so their
file sizes are larger than the `size.total_chars` each one reports; the reported number is
the compact serialisation a model would actually be charged for.
