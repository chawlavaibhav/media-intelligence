## Why this section

The Introduction is where this book's framework exists **as a framework**. It states all six
principles with their definitions, names the single obstacle all six are aimed at, states how they
are meant to be used together and — importantly — how they are not, and carries most of the book's
evidence: the tappers-and-listeners study, the Halloween candy research, the Israeli
advertising-template study, the popcorn campaign.

The six chapters each develop one principle. Taking a chapter would have produced one principle in
depth and no framework; taking the Introduction produces the framework with each principle at the
depth the authors themselves introduce it. Given the instruction to preserve the interacting
framework rather than reduce it to disconnected rules, the Introduction is the correct unit.

**Stated scope limit:** chapters 1–6 and the epilogue were **not** processed. Each principle object
therefore records what the Introduction says and supports, not the book's full case for that
principle. Where an object's evidence looks thin, that is scope, not a judgement about the book.

## Visual evidence — inspected; page layout does not exist

`visual_completeness: not_verified_page_layout`.

Every image in the book was enumerated and every distinct one was opened. **There are no figures,
tables, charts or diagrams anywhere in the processed section, and none in the book.** All nineteen
images are chapter-title graphics, cover art, or a small tick-mark ornament used beside checklist
items in later chapters. The book argues entirely in words.

**One real finding.** The section's own title — the single word "INTRODUCTION" — exists **only** as
a picture, a 238×39 JPEG with no alt text. The same holds for all six chapter titles: this EPUB sets
its top-level headings as images of words. A text-only extraction produces a section with no title
on it. Recovered here by opening the image. The fourteen sub-headings *inside* the section are real
text and extract correctly, so the structure below the top level is intact.

**Why the completeness value is not `verified_page_level`.** An EPUB reflows; there is no page to
inspect and no printed page number to cite. The figures were checked and there are none, but the
claim "nothing was lost from the page" cannot honestly be made about a file that has no pages. For
this source — continuous prose, nothing shown — the practical cost is low. The provenance cost is
real and is stated rather than disguised: **every object in this extraction locates itself by
section heading rather than by page range**, which is a weaker locator than the PDF-sourced books
in this batch carry.

## A note that governs how the evidence fields read

This source's support is overwhelmingly **published research by named third parties**: Elizabeth
Newton's 1990 Stanford dissertation, Joel Best and Gerald Horiuchi's Halloween study, a 1999 Israeli
advertising-template study and its follow-up training experiment, a 1985 ABC News poll.

SPEC-03 defines `empirical_within_source` as "the source reports its own measurement", and the fixed
characteristic list has no value for cited external research. The least assumptive faithful choice
was made: **`empirical_within_source` was not applied to any third-party study**, and each cited
study is instead recorded in full — researcher, design, comparison group and figures — in a caveat
marked `extractor_observed`.

The consequence must be stated plainly so the file is not misread: **this book's structured evidence
profile reads weaker than the book actually is.** Its best-supported claims and its bare assertions
carry similar characteristic sets. That is a gap in the vocabulary, not a verdict on the source, and
it is logged as a Lane C issue.
