# Provenance record — Claude C. Hopkins, *Scientific Advertising*, chapters 1–7
**CANON-003 Lane C, book 13**

**Book:** Claude C. Hopkins, *Scientific Advertising* (first published 1923). The local copy is an
Internet Archive scan of a 2009 Snowball Publishing / BN Publishing reprint, 88 PDF pages, 21
chapters.
**Section processed:** chapters 1–7, printed pages 1–24 (PDF pages 9–32).

## Why this section

Hopkins's chapters run 2–4 printed pages each, so no single chapter is large enough to expose his
reasoning system. Chapters 1–7 are the connected span that does:

- **Chapter 1** states where he thinks advertising knowledge comes from — keyed advertisements,
  traced returns, comparison, and promotion of a winner to a "fixed principle".
- **Chapters 2–4** give the three doctrines he derives from it: advertising is salesmanship,
  advertising must offer service, and mail order is the school that proves both.
- **Chapters 5–7** apply the doctrines to headlines, psychology and specificity.

**Stated scope limit:** chapters 8–21 were not processed. The notable exclusion is **chapter 15,
"Test Campaigns"**, which chapter 1 explicitly forward-references. This extraction's inventory of
what evidence the source actually supplies for its measurement claims therefore covers chapters 1–7
only, and is **not** the book's complete case. Whether chapter 15 reports measurements that these
chapters do not is **NOT VERIFIED**.

## Visual evidence — VERIFIED, and the finding is that there is none

`visual_completeness: verified_page_level`. All 24 pages of the section were rendered and inspected
before any claim was written. The section contains **zero** figures, illustrations, tables, diagrams
or reproduced advertisements. Every page is a single column of body text with a running header and a
page-number footer.

Two things follow, and they are different:

1. **No visual information is lost by text extraction.** For this source class the risk that
   defeated a spatially-argued source elsewhere in this batch is zero, and the visual pass correctly
   returns nothing rather than nothing-detectable.
2. **The source's own evidence is absent from the source.** Hopkins argues from specific
   advertisements — the Mead Cycle Company's, the incubator advertisement with and without its row
   of chickens, the brewer's copy, "Our net profit is 3 per cent" — and tells the reader to look at
   them and note their headlines, type and use of space. Not one is reproduced. This is not
   digitisation loss; a 1923 reader was in the same position. No better scan would repair it.

No claim in this extraction is recorded as visually demonstrated.

## Method note carried into every object

The source describes itself as scientific, exact and law-governed throughout. Those descriptions are
recorded as **the source's claims**. Each object's `evidence.characteristics` record what the source
actually supplies for the claim in question. Where the source asserts that measurement was performed
but reports no measurement, that gap is written into a caveat marked `extractor_observed` rather
than being recorded as `empirical_within_source`. Nothing was upgraded and nothing was dismissed.
