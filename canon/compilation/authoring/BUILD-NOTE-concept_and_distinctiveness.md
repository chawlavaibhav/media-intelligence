## 1. Decisions (10)

- CD-D1 The proposition and the product in it: one unique, persuasive promise; core by exclusion; one adjective; five big-idea questions and the thirty-year test; benefit load-bearing (unretellable without it); product as hero of the picture.
- CD-D2 Distinctive vs generic: remarkability by breaking a category expectation or leaving a question open; learn then refuse the category's worn images; the untested is where advantage lies (overreach, not reason, is the target); inversion only in full opposition, bounded by relevance.
- CD-D3 Borrowed forms and codes: borrowed equity only re-angled; templates are forms not ideas; no analogies; Indian-cinema type/dress/prop codes are dated and audience-scoped; colour identifies only if established earlier; keep an established mark.
- CD-D4 Story shape: customer hero, brand guide; truth × consequent conflict; no conflict no story; absence usually beats presence; stakes named and dosed; the three questions always answerable.
- CD-D5 Surprise vs curiosity vs release order: violate an expectation in frame one, one burr, no scene parade; surprise decays so open and fill a gap; the material is the order of release; name in ten seconds, end on the pack.
- CD-D6 Concreteness and credibility: actions and sensory detail; one individual; specifics not superlatives; flag the buyer or place; no exclamation marks.
- CD-D7 Feeling and register: decide the feeling first (what they remember is how they felt); the effective emotion is often not the obvious one; emotion with a rational excuse; interesting before funny; humour only for the few who can write it; celebrity/cartoon/vignette below average; respect and delight.
- CD-D8 Clear/clever and the fuse: the overlap and its two failure marks; flat then spin; 5–40 % travel against the quick get and the medium's dwell time; exaggeration knowingly and on a truth; puns and rhymes cleared first.
- CD-D9 Leaving out and surviving 15–30 s: grunt test; removal test; most with the least; the six-criteria sacrifice order (emotion, story, rhythm first) with its hedges; every device motivated and unnoticed; staging can carry attention; never two competing cues; strength by contrast.
- CD-D10 Sound for the idea: music channels an emotion already made (hedged "seems to"); source music, overuse; opening music must not promise a register the film does not keep; withdrawn dialogue; on-camera speech, supers = spoken words, jingles below average.
