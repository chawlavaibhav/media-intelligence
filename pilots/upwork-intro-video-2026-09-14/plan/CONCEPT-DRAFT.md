# Creative Director concept memo — DRAFT (pre-research, to be tested against preprod/*)

Status: written before COMMERCIAL-BRIEF / CANON-BRIEF / ROUTE-ANALYSIS landed. Every claim below
is provisional until checked against those three files. Do not build from this version.

## The problem in one line
An Upwork buyer scrolling near-identical "AI video creator" profiles must feel within five seconds
that this seller makes better ads and is faster — and must be told, without jargon, what to do next.

## Candidate concepts explored

### C1 — "The honest presenter" (candour as the hook)
A composed, premium AI-generated presenter opens with an admission — nobody in the film is real,
except the ads — and hands the film over to the work. The presenter returns once for the trust beat
("I'm the AI; Vaibhav is the human who checks every file") and once for the close. ~20 s of
presenter, ~40 s of work. Identity crossings: 2–3, all anchored to one portrait still.
+ Turns the AI presenter from a trust liability into the proof of capability.
+ Names the human owner → Upwork accountability preserved.
+ Presenter screen time small → drift risk small.
− Depends on image-to-video WITH native speech being dispatchable (route question).

### C2 — "One morning" (time as the spine)
Whole film happens between 09:00 and 13:00 IST. Open on morning light, brief arrives; the work
builds; close on midday light, files back. Speed demonstrated by the light moving, not a stopwatch.
+ Elegant, understated, Indian daylight; quick-commerce framing without saying it.
− On its own it lacks a hook; best used as the frame for C1, not instead of it.

### C3 — "The world becomes the ads" (progressive impossibility around a composed presenter)
Environment transforms around the presenter — wall becomes a feed, table becomes a marble ledge.
+ Most ambitious visually.
− Identity continuity through environment transformations is the exact thing the evidence says is
weak (reference person 0/2, cross-clip 0/2). High redraw exposure inside a ₹3,000 cap.
→ Keep the *idea* (escalating ambition) but move it INSIDE the ads: the deliverables themselves
escalate from static → text in motion → three-shot story → one genuinely ambitious hero clip. Every
escalation stays on the menu (each is a 10–15 s video ad or a static).

### C4 — Pure demo with voice-over (no presenter)
Safest production; weakest differentiation — this is what every competitor's reel looks like, and
it ignores the owner's instinct for a face.

## Working choice
C1 framed by C2, with C3's escalation carried by the ads. One presenter, three short takes, all
image-to-video from one accepted portrait still so the first frame pins identity. Speech only ever
on camera and only ever generated natively with the picture; the work sections carry no voice-over
(no second, mismatched voice) — they speak through exact code-set text and music.

## Speed, scale, breadth — demonstrated not asserted
- Speed: light moves from morning to noon between the open and the close; two exact time cards.
- Scale: one approved ad multiplies — four sizes fan out, six headline variants, one Hindi flip —
all code-composed from one plate, so it is exact and it is literally how the service works.
- Breadth: the escalation ladder (static → motion text → story clip → hero clip).

## Provisional script (spoken words only, target ≤ 90)
T1 (open, presenter): "Nobody in this film is real. Except the ads."
T2 (mid, presenter): "I'm the AI. Vaibhav is the human. We agree your words first; he checks
every file before it reaches you."
T3 (close, presenter): "Standard is twenty-four hours. Express is four. Send your product link —
you'll get a straight price."
End card: Adwisely mark · "Send your product link."
