# Maya — value spine for the 55 s Upwork intro

Owner's verdict on v4: "faster, at scale" is what every AI-video profile says; the buyer's gain was never named. v4 spoke the overview's bullet list. Here the speech carries the gain; the silent work carries the features.

## 1. The core value, in the buyer's words

**"I send a link and an offer; back comes a set of finished ads with my price written right, ready to upload, so this week's test actually runs."**

Not speed, not scale. The buyer tests weekly; what they lose when creative is late or the price is wrong is the test slot. The product is a test that runs on time. v4 sold the factory; the buyer is buying Tuesday.

## 2. Opening (8 s, ≤ 18 words incl. disclosure)

**A — PICK.** "Meta ads for this week's test. Your offer, word for word. I'm not real. The ads are." (17)
Buyer's search word, the outcome, and the one objection they hold about AI creative (the text), before the candour beat. No method, no speed.

**B.** "Meta ads, finished and upload-ready, every week you test. I'm not real. The ads are." (16)
Leads with the object received — finished, not drafts — and implies standing supply. Weaker on the text objection.

**C.** "Short of creatives to test? Finished Meta ads, your words exactly. I'm not real. The ads are." (17)
Names the pain in the buyer's phrase. A question opener selects hard but reads as pitch; use only if A tests flat on a stranger.

## 3. Closing (7 s, ≤ 16 words)

"Standard, twenty-four hours. Express, four hours. Send your product link. You get a straight price." (16; "twenty-four" is one beat)

Standard first, express second, CTA last. The IST window super rides this frame (never "4 hours" without it). "First test pack" moves to end-card small print.

## 4. Work supers (benefit, not feature; ≤ 6 words)

1. Four sizes — **Feed, Stories, Reels, WhatsApp. Ready.** The placements the buyer fills, not our count.
2. Hooks, then Hindi — **Six angles, one approval. Hindi too.** A week of tests from one sign-off; Hindi once, as promised.
3. Price check — **Caught here, not in your feed.** The fear is a wrong price live on Meta; the check keeps it off the account.
4. Video ads — **Reels for the same test. No shoot.** Video variants without a production; clip length is our constraint, not their gain.
5. Trust card — **You approve the words first.** / **Vaibhav checks every file before it reaches you.** Mandated wording, on screen only, so the presenter never narrates process. Line two is 8 words; the brief's sentence outranks the cap unless the owner trims it.
6. End card — **Send your product link. Straight price.** The last benefit: no call, no haggling. Standard line and Adwisely mark stay as v4.

## 5. What NOT to say (spoken)

1. **"The machine builds" / any pipeline narration.** Method is what identical profiles lead with; the buyer is not buying how. The silent build shows it.
2. **The inventory read aloud — six hooks, four sizes, 10–15 seconds, unlimited variants.** Spoken counts are capacity boasts. On screen, subordinate, only.
3. **Turnaround twice.** v4 said "twenty-four hours" at 3 s and at 44 s. Speed before the work is a claim; once after it, a term.
