# Creative Director concept — v2 (after COMMERCIAL-BRIEF; before CANON/ROUTE integration)

Supersedes CONCEPT-DRAFT.md. Built for the gate decision, not yet frozen.

## The fact that changed the plan
Upwork's help article "Profile introduction video" (support.upwork.com, article 34959172682003;
confirmed via search summary, not fetched) says the introduction video **must be a video of you
and no one else**. A fully AI-made film with a generated presenter and no owner in it breaks a
platform rule on the profile it is meant to sell. The live overview also tells buyers presenters
and lip-synced people are "not on the menu"; the profile's banned-words list includes avatar,
spokesperson, talking head.

## Recommended concept — "The human and the AI" (two-hander)
Two speakers, call and response. **Vaibhav, real phone footage** (4 short lines, ~10 s total,
one shooting session per the existing recording spec) and **"the AI"**, a generated presenter
whose speech is generated natively with the picture (the route the lab passed 6/6), anchored to
one portrait still so identity holds across 3 short takes. Between them, ~35 s of the work
itself, carried by music and exact code-set text — no voice-over, so no third mismatched voice.

Why this beats the alternatives:
- Satisfies Upwork's "video of you" rule (he is in it) while keeping his instinct for a
  character to camera and his rule that the film is the demo.
- The AI presenter is declared AI in the first two seconds, which turns the biggest trust risk on
  Upwork (is this seller real? is this AI slop?) into the hook, and names the accountable human.
- The buyer sees, not hears, the offer: a genuinely designed ad appears within 6 s.
- Cheap where it is risky (3 short AI takes), free where it is exact (all text, all layouts, all
  variants composed by code), ambitious where models are strong (fabric, steam, light, slow orbit).

## Fallbacks if the owner rules differently
- **Fully AI, accept the rule risk:** same film; Vaibhav's lines move to the AI presenter or to
  cards; ~14 s more AI presenter time (5 takes, higher drift exposure). Not recommended.
- **Fully AI, no presenter:** the work section expands to the whole film with cards; loses the
  hook and the human. Not recommended, but compliant with "no presenter" and cheapest.
- **AI likeness of Vaibhav:** not recommended — reference-person route 0/2 in the lab, and it
  edges the rule's "should not mislead clients about who you are".
