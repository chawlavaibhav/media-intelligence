# <Stream> Handoff

**COMMUNICATION STANDARD:** `shared/COMMUNICATION-STANDARD.md` applies. Explain technical ideas in plain English, including what they mean, why they matter and their practical consequence; use minimum sufficient wording; do not invent; separate evidence from inference.

**PURPOSE:** what this stream is for, one paragraph in plain English
**CURRENT STATE:** what exists right now, factually; explain unfamiliar internal terms needed to understand the state
**CURRENT APPROVED DECISIONS:** decisions the Controller has actually signed off, plus the practical consequence of each where it matters
**LAST COMPLETED TASK:** ID + one-line explanation of what it established
**CURRENT TASK / QUEUE:** ID(s), or "none — awaiting Controller"
**IMPORTANT OBSERVATIONS:** things a fresh worker needs to know before touching this stream; explain why each matters rather than only naming a metric/status
**OPEN QUESTIONS:** explain the uncertainty and what decision/work it blocks
**DEPENDENCIES:** what this stream is waiting on from another stream or the Controller, and why
**PROPOSED CROSS-STREAM CHANGES:** links to any `PROPOSED-INTEGRATION-CHANGE-*.md` files raised, with one-line plain-English consequence
**NEXT APPROVED TASK:** ID, or "none yet — do not self-assign one"
