# A-TEXT comparability core — frozen contract

**Status:** FROZEN. Four items, two routes, two unseeded repeats each. Maximum **16** generations.

**External spend authorised by this document: none.** It describes what a future authorised run
would do; it does not authorise it.

---

## The question, stated narrowly on purpose

> On four frozen shared items, does this route fail badly enough on exact Devanagari/Hinglish text
> that deeper text-focused spend is already unjustified?

That is all. It is **not** the scientific admission verdict for IMG-01 or IMG-02, and it is not a
measurement of creative quality, composition, identity or commercial appeal.

## The four items

| id | target | why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| ATEXT-01 | `शुभ दीपावली` | Devanagari, festival greeting — the highest-frequency production string |
| ATEXT-02 | `आज की डील` | Devanagari, ordinary commercial phrase |
| ATEXT-03 | `Aaj ki Deal` | the same phrase in Hinglish/Latin — isolates script from meaning |
| ATEXT-04 | `SAVE 20% • ₹999` | a commercial claim: digit, symbol, bullet and rupee sign together |

ATEXT-02 and ATEXT-03 are deliberately the same phrase in two scripts. If a route renders one and
not the other, the failure is about **script**, not about the words.

## Why the prompts are so bare

Each prompt asks for a plain 1:1 poster whose only textual content is the target: no logo, no
brand mark, no product, no person, no other text anywhere in the frame.

This deliberately isolates text rather than pretending to measure creative quality. A busy prompt
would produce a prettier picture and an unattributable failure.

## Repeats are unseeded, on both routes

Both IMG-01 and IMG-02 use **unseeded repeats**, even where a route exposes a seed. The first
comparison is therefore an **inherent-variance** comparison: what does this route do when asked the
same thing twice, as production would ask it?

This evidence may **not** later be pooled with held-seed evidence. They answer different questions
and averaging them produces a number that describes neither.

## Scoring

1. **Primary** — blind `transcribe`: the qualified judge never sees the target, commits to what it
   believes is drawn, and **our code** performs the exact comparison after the frozen
   normalisation rule.
2. **Diagnostic only** — `verdict`, where the judge is shown the target. A verdict may **never**
   override a primary transcription mismatch. Where the two disagree, that disagreement is itself
   the finding: it measures how much false-pass behaviour comes from showing a model the answer
   we hope for.

If no text judge qualifies, **zero image generations run.** There is nothing to score with.

## The hard elimination rule

A route becomes eligible for an immediate **text-specific deeper-spend stop** if it records
**zero exact matches** across all scoreable Devanagari/Hinglish A-TEXT opportunities.

State that conclusion only as a result **on this frozen screen**.
It is **not a universal model incapability**, and it must never be written as one: four items,
two repeats, one prompt style and one moment in a provider's deployment history do not establish
what a model can and cannot do.

## Any non-zero result is not promotion

A route that scores above zero has cleared **one narrow screen**. It **is not promotion**.

A-TEXT is **partial evidence only**. Full Stage-A survival still requires every other instrument
family that the route's scientific slot depends on, and none of those are qualified. No route may
be recorded as a complete **Stage-A survivor** on the strength of this screen.

## What may not be reported from this screen

- No customer-outcome **CpAO**. CpAO from customer outcomes remains Stage C only.
- No claim about identity, composition, subjective quality, video or audio.
- No claim that Canon improves model outcomes.
- No Registry row from an unqualified instrument, and none at all from a dry run.

## Trial accounting

One provider call is one trial — including a call that refuses, errors or times out. Those are
persisted with their reason and **no retry**. Retries authorised: **0**.
