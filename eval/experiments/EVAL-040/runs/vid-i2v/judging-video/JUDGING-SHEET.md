# Blind judging - vid-i2v (image-to-video on the accepted round-one stills)

32 clips I01-I32, order shuffled; names hide the route. Each case shows its source still first. Judge each clip against ITS case's contract. One line per clip: accept or reject plus a note.

## VID-I2V-01 - clips I04, I14, I16, I21, I25, I27, I31, I32 (source still: VID-I2V-01__plate.png)
- ACCEPT only if the viewpoint visibly travels around the bottle (a different side of the bottle is seen at the end than at the start).
- ACCEPT only if the bottle keeps the shape, size, cap and blank label of the first frame throughout; REJECT if it warps, drifts on the surface, or grows lettering.
- REJECT if the highlight on the glass flickers, or sits at the same spot on the bottle in the first and last frame while the viewpoint has changed.
- REJECT if speech or music is present, or any lettering.

## VID-I2V-02 - clips I02, I05, I07, I12, I17, I18, I24, I26 (source still: VID-I2V-02__plate.png)

## VID-I2V-03 - clips I01, I06, I08, I09, I11, I20, I23, I28 (source still: VID-I2V-03__plate.png)
