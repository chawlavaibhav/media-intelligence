FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic video scene (6 shots, ~40 seconds, 16:9).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, low-volume confrontation between two characters in a public setting. The emotional core is betrayal and discovery. The technical priority is maintaining an unwavering spatial anchor (the 180-degree line) to ensure the viewer understands the physical opposition of the characters without needing wide establishing shots.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." Use tight framing and static compositions to trap the characters in their discomfort, using the white marble of the table as a sterile, cold divider between them.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Emotional tension (The eyes/micro-expressions).
2. The catalyst (The concealed object).
3. The spatial conflict (The physical distance across the table).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st: Eyes (intensity/avoidance), 2nd: The hand/object (evidence), 3rd: Negative space of the café.
surface_finish_per_key_object: Marble table -> glossy/direct; Coffee cups -> matte/diffuse; Concealed photograph -> glossy/direct.
implied_light_source: Large window to the side (Right of frame), creating soft directional light and subtle shadows on the faces.
placement_zone: Left zone (Person A) and Right zone (Person B) to strictly maintain the 180-degree line.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Setting: Modern, minimalist café. White marble table. Neutral tones.
- Casting: Two actors capable of conveying intensity through stillness.
- Lighting: High-key but soft, side-lit to emphasize facial contours.
- Camera: Static tripod shots. Shallow depth of field (f/2.8) to isolate subjects.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (6s): Cinematic medium shot, 16:9. Two people sitting opposite each other at a white marble café table. Person A on the left, Person B on the right. Soft side-lighting from a window. Static shot. Establish tension.
Shot 2 (8s): Medium close-up of Person A, off-center left. Eyes narrowed, focused intensely on Person B. Expression of restrained anger. Shallow depth of field, background blurred café. Static shot.
Shot 3 (7s): Medium close-up of Person B, off-center right. Looking back at Person A. Eyes darting, slight jaw tension, expression of guilt. Shallow depth of field. Static shot.
Shot 4 (5s): Close-up insert, top-down angle. A hand from the left slides a glossy photograph across the white marble table surface. Focus on the photograph. Static shot.
Shot 5 (7s): Tight close-up of Person A, off-center left. Frame focused on the lower face and chin. Stern, cold expression. Static shot.
Shot 6 (7s): Medium close-up of Person B, off-center right. Person B breaks eye contact and looks away toward the window. Deflated posture, silence. Static shot.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- The photograph: Must be a consistent image across any potential iterations (e.g., a specific house or document).
- Color Palette: Desaturated whites, greys, and a single accent color (e.g., navy blue) in the clothing.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Ambient café noise (muffled chatter, clinking porcelain) lowered in volume during dialogue to create a "vacuum" effect. Dialogue is whispered and cold.
- Edit: Straight cuts. No transitions.
- Pace: Slow, allowing the silence to build between lines.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Two people opposite at café table? Yes.
- Tense/Restrained dialogue? Yes.
- Spatial relation always understood? Yes (L/R consistency).
- Exactly six shots? Yes.
- Avoid elaborate camera movement? Yes (All static).
- Cinematic feel? Yes (Shallow DOF, motivated cuts).
- Duration ~40s? Yes (6+8+7+5+7+7 = 40s).
- Aspect ratio 16:9? Yes.
