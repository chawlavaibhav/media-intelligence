FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end, minimalist commercial shot for a Mosambi (sweet lime) sparkling drink. The image must move away from traditional ethnic tropes, instead utilizing a "Contemporary Urban Indian" aesthetic—characterized by clean lines, architectural minimalism, and a sophisticated color palette. The goal is to position the drink as a premium lifestyle choice for young professionals.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Architecture of Refreshment." A hyper-realistic, chilled glass bottle of the sparkling drink placed on a matte, warm-grey architectural surface. The visual tension is created by the contrast between the rigid, clean lines of the environment and the organic, chaotic beauty of carbonation bubbles and frosted condensation.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. The Product (Hero): Premium glass bottle, chilled, unmistakably the focus.
2. The Sensory Experience: Effervescence (bubbles) and frost (coldness).
3. The Ingredient: A single, precision-cut slice of fresh mosambi for flavor identification.
4. The Brand Positioning: Sophisticated, urban, minimal.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st read: The bottle (high tonal contrast and verticality); 2nd read: Carbonation bubbles (internal texture); 3rd read: Mosambi slice (color cue/ingredient).
surface_finish_per_key_object: Glass bottle -> glossy/direct; Liquid -> glossy/direct; Condensation droplets -> glare; Architectural surface -> matte.
implied_light_source: Large soft-box window light from the top-left.
placement_zone: Right-of-center zone to create a dynamic, asymmetric balance that suggests an open, modern space.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Construction Plan:
1. Background: A seamless, muted warm-grey or light-beige matte surface with a subtle concrete-like texture.
2. Subject: A sleek, contemporary glass bottle containing a pale, translucent citrus-yellow liquid.
3. Effects: Heavy, realistic condensation (micro-droplets) on the exterior glass. Visible, rising sparkling bubbles within the liquid.
4. Prop: A single, thin, perfectly circular slice of mosambi resting at the base of the bottle, partially overlapping the bottle's footprint.
5. Lighting: Soft, directional light from the left creating a clean, vertical specular highlight along the bottle's curve.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Commercial studio photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A premium, sleek glass bottle of sparkling Mosambi drink, chilled with heavy condensation and frost droplets on the exterior. The liquid inside is a pale, translucent citrus yellow with millions of tiny, effervescent sparkling bubbles rising. The bottle is placed off-center on a minimal, matte, warm-grey architectural concrete surface. A single, precision-cut, thin slice of fresh mosambi lime rests elegantly at the base. Lighting is a large, soft window light from the top-left, creating a clean, sophisticated vertical specular highlight on the glass. High-end product photography, 8k resolution, sharp focus, minimal composition, sophisticated urban aesthetic, neutral warm tones, hyper-realistic textures, no text, no people.
