FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
- One (1) premium advertising still image, aspect ratio 4:5, delivered at 4000×5000 px (300 dpi equivalent), RGB, retouched to print/social ad quality.
- Clean top-third negative space reserved for brand logo/tagline to be composited in post (not baked into the generative output).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The client is launching a modern Indian sparkling beverage (mosambi + sparkling water) targeting urban 22–35 year-olds who associate "premium" with restraint, texture, and craft rather than logos or heritage motifs. The image must instantly read as a serious, adult beverage-brand product shot — closer to a boutique tonic/craft-soda or premium cold-pressed juice campaign than a mass-market soft drink — while the fruit itself (mosambi) remains visually identifiable so the flavor promise isn't lost. No stereotypical "Indian-ness" (no paisley, henna, marigolds, elephants, flags, saris, temples). No celebrity, no third-party branding, no external references.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Quiet Fizz" — a single frozen moment of understated luxury: a slim glass bottle of the drink standing on warm travertine stone, condensation beading on the glass, a halved mosambi resting beside it with visible juicy flesh, and a filled glass mid-fizz catching soft window light. The mood is that of a design-forward café or a minimalist kitchen counter in a contemporary Indian home — natural materials (stone, linen, brass, matte ceramic) replace clichéd cultural signifiers, doing the cultural work through material and color sensibility rather than iconography. The mosambi itself becomes the only "Indian" cue needed — familiar, unforced, and appetizing.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product bottle (hero, largest, sharpest focus, brightest highlight) — communicates "premium sparkling mosambi drink."
2. Halved mosambi fruit + juice glass — communicates flavor authenticity and refreshment.
3. Condensation, bubbles, light — communicate coldness/carbonation/craft.
4. Negative space top-third — reserved for wordmark/tagline (added later, not generated).
5. Background props (stone, linen, brass) — support premium/contemporary mood, never compete with product.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: warm stone beige/travertine background, muted terracotta shadows, mosambi's pale green-yellow flesh as the accent "pop" color, brushed brass/copper for warm metallic accents, off-white linen texture. No neon, no primary red/blue soda colors.
- Materials: honed stone slab surface, linen napkin with visible weave, brushed brass small dish or spoon, matte-finish glass bottle (not glossy plastic-shiny), thin crown cap or cork-topped closure in matte bronze.
- Bottle design: tall slim glass silhouette (mineral-water proportions, not cola-bottle curves), subtle pale sage-tinted glass, minimal wraparound label covering roughly the middle third, thin embossed line-art of a mosambi leaf/branch as the only decorative motif, small clean wordmark space (left blank/placeholder for logo lock-up).
- Lighting: single soft directional key light (simulating window light) from upper left, gentle rim light upper right to separate bottle edge from background, soft natural shadow falling right, no harsh studio glare, no theatrical colored gels.
- Composition: vertical 4:5 frame; bottle placed on right-of-center vertical third, filling ~65–70% of frame height; glass with drink positioned lower-left, slightly out of crisp focus (shallow depth via longer lens compression); halved mosambi fruit in the immediate foreground bottom edge, partially cropped for intimacy; top third left largely negative/soft-focus stone texture for logo placement.
- Depth of field: product bottle tack-sharp, foreground fruit slightly soft, background very soft bokeh — simulating macro/product lens (~100mm equivalent, f/6.3–f/8).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Style reference: contemporary editorial beverage/product photography (think boutique tonic or artisanal soda campaigns), not soft-drink advertising, not food-blog flat-lay.
- Camera behavior: product-photography macro lens simulation, eye-level to slightly elevated angle (~15° above horizontal), tripod-stable framing, no wide-angle distortion.
- Lighting behavior: single large soft key (softbox/window simulation) camera-left, subtle bounce fill camera-right, controlled specular highlight on bottle shoulder and cap only.
- Styling detail: condensation droplets realistic and physically consistent with light direction; bubbles in glass fine and natural (not cartoon-large); mosambi rendered anatomically correct — larger and more oblate than a lime, pale yellow-green mottled skin, pithy white interior, pale straw-yellow juicy segments (must not look like an orange or lemon).
- Color grade: warm neutral grade, slightly desaturated background, saturation boost reserved only for the mosambi flesh and drink liquid to keep eye on product.
