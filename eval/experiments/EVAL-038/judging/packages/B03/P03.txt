FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end commercial product shot for a Mosambi sparkling drink targeting urban Indian millennials/Gen Z. The image must communicate "sophisticated refreshment" by avoiding folk clichés and instead using a modern, minimalist visual language. The product must be the singular focus.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Contemporary Crisp." A high-contrast, minimalist composition featuring a chilled, condensation-covered glass bottle of the drink set against a muted, modern architectural surface. The visual tension is created by the vibrant yellow-gold of the mosambi liquid against a cool, neutral, contemporary background.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Identity (Hero bottle/label).
2. Sensory Appeal (Carbonation bubbles, ice-cold condensation).
3. Ingredient Origin (A single, precision-cut slice of fresh mosambi).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st read: The bottle (dominant cue: size and central contrast); 2nd read: The bubbles/droplets (dominant cue: specular highlights); 3rd read: The mosambi slice (dominant cue: saturated color).
surface_finish_per_key_object: Glass bottle -> glossy/direct; Liquid -> diffuse; Condensation droplets -> glossy/direct; Background surface -> matte/diffuse.
implied_light_source: Large softbox, top-rear (11 o'clock position) creating a rim light and long, soft shadows.
placement_zone: Right zone; provides room for the mosambi slice and negative space to create a sophisticated, asymmetrical balance.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Construction Plan:
1. Background: A dark, muted teal or slate-grey polished concrete surface.
2. Subject: A sleek, modern glass bottle containing a pale gold carbonated liquid.
3. Styling: Heavy, fine-mist condensation on the glass. Small, sharp bubbles clinging to the interior walls.
4. Prop: One thin, translucent, circular slice of fresh mosambi placed slightly in front and to the left of the bottle.
5. Lighting: Top-rear key light to define the silhouette (rim lighting) and create a bright specular strip down the side of the glass.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Commercial product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A sleek, modern glass bottle of sparkling mosambi drink, chilled with heavy fine-mist condensation and glistening droplets. The liquid is a translucent pale gold with active carbonation bubbles. The bottle is positioned in the right third of the frame. Beside it, a single, thin, precision-cut slice of fresh mosambi. The setting is a minimalist, matte slate-grey polished concrete surface. Lighting: a large softbox from the top-rear creating a bright, clean rim light along the bottle's edge and a soft, diffused shadow stretching forward. Shallow depth of field with a blurred, neutral architectural background. High tonal contrast between the gold liquid and the cool grey ground. Sophisticated, clean, urban aesthetic.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
The brand label on the bottle must be composited deterministically after generation to ensure typography and logo correctness.
