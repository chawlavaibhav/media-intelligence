## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Product Setup:**  
1. Premium glass bottle (modern, clean design — not decorative or ornamental) filled with golden mosambi-coloured sparkling water
2. Professional label applied (product name, design pre-determined for legibility at image scale)
3. Bottle positioned standing upright in upper-right frame zone
4. Liquid clarity and visible carbonation bubbles critical — carbonation catch is a key visual signal of "refreshing"

**Ground:**  
Minimal, clean surface — white, pale grey, or subtle warm-neutral matte finish. No texture, pattern, or decoration. Function: tonal separation only.

**Lighting:**  
- **Key light:** Large soft source (5×7 ft softbox or equivalent diffusion) from upper-left at 45° angle, ~50° above horizontal. Creates gentle diffuse shadow beneath bottle and soft, controlled highlight on front of glass showing premium quality without harshness or lens artifacts.
- **Fill/Bounce (optional):** Minimal white bounce board or fill light opposite key to keep shadows clean and side-lighting subtle, maintaining brightness and "refreshing" mood without flattening depth.
- **Backlight (optional):** Very subtle rim light from far side if needed to separate bottle edge, but secondary — key light dominance is maintained.
- **Practical:** Lighting is tuned so that carbonation bubbles and liquid clarity read bright and fresh; bottle glass shows premium quality through controlled speculars; background remains plain.

**Camera Position & Angle:**  
Straight-on or very slight low angle (to show liquid interior and bottle top) at neutral height approximately mid-bottle. Focus: tack-sharp on entire bottle and liquid interior.

- Off-centre placement in zone (not grid-based)
- Classical balance (bottle weight offset by bright ground; tonal weight agrees with size)
- Tight fit with deliberate breathing gap (bottle has space; frame edges do not trap it)
- No secondary focal elements; bottle is sole statement

**Avoidances (brief-driven):**  
✗ No garnish (fruit slice, leaf, etc.)  
✗ No straw  
✗ No hand  
✗ No secondary beverages  
✗ No ornamental "Indian" visual elements (sari fabric, patterns, temple references, etc.)  
✗ No ice (unless minimal, clean condensation only — avoid cliché "cold drip" trope)  
✗ No vintage or retro styling (contemporary premium only)
