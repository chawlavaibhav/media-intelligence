FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium, print/social-ready advertising still image, 4:5 aspect ratio (e.g., 1600×2000 px minimum, scalable to 4000×5000 px for print), full colour, hero product photography style. Single final image only.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Launch-style hero shot for a new Indian mosambi + sparkling water beverage, targeting urban 22–35 year-olds who associate themselves with contemporary design, wellness, and craft beverage culture rather than mass-market soft drinks. The image must read as premium and modern first, "Indian" second — through ingredient authenticity and material quality rather than décor, motifs, or costume signalling. Product recognisability and desirability are the primary success metrics.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Mosambi, elevated." A single slim aluminium can stands centre-stage in a moody, softly lit still-life set — caught mid-moment as a citrus-bright splash of carbonated water arcs around it, with a halved mosambi and glistening droplets grounding the drink in real fruit. The palette (muted charcoal, stone, brass, and the drink's own chartreuse-gold) does the cultural work: warm, sun-washed, orchard-adjacent, but styled like a design-object, not a soda ad. No people, no landmarks, no folk motifs — just craft, light, and fruit.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product (can) — unmistakable hero, largest, sharpest, centre-weighted.
2. Freshness cue — splash + citrus slice + droplets, directly interacting with the can.
3. Mood/positioning — lighting, background gradient, surface reflection (premium, calm, adult).
4. Brand mark — small, confident wordmark on can body only (no additional on-image text/copy).
No headline, no price, no logo lockup outside the can — keeps the product as the only communicator.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Format: vertical 4:5, product occupying middle third, slight low-angle hero framing.
- Palette: deep charcoal-to-soft-moss gradient background; can body in warm chartreuse-to-ivory gradient with a thin brushed-brass accent ring; no red/orange soda cues.
- Materials: matte-finish aluminium can (not glossy soda-can plastic look), wet stone or dark honed-marble surface for reflection.
- Props: one halved mosambi (cut face visible, pale green-gold flesh), 2–3 loose citrus segments, scattered water droplets, a controlled splash arc with visible fine bubbles — no overflowing "fizz mess."
- Lighting: soft key light top-left (large softbox feel), subtle warm rim light right edge separating product from background, gentle fill to retain shadow detail; no harsh commercial soda flash.
- Depth: shallow depth of field, background softly defocused with faint warm bokeh (suggesting orchard light without literal leaves/trees dominating).
- Explicitly avoided: turmeric-hand shots, marigolds, paisley, saris, monuments, auto-rickshaws, neon Bollywood palettes, plastic-bottle soda tropes, cluttered ice-and-splash chaos.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Generate base composition via single text-to-image prompt (below) at 4:5, high detail, photographic realism.
2. Select best candidate for: can geometry accuracy, splash realism, correct 4:5 framing, absence of stray text artefacts.
3. Post-production pass (deterministic, non-generative): clean up label surface, add vector-based brand wordmark/logo, colour-grade for premium consistency, refine droplet highlights, confirm crop to exact 4:5.
4. Final QC against hard constraints before delivery.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Single final prompt (image, one shot):

"Ultra-premium studio product photograph, vertical 4:5 composition, a single slim matte-finish aluminium beverage can centred slightly below frame middle, label surface left deliberately clean/blank (no readable text or logos), can body colour a soft chartreuse-to-pale-ivory gradient with a thin brushed brass accent ring near the top. The can sits on a dark honed-marble wet surface with a subtle mirror reflection. Around the can, a dynamic but controlled arc of sparkling water splash frozen mid-motion with fine, realistic bubbles, alongside a freshly halved mosambi (sweet lime) showing pale green-gold flesh, two loose citrus segments, and scattered natural water droplets on the can and surface. Background is a smooth dark charcoal-to-soft-moss gradient with soft warm defocused bokeh, no landmarks, no people, no folk motifs, no plastic soda-bottle aesthetic. Lighting: large soft key light from top-left, subtle warm rim light on right edge separating product from background, gentle fill light preserving shadow detail, cinematic but clean commercial studio lighting. Shot on a macro/product lens look, shallow depth of field, background softly out of focus, product in crisp sharp focus. Mood: sophisticated, modern, adult, refreshing, minimal, high-end beverage advertising, no text anywhere in image, no watermark, no extra props, no ice cubes, no straws."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Brand wordmark/logo on can: added in post as a vector overlay (not generated) to guarantee crisp, correct typography — placed on the pre-left-blank label zone.
- Final colour grade and contrast pass: standard non-generative post (LUT/curves) for cross-platform consistency.
- Exact 4:5 crop/canvas confirmation and export at required resolutions.
- Any nutritional/legal disclaimer text (if required by client) added as deterministic overlay, kept off primary visual zone.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
Not applicable — static image deliverable only.

- 4:5 aspect ratio: ✅ specified in canvas and prompt.
- Product is unmistakable hero: ✅ centred, sharp, largest element.
- No celebrity: ✅ no people in image.
- No external website: ✅ none referenced or shown.
- Avoids clichéd "Indian" visual shorthand: ✅ explicitly excluded in prompt and visual system.
- Avoids cheap soft-drink look: ✅ matte can, muted palette, controlled splash, premium lighting.
