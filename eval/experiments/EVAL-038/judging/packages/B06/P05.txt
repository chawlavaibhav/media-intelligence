OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The image's job is to sell desirability and perceived craftsmanship in a single frame on a product listing page, where shoppers scan fast and zoom into dial/case detail before purchase. Success = the shopper immediately reads "precision mechanical watch, quality materials" without needing copy, and the image survives close inspection (dial texture, hand shapes, crystal dome, strap grain) since e-commerce shoppers pixel-peep before buying a $ item like this. Constraint: this is a real product with fixed geometry — the image must look like an actual photograph of that specific watch, not a stylized or reinterpreted CGI object.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
A tightly-composed three-quarter studio product shot — the watch resting at a slight angle on a dark, softly lit surface, strap curving naturally into frame — lit the way high-end watch catalogs (the kind used by real Swiss brands) light steel and sunburst dials: one raking key light to draw out the sunburst brushing on the dial and the brushed-steel case flanks, a controlled highlight arc on the domed sapphire crystal that reveals depth without blowing out the hour markers, and a soft gradient dark background that keeps all visual attention on the watch. No lifestyle props, no wrist, no floating CGI — just an honest, expensive-looking macro-leaning studio photograph.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary read (first 0.5s): "This is a serious mechanical watch" — silhouette, proportion, case-to-strap relationship.
2. Secondary read: dial craftsmanship — sunburst blue finish, silver baton markers and dauphine hands catching light, clean dial (no date window) signaling deliberate, uncluttered design.
3. Tertiary read: material quality — brushed steel texture on case flank/lugs, sapphire crystal dome causing a controlled light bend, leather grain on the strap.
4. Background/composition stays fully subordinate — dark, non-competing, providing negative space in the upper third usable for a logo/price overlay if the page template needs it, without that being the image's main content.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Framing: three-quarter front-left angle, watch face tilted ~20–25° toward camera, case slightly elevated on the far side so the dial's sunburst pattern catches a visible gradient sweep.
- Lens/optics language: macro-photography feel, ~100mm macro equivalent, f/7.1–f/8 so the whole watch face and near case edge are crisp while the strap tail and background fall into soft, shallow bokeh.
- Lighting: one large soft key light upper-left at ~45°, raking across the dial to reveal sunburst brushing; a narrow strip light/reflector on the right to trace a clean highlight along the case's brushed flank and crystal dome edge; soft fill from below to prevent the underside of the lugs and strap from going full black; a small rim light behind to separate the case silhouette from the background.
- Background: seamless dark charcoal-to-black gradient, warm-neutral, no texture, no props, no hands/wrist, no water/rocks/other generic CGI staging.
- Surface: watch rests on a dark walnut or matte black solid surface with a very subtle reflection of the case underside (not mirror-glossy, just enough to ground the object physically).
- Color grading: rich deep navy dial blues, cool brushed-steel silvers, warm dark-brown leather, neutral-to-slightly-warm background — contrast tuned so blue dial and silver hands are the brightest saturated elements.
- Composition/negative space: watch occupies center-to-lower two-thirds of frame; upper third kept darker/emptier for optional page overlay use without cropping the product.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Single AI-generated still image, generated at high resolution in 4:5 native aspect ratio (no post-crop from a square or 16:9 source, to avoid losing detail). One generation pass targeting photorealistic studio product photography, followed by light retouch-style post-processing (contrast/dodge-burn refinement, dust/spec cleanup, color calibration) treated as standard e-commerce retouching, not content alteration. No compositing of multiple watches or props; single coherent scene, single subject.
