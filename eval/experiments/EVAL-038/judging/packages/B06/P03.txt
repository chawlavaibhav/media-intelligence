## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Environment:** Controlled studio or photorealistic 3D render matching studio-lighting discipline.

**Lighting setup:**  
- Key light: 1× large soft source (simulated softbox, 80–120 cm equivalent width). Position 45° horizontal left, 60° vertical above dial plane.  
- Fill: None. Single source only per . Shadows remain directional and definition-rich.  
- Background: Evenly lit to mid-tone (warm gray, soft beige, or off-white offering tonal separation from dark dial and brown strap per .

**Surface preparation:**  
- Dial: Sunburst finish clean and visible; no gloss or protective coating that would flatten radial texture.  
- Case: Brushed steel unpolished but immaculate; directional scratching pattern shows under key light.  
- Crystal dome: Clean, no fingerprints; one soft specular reflection intended per .  
- Hands and markers: Polished silver, showing consistent highlights aligned with key-light direction.  
- Leather strap: Clean, naturally conditioned (no silicone shine, no artificial gloss).  
- Wrist/arm: Neutral skin tone, groomed but not oiled; natural color without color grading.

**Camera angle and framing:**  
- Angle: 3/4-front view (≈35° rotation from flat dial position, slight tilt raising the crystal dome edge toward camera to show case profile and dome convexity).  
- This angle maximizes visible surfaces per  (full dial, case thickness, strap, wrist context).  
- Aspect: 4:5 portrait (brief-fixed parameter).  
- Composition: Watch in upper-center, wrist/forearm filling lower third, clean negative space above and to the right.  
- Focus plane: Dial at critical sharpness; case and crystal in focus; wrist/arm slightly softer if shallow depth of field, or full depth if studio aesthetic demands sharpness throughout.

**Post-processing:**  
- Preserve material authenticity; no heavy retouching or unrealistic material smoothing.  
- Ensure sunburst dial texture remains legible (do not over-sharpen radial scratches; do not obliterate them).  
- Dial brightness: revealed by lighting, not artificially lifted. Dark blue saturation natural, not oversaturated.  
- Color grading: warm, neutral, premium — no cool cast, no excessive contrast.  
- Cropping/aspect: Maintain 4:5 ratio precisely; no letterboxing or padding.
