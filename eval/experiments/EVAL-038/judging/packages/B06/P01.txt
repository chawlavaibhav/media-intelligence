FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium, commercially usable e-commerce hero image, 4:5 aspect ratio (e.g., 1600×2000 px minimum, delivered as high-res sRGB JPEG/PNG plus a layered master), depicting the Aster Meridian 38 watch as a single, photorealistic still-life product shot.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The image must function as a primary product-page hero: it needs to sell craftsmanship and material quality at a glance, hold up under zoom/crop for a PDP gallery, and read cleanly on a light or dark storefront background. The customer wants a grounded, tactile, "real object" feel rather than a synthetic floating-CGI look, and demands strict fidelity to fixed product geometry — no reinterpretation of case size, dial layout, hands, crystal, or strap.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
A intimate, studio-lit tabletop still life: the watch rests naturally on a dark walnut surface with its dark brown leather strap coiled loosely beside/behind the case, photographed at a low three-quarter hero angle that lets a single large soft key light rake across the dial to ignite the blue sunburst pattern and pick out the polished bevels, dauphine hands, and dome of the sapphire crystal. This is presented as an aspirational, editorial-grade product photograph — the kind seen in luxury watch catalogs — not a rendered CGI floating object, because the surface contact, real shadow falloff, and material specularity are what sell craftsmanship and desirability believably.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary: dial and crystal (craftsmanship, sunburst finish, precision of hands/markers) — largest, sharpest, most lit area, positioned in upper-middle third.
2. Secondary: case material (brushed steel finish, polished bevel edge, crown) — visible along the case flank, catching a distinct rim light.
3. Tertiary: strap material and stitching (leather grain, tone, craftsmanship of tailoring) — lower third, softly lit, leading the eye out of frame.
4. Supporting: clean negative space at top ~15% of frame and soft background falloff, preserving room for a retailer to later overlay logo/price without a text layer being baked into this deliverable.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Aspect ratio: 4:5, vertical.
- Palette: deep sunburst blue (dial) + brushed cool steel + warm dark walnut brown (strap + surface) + near-black charcoal gradient background — a restrained, premium 3-tone palette.
- Lighting: single large soft key (softbox equivalent, ~45° top-left), gentle fill from camera-right to open case shadow detail, small hard kicker to define bezel edge, background lit separately and slightly dimmer to create a soft vignette.
- Camera language: macro-adjacent product photography — 100mm macro equivalent, low tripod-height three-quarter angle (~35–40° above horizontal), shallow-to-moderate depth of field (dial and nearest hands tack sharp, strap coil and background softly falling off), no wide-angle distortion of the case.
- Surface/props: single dark walnut wood surface with soft reflection, no clutter, no extra props, no hands/fingers holding the watch.
- Mood: quiet luxury, tactile, confident — not flashy, no lens flares, no exaggerated CGI gloss.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Generate base image from the single prompt below at high resolution, framed natively in 4:5.
2. Generate 4–6 candidate variants (same prompt, seed variation only) to allow selection of the version with most accurate hand geometry, marker spacing, and crown placement.
3. Select the candidate with: correct hand count/shape (dauphine, silver, two hands only — hour and minute, plus seconds hand if present must be thin/simple and not mistaken for a date complication), no visible date window, symmetric baton markers, correctly domed (not flat) crystal.
4. Non-generative retouch pass (deterministic, manual/Photoshop-style): 
   - Verify and, if needed, locally correct dial symmetry and marker alignment.
   - Clean any sensor-noise/artifact specks on the crystal or dial.
   - Calibrate blue channel so the dial reads as a true deep sunburst blue (not teal/purple) under both display and print color profiles.
   - Sharpen dial/hands selectively; keep strap and background at softer sharpness to preserve depth hierarchy.
   - Confirm strap is dark brown leather with visible grain/stitching, not black or reddish.
5. Final color grade: subtle contrast lift, gentle vignette reinforcement, export sRGB with embedded profile for e-commerce use.
