FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end B2B promotional asset for Aight's media API. The challenge is to communicate aggressive festive pricing (₹9/₹99) while maintaining the visual authority of an AI infrastructure company. The design must avoid "discount retail" tropes (no bursts, no loud reds/yellows, no "SALE" terminology) and instead rely on Swiss-style typographic hierarchy and a premium, minimalist festive palette.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Infrastructure Elegance." The poster treats the prices as architectural elements rather than discounts. A deep, matte "Infrastructure Blue" background is contrasted with a singular, refined gold accent—a nod to the festive season that feels like a luxury product render rather than a holiday decoration. The layout is driven by a strict vertical typographic stack.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary Hook: The prices (₹9 and ₹99) via extreme scale and gold contrast.
2. Secondary Context: The modality (Image and Video generation) via proximity.
3. Tertiary Brand/Trust: Aight logo and the value proposition "Where Indian businesses buy AI."

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st: The gold prices (Size/Color contrast) -> 2nd: The "Image/Video" labels (Proximity) -> 3rd: The Aight logo/tagline (Position).
surface_finish_per_key_object: Background -> matte; Prices/Gold accents -> glossy/direct; Typography -> diffuse.
implied_light_source: A single soft overhead studio light from the top-left, creating subtle depth on the 3D gold elements.
placement_zone: Right-central zone; used to create a modern, off-center tension that avoids the static feel of a retail flyer.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Base: Deep matte midnight-indigo background.
2. Element: A single, hyper-realistic 3D abstract gold "shimmer" or geometric thread weaving behind the text, suggesting both AI neural networks and festive gold zari.
3. Typography: Use a premium, heavy geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Montserrat). 
   - "IMAGE" and "VIDEO" in small, tracked-out white uppercase.
   - "₹9" and "₹99" in massive, bold gold.
4. Layout: Stack the two pairs vertically.
5. Footer: Place the Aight logo and "Where Indian businesses buy AI" in the bottom-left for classical balance.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
A premium, minimalist B2B corporate poster for an AI infrastructure company. 4:5 aspect ratio. Background is a solid, deep matte midnight-indigo. The composition is dominated by clean, high-contrast Swiss-style typography. The text "IMAGE" (small, white, sans-serif) is paired with "₹9" (massive, bold, polished 3D gold). Below it, "VIDEO" (small, white, sans-serif) is paired with "₹99" (massive, bold, polished 3D gold). A single, elegant, thin 3D gold ribbon of light weaves subtly through the background, suggesting a neural network and a festive Indian gold thread. Studio lighting, cinematic depth of field, ultra-high resolution, architectural precision. No "sale" badges, no clipart, no retail clutter. Professional, serious, high-tech.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aight logo: Vector placement in bottom-left.
- Tagline: "Where Indian businesses buy AI" in a clean, medium-weight white sans-serif.
- Price Symbols: Exact ₹ glyphs must be rendered deterministically to avoid AI hallucination.
