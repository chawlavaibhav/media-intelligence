# FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

## DELIVERABLE
One vertical promotional poster, 4:5 aspect ratio, static image. Premium AI infrastructure brand positioning for Aight during Indian festive season, communicating pricing-first commercial proposition (image generation ₹9, video generation ₹99) without discount-retailer aesthetics. Typography carries the visual hierarchy and message weight.

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The brief requires a single poster that achieves two simultaneous goals in tension: (1) make two specific prices immediately visible and understandable, and (2) maintain premium positioning as a serious AI infrastructure company serving Indian businesses, not a discount marketplace. The resolution is to use typography and information hierarchy as the visual system itself — prices become the dominant read through scale and contrast, but the surrounding typographic framework establishes authority and seriousness. The 4:5 vertical aspect is fixed by the brief; it serves the vertical stacking of information hierarchy.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
**Pricing as Architecture.** The two price points (₹9 / ₹99) form the visual and informational spine of the poster. They are not *announced* — they are *placed* and *proportioned* as the structural hierarchy. Below and above them sits the brand identity and use-case labeling (Image / Video), then supporting context (for Indian businesses, for what you build). The poster reads as a merchant's counter or business ledger: clean, itemized, trustworthy. Festive season warmth enters through color temperature and subtle visual texture, not decoration.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY

**1st Read (Dominant):** Prices in Indian Rupees — ₹9 paired with "Image", ₹99 paired with "Video" — rendered in scale large enough to read from 1.5 meters. Exact numerals are the sole visual stress point.

**2nd Read:** Brand identity — "Aight" wordmark + tagline "Where Indian Businesses Buy AI" (or compact variant). Positioned to frame or anchor the pricing pair.

**3rd Read:** Supporting value statement — one sentence on what the user actually gets (e.g., "Prepaid in rupees. No hidden charges. Start today.") or benefit pairing (e.g., "Image generation starting ₹9 / Video generation starting ₹99").

**4th Read (Micro):** Call-to-action and domain — "getaight.ai" + "Talk to us on WhatsApp" or similar (bottom safe area).

**Festive Season Signal:** Present in color warmth (gold/saffron undertone to neutral palette) and pacing (breathing room between elements suggests prosperity, not urgency).

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM

**attention_order:** 
- 1st: ₹9 and ₹99 numerals by scale and tonal contrast (light ground, dark or accent-colored text).
- 2nd: Image / Video use-case labels, positioned immediately adjacent to prices.
- 3rd: Aight brand statement and supporting copy, providing context.

**surface_finish_per_key_object:**
- Price numerals: direct/matte typography on neutral ground (no gloss or glare; legibility is the finish).
- Brand wordmark: matte or diffuse (professional, not reflective).
- Background/ground: diffuse/matte (neutral base, supports text legibility).
- Accent elements (if any dividing lines or geometric guides): thin, matte, subordinate to typography.

**implied_light_source:**
- Frontal, even illumination (editorial/printed poster convention). No directional shadow is implied; the poster is an artifact of the page, not a scene. Light is even and domestic, befitting a ledger or counter display.

**placement_zone:**
- Subject (price pair + labels) sits in the upper-middle zone of frame (roughly top third to mid-frame), justified by commercial primacy: the first thing a business audience needs to know is cost. Below it, brand identity and CTA. This keeps the eye moving downward through the hierarchy, standard in vertical poster composition.

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Input:** Website snapshot (getaight.ai), brand positioning, price points (₹9, ₹99), target audience (Indian businesses), aspect ratio (4:5 vertical), tone (premium infrastructure company, not discount).

**Process:**
1. **Typography Foundation:** Select a professional sans-serif typeface family (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or equivalent with strong numerals). Establish a three-level hierarchy: Headline (prices, 84–120pt effective), Subhead (brand + use-case labels, 32–48pt), Body (supporting copy, 14–18pt).
2. **Price Prominence:** Render ₹9 and ₹99 as a paired dual-column layout or stacked pair (Image/₹9 above Video/₹99, or side-by-side depending on width). Ensure numerals are the largest single elements on the poster.
3. **Color Palette:** 
   - Base: Off-white or warm neutral ground (e.g., #F5F2ED or #FEFCF8 with subtle warmth for festive season).
   - Primary text: Dark charcoal or deep navy (e.g., #1A1A1A or #0F1C2E).
   - Accent: Optional warm tone (gold, saffron-adjacent, or brand accent color if Aight has one; apply sparingly to price numerals or dividing line).
   - Supporting text: Mid-gray (e.g., #666666).
4. **Composition Grid:** Establish a vertical rhythm with 4–5 key zones: (top) brand/logo area (compact, 8–12% of height), (primary) price zone (35–45%), (secondary) label + subheading (15–20%), (tertiary) supporting copy (15–20%), (footer) CTA + domain (8–12%).
5. **Edge Treatment:** This is a text-heavy, single-purpose poster. Edges define the frame boundary only; no internal framing elements. Margins are consistent and generous (minimize visual tension, maximize legibility).
6. **Accent Graphic (Optional):** A single thin horizontal rule or subtle geometric element (e.g., a minimal bracket or subtle festive motif: a small diya outline, rangoli-inspired line, or merchant's ledger mark) can separate zones without competing with type.
7. **Finalization:** Export as high-resolution raster (300 DPI or vector source) in 4:5 ratio. Ensure all text is anti-aliased and legible at reduced sizes (poster will likely be shared as digital tile, thumbnail, and print). Run contrast check (WCAG AA minimum for all copy).
