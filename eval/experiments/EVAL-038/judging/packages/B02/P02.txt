FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end, "infrastructure-grade" advertisement for Aight's media API. The goal is to communicate extremely competitive pricing (₹9/₹99) to Indian businesses during the festive season without descending into "discount flyer" aesthetics. The prestige is maintained through Swiss-style typographic rigor and a sophisticated, minimalist approach to festive cues.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Architecture of Value." Instead of a sale announcement, the poster treats the pricing as a technical specification. By placing high-contrast, oversized numerals within a strict, professional grid against a premium dark palette, the low price is framed as an efficiency of scale rather than a bargain.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary: Unit Costs (₹9 and ₹99) — the immediate commercial hook.
2. Secondary: Product Categories (Image Generation / Video Generation) — the "what."
3. Tertiary: Brand Identity (Aight logo and "Where Indian businesses buy AI") — the "who" and trust anchor.
4. Quaternary: Call to Action (getaight.ai) — the "how."

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st: The numerals "₹9" and "₹99" (Scale/Contrast); 2nd: The labels "Image" and "Video" (Proximity); 3rd: Aight Logo (Position).
surface_finish_per_key_object: background -> matte; numerals -> glossy/direct; festive accents -> glare.
implied_light_source: Single soft spotlight from top-left.
placement_zone: Center-left zone; allows the typographic block to dominate while leaving negative space for a subtle festive motif on the right to balance the composition.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Base: Deep obsidian matte background with a very subtle radial gradient (dark navy to black).
2. Typography: Use a heavyweight, geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Montserrat).
3. Layout: A strict vertical grid. Top half: "₹9" aligned left, with "IMAGE GENERATION" in a smaller, tracked-out uppercase font immediately below it. Bottom half: "₹99" aligned left, with "VIDEO GENERATION" below it.
4. Festive Element: A single, ultra-thin, gold-foil geometric line-art representation of a Diya or a Mandala segment, placed partially off-frame on the right edge. It must be a precise vector, not a photo, to maintain the "tech company" feel.
5. Branding: Aight logo in crisp white at the bottom-left; tagline "Where Indian businesses buy AI" in light weight, centered at the very bottom.
6. Finishing: Add a subtle film grain to the background to remove "digital flatness" and enhance the premium feel.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Generation Prompt:
A premium, minimalist 4:5 promotional poster for an AI infrastructure company. Background is a matte obsidian and deep navy gradient. The composition is dominated by high-contrast white geometric typography. On the left side, two distinct blocks of text: a massive "₹9" with the words "IMAGE GENERATION" in small, elegant, spaced-out sans-serif capitals underneath, and below that, a massive "₹99" with "VIDEO GENERATION" in the same style. On the right side, a single, sophisticated, ultra-thin gold-foil vector line-art of a stylized Indian Diya, partially cropped by the frame. Soft top-left studio lighting. 8k resolution, architectural layout, Swiss design style, corporate prestige, high-fashion tech aesthetic, no clutter, no stock photos of fireworks.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- All text (₹9, ₹99, Image Generation, Video Generation, Aight logo, and tagline) must be composited deterministically using a geometric sans-serif typeface to ensure perfect glyphs and alignment.
- The 4:5 aspect ratio is a hard constraint.
