FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
9:16 UGC-style performance video (approx. 25 seconds).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a credible, creator-led video for an Indian D2C skincare brand. The goal is to drive conversion for a 2% Salicylic Acid serum targeting oily/blemish-prone skin by emphasizing the "fragrance-free/non-irritating" benefit as the primary purchase reason.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Honest Routine": A natural, direct-to-camera testimonial from a believable Indian woman in a home setting. The visual style mimics high-quality smartphone content (UGC) but applies professional lighting and composition to ensure the product looks premium.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Pain Point: Struggling with oily/blemish-prone skin.
2. Solution: Introduction of the 2% Salicylic Acid Serum.
3. Key Benefit (Purchase Reason): Fragrance-free and non-irritating.
4. Proof: Visual demonstration of texture and application.
5. Call to Action: Direct prompt to purchase.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st: Woman's eyes (direct address), 2nd: Product packaging (visual contrast), 3rd: Serum texture on skin.
surface_finish_per_key_object: Serum Bottle -> glossy/direct; Serum Liquid -> glossy/direct; Skin -> matte/diffuse.
implied_light_source: Large window to the side (Natural Daylight).
placement_zone: Off-centre (Left/Right alternating) to mimic natural creator movement; Centre only for the hero product shot to signify importance.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Talent: One Indian woman (25-35), natural look, minimal makeup, believable "skincare enthusiast" persona.
- Setting: A bright, clean domestic space (bathroom or bedroom) with a neutral-toned background.
- Lighting: High-key, soft lighting mimicking a large window.
- Camera: 9:16 vertical aspect, handheld feel (slight organic motion) but stable.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (0-5s): Medium close-up, 9:16. A believable Indian woman with natural skin, speaking directly to camera in a bright room. Natural handheld movement. Soft side-lighting from a window. She looks frustrated but hopeful.
Shot 2 (5-10s): Close-up, 9:16. The woman holds a sleek, minimal D2C skincare bottle. The bottle is glossy glass with a white label. One clear specular highlight on the shoulder of the bottle. Subject is off-centre.
Shot 3 (10-17s): Extreme close-up/Macro, 9:16. A glass dropper releasing a clear, viscous, glossy serum drop onto a cheek. The skin is matte. Soft, bright daylight. Focus is sharp on the droplet.
Shot 4 (17-22s): Medium close-up, 9:16. The woman speaking to camera, smiling confidently. She is positioned in the opposite off-centre zone from Shot 2. Natural, soft window light.
Shot 5 (22-25s): Close-up, 9:16. The product bottle held next to the woman's face. Both are centred. High-key, bright, clean domestic background.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Text Overlays (Deterministic): 
    - Shot 1: "Oily skin struggle? 😫"
    - Shot 2: "2% Salicylic Acid Serum ✨"
    - Shot 4: "Fragrance-Free = No Irritation ✅"
- Branding: Fictional D2C logo on bottle (Composite).

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Natural voice-over (recorded as if on a phone mic but clear). Ambient "room tone" for authenticity. Light, upbeat, lo-fi background track.
- Edit: Quick, energetic cuts. No cinematic transitions; simple straight cuts to maintain UGC credibility.
- Pace: Fast, following the rhythm of the speech.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- 9:16 aspect? Yes.
- Indian woman? Yes.
- 2% Salicylic Acid? Yes.
- Fragrance-free purchase reason? Yes.
- No clinical stats/medical claims? Yes.
- Duration ~25s? Yes.
- UGC feel? Yes.
