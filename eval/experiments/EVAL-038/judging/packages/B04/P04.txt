FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 9:16 vertical UGC-style performance video, ~25 seconds, single continuous creator narrative across 4 cuts, captioned, feed/reels-ready (Instagram/YouTube Shorts/Meta Ads placement), for a fictional Indian D2C skincare brand.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer wants a believable, low-polish creator testimonial (not a TVC) that drives purchase intent for a fragrance-free 2% salicylic-acid serum aimed at oily, blemish-prone adult skin. Success = viewer perceives one real woman genuinely explaining why this product earned a place in her routine, retains attention through a fast hook, and walks away with exactly one clear, non-medical purchase reason. Compliance constraint is central: no invented efficacy %, no "clinically proven," no dermatologist endorsement, no cure/treatment language — only descriptive, honest cosmetic framing (oil control feel, lightweight texture, fragrance-free, ingredient percentage as a factual formulation detail, not a claim of outcome).

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The serum I reach for on my oily days" — a relatable, slightly imperfect, phone-shot confession from a woman who tried multiple products, found this one comfortable to use daily, and states one honest personal reason she keeps repurchasing it: it controls shine without stinging or drying her skin out. No lab talk, no doctor, no before/after miracle — just a credible daily-use moment.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary (must land): One clear purchase reason — "controls oil without drying/irritating skin, fragrance-free, comfortable daily use."
2. Secondary: Product identity — brand name, product name, key formulation fact (2% salicylic acid, fragrance-free) shown as on-screen text, not spoken as a clinical claim.
3. Tertiary: CTA — soft, platform-native ("link in bio" / "available online now"), no named external URL.
Hierarchy is delivered in this order across the 25s: Hook (relatable problem) → Credibility beat (what she tried before / what changed) → Demo (visual proof of texture/use) → Single reason + CTA.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Aspect ratio: 9:16, full-bleed vertical, shot as if on a smartphone front camera + one cutaway on rear camera for product macro.
- Look: natural handheld/selfie framing, slight handshake, real bathroom/bedroom daylight (soft window light, no studio grade), skin shown with visible natural texture/pores — explicitly NOT airbrushed or beauty-filtered.
- Wardrobe: casual home clothing (plain tee/kurti), minimal/no makeup, hair loosely tied — signals authenticity.
- Color: warm-neutral natural skin tones; brand accent color (sage-green/off-white, per packaging) appears only via product and on-screen text chips, never color-grading the whole frame.
- Captions: bold sans-serif burned-in captions, word-synced, bottom-safe zone, brand accent color for 2–3 key words per line (e.g., "fragrance-free," "2% salicylic acid," "oil control").
- No stock-ad transitions, no slow-mo glam shots, no glossy studio product tabletop — keep product shot handheld/macro on real skin/hand.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Format: 4 shots, straight cuts (no dissolves), total ~25s, vertical 9:16, 24–30fps handheld aesthetic.
Casting: one Indian woman, mid-20s–early-30s, natural presentation, conversational Hinglish-leaning English delivery, warm/direct eye contact with lens.
Fictional brand: "Clarra Skin" — Product: "Clarra 2% Salicylic Acid Serum" (fragrance-free), simple minimal white/sage pump bottle with visible small label reading "Fragrance-Free · 2% Salicylic Acid" (fictional, generic pharmacy-style label, no real regulatory marks/certifications invented).

SHOT LIST

Shot 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:03, ~3s)
Purpose: Stop scroll, establish relatability.
Framing: Close-up selfie-style, face + shoulders, slight handheld sway, natural window light.
Action/VO: She looks straight into lens, mid-sentence energy: "If you have oily, acne-prone skin like me... you need to hear this."
On-screen text: none yet (let hook breathe).

Shot 2 — PROBLEM/CREDIBILITY (0:03–0:09, ~6s)
Purpose: Build trust via honest past-experience framing (no stats).
Framing: Same selfie angle, she picks up the Clarra bottle from a shelf/counter into frame, turns it slightly.
Action/VO: "I've tried so many serums — some broke me out more, some just dried my skin out completely. This is the one I actually stuck with."
On-screen text (appears late in shot): "Clarra 2% Salicylic Acid Serum" (small, lower third).

Shot 3 — DEMO (0:09–0:18, ~9s)
Purpose: Natural product demonstration, tactile proof.
Framing: Slight angle change (rear-camera macro cut) — hand dispenses one pump onto fingertip, then cuts back to selfie as she pats it onto cheek/jaw, visible sheen/absorption.
Action/VO: "It's fragrance-free, super lightweight, and it actually sinks in fast — no sticky, no stinging." (she pats product in, natural facial reaction — comfortable, casual smile)
On-screen text: "Fragrance-Free" then "Lightweight Gel-Serum" (sequential caption chips).

Shot 4 — SINGLE REASON + CTA (0:18–0:25, ~7s)
Purpose: Deliver the one clear purchase reason and close.
Framing: Back to tight selfie close-up, direct eye contact, calmer pace.
Action/VO: "Honestly, it's the only one that controls my oil without leaving my skin tight or irritated — that's why it's stayed in my routine." Beat. "Link in my bio if you want to try it."
On-screen text: Brand logo lockup "Clarra Skin" + CTA text "Available Online Now" (no URL shown).
