Produce a high-converting, credible "creator-style" ad for a 2% salicylic acid serum. The goal is to pivot away from "clinical/corporate" and toward "peer-to-peer recommendation," focusing on the specific benefit of being fragrance-free for oily/blemish-prone skin.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Honest Routine": A relatable Indian woman shares her frustration with fragrance-heavy actives that irritate her skin, presenting this serum as the "quiet but effective" alternative for oil control.

5. CTA: Direction to purchase.
