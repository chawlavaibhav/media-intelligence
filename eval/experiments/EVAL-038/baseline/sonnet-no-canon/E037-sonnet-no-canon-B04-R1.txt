FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 9:16 vertical video, ~25 seconds, single continuous creator-style ad ("scroll-stopper" UGC ad) for a fictional Indian D2C skincare brand, featuring one Indian woman speaking to camera, demonstrating the product, and delivering one clear purchase reason. Final format: 1080×1920, 24–30fps, burned-in captions, platform-ready (Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts style), no website/URL shown.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The brief calls for performance advertising that mimics authentic creator content rather than a studio commercial. Success = viewer believes a real person is recommending a real product, retains attention through a natural hook–problem–demo–payoff–CTA arc, and remembers exactly one reason to buy. Constraints require restraint: no fabricated clinical data, no medical/guarantee language, no external site, natural (not aspirational-glam) visual treatment.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
A relatable young Indian woman films herself in her bedroom/bathroom, mid-skincare-routine, and tells the camera the one thing that made her switch to this serum: it treats oily, breakout-prone skin without the strong smell or heavy/sticky feel she hated in other salicylic acid serums. The entire video is built around this single sensory-and-lifestyle purchase reason — "fragrance-free, lightweight, sinks in fast" — never a clinical percentage-improvement or medical promise.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Hook (attention): "If you have oily, breakout-prone skin, listen to this."
2. Relatable problem: other serums smell strong or feel greasy by afternoon.
3. Product demo (proof): shows real application, quick absorption.
4. Single purchase reason (core payoff): fragrance-free + lightweight + non-sticky = why she keeps repurchasing.
5. Brand recall + soft CTA: product name spoken + on-screen text, "link in bio" (no URL).
No statistics, no "clinically proven," no "cures acne," no before/after claim.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Aesthetic: authentic phone-shot UGC — handheld micro-shake, natural window light, no cinematic grade, no beauty filter, visible ordinary bedroom/bathroom.
- Talent: Indian woman, mid-20s–early 30s, warm brown skin tone, natural skin texture (slight visible shine/oiliness consistent with target skin type, not exaggerated or "flawless"), minimal makeup, casual wardrobe (oversized tee or home kurta).
- Product: matte white/sage-green dropper bottle, minimal typography label reading "Rooh Clarity Serum — 2% Salicylic Acid, Fragrance-Free," simple modern logo mark.
- Framing: vertical selfie-style compositions (arm's-length or propped phone), occasional close-up insert for demo texture.
- Color: natural, slightly warm, unfiltered — consistent with real creator footage, not commercial gloss.
- On-screen text: lightweight caption style matching native platform captions (not brand-heavy motion graphics), plus one small end-card text with brand name.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Format: 9:16, 1080×1920, 24–30fps, ~25s total, 5 shots, jump-cut style edit.
Location: home bedroom or bathroom with natural daylight (window light, no studio lighting rig look).
Camera: simulated phone camera (selfie framing + one handheld demo insert), slight natural shake, no gimbal-smoothness.
Wardrobe/Props: casual homewear, the serum bottle, no other product clutter.
Talent direction: conversational pacing, natural pauses, genuine smile, no scripted-sounding delivery — treat as if talking to a friend.
Continuity: same actress, wardrobe, location, lighting and bottle design across all 5 shots.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:03, 3s)
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 phone-style selfie video. A young Indian woman, mid-20s, warm brown skin, natural everyday makeup, wearing a casual oversized t-shirt, sits in a softly lit bedroom near a window with natural daylight. Handheld phone framing, slight natural camera shake, close-medium shot, direct eye contact with lens. She holds a matte sage-green serum bottle near her collarbone and says with a casual, slightly urgent tone: 'Okay if you have oily, breakout-prone skin, listen to this.' Authentic creator-content look, no cinematic color grade, no beauty filter."

Shot 2 — PROBLEM (0:03–0:09, 6s)
Prompt: "Continuation of same woman, same room, same lighting and wardrobe, medium selfie framing, natural handheld motion. She speaks candidly with mild hand gestures, slightly furrowed brow of relatability: 'I've tried so many salicylic acid serums but they either smell so strong or leave my face feeling greasy by noon.' Natural skin texture visible, unfiltered, authentic UGC tone."

Shot 3 — DEMO (0:09–0:16, 7s)
Prompt: "Same woman, same setting, camera shifts to a closer demo angle at chest/face height. She dispenses two drops of a light serum from a dropper bottle onto her fingertip, then pats it gently onto her cheek and jawline in front of a mirror or window light. Include one brief macro insert (1–2 seconds) of the clear lightweight serum texture absorbing into skin. Natural sound of tapping/patting. She says: 'This one's fragrance-free, just two pumps... and it literally sinks in in like ten seconds, no residue.' Real, unpolished demonstration, no gloss VFX."

Shot 4 — PURCHASE REASON PAYOFF (0:16–0:22, 6s)
Prompt: "Same woman returns to direct-to-camera medium selfie framing, same lighting/location, holding the serum bottle at chest level with a satisfied, genuine smile. She says: 'That's literally why I keep repurchasing it — it calms my breakouts without the heavy serum smell or sticky feel.' Natural delivery, authentic pacing, no exaggerated expression."

Shot 5 — CTA / PACKSHOT (0:22–0:25, 3s)
Prompt: "Close-up shot of the same matte sage-green serum bottle held up to camera by the woman, her face softly out of focus in background, natural window light. Label clearly readable: 'Rooh Clarity Serum — 2% Salicylic Acid, Fragrance-Free.' She says: 'It's called Rooh Clarity Serum, go check it out.' Small on-screen text overlay appears: 'Rooh Clarity Serum' and 'Link in bio' (no URL shown). Authentic phone-camera finish, no polished commercial lighting."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Product label text and logo: fixed asset, applied identically in every shot (not left to per-shot regeneration) to avoid brand/label drift.
- Burned-in captions: added in post-production, synced to actual spoken lines, matching platform-native caption styling.
- End-card text ("Rooh Clarity Serum" / "Link in bio"): inserted as a deterministic text overlay, not generated imagery, to guarantee accurate spelling and no accidental URL/claim insertion.
- Ingredient/percentage claim ("2% Salicylic Acid, Fragrance-Free") is treated as fixed label copy, never spoken as a "clinically proven" or performance statistic.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Voice: natural conversational Indian-English/Hinglish-inflected delivery, same actress throughout, synced lip movement to dialogue.
- Music: optional low-volume trending-style ambient beat under dialogue (not a swelling commercial score), kept subordinate to voice.
- SFX: natural room tone, subtle product/tapping sound during demo.
- Edit style: quick jump cuts between the 5 shots (no dissolves/wipes), consistent with authentic creator editing; minimal color grading (light warmth only, no LUT-heavy commercial polish).
- Captions: burned in, word-synced, standard UGC caption style.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Risk: overly glossy/AI-perfect look reading as "TVC not UGC" → mitigated via handheld shake, natural light only, unfiltered skin texture instruction in every prompt.
- Risk: actress inconsistency across shots (face/wardrobe/location drift) → mitigated by repeating identical talent/location/lighting/wardrobe description in every shot prompt.
- Risk: invented clinical claims ("clinically proven," "reduces acne by X%") → explicitly excluded from all scripted lines; only sensory/lifestyle language used.
- Risk: medical/guarantee language ("cures," "eliminates acne") → avoided; language limited to "calms breakouts," a non-medical, experiential phrase.
- Risk: accidental display of a website URL → CTA scripted as "link in bio" with no domain text anywhere in overlay or dialogue.
- Risk: label illegibility/brand drift → label design and text treated as a fixed deterministic asset applied identically across shots.
- Risk: mismatched lip sync or unnatural pacing → dialogue kept short, conversational, with natural pauses built into each shot's script.
