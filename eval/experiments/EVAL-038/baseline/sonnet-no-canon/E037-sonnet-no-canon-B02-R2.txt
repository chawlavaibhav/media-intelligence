FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One (1) static poster, 4:5 aspect ratio (recommended output 1080×1350 px, scalable to 2160×2700 for print), single final composited file (PNG/PDF-ready). No alternate versions.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The client (Aight) needs a festive-season acquisition poster whose entire job is to make two numbers — ₹9 for image generation and ₹99 for video generation — register instantly and credibly, while the overall artifact still reads as enterprise AI infrastructure (per aight.ai's actual brand voice: "Where Indian businesses buy AI," prepaid rupee wallet, hard caps, one domestic GST invoice, founder-level support) rather than an e-commerce sale banner. Success = a viewer understands both prices in under 2 seconds AND perceives the brand as a serious, trustworthy AI platform for businesses, not a consumer discount app.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Price Sheet, Not the Sale Banner." Treat the poster like a minimal enterprise price card — the kind a serious infra company (cloud billing, payments API) would publish — and let a single festive gesture (a warm gold light gradient, evoking diya/sunrise light without any literal festive iconography) be the only seasonal signal. Typography carries 90% of the message; the festive layer is atmospheric, not decorative.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Brand identity — small, quiet, top: "aight." wordmark (lowercase, period, exactly as brand uses it).
2. Seasonal context tag — small pill/label: "Festive Season Pricing" — establishes relevance without shouting "SALE."
3. THE OFFER (dominant visual mass, center):
   - "Image generation" → "₹9" (large)
   - "Video generation" → "₹99" (larger, since video is the higher-value/harder product — establishes clear price-value logic, not arbitrary numbers)
   Both prices set at near-equal massive scale so neither reads as an afterthought; differentiated by weight/position, not color gimmicks.
4. Brand proof line — mid-small: "Where Indian businesses buy AI." (verbatim brand tagline).
5. Trust footer — small caps: "Prepaid rupees · Hard caps · One domestic GST invoice."
6. CTA — bottom: "Talk to us" + "getaight.ai"

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: near-black charcoal (#0E0E10) base, warm marigold-gold (#E8A33D–#F2B84C gradient) as the single accent, off-white/ivory (#F4F1E8) for primary type. No red/green/multicolor festive clichés, no confetti, no rangoli/diya clip art.
- Light motif: a soft, large-radius warm gold glow rising from the lower third of the frame, blending into black — abstract enough to read as "premium ambient light" to a general audience and as "diya/festive warmth" to an Indian audience. This is the only "festive" cue.
- Typography: a single modern grotesk/sans family (e.g., a geometric-humanist sans in the style of Inter/Neue Haas). Numerals in bold/black weight, labels in regular/medium with wide tracking and small size — classic infra/SaaS pricing-page hierarchy (label small-caps above, numeral huge below).
- Grid: strict left-aligned or centered vertical grid, generous negative space top and sides, no clutter, no stock photography, no human figures — this is what keeps it "infrastructure" rather than "retail."
- Texture: very subtle fine grain/noise over the black field to avoid a flat digital-ad look and add print-poster quality.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Two-layer build for reliability and premium finish:
1. AI-generated BACKGROUND PLATE only — dark gradient field with the warm gold glow, subtle grain, no text at all (text-in-generation is unreliable at this precision).
2. Deterministic TYPOGRAPHY OVERLAY — all copy (wordmark, prices, labels, tagline, footer, CTA) set as vector/real text in a design tool and composited on top, using the exact brand copy captured from getaight.ai. This guarantees pixel-perfect, correctly-spelled, on-brand type — critical since typography is explicitly the hero device.
3. Final export at 4:5, color-graded to lock contrast between ivory text and charcoal field for legibility at thumbnail size (this poster will likely be viewed small on WhatsApp/social first).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Risk: AI-generated text renders garbled/misspelled prices → Mitigated by generating a text-free background plate and applying all copy deterministically.
- Risk: Festive treatment drifts into discount-flyer territory (starbursts, "SALE," multicolor confetti) → Mitigated by restricting palette to charcoal + single gold accent and banning festive clip-art/iconography in the prompt.
- Risk: Two prices compete/confuse hierarchy → Mitigated by giving both prices dominant, near-equal visual weight but placing "Video ₹99" slightly larger and inside the warmest part of the glow, since it's the higher-value product; both remain unmistakably legible at thumbnail size.
- Risk: Poster feels generic/tech-stocky → Mitigated by using brand's own verbatim language (from getaight.ai) for tagline and trust line, anchoring it specifically to Aight rather than a generic AI brand.
- Risk: Glow gradient overpowers text contrast → Mitigated by constraining glow to lower third and using ivory/near-white type with sufficient contrast checked at export.
