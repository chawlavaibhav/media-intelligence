FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One static, photoreal 4:5 (2000×2500 px, sRGB, JPEG/PNG master) e-commerce hero image of the Aster Meridian 38, suitable as the primary PDP (product detail page) image. Single frame, no variants.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer needs a commercially safe, high-trust hero shot: one that reads instantly as "premium mechanical watch," lets a shopper evaluate dial finish, case brushing, hand/marker style, crystal dome and strap material at a glance, and does so without inventing or omitting any spec (critically: no date window). "Avoid generic floating CGI" signals the customer wants tactile, photographic credibility over sterile render-on-white commerce shots — so the solution should look like real studio product photography, grounded on a surface, not a weightless 3D asset.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Quiet Craft" — a three-quarter studio macro shot of the watch resting on a dark honed-stone surface, strap softly coiled beside/under the case. A single sculpted soft-box light sweeps a controlled specular highlight across the brushed case and sunburst dial, revealing radial brushing, hand geometry and marker facets, while the leather strap's grain and stitching sit in gentle shadow-fall focus. This grounds the product physically (defeats "floating CGI" concern) while still isolating it enough for clean e-commerce use.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Watch as singular hero, dial and hands in critical sharp focus (craftsmanship + desirability).
2. Case brushing and bezel chamfer catching the key light (material quality).
3. Sunburst dial texture and silver baton markers/dauphine hands (dial detail, correctness of spec).
4. Domed sapphire crystal's subtle curved reflection (premium cue, no distortion of dial info).
5. Leather strap grain/stitching, softly resolved (secondary material story).
6. Neutral dark gradient background — supports, never competes.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: deep navy sunburst blue, warm dark-brown leather, cool steel silver, charcoal/graphite background.
- Angle: 3/4 hero angle, dial tilted ~20° toward camera, watch occupying 60–70% of frame height, positioned on a lower-third/rule-of-thirds axis with negative space above for page UI overlay.
- Lighting: one large soft key top-left at ~45°, soft fill opposite to prevent black-crush, thin rim light along case edge to separate from background, one controlled specular streak crossing the dial's sunburst grain.
- Lens/DOF: 90mm macro look, medium-shallow DOF — dial, hands, crystal and near-side case sharp; strap tail and far background ease into soft focus.
- Surface: dark honed slate/stone, very soft contact shadow, no distracting props, no text, no logos, no watermark.
- No date window, no added complications, no numerals — dial stays true to spec (baton markers, dauphine hands, plain sunburst blue).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Treat as a single simulated studio photography setup rather than a floating render:
1. Ground the watch on a real-feeling surface with visible micro-contact shadow.
2. Light it like a photographed object (single key + fill + rim), not flat ambient CGI light.
3. Emphasize material physics: brushed metal anisotropic highlight, leather micro-grain, sapphire dome curvature with a subtle realistic reflection (not a flare).
4. Compose for commerce: centered-ish, breathing room, crop-safe for PDP thumbnails.
5. Post: fine local contrast on dial/case, controlled highlight rolloff on crystal, accurate blue calibration, gentle sharpening on markers/hands only, final crop locked to 4:5.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
Key risks and mitigations:
- Model adds a date window/cyclops → explicit repeated negative prompt + final visual QA check on dial at 100% zoom before approval.
- Generic floating/CGI look → mitigated by surface contact, shadow, coiled strap, photographic lighting language instead of "render," "3D," "CGI" terms.
- Wrong hand style (sword/skeleton) or numerals → explicitly named and negative-prompted.
- Extra complications (subdials, rotating bezel scale) → explicitly excluded.
- Oversaturated/incorrect blue → color temperature and "true-to-life" language plus deterministic grading pass.
- Logo/watermark/text intrusion → explicit exclusion.
- Case proportions distorted (ellipse instead of round 38mm) → angle constrained to 20° tilt, round case explicitly stated.
QA checklist before delivery: dial free of date cutout; markers are batons only; hands are dauphine style (hour/minute) plus thin seconds hand; crystal shows dome curvature without obscuring dial; strap is dark brown leather with grain/stitching visible; background is neutral, uncluttered; crop is exactly 4:5.
