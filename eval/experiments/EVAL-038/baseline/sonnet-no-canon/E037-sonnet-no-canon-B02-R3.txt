FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One (1) static poster, 4:5 aspect ratio, print/social-ready master at 2160×2700px (scalable), RGB, for digital + optional print use.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer needs a single festive-season poster for Aight (getaight.ai) that makes ₹9 (image) and ₹99 (video) instantly legible as the headline commercial fact, while the overall design reads as enterprise AI infrastructure — closer to a cloud-platform pricing sheet than a sale banner. Per the site's own positioning ("Where Indian businesses buy AI," prepaid rupee wallet, hard caps, one domestic GST invoice, founder-led support), the brand voice is plain, confident, financially precise, and unglamorous-by-design. Festivity should be present as light/warmth, not as decoration that competes with the numbers.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Rate Card." Present the two prices exactly the way Aight already talks about billing on its site — as a clean, tabular, invoice-like line-item card (label left, tabular-numeral price right, thin rule between rows) — the same visual grammar as a cloud pricing page or a GST invoice. Festive season is expressed only as a single warm diya-glow gradient bleeding from behind the card, echoing "the counter is open" language from the brand without any retail-sale visual clichés (no confetti, starbursts, "OFFER," crossed-out prices, or generic Diwali clip art).

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Brand mark: "Aight ." (small, top-left, quiet)
2. Context line: "Festive season pricing" (small caps, top-right, understated — signals occasion without shouting sale)
3. HERO — the rate card (dominant visual weight, center):
   - "Image generation" — "₹9 / image"
   - thin rule
   - "Video generation" — "₹99 / video"
4. Credibility line (support, mid-lower third): "Prepaid rupees. One GST invoice. No surprise bills." — directly sourced from site language, reinforces "serious infra" not "discount."
5. CTA + URL (bottom, small, permanent-looking): "Talk to us — getaight.ai"
6. Legal/footer microtype (very small, bottom edge): "Ashva Intelligence Pvt Ltd · Prices per generation, prepaid wallet."

Typography carries the hierarchy through scale and weight jumps only — no icons, no illustration, no discount badges.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: near-black charcoal base (#0B0B0C), warm off-white text (#F4EFE8), single festive accent — deep amber/gold (#D8A24A to #F2C879 gradient) used only for the price digits and the background glow; a hairline stroke color (#3A3733) for rules/grid.
- Grid: strict 12-column layout, generous margins (≥8% of frame on all sides), everything left/right aligned to the grid — no floating or tilted elements.
- Type system: two families only.
  - Display/label sans: geometric grotesk, e.g. an Inter/Helvetica-Now-class face, medium/regular weight, wide tracking on small caps.
  - Numerals: monospace/tabular-figure face (invoice/ledger feel) for ₹9 and ₹99 specifically — largest type on the page, weight bold, color gradient gold-on-dark.
- Motif: one soft, radial diya-light gradient bloom positioned behind the rate card, warm amber fading to black — the only "festive" cue, no literal diya, rangoli, marigold, or lantern illustrations.
- Whitespace: minimum 40% of frame kept clean/dark to preserve premium, infra-brand restraint.
- No stock-photo people, no product screenshots, no AI/tech clichés (no glowing circuit boards, robots, or neon).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Generate a textless background plate: dark charcoal gradient field with a single warm amber radial glow positioned at center-frame, subtle fine film-grain/noise texture, faint 1px grid hairlines suggested in the negative space (ledger/graph-paper feel), 4:5 composition, large clean negative-space zones reserved top, center, and bottom for text overlay. No typography, no logos, no numerals generated by the model (text accuracy is handled deterministically in post — see next section).
2. Composite deterministic typography, rate-card rules, brand wordmark, and CTA in a design tool (vector-based) using the exact copy specified above, on the grid specified above.
3. Color-correct so the amber glow reads festive without shifting the charcoal base warm/orange (keep base neutral-cool to preserve "infra," not "diwali sale").
4. Export master at 2160×2700 (4:5), plus locked-ratio scale-downs for social use.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- All typography, including both prices (₹9, ₹99), brand wordmark "Aight .", CTA, URL, and legal line, is set as vector text in a design tool — never left to the image generator — to guarantee 100% accuracy of numerals, currency symbol, spelling, and brand mark.
- Grid, rule lines, and card layout are built as deterministic vector shapes.
- Only the atmospheric background (glow, grain, hairline texture) is produced generatively; it contains no text or brand elements.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Risk: AI-generated text misrendering price digits or currency symbol → mitigated by keeping all text fully deterministic/vector, generated art is text-free.
- Risk: design drifting into "discount flyer" territory (starbursts, "SALE," crossed-out prices) → explicitly excluded from copy and visual system; only one festive cue permitted (amber glow).
- Risk: festive glow overpowering premium/infra tone → constrained to a single soft radial bloom on neutral charcoal, not a warm-tinted overall palette.
- Risk: hierarchy confusion between two prices → enforced via identical row structure, aligned rule, and matched type scale so both prices read with equal, immediate clarity.
- Risk: brand tone mismatch → credibility line and CTA phrasing pulled directly from Aight's own site language (prepaid rupees, GST invoice, "Talk to us").
