# FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

## DELIVERABLE
One static promotional poster, 1080px × 1350px (4:5 vertical aspect ratio), RGB colour space, 72dpi, for web and print distribution (mobile-first, festive-season campaign).

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Aight seeks a festive-season promotional poster that renders ₹9 (image generation) and ₹99 (video generation) pricing immediately legible while preserving premium, infrastructure-grade brand positioning. The poster must signal control, precision, and founder-led trust—not retail discount. Target audience: Indian B2B teams evaluating AI spend during festive buying cycle. Typography and spatial hierarchy must carry all commercial and contextual information; minimal or absent decorative elements.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
**"Engineered Pricing for Indian Teams"**

Frame the two-tier pricing as precise, controlled offerings aligned with Aight's core merchant philosophy: prepaid rupees, hard caps, one domestic GST invoice, founder-answerable support. The festive season is the temporal hook for adoption urgency, not the discount narrative. Typography hierarchy and white space are the poster's visual system; text scale, weight, and placement do all perceptual work. Prices read as infrastructure parameters, not bargain bait.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY

**1st Read (Dominant):** Aight wordmark + price structure: "Image ₹9 | Video ₹99"  
Cue: Brand placement (top), large-scale pricing in geometric sans-serif, high contrast against background.

**2nd Read (Secondary):** Core control proposition: "Prepaid rupees. Hard caps. One invoice. Designed for Indian teams."  
Cue: Medium-sized, medium-grey type, positioned below pricing, reading as reassurance/proof of seriousness.

**3rd Read (Tertiary):** Festive season frame + call to action: "This Festive Season. Talk to us at getaight.ai"  
Cue: Smaller type, lower placement, natural reading conclusion, soft CTA (website-forward, not aggressive).

**Rationale:** Indian B2B decision-makers scan for three things in sequence: brand/credibility, price/specification, next step. Hierarchy respects this cognitive order.

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM

**attention_order:**  
1st: Aight wordmark/logo, positioned upper-centre (brand authority, immediate recognition, reads first by established placement convention).  
2nd: ₹9 and ₹99 displayed as left/right pair in large bold type with supporting labels ("Image" / "Video" above each), separated by thin vertical divider; read by scale contrast and central placement.  
3rd: Supporting copy and CTA in progressively smaller, lower-contrast type; read by sequential tonal reduction toward poster base.

**surface_finish_per_key_object:**  
Aight wordmark: matte/flat (no gloss, brand-standard finish, clean).  
Pricing figures (₹9 / ₹99): matte/direct (technical, precise, no flourish or reflection).  
Supporting text and background: matte/diffuse (low visual competition, reading-comfortable, no glare).

**implied_light_source:**  
Studio-neutral, evenly distributed (not sunlit, not dramatic). Typography poster; lighting is conceptual, not photographic. No direction implied—even illumination throughout to avoid visual distraction from text hierarchy. Not governed by localized source (no shadow, no spotlight).

**placement_zone:**  
Aight wordmark: upper-centre zone (~12–18% from top), centred horizontally. Reason: brand authority and immediate legibility.  
₹9 / ₹99 pricing pair: central zone (~40–55% from top), off-axis horizontally with symmetrical margins. Reason: visual weight balance, white-space breathing room, avoids edge tangency per CA-D2 / CA-D3.  
Supporting copy: mid-lower zone (~62–72% from top), centred. Reason: natural reading flow, secondary emphasis.  
CTA and website URL: lower zone (~85–92% from top), centred. Reason: reading conclusion, calls eye downward to action.

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

1. **Canvas Setup:**  
   - 1080 × 1350 pixels, 72 dpi, RGB colour mode.  
   - Background: pure white (#FFFFFF) or off-white (#F9F9F9) for professional/neutral.

2. **Layer 1 — Aight Branding (Top):**  
   - Aight official logo/wordmark (sourced from brand guidelines).  
   - Positioned centred horizontally, ~90–120 pixels from top edge.  
   - Size: ~150–180 pixels wide (scales legibly on mobile and print).  
   - Colour: Aight brand primary colour or dark charcoal (#1A1A1A) if brand colour unavailable.

3. **Layer 2 — Primary Pricing Structure (Central):**  
   - Two-column layout, left and right of vertical divider.  
   - **Left column:**  
     - Label: "Image" (smaller type, ~24–28px, medium grey #666666).  
     - Price: "₹9" (large bold type, ~120–140px, dark charcoal #1A1A1A, weight: bold or black).  
   - **Divider:** Thin vertical line (1–2px, medium grey #CCCCCC), ~50–80 pixels tall, centred between columns.  
   - **Right column:**  
     - Label: "Video" (same style as left label).  
     - Price: "₹99" (same style as left price).  
   - Positioning: centred vertically ~40–55% from top, full width with symmetrical side margins (~60–80px each).  
   - Typeface: Geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, *Poppins*, *Public Sans*; modern fintech/AI aesthetic).

4. **Layer 3 — Supporting Copy (Mid-Lower):**  
   - Single line or two-line block: "Prepaid rupees. Hard caps. One invoice. Designed for Indian teams."  
   - Type: ~20–24px, medium grey (#555555), medium weight.  
   - Alignment: centred, positioned ~62–72% from top.  
   - Optional sub-line (if space permits): "One prepaid wallet. One domestic GST invoice. Hard caps per team. Your spend, your control." (smaller, lighter grey, supporting detail).

5. **Layer 4 — Festive Season Visual Anchor (Optional, Minimal):**  
   - Small text reference: "This Festive Season" or "For Indian Teams, This Season" (~16–18px, light grey #888888, medium weight).  
   - Positioned ~78–82% from top, OR integrated into upper margin of CTA block.  
   - No emoji, icon, or visual decoration; text-only to maintain professional tone.

6. **Layer 5 — Call to Action & Footer (~85–92% from top):**  
   - Primary CTA: "Talk to us" (bold, ~24–28px, dark charcoal, or underlined link style).  
   - Secondary line: "Start at getaight.ai" (OR simpler: just website URL).  
   - Website: "getaight.ai" (~16–18px, medium grey, or brand colour if appropriate).  
   - Optional: WhatsApp icon + "Message us" (if brief permits direct contact integration; small, ~14px).  
   - All elements centred horizontally, with tight vertical spacing (8–12px between lines).

7. **Margins & Safe Zone:**  
   - Left/right margins: ≥60px (ensure text doesn't abut frame edge).  
   - Top margin: ≥50px (Aight logo breathing room).  
   - Bottom margin: ≥40px (CTA and URL clear of edge).  
   - No accidental near-tangencies between text and frame edges (per CA-D3).

8. **Final QA:**  
   - Test legibility at 50% scale (mobile thumbnail size).  
   - Verify colour contrast ratios meet WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for body text).  
   - Confirm pricing (₹9 / ₹99) is the visual focal point (largest, darkest, most centred).  
   - Ensure no discount language ("Limited," "Save," "Offer," etc.); all copy emphasizes control and infrastructure.
