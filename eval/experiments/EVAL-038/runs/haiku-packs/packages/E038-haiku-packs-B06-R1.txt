Communicate the Aster Meridian 38 as a precision-crafted mechanical timepiece through a photograph that reveals material finishes (brushed stainless steel, sunburst dial, silver accents, leather strap), demonstrates the watch in its functional context (on wrist), and sustains commercial effectiveness on a premium product page. ## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
Watch mounted on wrist with arm angled to display the dial face symmetrically and prominently within the portrait frame. Directional key light from upper-left (45° above horizontal) reveals the brushed steel case texture, catches subtle speculars on silver hour markers and hands, and illuminates the sunburst dial pattern evenly without shadow. Wrist and strap ground the watch in use without descending into lifestyle imagery. Warm-neutral background separates the leather strap by tone. Minimal styling, maximum material legibility.

- Silver baton hour markers: glossy/direct—small, high-contrast speculars consistent with source position.  
- Silver dauphine hands: glossy/direct—speculars aligned with implied source; no falloff due to distance (PA-D2 rule on direct reflection).

**implied_light_source:**

Single nameable source; all case texture, hand speculars, and dial sheen consistent with this placement. Medium softness (~3–4 foot soft modifier at working distance) to preserve material character without harsh shadows (PA-D3: shadow edge softness agrees with specular highlight size on silver elements).

**placement_zone:**  
Watch dial positioned left-of-centre in upper-middle portion of frame (~30–45% from left edge, occupying vertical middle 40–50% of frame height). Wrist/arm enters from lower-right, exits frame edge with slight deliberate gap (CA-D3: busy scene with arm and watch, edges stop mattering; gap is intentional, not accidental near-tangency). ## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**1. Subject Setup**  
- Watch mounted on wrist/forearm; arm model or wrist form with neutral or female-presenting skin tone, natural appearance.  
- Arm positioned at approximately 40° angle to camera lens axis, diagonal from lower-right toward upper-left of frame.  
- Watch dial tilted 3–5° toward camera (not flat, not rotated away); tilt preserves dimensional case profile while presenting dial face clearly.  
- Hands set to ~10:10 (conventional watch-photography position showing maximum dial/brand space).  
- Sapphire crystal: ensure domed profile visible; no fingerprints, dust, or interior reflections.

**2. Framing**  
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 (portrait/vertical orientation); 4 units wide, 5 units tall.  
- Watch dial occupies middle 40–50% of frame height; dial face not centred vertically but positioned in upper-middle zone.  
- Arm enters frame from lower-right corner, exits lower frame edge with ~0.5–1 inch gap (slight deliberate gap per CA-D3).  
- Background visible on right and left edges; bottom edge shows arm/strap; top edge shows negative space above watch.

**3. Lighting**  
- Key light: 45° from upper-left, medium diffusion (soft modifier 3–4 feet at working distance relative to watch size); power sufficient to reveal brushed case texture and create distinct speculars on silver markers/hands without blown highlights.  
- Fill light: minimal or none; preserve material contrast and tonal separation (PA-D5).  
- Backlight/rim light: none unless needed for leather strap separation; if used, position to graze strap edge only, not to create halo or compete with key.  
- No light sources visible in frame; all illumination appears to originate from single directional source (PA-D4: fictional source consistency).

**4. Background**  
- Tone: warm-neutral mid-tone (approximate 18% gray in daylight balance), or subtle warm-white (off-white, not pure white).  
- Texture: none or extremely subtle; no pattern, gradient, or bokeh that competes with watch.  
- Separation: background tonal value selected to separate dark brown leather strap by contrast (PA-D5: product-to-ground tonal contrast survives grayscale check).  
- Distance: sufficiently distant to remain unsharp without required blur (depth of field natural, not artificial bokeh).

**5. Exposure and Colour**  
- White balance: neutral daylight (~5500 K) or warm-daylight (~5000 K) to render brushed steel naturally and deep blue dial with full saturation.  
- Exposure: expose for dial face and case midtones; silver markers and hands allowed to catch bright speculars without clipping; skin tone on wrist warm and natural (not desaturated).  
- Colour accuracy: blue dial saturation preserved; silver rendered as bright without cast; leather strap warm brown, not muddy or oversaturated.  
- Contrast: moderate; avoid flat, avoid harsh. Material character (brushed texture, sunburst pattern) revealed by lighting, not by contrast slider.

**6. Postproduction**  
- Retouching: minimal and only to support already-implied lighting. Remove blemishes, dust, fingerprints on crystal only; do not alter case geometry, dial pattern, hand positions, or strap texture.  
- Enhance material clarity: sharpen brushed-steel texture and sunburst dial pattern only enough to make the implied key-light direction visible; no artificial enhancement or surrealism.  
- Colour grading: none beyond white-balance correction; do not introduce filter, vignette, or tone mapping. Preserve photographic realism.  
- Crop: final crop to exact 4:5 ratio after capture; reframing allowed only to correct for framing errors, not to alter composition intent.

## DOCTRINE_DEVIATIONS

**CA-D6: overridden because brief specifies "4:5" aspect ratio as a fixed commercial format parameter.** CA-D6 requires aspect ratio to be justified by scene shapes (fre_003). However, the brief states "premium 4:5 e-commerce hero image" with 4:5 as an explicit format requirement, not a compositional choice. 4:5 is a fixed deliverable parameter (portrait orientation for vertical-feed e-commerce platforms), not derived from composition reasoning. Scene shapes (watch dial, wrist) are vertical-leaning and align with 4:5 post-hoc, but do not constitute the decision driver. Fixed parameters override doctrine composition defaults. Brief clause: "Create one premium 4:5 e-commerce hero image."

**PA-D1: Canon gap noted for sunburst dial surface.** PA-D1 requires every key object to declare one finish from the diffuse/direct/glare contrast set. The deep blue sunburst dial is a radial anisotropic brushed surface, which Canon does not model (PACK LIMIT: "no accepted claim covers…anisotropic brushed/sunburst surfaces — the diffuse/direct/glare contrast set (PA-D1) does not model radial anisotropic glow"). The sunburst finish is functionally diffuse (no mirror reflectivity) but exhibits radial anisotropic character (directional micro-brushing creates apparent glow in radial direction under angled light). Treated as diffuse in PA-D1 declaration, but lighting design (directional key at 45° upper-left) will reveal sunburst pattern by exploiting the anisotropic surface response to directional light, making the radial pattern visually prominent. No deviation from PA-D1 doctrine itself; gap identified and accounted for in lighting recipe. Brief clause: "deep blue sunburst dial" (fixed product specification).
