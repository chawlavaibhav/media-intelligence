# FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

## DELIVERABLE
A 16:9 landscape cinematic video, six static shots, ~40 seconds total. Two people seated at a café table. One discovers the other's concealment through dialogue and reaction. Dialogue is tense and restrained. Spatial relationship between subjects remains clear throughout via camera positioning and line maintenance.

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer requests a compact dialogue scene (two people, one setting, discovery tension) produced as cinematic coverage rather than mechanical shot-reverse-shot. The brief mandates exactly six shots, minimal camera movement, and persistent spatial clarity. The 16:9 fixed aspect ratio, six-shot limit, and ~40-second duration are forcing constraints. The emotional core is discovery and the inability to hide—visual restraint mirrors dialogue restraint.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
Maintain a stable 180-degree arc using three framing strategies: (1) a wide two-shot establishing spatial relationship, (2) alternating medium close-ups on each person's reaction, (3) return to wides or two-shot to reset and drive emotional beats. No tracking, no pans—all camera positions are static. Composition changes at every cut to support attention flow. Sightlines persist across all cuts; the viewer always knows who faces whom.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. **Primary (Emotion):** The creeping certainty of discovery and the suffocation of the concealing person.  
2. **Secondary (Story):** The probing question, the deflection, the moment of exposure, the surrender.  
3. **Tertiary (Rhythm):** Quiet pauses between dialogue lines; the scene is not rushed.  
4. Spatial clarity is a structural requirement, not decoration—it guarantees the viewer tracks the power dynamic.

## VISUAL_SYSTEM

**attention_order:**  
1st read: The speaker's face and eyes in each shot (dominant by contrast and cultural salience).  
2nd read: The listener's micro-reaction (in wide shots, visible in frame; in mediums, cut to close-up).  
3rd read: Café environment—table, background, implied location.

**surface_finish_per_key_object:**  
Person A face: diffuse/matte (neutral, searchlight quality).  
Person B face: diffuse/matte (neutral, searchlight quality).  
Café table surface: matte/diffuse (no glare distraction).  
Window or café background: diffuse, darker in tone than subjects (supporting role, no glare).

**implied_light_source:**  
One nameable source: soft window light from the left side of frame (typical café), diffused and cool-toned. Direction: 45° from left front, entering the scene plane. This is fictional but plausible; it justifies even lighting across both faces without visible key/fill instrument separation.

**placement_zone:**  
Wide shots (1, 4, 6): Person A occupies left third of table, Person B occupies right third. Reason: maintains clear sightline, separation reinforces tension, allows both faces to be seen without overlap. Reason for three-zone use: sparse, ordered scene (CA-D2 default).  
Medium on A (Shot 2): Center-left of frame, reason: A is driving conversation; slight compositional dominance mirrors narrative agency.  
Medium on B (Shot 3): Center-right of frame, reason: B is reactive and contained; mirrored composition echoes A's, creating visual symmetry of roles.

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Pre-production:**  
- Finalize dialogue (assumed provided separately; tension tone is implicit in blocking, not shouted delivery).  
- Select café location with window light source or position key light to simulate café window direction (soft, diffuse, left side).  
- Mark three tape marks on floor/camera positions for the six static shots (two wide, two medium-A, one medium-B, one two-shot or wide variant).

**Shot-by-shot blocking:**  

| Shot | Type | Duration | Position & Frame | Subject Position | Dialogue/Action | Purpose |
|------|------|----------|------------------|------------------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | Wide | ~5 sec | Camera ~6 feet back, table-height, angled 30° to A | A frame-left third, B frame-right third | A studies B; B avoids eye contact, fidgets slightly | Establish relationship, spatial clarity, B's discomfort |
| 2 | Medium | ~4 sec | Camera on A's side, 3 feet away, chest-up frame | A center-left | A asks probing question; vocal restraint, intensity in eyes | Isolate A's certainty; sightline toward frame-right (toward B) |
| 3 | Medium | ~4 sec | Camera on B's side (opposite side of 180° arc), 3 feet away, chest-up frame | B center-right | B deflects, looks away briefly, small gesture of denial | Isolate B's collapse of composure; sightline toward frame-left (toward A) |
| 4 | Wide | ~5 sec | Camera return to Shot 1 position or slight shift, table-height | A frame-left, B frame-right (slightly tighter than Shot 1) | A shifts forward/posture hardens; B recognizes A's knowledge | Reset visual, escalate emotional beat, both visible |
| 5 | Medium | ~5 sec | Camera on A's side, slightly closer than Shot 2 | A center-left, more prominent | A makes direct statement (accusation or clarification); no evasion | A's authority peaked; no room for B to hide |
| 6 | Two-shot or tight wide | ~6 sec | Camera wide but tighter than Shot 1, or slight reframing of A+B together | A frame-left, B frame-right, closer separation | B accepts/surrenders through subtle expression (eyes drop, small nod, silence); A's gaze softens | Resolution—recognition, no dialogue required; B's isolation ends |

## AUDIO_AND_EDIT

**Dialogue:**  
Assumed provided separately with the following delivery:  
- Shot 1: B's silence, soft ambient.  
- Shot 2: A speaks (one line or phrase, ~3 sec of speech).  
- Shot 3: B responds or deflects (~2–3 sec of speech).  
- Shot 4: Silence or short reaction; A may speak a challenge (~2 sec).  
- Shot 5: A's final statement or clarification (~3–4 sec).  
- Shot 6: Silence or a single word from B; predominantly quiet.

**Edit:**  
All cuts are synchronous with dialogue or emotional beats. No overlap editing. Each shot is held for its full duration; silent beats are preserved. The final moment (Shot 6) holds 6 seconds to allow B's acceptance to fully register without dialogue.

**Sound design:**  
Café ambience: low, continuous, non-intrusive. A subtle musical underscore (optional, brief only if it reinforces restraint—no swelling). Final moment: ambience drops slightly to emphasize silence and recognition.

## FAILURE_PREVENTION

**CA-D1: pass** — Three reads established per shot: (1st) speaker's face/eyes, dominant by salience and emotion; (2nd) listener's reaction, visible in wides and cut to close-up; (3rd) environment/table/background. No competing cues per beat.

**CA-D2: pass** — All placements stated as zones with explicit reasons; no named ratios or coordinate rules used. (Left third, right third, center-left, center-right — all zone descriptions, not ratios.)

**CA-D3: pass** — Wide shots use tight fit with deliberate gap (sparse, ordered scene per CA-D3 default). Medium shots focus on face; edge treatment is implicit (faces fill frame, not competing with edges).

**CA-D4: pass** — Café background and window are present but darker and thinner than subjects; they do not frame subjects, they contextualize.

**CA-D5: pass** — Declared balanced. Tonal weight and compositional weight are symmetrical: A on left-third, B on right-third, equal visual weight. Restraint requires visual stability; restless composition would undermine dialogue.

**CA-D6: pass** — Fixed 16:9 aspect ratio justified by scene requirement for spatial clarity across width of café table two-shot. Horizontal emphasis suits restraint and distance of relationship.

**CA-D7: pass** — Five cuts across six shots.  
Cut 1→2: New information (move to A's face); motivation (A speaks, sightline off-frame).  
Cut 2→3: New information (move to B's reaction); motivation (A's words land, B deflects).  
Cut 3→4: New information (reset to wide, A's posture shift visible); motivation (A's certainty grows, dynamic changes).  
Cut 4→5: New information (return to A's face, closer than before); motivation (A makes final statement).  
Cut 5→6: New information (both visible again); motivation (moment of mutual recognition).

**CA-D8: pass** — No conflicting criteria sacrifice. Emotion (discovery, tension, surrender) is the priority; story (probing, deflection, exposure) supports it; rhythm (pacing via silence and dialogue beat) is secondary. No lower-ranked criteria (eye-trace, planarity, 3D space) are sacrificed.

**CA-D9: pass** — 180° arc maintained throughout all six shots.  
- Wide shots: Camera position is constant, both subjects visible from same side of the line.  
- Medium on A (Shot 2): Sightline frame-right (toward B); camera is on A's side of the line.  
- Medium on B (Shot 3): Sightline frame-left (toward A); camera is on B's side of the line, opposite Shot 2 per the arc.  
- Returns to wide: Re-establish line position. Screen direction and side-of-frame persist across all cuts; line crossing does not occur.

**CA-D10: pass** — No shot outlasts its describable content.  
- Shot 1: "Two people at table, one studying, one avoiding." ~5 sec.  
- Shot 2: "Woman asks, eyes intense." ~4 sec.

- Shot 5: "Woman states final accusation/truth." ~5 sec.  
- Shot 6: "Both visible, moment of acceptance." ~6 sec.  
All times are content-appropriate.

**CA-D11: pass** — No camera movement; stillness is chosen. All six shots are static. Stillness is motivated by the dialogue-driven scene and emotional beats; no camera technique is required. Brief constraint "avoid elaborate camera movement" is satisfied maximally.
