# FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

## DELIVERABLE
One 9:16 vertical video, ~25 seconds, shot in a single extended take or minimal cuts. Credible Indian woman, direct-to-camera. Product demonstration, clear purchase reason, no claims. Finished asset ready for D2C feed deployment (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok).

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Produce a UGC-authentic short-form video that builds trust through a relatable creator demonstrating a niche skincare product (2% salicylic-acid serum, fragrance-free, for oily/blemish-prone skin). The video must feel like peer-to-peer credible advice, not advertising, while maintaining visual control and commercial usability. The single clear purchase reason is: the serum's lightweight, fast-absorbing texture that doesn't feel greasy — a direct solution to the stated concern. No medical claims, statistics, or brand guarantees.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
**Creator as peer problem-solver.** An Indian woman, speaking directly to camera in her own voice, identifies a specific frustration (heavy serums for oily skin), introduces the product as a solution she uses herself, naturally demonstrates application and texture, and closes with a single, unforced call to action. The visual language is intimate and controlled — soft, flattering light, tight framing, product in hand — rather than staged or glossy. Trust comes from authenticity and relatable detail, not production spectacle.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
**First (0–5 sec):** Establish the problem and the speaker's credibility. Direct address: "If you have oily, acne-prone skin, you know how hard it is to find a serum that actually absorbs. Everything feels heavy or sticky." Tone: conversational, knowing.

**Second (5–18 sec):** Demonstrate the product. Show the bottle, dispense serum onto fingertip (1–2 drops), apply to cheek or forehead, pat in gently, observe absorption in real time. Narrate the benefit: "This one's fragrance-free, goes on completely clean, absorbs in seconds, doesn't leave you feeling greasy. And 2% salicylic acid actually works for the breakouts."

**Third (18–25 sec):** Call to action and close. Direct eye contact, single statement: "Shop now [link in bio]" or "Get it here" (depending on platform). Optional: a brief smile or touch-the-skin moment showing the final, absorbed result.

**One purchase reason:** Fast absorption + lightweight texture = no greasy, heavy-serum frustration.

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM

**attention_order:**  
1st read — Speaker's face and direct eye contact (contrast of focused gaze, frontal position, frame dominance).  
2nd read — Serum in hand and being applied (product movement, change in composition, introduction of the bottle/dispenser).  
3rd read — Serum on skin post-application and absorption (texture change, gloss fading to natural dewy finish).

**surface_finish_per_key_object:**  
- Serum on skin: glossy/direct (wanted highlight showing the wet, absorbing serum layer; reads as hydration and efficacy).  
- Serum bottle/pump: matte/diffuse (frosted or uncoated glass; clean and professional, non-distracting).  
- Speaker's facial skin: diffuse (natural tone, soft catchlight from key light; flattering and trustworthy).

**implied_light_source:**  
Soft frontal + overhead keylight (mimics natural daylight or professional beauty softbox), positioned camera-forward or slightly above, creating soft shadows and a gentle highlight on the serum-moistened skin. Fictional source: "natural window light or a pro beauty ring light." Direction: from above and forward, flattering to the face, revealing the serum's glossy layer without harsh shadows.

**placement_zone:**  
Speaker occupies the upper half to upper two-thirds of the frame (face and shoulders); hands and product demonstration occupy the lower half and centre. Reason: credibility and direct gaze (viewer connection) is established first in the visual hierarchy; product detail and texture are shown in context of the body/hands in the same shot, avoiding a disorienting cut and maintaining the intimate, peer-to-peer tone.

---

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Setup (single location, minimal equipment):**
- **Location:** Indoor space with soft, diffuse natural light (window-lit interior) OR a controlled studio setup with one soft key light (5K or 8K daylight-balanced softbox) positioned slightly above and forward of the speaker.
- **Camera:** Fixed on tripod, 9:16 framing (vertical, full-screen mobile). Minimal camera movement — if any drift, only a very slow push-in to emphasize the speaker's face in beats 1–2, then a subtle pull-back or slight tilt down as hands enter the frame for the demo. No shaky, handheld aesthetic; the intentional control is part of the credibility.
- **Framing:** Tight fit with deliberate gap. Top of frame at mid-forehead or hairline; bottom frame just below the hands when product is applied. Speaker slightly off-centre (rule of thirds region) in beats 1–2; as hands enter, composition shifts slightly to accommodate both face and product.
- **Serum product:** Dispense into a clean fingertip from the bottle/pump (1–2 drops, realistic quantity). Apply to a visible face zone (cheek or forehead, or both). Allow ~3–4 seconds of on-camera absorption — the serum should visibly reduce gloss and integrate into the skin during the shot.
- **Sound:** Direct audio (voice recorded on-set or in post, synced tightly). No background music or ambient bed — the creator's voice is the primary track. Optional: subtle room tone or one neutral ambient track at very low volume, so it feels like a real space, not silence.

## GENERATION_PROMPTS

### FINAL GENERATION PROMPT (single shot, extended take):

**Shot 1 — "Creator Direct-to-Camera & Product Demo" (0–25 sec, 9:16 vertical)**

**Framing & Setup:**
Vertical 9:16 frame, fixed tripod camera. Indian woman (late 20s–early 40s, medium to medium-dark skin tone, natural makeup, approachable appearance) positioned slightly left-of-centre in the upper two-thirds of frame, shoulders and face fill the top half. She is seated or standing at a neutral depth, well-lit by soft, diffuse frontal + overhead light.

**Beats & Narration:**

*Beat 1 (0–5 sec):* Woman speaks directly to camera. Conversational, intimate tone. Script (or direction for natural delivery): "If you have oily, acne-prone skin, you know the struggle — every serum feels too heavy, it sits on top of your skin all day." She touches her own cheek gently during this line, or just maintains eye contact.

*Beat 2 (5–18 sec):* She gestures to the serum bottle (in frame, lower half). Picks it up, shows the label briefly (readable but not didactic). Dispenses 1–2 drops onto a clean fingertip. "This one's different. Fragrance-free, 2% salicylic acid, and it actually absorbs." She applies the serum to her cheek or forehead (visible in frame), patting it in with a gentle fingertip motion. Over 4–5 seconds, the serum visibly integrates — the glossy wet layer fades into a natural dewy finish as she speaks: "Watch — goes on clean, absorbs in seconds. No greasy layer sitting there all day. You actually feel it working."

*Beat 3 (18–25 sec):* She looks back at the camera, slight pause, confident tone. "Shop now. Link in bio." Optional: a brief, natural smile or a final touch to her skin showing the final absorbed result (dewy but not greasy).

**Lighting:**
Soft, even, flattering light. Key light at 45–60° from above and camera-forward. Fill light to reduce shadows on the off-side of the face (soft, diffuse). No harsh shadows. The serum on the skin should catch a subtle, soft highlight (consistent with the soft key source), showing its wet, absorbing quality. No glare on the bottle label; angle the bottle if needed to avoid distracting speculars.

**Camera Movement:**
Negligible or very subtle. Option A: completely static (preferred for UGC authenticity). Option B: a very slow 5–10-degree tilt downward as hands enter the frame (~5 sec mark), or a barely-perceptible push-in during beat 2 to emphasize the product detail. No pan, no zoom snap, no handheld shake.

**Color & Tone:**
Warm, neutral, clean. Skin tone rendered accurately and flatteringly (no excessive saturation or cooling). The serum should read as clear or subtle golden/amber (depending on actual product formulation) against the skin — distinct visually from the skin itself. Background should be neutral and out of focus, or a simple, clean interior (white, soft grey, or muted beige wall; no busy pattern).

**Audio:**
Direct voice, clear and natural. No radio-static effect, no artificial enhancement. Room tone or one subtle ambient track at very low level (optional). No background music, no voiceover layer.

**End Frame:**
Final frame holds on the speaker's face, either a direct gaze or a final touch-to-skin moment, for ~1 second after the call-to-action line, to avoid a jarring cut.

## DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS

- **Speaker:** Casting brief — Indian woman, ages 25–45, oily/blemish-prone skin type (or appearance thereof), natural makeup, relatable and credible demeanor. Avoid hyper-polished beauty-influencer aesthetic; lean toward "real person" UGC casting.
- **Serum bottle/packaging:** Use the actual product or a realistic mock-up. Label must be readable but not jarring (secondary element in frame).
- **Script/Talking Points:** Provide the creator with the talking points above (problem, product feature, benefit, CTA) but allow natural delivery — avoid scripted-sounding phrasing. The goal is conversational authenticity, so slight variations in wording are encouraged.
- **Location:** Clean, neutral interior with soft natural or controlled light. No branded set dressing; this is a home/creator space aesthetic, not a retail environment.
- **Post-Production Color Grade:** Warm, skin-flattering, clean. White balance neutral to slightly warm. No heavy color grading or moody filters; the mood comes from the light character and the speaker's tone, not from post-LUT.

## AUDIO_AND_EDIT

**Audio Mix:**
- Direct voice (speaker's monologue) at primary level.
- Optional subtle room tone or single ambient track (very low volume, ~–20 dB) to avoid dead silence.
- No music, no sound effects, no voiceover layer.
- Clean audio in; no hum, room noise, or wind.

**Editing / Cut Plan:**
Single extended take preferred. If multiple takes are needed for coverage:
- Take the best single continuous performance (0–25 sec) as the primary cut.
- If beat 2 (product demo) needs a cut for timing or retake, make one cut **inside the serum-application moment** (e.g., cut on her hand just after touching the skin, so the viewer doesn't see a jump in the absorption). Keep this cut invisible; the audio continues unbroken, and the visual change is masked by hand movement or a brief blink/gaze shift.
- No jump cuts, dissolves, or speed ramps. If multiple takes exist, conform them into one seamless performance via careful trim and sync.
- Final frame holds for ~1 second after the CTA line, then fade to black (optional) or a static end-card (optional, depending on platform spec).

**Pacing:**
- Beats 1–2: ~15 seconds. Enough time for the demo to read clearly and for the serum to visibly absorb on skin.
- Beat 3: ~10 seconds. Enough for the CTA and a beat of silence (or breath) so the viewer has time to register the call to action.
- Total: ~25 seconds (as per brief).
