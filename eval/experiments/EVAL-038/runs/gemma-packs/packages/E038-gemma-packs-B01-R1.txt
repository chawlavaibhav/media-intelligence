FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Vertical Video Ad (9:16), ~30 seconds.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Convert the operational frustration of Indian PG/Hostel owners (the "1990s way" of managing via notebooks and WhatsApp) into a desire for the "2026 way" (RentOK). The transition must be visceral: from claustrophobic, high-stress chaos to clean, effortless digital control.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Great Reset." A high-tempo montage of "Administrative Noise" (physical registers, notification overload, tenant disputes) that is abruptly silenced by the RentOK interface, shifting the mood from anxiety to authority.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. The Pain: Operational leakages, "jhikjhik" (hassle), and missed follow-ups.
2. The Solution: RentOK—the clean, all-in-one system.
3. The Value: Autopay, Digital KYC, and Floor Plan management.
4. The CTA: "Get free demo" at rentok.com.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st: Owner’s stressed expression (Emotion) $\rightarrow$ 2nd: Cluttered physical ledger (Contrast) $\rightarrow$ 3rd: RentOK app interface (Resolution).
surface_finish_per_key_object: Ledger/Paper -> matte/diffuse, Smartphone screen -> glossy/direct, Tea glass -> glare.
implied_light_source: Harsh, flickering fluorescent overhead (Chaos sequence) $\rightarrow$ Warm, soft natural window light (Order sequence).
placement_zone: Off-centre zones; used to emphasize the surrounding clutter during the chaos phase.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Setting: A typical Indian PG manager's desk (crowded with files, a calculator, a half-empty tea glass, and multiple phones).
- Casting: An Indian male/female (40-55) who looks like a seasoned property owner—credible, not a model.
- Color Grade: De-saturated, slightly yellow/green tints for "Chaos"; High-contrast, clean whites and RentOK brand colors for "Order."
- Pace: 0-12s (Hyper-fast cuts, 0.5s-1s per shot); 13-30s (Smooth, rhythmic, 3-4s per shot).
