FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic video scene (6 shots, ~40 seconds, 16:9).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, restrained confrontation between two characters at a café. The objective is to convey a discovery of betrayal/concealment through visual subtext and spatial clarity, avoiding the sterility of mechanical coverage by utilizing a detail-cut and varying the emotional weight of the shots.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." The scene focuses on the micro-expressions of the characters and the physical presence of the concealed evidence, using a strict 180-degree axis to lock the viewer into the claustrophobic tension of the table.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Emotional State: Tense, restrained, accusatory (Primary).
2. Spatial Relationship: Opposite each other at a table (Secondary).
3. The Catalyst: The concealed object (Tertiary).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
attention_order: 1st read: Eyes (or the Evidence object); 2nd read: Hands/Mouth tension; 3rd read: Café environment.
surface_finish_per_key_object: Table -> matte; Coffee cup -> glossy; Evidence (Paper) -> diffuse.
implied_light_source: Large window, side-lighting from the left.
placement_zone: Off-centre Left/Right zones to maintain the 180-degree line and emphasize the distance between characters.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Lighting: High-contrast side-lighting (Chiaroscuro effect). Deep shadows on the side of the face facing away from the window.
- Color Palette: Cold blues and greys in the background, muted earth tones for the table and clothing.
- Depth of Field: Shallow (f/2.8) for close-ups to isolate characters from the café background.
- Camera: Static shots. No pans, tilts, or zooms.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (4s): Wide shot, 16:9. Two people sitting opposite each other at a long rectangular wooden café table. Side profile. Cold morning light from a large window on the left. Shallow depth of field. Cinematic film stock, muted colors.
Shot 2 (7s): Medium close-up, 16:9. Person A (The Discoverer) in the left zone. Eyes fixed intensely on Person B. Restrained expression, jaw tight. Side-lit from the left, deep shadows on the right side of the face. 
Shot 3 (7s): Medium close-up, 16:9. Person B (The Concealer) in the right zone. Avoiding eye contact, looking down at the table. Nervous hand fidgeting with a coffee cup. Side-lit from the left.
Shot 4 (6s): Extreme close-up, 16:9. A crumpled white piece of paper (the evidence) resting on the matte brown wooden table. High contrast. Shallow depth of field.
Shot 5 (8s): Close-up, 16:9. Person A in the left zone. Speaking calmly but firmly. Focus on the mouth and lower face tension. Side-lit from the left.
Shot 6 (8s): Close-up, 16:9. Person B in the right zone. Looking up slowly to meet Person A's eyes. A long, heavy silence. Expression of defeated resignation. Side-lit from the left.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aspect Ratio: Fixed 16:9.
- Shot Count: Exactly 6.
- Dialogue: Tense, restrained, low volume.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Edit: Straight cuts.
- Audio: Ambient café noise (distant chatter, clinking spoons) lowered in volume during close-ups to create a "sonic vacuum" around the characters.
- Foley: The sharp sound of the paper being touched in Shot 4; the soft clink of a cup in Shot 3.
- Music: No music; only a low-frequency atmospheric drone to heighten tension.
