FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 premium promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The objective is to communicate a highly competitive price point (₹9 for images, ₹99 for video) to Indian B2B customers during the festive season without compromising the brand's identity as a serious, reliable AI infrastructure provider. The "premium" feel is achieved by avoiding retail tropes (starbursts, loud reds, cluttered text) and instead using a high-contrast, minimalist "Dark Mode" aesthetic with luxury festive accents.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Precision Festive." The design pairs the mathematical rigor of an API (grid systems, dark mode, clean typography) with the warmth and prestige of Indian festivities (deep gold and saffron light). The price points are treated as "architectural elements" rather than "discounts," making them feel like standard, efficient infrastructure costs.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. THE VALUE (Focal Point): ₹9 / ₹99 (Massive, bold, immediate).
2. THE PRODUCT (Context): Image Generation / Video Generation.
3. THE TARGET/OCCASION (Secondary): Festive Edition for Indian Businesses.
4. THE TRUST SIGNALS (Tertiary): One domestic GST invoice. No forex fees.
5. BRANDING (Anchor): Aight logo and getaight.ai.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Obsidian Black (#0B0B0B) background; Champagne Gold (#D4AF37) and Saffron (#F4C430) for light accents; Crisp White (#FFFFFF) for primary typography.
- Typography: High-contrast geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Montserrat).
    - Prices: Ultra-bold, heavy weight, oversized.
    - Labels: Medium weight, all-caps, wide tracking (letter-spacing).
    - Details: Light weight, clean, centered.
- Composition: Centered, symmetrical grid. Significant negative space to ensure a "gallery" or "infrastructure" feel.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. GENERATE BACKGROUND: Create a high-fidelity abstract 3D render representing "generative AI" using a luxury festive palette.
2. TYPOGRAPHIC OVERLAY:
    - Place the Aight logo at the top center.
    - Create a two-column grid in the center.
    - Left Column: "IMAGE" (small, white, tracked) above "₹9" (massive, gold).
    - Right Column: "VIDEO" (small, white, tracked) above "₹99" (massive, gold).
    - Below the grid: "Festive Edition for Indian Businesses" (medium white).
    - Bottom anchor: "One domestic GST invoice. No forex fees." (light grey/white).
    - Footer: "getaight.ai" (medium white).
