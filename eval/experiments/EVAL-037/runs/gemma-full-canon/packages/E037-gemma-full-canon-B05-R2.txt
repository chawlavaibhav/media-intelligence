FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic short scene (Video)

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, low-volume confrontation between two characters at a café. The visual priority is spatial orientation (maintaining the 180-degree rule) and emotional restraint, conveyed through framing and timing rather than camera movement.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Quiet Fracture." The scene uses a rigid, static formal structure to mirror the emotional paralysis and restraint of the characters. The tension is built through the contrast between the mundane environment (café) and the gravity of the revelation.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Relation (Where they are)
2. Emotional Subtext (Tension/Betrayal)
3. Narrative Reveal (The concealed item)
4. Environment (Café atmosphere)

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9
- Lighting: Soft, directional window light creating subtle shadows on faces.
- Palette: Muted tones, desaturated teals and warm woods.
- Camera: Static shots, shallow depth of field to isolate characters from the background.
- Composition: Strict adherence to the 180-degree axis.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Setting: A quiet corner of a modern café, small square table.
- Characters: Person A (Accuser), Person B (Secret Keeper).
- Pacing: Slow cuts, allowing silence to occupy the space between lines.

GENERATION_PROMPTS

Shot 1: Establishing Wide Shot (6s)
Prompt: Cinematic wide shot, profile view of two people sitting opposite each other at a small square wooden table in a quiet, minimalist urban café. Soft morning window light. 16:9, static camera, shallow depth of field. The atmosphere is heavy and still.

Shot 2: Medium Shot - Person A (8s)
Prompt: Cinematic medium shot of Person A. Steady, piercing gaze, expression of controlled disappointment. Soft side-lighting. Background is blurred café interior. 16:9, static camera. Person A speaks calmly, lips barely moving.

Shot 3: Medium Shot - Person B (8s)
Prompt: Cinematic medium shot of Person B, facing Person A. A momentary freeze, eyes darting slightly before settling on Person A. Expression of guilt and restraint. 16:9, static camera. Soft window light.

Shot 4: Close-up Detail (6s)
Prompt: Cinematic extreme close-up of a hand on the café table. The hand slowly tightens around a white ceramic coffee cup, knuckles whitening. Beside the cup lies a folded piece of paper (the evidence). 16:9, static camera, macro lens, shallow depth of field.

Shot 5: Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) - A to B (6s)
Prompt: Cinematic over-the-shoulder shot from behind Person A's shoulder, looking at Person B. Person B is framed by the shoulder of A, creating a sense of confrontation. Person B opens their mouth to speak but hesitates. 16:9, static camera.

Shot 6: Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) - B to A (6s)
Prompt: Cinematic over-the-shoulder shot from behind Person B's shoulder, looking at Person A. Person A remains motionless, staring with a cold, knowing expression. 16:9, static camera. Fade to black.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Axis of Action: A strict 180-degree line drawn through the center of the table; the camera never crosses this line.
- Prop: A single folded piece of paper placed centrally on the table in Shot 4.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Ambient café noise (distant clinking of spoons, low hum) suddenly dipping in volume during dialogue.
- Dialogue: Low-volume, whispered tones.
- Edit: Hard cuts between shots. No transitions. Total duration: 40 seconds.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To prevent spatial confusion: Every shot must maintain the left-to-right relationship (Person A always on the left side of the frame or the foreground shoulder on the left; Person B always on the right or the foreground shoulder on the right).
- To prevent "mechanical" feel: Ensure lighting is cinematic (chiaroscuro effect) rather than flat, and focus on the micro-expressions of the actors.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Two people opposite each other? Yes.
- Concealment plot? Yes.
- Tense/restrained/no shouting? Yes.
- Spatial clarity maintained? Yes (180-degree rule).
- Exactly six shots? Yes.
- No elaborate movement? Yes (All static).
- Cinematic feel? Yes (Lighting/Depth of field).
- Duration ~40s? Yes (6+8+8+6+6+6 = 40).
- Aspect ratio 16:9? Yes.
- No external websites? Yes.
