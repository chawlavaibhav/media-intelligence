FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
40-second cinematic scene (6 shots, 16:9).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, low-volume confrontation between two characters at a café. The visual priority is spatial clarity (maintaining the 180-degree rule) and emotional restraint, using static framing to amplify the psychological pressure.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." Instead of using camera movement to create energy, the scene uses stillness and a "ping-pong" editing rhythm to create a claustrophobic feeling of entrapment, despite the open café setting.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Orientation: Establish the opposite positions immediately.
2. Power Shift: Transition from the "accuser" (Person A) to the "accused" (Person B).
3. The Catalyst: The physical evidence of the concealment.
4. The Result: The emotional impasse.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Muted teals and warm ambers; high contrast but desaturated.
- Lighting: Soft side-lighting from a window, creating partial shadows on faces to suggest duality/secrets.
- Composition: Tight framing with shallow depth of field to isolate the characters from the café background.
- Camera: Static shots. No pans, tilts, or zooms.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot 1: Establishing Medium Wide (Profile) - 7s
- Shot 2: Medium Close-Up (Person A) - 7s
- Shot 3: Medium Close-Up (Person B) - 7s
- Shot 4: Close-Up (Table/Object) - 7s
- Shot 5: Tight Close-Up (Person A) - 6s
- Shot 6: Medium Wide (Profile) - 6s

GENERATION_PROMPTS
- Shot 1: Cinematic medium wide shot, 16:9. Profile view of a man and a woman sitting opposite each other at a small round marble café table. Soft window light from the side. Muted color grade, shallow depth of field. Static camera.
- Shot 2: Cinematic medium close-up, 16:9. Person A's face. Expression is cold, steady, and expectant. Soft side-lighting, blurred café background. High detail on eyes. Static camera.
- Shot 3: Cinematic medium close-up, 16:9. Person B's face. Expression of suppressed panic, eyes darting slightly, jaw tight. Soft side-lighting, blurred café background. Static camera.
- Shot 4: Cinematic close-up, 16:9. Top-down angle focusing on the marble table surface. A hand slowly slides a folded piece of paper across the table toward the other person. Static camera.
- Shot 5: Cinematic tight close-up, 16:9. Extreme close-up on Person A's eyes. Unblinking, intense, focused. Soft dramatic lighting. Static camera.
- Shot 6: Cinematic medium wide shot, 16:9. Profile view of both characters. They remain frozen in silence, the distance between them feeling vast. Ambient bokeh of a café in the background. Static camera.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (Hard constraint).
- Shot Count: Exactly 6 (Hard constraint).
- Camera Movement: Zero (Hard constraint).

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Ambient café noise (clinking cups, distant chatter) lowered in volume. Dialogue is whispered/low-toned. Heavy emphasis on the sound of the paper sliding across the marble (Shot 4).
- Edit: Hard cuts. No transitions. Rhythmic timing following the production recipe.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Spatial Logic: To ensure the viewer always understands positioning, Shot 1 and Shot 6 serve as "anchor" profiles. Shots 2 and 3 must strictly adhere to the 180-degree axis (Person A always looking right, Person B always looking left).
- Tone Control: Prompts specify "cold," "suppressed," and "frozen" to prevent the AI from generating exaggerated emotional expressions (shouting/crying).
