FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Single premium advertising image, 4:5 aspect ratio.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end visual for a Mosambi sparkling drink targeting urban Millennials/Gen Z Indians. The image must pivot away from traditional "ethnic" tropes toward a "global-modern" aesthetic. Success is defined by the product looking like a sophisticated beverage (similar to a premium tonic or craft soda) rather than a mass-market soft drink, while remaining instantly recognizable as Mosambi-based.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Minimalist Effervescence." A high-contrast, architectural still-life composition focusing on the interplay of light, cold glass, and the specific pale-yellow hue of mosambi. The image uses a muted, contemporary color palette and a "quiet luxury" approach to signify premium positioning.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. The Product (Hero): The bottle/glass as the central focal point.
2. Refreshment: Visual cues of extreme cold (condensation) and carbonation (bubbles).
3. Ingredient: Subtle, sophisticated presence of fresh mosambi.
4. Vibe: Sophisticated, urban, clean.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Pale Citrine (liquid), Muted Sage or Warm Slate (background), Crisp White (highlights).
- Lighting: Soft, directional side-lighting (simulating late afternoon urban window light) to create depth and accentuate textures.
- Composition: Center-weighted, slightly low-angle shot to give the product stature. Minimalist environment.
- Texture: Matte background vs. reflective glass and wet condensation.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Subject: A sleek, unlabeled premium glass bottle containing a pale-yellow sparkling liquid.
- Setting: A clean, matte-finished stone or concrete plinth in a neutral muted tone.
- Accents: A single, perfectly sliced translucent wheel of mosambi resting against the base of the bottle or floating subtly in a secondary highball glass beside it.
- Atmospheric Effects: Fine, tight carbonation bubbles clinging to the glass; a heavy layer of micro-condensation (dew) on the exterior.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Hyper-realistic commercial product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A premium, sleek glass bottle of pale-yellow sparkling mosambi drink as the central hero. The bottle is covered in crisp, cold condensation droplets. The liquid inside is effervescent with tiny, sophisticated carbonation bubbles. The bottle sits on a minimalist, matte-finished warm slate grey stone pedestal. Beside the bottle, a single, thin, translucent slice of fresh sweet lime (mosambi) rests elegantly. Lighting is soft, cinematic side-lighting with deep soft shadows, creating a high-end architectural feel. Background is a seamless, blurred neutral studio wall in a muted sage tone. No labels, no text, no people. Ultra-sharp focus on the glass texture and droplets. 8k resolution, shot on 85mm lens, f/2.8. Avoid any traditional Indian patterns, fabrics, or clichés.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To avoid "cheap soft drink" look: Prohibit plastic textures, neon colors, and exaggerated "splash" effects.
- To avoid "clichéd Indian" look: Prohibit paisley, marigolds, traditional fabrics, and ornate gold carvings.
- To ensure "Product Hero": Use a shallow depth of field to blur the background and keep the bottle in razor-sharp focus.
