FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end, infrastructure-grade promotional asset for Aight's media API. The goal is to communicate aggressive festive pricing (₹9/₹99) to Indian businesses without sacrificing the brand's positioning as a serious, reliable B2B AI utility. The "premium" feel is achieved by avoiding retail tropes (loud colors, sales badges) and instead using architectural composition, a dark professional palette, and a minimal "festive" atmosphere.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Infrastructure of Celebration." The design treats the pricing not as a discount, but as a precise utility specification. The festive element is reduced to a sophisticated lighting mood—a golden, warm glow bleeding into a deep, technical dark-mode environment—suggesting a Diwali lamp (diya) exists just outside the frame.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRIMARY (The Hook): The prices "₹9" and "₹99" as the largest visual elements.
2. SECONDARY (The Product): "Image Generation" and "Video Generation" labels.
3. TERTIARY (The Context): "Festive Season API Pricing for Indian Businesses."
4. QUATERNARY (The Brand Trust): "Domestic GST. Prepaid Rupees. No Forex."
5. FOOTER: Aight logo and "getaight.ai".

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Deep Obsidian/Charcoal background (#0A0A0A) contrasted with a high-saturation "Festive Gold" (#FFD700) for the pricing and key accents.
- Typography: Heavy, Swiss-style geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Helvetica Now). Extreme weight contrast (Black for prices, Light/Medium for labels).
- Composition: Asymmetric grid. The pricing is stacked vertically, aligned to a strict left margin, creating a "technical specification" look.
- Lighting: Cinematic "rim lighting" or "light spill" from the right edge of the poster in warm gold, creating a subtle gradient across the dark void.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. GENERATE BACKGROUND: Create a high-resolution 4:5 dark void with a sophisticated golden light spill from the right side.
2. TYPOGRAPHIC OVERLAY: Layer text using a strict grid. Use "Black" weight for the currency and numbers to create a massive visual anchor.
3. FINISHING: Apply a subtle film grain and a slight chromatic aberration to the edges of the gold text to give it a "digital hardware" feel.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Prompt: "A professional, high-end 4:5 B2B tech poster background. Deep obsidian black matte surface. On the right edge, a soft, sophisticated golden warm light spill creates a subtle atmospheric gradient across the frame, suggesting a luxury festive lamp just out of view. Cinematic lighting, 8k resolution, minimalist architectural style, zero clutter, void space for typography. The mood is serious, premium, and industrial. No text."

- The 4:5 aspect ratio must be strictly maintained.
