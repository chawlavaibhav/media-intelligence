FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
9:16 Vertical UGC-style Video (approx. 25 seconds).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-conversion, low-fidelity performance ad that leverages the "trusted peer" aesthetic. The goal is to position the 2% Salicylic Acid serum as a practical, irritant-free solution for oily skin, avoiding "corporate" polish to increase authenticity and trust.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Honest Routine": A relatable Indian creator sharing her specific solution for midday oil and congestion, highlighting the fragrance-free aspect as a key differentiator for sensitive, blemish-prone skin.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Hook: The frustration of oily/congested skin.
2. Solution: Introduction of the 2% Salicylic Acid Serum.
3. Primary Purchase Reason: Effective pore-clearing without the irritation of added fragrances.
4. Proof: Visual demonstration of texture and absorption.
5. Action: Clear CTA to purchase.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Talent: Indian woman (25-35), natural skin (minimal makeup), relatable appearance.
- Environment: Well-lit bathroom or bedroom with natural window light.
- Camera: Handheld smartphone aesthetic (slight organic movement).
- Palette: Natural skin tones, white/neutral backgrounds, product packaging colors.
- Text Overlays: Simple, sans-serif "TikTok/Reel" style captions for key points.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Framing: Medium close-up (MCU) and Close-up (CU).
- Lighting: Soft, natural daylight.
- Audio: Direct-to-camera sync sound (lavalier or phone mic), minimal ambient background noise.
- Edit: Fast-paced cuts on the beat, organic transitions.

Shot 1 (0-4s)
- Purpose: Hook.
- Prompt: 9:16 handheld smartphone footage. A relatable Indian woman in her late 20s, looking into the camera in a bright bathroom. She is pointing to her T-zone, looking slightly frustrated but friendly. Natural skin texture, no heavy makeup. High quality, UGC style.
- Dialogue: "If your skin feels like an oil slick by 2 PM, you know the struggle."

Shot 2 (4-9s)
- Purpose: Product Intro.
- Prompt: 9:16 close-up. The woman holds up a minimalist skincare bottle (2% Salicylic Acid Serum). The label is clear and legible. She smiles at the camera. Natural lighting, blurred bathroom background.
- Dialogue: "I've been using this fragrance-free 2% Salicylic Acid serum, and it's a game changer."

Shot 3 (9-18s)
- Purpose: Demo & Purchase Reason.
- Prompt: 9:16 extreme close-up. A few clear drops of serum being applied to a cheek and blended in with fingers. The serum is lightweight and absorbs quickly without leaving a greasy residue. Natural skin pores visible.
- Dialogue: "Because it's fragrance-free, it actually clears my pores without that stinging or irritation I usually get from other actives."

Shot 4 (18-25s)
- Purpose: Result & CTA.
- Prompt: 9:16 medium close-up. The woman looks refreshed, skin looking matte but hydrated. She gives a thumbs up or a confident nod to the camera.
- Dialogue: "Just simple, effective ingredients. Grab yours at the link below."
