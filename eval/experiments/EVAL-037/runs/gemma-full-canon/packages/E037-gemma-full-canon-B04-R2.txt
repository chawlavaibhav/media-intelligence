OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-conversion, authentic-feeling "creator" video for a D2C skincare serum. The goal is to move the viewer from identifying with a common problem (oily/blemish-prone skin) to trusting a specific solution (fragrance-free 2% Salicylic Acid) via a relatable peer recommendation.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Honest Essential": A low-fidelity, high-trust testimonial where a woman shares her "secret" for managing oily skin, emphasizing the lack of fragrance as a key benefit for skin that is already stressed or sensitive.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. THE HOOK: Identification with oily/blemish-prone skin struggles.
2. THE SOLUTION: Introduction of the 2% Salicylic Acid serum.
3. THE DIFFERENTIATOR (Purchase Reason): Fragrance-free formulation (reducing irritation).
4. THE PROOF: Visual demonstration of texture and application.
5. CALL TO ACTION: Direct nudge to purchase.

- Setting: A bright, naturally lit Indian home environment (modern bathroom or bedroom with a window).
- Talent: Indian woman (25-35), natural look, minimal "no-makeup" makeup, visible but healthy skin texture (no heavy blurring filters).
- Lighting: Soft, directional daylight to create a "shot on iPhone" feel.
- Color Palette: Neutral backgrounds (whites, creams, light wood) to make the product packaging pop.
- Framing: Close-up (CU) and Medium Close-up (MCU).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot 1: Talking head (MCU). Talent addresses the camera directly.
- Shot 2: Product B-roll (CU). Quick cut showing the bottle and texture.
- Shot 3: Application (CU). Talent applies serum to cheek/forehead.
- Shot 4: Closing (MCU). Talent smiles, holding the product next to her face.
