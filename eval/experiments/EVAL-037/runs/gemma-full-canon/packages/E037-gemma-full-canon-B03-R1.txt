FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Position a new Mosambi sparkling drink as a sophisticated, contemporary beverage for urban Indian millennials/Gen Z. The goal is to elevate the traditional familiarity of mosambi (sweet lime) into a premium "lifestyle" category, avoiding ethnic clichés and low-end soda aesthetics.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Modern Refreshment" — A high-end, minimalist product portrait that emphasizes texture (effervescence, condensation) and light (soft urban daylight) to signal sophistication and purity.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero: The drink (color, carbonation, cooling effect).
2. Ingredient Cue: A single, elegantly placed mosambi element.
3. Brand Vibe: Sophisticated, clean, urban, premium.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Pale citrine, frosted glass, muted sage, and soft cream backgrounds.
- Lighting: Natural "golden hour" light filtered through a window (gobo lighting), creating soft, architectural shadows.
- Texture: Macro focus on carbonation bubbles and heavy condensation droplets on the glass.
- Composition: Low-angle, center-weighted "hero" shot. Minimalist environment (e.g., a honed travertine or matte concrete surface) to ground the product in a contemporary urban setting.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot Type: Commercial product photography / Macro.
- Framing: 4:5 Aspect Ratio.
- Styling: A sleek, tall, minimalist glass bottle or premium highball glass. The liquid is a pale, translucent yellow-green. One thin, perfectly circular slice of mosambi floating near the surface.
- Atmosphere: Crisp, cold, airy. No cluttered backgrounds.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
High-end commercial product photography of a premium sparkling mosambi drink in a sleek, minimalist glass bottle. 4:5 aspect ratio. The liquid is a pale, translucent citrine yellow-green with visible, crisp effervescent bubbles rising. The glass is covered in fine, cold condensation droplets. A single, thin, elegant slice of fresh mosambi is subtly integrated. The bottle sits on a honed light-grey travertine surface. Lighting is soft, natural daylight coming from a side window, creating gentle architectural shadows and bright, specular highlights on the glass. Background is a blurred, minimalist contemporary urban interior in muted cream and sage tones. Ultra-sharp focus on the bottle, 8k resolution, sophisticated aesthetic, clean composition, luxury beverage advertising style.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Post-production: Color grade to ensure the yellow-green of the mosambi looks natural and appetizing, not neon or synthetic.
- Typography (if added later): Clean, wide-kerning sans-serif typeface.

N/A (Static Image).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Prevent "cheap soda" look: Avoid bright, saturated primary colors and plastic textures.
- Prevent "cliché Indian" look: Strictly exclude paisley, mandalas, traditional fabrics, or overly ornate gold accents.
- Ensure Hero Status: Keep the background heavily blurred (shallow depth of field) so the product is the sole focus.
