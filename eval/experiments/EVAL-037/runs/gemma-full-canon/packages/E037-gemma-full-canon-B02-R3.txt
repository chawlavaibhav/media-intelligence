FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The goal is to communicate a high-value price point (₹9 for images, ₹99 for videos) to Indian businesses during the festive season without sacrificing the brand's identity as a serious, professional AI infrastructure provider. The design must avoid "discount" visual tropes (loud colors, starbursts, cluttered layouts) and instead use the language of premium tech branding: minimalism, rigorous typography, and intentional negative space.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Festive Ledger." The design treats the pricing not as a "sale," but as a transparent, fixed cost of infrastructure. By using a structured, grid-based layout reminiscent of a technical specification sheet or a high-end architectural portfolio, the "discount" prices are elevated to "efficient units of production."

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary: The Prices (₹9 | ₹99) — The core commercial hook.
2. Secondary: The Assets (IMAGE | VIDEO) — Defining what is being bought.
3. Tertiary: The Framing (Scale your festive presence) — Contextualizing the offer for the season.
4. Foundation: Brand Identity (Aight logo + "Domestic GST. Prepaid Rupees.") — Establishing trust and infrastructure legitimacy.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A) background with stark White (#FFFFFF) typography. A single accent color: Metallic Champagne Gold (#D4AF37) used exclusively for a thin, technical ornament.
- Typography:
    - Prices: Extra-bold, oversized sans-serif (Swiss style) for maximum impact.
    - Labels: Monospace font (e.g., JetBrains Mono or Roboto Mono) to signal "API/Developer/Infrastructure."
    - Body/Header: Clean, medium-weight sans-serif.
- Imagery: No stock photos. The "festive" element is a single, sophisticated gold-wireframe geometric motif in the corner—a hybrid between a traditional mandala and a neural network graph—rendered in a 1pt line weight.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Layout: Use a strict 4:5 vertical grid.
2. Header: Place "Scale your festive presence" at the top left, small and understated.
3. Centerpiece: Divide the center into two equal vertical columns.
    - Left Column: Large "₹9" centered, with "IMAGE" in monospace underneath.
    - Right Column: Large "₹99" centered, with "VIDEO" in monospace underneath.
    - A thin vertical gold line separates the two columns.
4. Footer: The Aight logo at the bottom left. To the right, a clean line of text: "Prepaid Rupees. Domestic GST. Serious AI."
5. Ornament: Place the gold technical mandala in the top right or bottom right, partially bleeding off the edge to create a modern, asymmetrical feel.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Precise text placement (Header, Footer) should be handled via layout software to ensure the "infrastructure" feel isn't ruined by generative text artifacts.
- The Aight logo must be placed as a vector overlay.
- The specific "Domestic GST" tagline must be rendered in a precise monospace font.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid: Any imagery of fireworks, sweets, or traditional clothing, which would trigger the "discount flyer" feel.
- Avoid: Using red or bright yellow, which are associated with retail sales.
- Avoid: Centering the text in a way that feels like a greeting card; keep it aligned to a technical grid.
