FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic short scene (Video), 16:9 aspect ratio, ~40 seconds.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-tension, restrained dramatic encounter between two people at a café. The narrative focus is the psychological shift following a revelation of deceit. The technical priority is spatial clarity and cinematic framing without utilizing complex camera choreography.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." The scene utilizes a rigid, formal framing system to mirror the emotional constriction of the characters. Instead of movement, tension is built through alternating tight perspectives and a final profile shot that emphasizes the emotional distance between the two characters despite their physical proximity.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Orientation (Establishment of the "Opposite" dynamic).
2. The Reveal (The moment of discovery/confrontation).
3. Internal Reaction (The restraint and panic).
4. The Resultant Gap (The final emotional stalemate).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Desaturated teals and warm ambers (complementary contrast).
- Lighting: Soft side-lighting from a nearby café window; high contrast in the shadows to emphasize facial contours.
- Lens: Shallow depth of field (f/2.8 or lower) to isolate characters from the background.
- Composition: Strict adherence to the 180-degree rule to ensure spatial orientation.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot Count: 6
- Camera Movement: Static or minimal slow-creep (zolly/push-in).
- Pacing: Slow cuts, allowing silence to linger.
- Acting Direction: Minimal gestures; focus on eyes and micro-expressions.

GENERATION_PROMPTS

Shot 1: Medium Wide Shot.
- Prompt: Cinematic medium wide shot, 16:9. Two people sitting opposite each other at a small circular café table. Soft natural window light from the side. Muted tones, shallow depth of field. Static camera. The characters are still, an oppressive silence between them. 4K, highly detailed textures of wood and fabric.
- Duration: 6s
- Purpose: Establish spatial relationship and environment.

Shot 2: Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) from Person A to Person B.
- Prompt: Cinematic OTS shot, 16:9. Focus on Person B's face. Person B looks anxious, blinking slowly, eyes darting. Person A's shoulder and back of head are in the foreground, softly blurred. Soft bokeh café background. Static camera. High emotional tension.
- Duration: 7s
- Purpose: Establish the target of the discovery and their vulnerability.

Shot 3: Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) from Person B to Person A.
- Prompt: Cinematic OTS shot, 16:9. Focus on Person A's face. Person A has a cold, steady expression, staring intensely. Person B's shoulder is in the foreground, blurred. Lighting emphasizes the hardness of Person A's gaze. Static camera.
- Duration: 8s
- Purpose: Establish the power dynamic and the delivery of the revelation.

Shot 4: Tight Close-Up (CU) of Person B.
- Prompt: Extreme close-up, 16:9. Person B's face. A slight swallow, a twitch of the lip, eyes filled with trapped panic. Very shallow depth of field. Soft side-lighting. Static camera. 
- Duration: 6s
- Purpose: Internalize the panic of the concealer.

Shot 5: Tight Close-Up (CU) of Person A.
- Prompt: Extreme close-up, 16:9. Person A's face. Deadpan expression, blinking once slowly. The look of profound disappointment. Soft side-lighting. Static camera.
- Duration: 6s
- Purpose: Internalize the resolve of the discoverer.

Shot 6: Medium Profile Shot (Two-Shot).
- Prompt: Cinematic medium shot, 16:9. Side profile of both characters sitting opposite each other. The table is between them, creating a physical barrier. They do not look at each other now; they both look straight ahead. Wide gap of negative space between them. Static camera. Fade to black.
- Duration: 7s
- Purpose: Resolve the scene by visualizing the emotional divide.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Dialogue (to be added in post/audio): 
  - Person A: "I found the documents." (Shot 3)
  - Person B: "I can explain." (Shot 4)
  - Person A: "Don't." (Shot 5)

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Ambient café noise (distant clinking of cups, low murmur) reduced to a low hum during close-ups. No music; only the sound of breathing and the spoken dialogue.
- Edit: Hard cuts. No transitions except for the final fade to black.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To avoid "mechanical coverage," ensure the close-ups (Shots 4 and 5) are tighter than standard coverage, focusing on the eyes to create an intimate, claustrophobic feeling.
- To ensure spatial clarity, the 180-degree axis must be strictly maintained between Shot 1, 2, 3, and 6.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Two people opposite at café: Yes.
- Concealed secret plot: Yes.
- Tense/Restrained/No shouting: Yes.
- Spatial relationship clear: Yes (Wide -> OTS -> Profile).
- Exactly six shots: Yes.
- No elaborate movement: Yes (All static).
- Cinematic feel: Yes (Shallow DOF, specific lighting, tight framing).
- Duration ~40s: Yes (6+7+8+6+6+7 = 40s).
- Aspect Ratio 16:9: Yes.
- No external websites: Yes.
