DELIVERABLE
One premium advertising image (4:5 aspect ratio).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Position a Mosambi sparkling drink as a high-end, contemporary beverage for the urban Indian millennial/Gen-Z demographic. The goal is to elevate the "common" mosambi fruit into a sophisticated lifestyle product, moving away from traditional "ethnic" beverage tropes toward a global, minimalist luxury aesthetic.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Pure Effervescence." A hyper-realistic, studio-lit composition that focuses on the physical tension between the stillness of a premium glass and the kinetic energy of sparkling water. The image will use a monochromatic, muted color palette to allow the natural, vibrant yellow-green of the mosambi to act as the primary light source and focal point.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Refreshment (Visualized via heavy condensation and active carbonation).
2. Premium Quality (Visualized via architectural glassware and minimalist composition).
3. Natural Origin (Visualized via a single, perfectly sliced piece of fresh mosambi).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Sage green, pale citrine, off-white, and deep charcoal accents.
- Lighting: "Soft-box" side lighting to create rim highlights on the glass and translucency within the liquid.
- Composition: Centered, slightly low-angle shot to give the product "stature."
- Texture: Contrast between the matte finish of a stone plinth, the smooth transparency of glass, and the organic texture of the mosambi pulp.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Base: A minimalist, matte light-grey stone or concrete plinth.
2. Hero: A tall, slender, modern highball glass filled with the pale yellow-green sparkling drink.
3. Accents: High-density condensation droplets on the exterior of the glass; a single, thin, translucent slice of mosambi floating near the surface or resting against the inner rim.
4. Atmosphere: A soft, neutral gradient background (off-white to sage) to eliminate distracting environments.
5. Action: Micro-bubbles (effervescence) clinging to the glass walls and rising in thin columns.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
High-end commercial beverage photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A tall, sophisticated modern highball glass filled with a pale yellow-green sparkling mosambi drink, centered on a minimalist matte light-grey stone plinth. The glass is covered in realistic, crisp condensation droplets. Inside the drink, tiny, sharp effervescent bubbles rise in columns. A single, perfectly thin, translucent slice of fresh mosambi (sweet lime) is elegantly positioned inside the glass. Lighting is soft studio side-lighting, creating a luminous glow through the liquid and a sharp rim light on the glassware. Background is a clean, blurred gradient of off-white and muted sage green. Hyper-realistic, 8k, macro detail, focus on liquid textures and transparency, minimalist luxury aesthetic, no text, no people.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To avoid "cheap soft drink" look: Ensure no plastic bottles, no neon colors, and no artificial "splash" effects; keep the liquid's color natural and pale.
- To avoid "clichéd Indian" shorthand: Explicitly exclude patterns (mandala/paisley), traditional fabrics, or warm saffron/bright orange tones.
- To ensure "hero" status: Use a shallow depth of field to blur the background entirely, keeping only the glass and plinth in sharp focus.
