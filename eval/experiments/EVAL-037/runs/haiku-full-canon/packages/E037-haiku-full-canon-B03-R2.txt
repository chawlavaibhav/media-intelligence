**OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**
Create a hero-product advertisement that positions a mosambi sparkling drink as a premium, contemporary beverage for affluent urban Indian adults. The image must:

**CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA**
A sun-lit, minimalist still-life moment capturing the product in its most essential, desirable state: a clear glass or bottle filled with translucent mosambi liquid and visible carbonation, set in an understated, light-filled environment that feels aspirational and contemporary. The mosambi's natural golden-amber color and the optical properties of carbonation (bubbles, refraction) carry the entire cultural and sensory narrative. No lifestyle prop clutter.

**MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY**
1. **Primary:** Product form, color, clarity, fizz—the drink itself as the singular hero
2. **Secondary:** Golden-amber tone of mosambi (signals origin fruit; no artificiality)
3. **Tertiary:** Lightness, clarity, premium glassware—signals sophistication and quality

**VISUAL_SYSTEM**
- **Color palette:** Warm whites, soft cream/off-whites, and the natural warm-golden translucency of mosambi liquid. Minimal secondary colors.
- **Lighting:** High-key, directional natural light (or simulation thereof) that emphasizes the liquid's golden tone and carbonation bubble refraction. No dramatic shadows; clarity and luminosity are the mood.
- **Composition:** 4:5 vertical frame. Product positioned in the upper-to-middle third of frame, angled to maximize optical interest (bubble visibility, liquid translucency, rim/edge detail). Negative space below or around to emphasize premium positioning and breathing room.
- **Glassware/Vessel:** Either a modern, simple clear glass (highball, rocks, or bespoke taper) or a minimal, contemporary bottle with clean typography. No ornate glassware; no embellishment. The product's color and clarity must be the visual protagonist.
- **Surface/Environment:** Seamless or softly graduated light background (white, cream, or very pale warm tone). A single, subtle secondary surface (light marble, pale stone, or diffuse wood) is optional but only if it does not compete with the product. Prefer simplicity.
- **Depth/Focus:** Selective focus (shallow depth of field acceptable) on the glass/bottle to isolate it from environment. Ensure carbonation bubbles and liquid meniscus are tack-sharp.

---

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**
1. **Setup:** Light tent or continuous studio lighting configured for high-key backlit or rim-lit clarity (emphasizes liquid translucency and bubbles). Position key light at 45° to front-left to catch refraction and fizz.
2. **Glassware:** Source a clean, contemporary clear glass or minimal bottle with modern typography (if bottled). Fill with actual mosambi juice + sparkling water or a visually equivalent stand-in (ensure golden-amber color is authentic).
3. **Bubbles:** Use fresh carbonation (immediately before shoot) or, if using still photography standin, fine food-grade glycerin + clear gel + micro air-bubble suspension for lasting bubble visibility in-frame.
4. **Styling:** No garnish (no mint, no citrus wheel, no straw). Product only. Optional: single droplet or condensation on exterior glass to signal cold/freshness, but only if subtle and not gestural.
5. **Camera:** High-resolution (minimum 4000px on long edge). Shoot tethered to monitor for real-time bubble and refraction verification.
6. **Post-production:** Minimal retouching. Enhance clarity of liquid, deepen mosambi golden tone slightly (if needed), ensure background is clean and white-balanced. No artificial enhancement of bubbles unless they have faded; maintain photorealism.

**DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS**

**FAILURE_PREVENTION**
1. **Product visibility:** If the background becomes too warm or visually busy, the mosambi liquid will read as generic amber liquid rather than distinctive mosambi. Enforce strict white/cream background discipline.
2. **Carbonation realism:** Generative models often produce "fake" bubble patterns (too uniform, too small, or spatially incoherent). Reference real carbonation photography and adjust post-generation.
3. **Luxury slide:** Avoid over-styling. A single garnish, a fancy coaster, or a second object tips the image into "premium soft drink" territory. Enforce product-only rule in review.
4. **Cultural authenticity vs. cliché:** Do not add cultural signifiers (texture, pattern, color, compositional trope) in an attempt to signal "Indian-ness." The mosambi itself is the cultural anchor; let it rest there.
5. **Liquid clarity:** If the liquid appears murky, opaque, or has a plastic look, regenerate with explicit instruction for glass-like transparency and natural juice tone.
