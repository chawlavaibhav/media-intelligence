**FAILURE_PREVENTION**

1. **Geometry drift**: Confirm case diameter, hand shape, marker style, and strap attachment against product spec before final render/shoot. No scaled or proportional shortcuts.
2. **Dial detail loss**: Ensure sunburst pattern is visible at final resolution (test at 2000+ px width minimum for e-commerce use).
3. **Sapphire dome**: Confirm it appears as a clear, domed surface with subtle reflection—not flat, not over-glossy, not invisible.
4. **Lighting failure**: Over-bright key light will wash out sunburst; too dim will hide texture. Test render/shoot in multiple angles before committing.
5. **Background distraction**: Ensure background remains neutral and soft; any pattern or color should complement, not compete.
6. **Wrist realism**: If using 3D wrist model, ensure skin tone, proportions, and angle feel natural. If photographing, ensure model's hand/wrist is relaxed and properly positioned.
7. **Color cast**: Avoid cool/blue casts that flatten the dial; warm tone should feel luxurious, not filtered or artificial.
8. **File spec**: Deliver at minimum 2400 × 3000 px (4:5, 300 DPI equivalent or 72 DPI @10" × 8" minimum) for e-commerce hero use.
