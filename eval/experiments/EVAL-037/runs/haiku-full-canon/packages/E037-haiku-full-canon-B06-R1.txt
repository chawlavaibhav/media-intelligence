**DELIVERABLE**

One premium 4:5 (vertical) e-commerce hero image, static, RGB 8-bit, suitable as primary product showcase on high-end product page. Aspect ratio critical for mobile and desktop banner use.

---

**OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**

Produce a single photograph-realistic image that showcases the Aster Meridian 38 as a genuine, desirable mechanical watch. The image must prioritize dial legibility, material authenticity (brushed steel, leather, domed crystal), and wrist-wear context while maintaining clean commercial composition. The goal is earned premium positioning through honest product display, not artifice.

---

**CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA**

**Wrist-centric, dial-forward composition**: The watch is worn on a relaxed wrist, photographed from a 3/4 angle that places the dial at ~60° to camera. This stance is natural (not floating), demonstrates proportional wearability at 38mm, shows the leather strap authentically, and positions the dial's sunburst finish as the hero visual. Lighting is clean, directional, and reveals the domed sapphire crystal and case brushing without theatricality.

---

**MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY**

1. **Primary**: Dial design and sunburst finish (color, legibility, hand-marker contrast)
2. **Secondary**: Case material authenticity (brushed stainless steel surface quality)
3. **Tertiary**: Leather strap texture, case proportionality (38mm scale credible on wrist)
4. **Atmospheric**: Craftsmanship is implied through honest lighting and material rendering

No overlays, no text, no floating badges. The product speaks.

---

**VISUAL_SYSTEM**

- **Color palette**: Deep blue sunburst dial is hero; neutral gray-white background (no high contrast color distraction); brown leather strap warm anchor
- **Lighting direction**: Slightly raked (20–30° from dial plane) to emphasize sapphire dome reflection and brushed case texture; avoids flat, shadowless rendering
- **Focus and depth**: Watch dial and case in critical focus; wrist/strap softer but legible (shallow depth of field acceptable, ~50mm equivalent focal length framing)
- **Background**: Soft, neutral (light gray or off-white gradient); no props, no context, no lifestyle clutter
- **Texture and finish**: Brushed steel surface micro-detail visible; leather grain visible on strap; sapphire dome catches light subtly (no blown highlight)

---

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**

1. **Watch positioning**: Placed on a real human wrist (or wrist form matching adult male proportions), approximately face-up at 45° tilt, rotated so dial faces 3/4 toward camera. Strap drapes naturally; no cinching or artificial tension.
2. **Lighting setup**:
   - Key light (soft, medium softbox or diffused): positioned 30° left-of-dial, 25° above horizontal, at ~2 meters. Intensity sufficient to render dial detail and catch sapphire dome.
   - Fill light (gentle, no hard shadow): opposite side, ~0.5 intensity ratio; prevents dark side of case from reading as black void.
   - No back light; watch should not feel separated from background.
3. **Camera position**: Perpendicular to wrist plane, framed to include watch and ~15–20 cm of wrist/lower forearm above and below. 4:5 framing places watch off-center slightly (dial in upper-middle third of frame).
4. **Optical**: 50mm equivalent focal length, f/4–f/5.6 aperture (dial and case sharp, subtle falloff on forearm).
5. **Post-production**: Neutral white balance (daylight temp); subtle contrast and clarity to emphasize dial sunburst and case brushing; no heavy saturation, no HDR look; color accuracy for blue dial and brown leather essential.

**DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS**

- **Watch geometry**: Case diameter 38mm, dial color and marker positions, hand shapes, strap attachment—these are product facts and must not be altered by generation process. If generation fails to preserve specs, reject and regenerate with stricter constraints.
- **Strap color**: Dark brown, not tan or red-brown.
- **Dial detail**: Sunburst finish must be visible (radial brushing pattern); not flat or matte.
- **Wrist context**: Real human wrist, proportionally appropriate to 38mm case (credible fit, not oversized).

**FAILURE_PREVENTION**

- **Dial illegibility**: If dial reads dark or sunburst pattern is invisible, reject. Generation should emphasize luminosity and radial texture.
- **Floating product**: If watch appears weightless or suspended, reject. Wrist must read as natural support.
- **Oversized case appearance**: If 38mm reads as 44mm or larger, reject. Reference proportional wrist-to-case ratio in prompt or retry.
- **Strap material confusion**: If leather strap reads as rubber, fabric, or synthetic, reject.
- **Sapphire dome absent**: If dome reads flat or crystal boundary is invisible, reject; dome's curvature and reflectivity are material proof-points.
- **Artificial lighting**: If image reads as 3D render or CGI, reject. Aim for photographic authenticity.
