**DELIVERABLE**  
25-second vertical (9:16) direct-to-camera UGC-style product demonstration video for a 2% salicylic acid fragrance-free facial serum.

---

**OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**  
Demonstrate the product naturally and authentically in a short-form mobile video that feels like creator content, not advertising. One Indian woman speaks directly to her audience, shows the product in use, and delivers one honest purchase motivator. Tone: relatable peer, not expert or influencer persona. Commercial quality without slickness. No medical or statistical claims. Goal: viewers see themselves in the scenario and understand why they'd buy this serum.

---

**CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA**  
**One clear reason: Fragrance-free formulation for oily skin that doesn't irritate.**

This is observable, relatable, and credible. Oily, blemish-prone skin often reacts poorly to fragrant products. The serum is introduced and discussed as the solution to that specific friction point—not miraculous results, but honest product fit. The talent models normal skincare behavior, speaking as though to a friend.

---

**MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY**  
1. **Primary**: "This is fragrance-free—it won't irritate oily skin."  
2. **Secondary**: The serum is lightweight and practical to use daily.  
3. **Tertiary**: The product exists and is available to purchase (implied brand/CTA).

No mention of acne clearing speed, clinical efficacy, percentages as benefits, or comparative claims.

---

**VISUAL_SYSTEM**

- **Setting**: Bathroom or bedroom corner, bright natural light or soft warm key light. Minimal clutter. Clean but lived-in (not sterile).
- **Talent**: Indian woman, early-to-mid thirties, mid-long hair, minimal makeup or natural makeup, wearing casual home clothing (t-shirt, no costume).
- **Product**: Serum bottle clearly visible in each shot; label readable in close-ups.
- **Camera work**: Static or minimal handheld movement. Direct eye-line to lens. Close-ups of hands and product; medium shots of face and chest. No filters, minimal color grading (clean, slightly warm, natural).
- **Framing**: Composed to avoid unused headroom; product held at chest/shoulder height for easy visibility.

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**

| Shot | Purpose | Duration | Description |
|------|---------|----------|-------------|

"A 25-second vertical UGC-style skincare video. An Indian woman in her early thirties, wearing casual home clothing, stands in a bright bathroom or bedroom corner. **DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS**

- **Product bottle**: Must be the actual/accurate 3D asset or photography of the serum product. Non-negotiable.  
- **Text overlay** (if included): Brand name, product name, static tagline or purchase CTA. Use sans-serif font, white or dark, 2–3 second duration at close or end frame.  
- **Audio**: Real voice (recorded talent audio, not AI/synthetic). No background music unless at very low volume and only under final 2–3 seconds. No sound effects.  
- **On-screen labels/captions** (optional): If UGC-posted format requires platform captions, keep them minimal and aligned with the spoken word—no additional claims.

---

**AUDIO_AND_EDIT**

- **Voiceover**: Recorded live during shoot. Natural speech, no post-processing, no heavy compression. Allow slight ambient bathroom/room sound (tape sound, very minimal). No music track, OR soft, minimal ambient music bed under final 3 seconds only.  
- **Edit style**: Straight cuts between shots. No transitions (fade to black okay for opening/closing). No slow-motion, speed ramps, or stylized edits. Pacing: natural conversational speed, not rushed.  
- **Sound design**: Minimal. Possible: soft sound of serum being applied (tactile, very subtle). That's it.  
- **Final audio export**: Stereo mix, balanced for mobile phone playback.

---

**FAILURE_PREVENTION**

1. **Avoid medical language**: Do not use "treatment," "heal," "cure," "acne-fighting," "clinically," "dermatologist-tested," "proven to," or similar. Use only "works for," "helps," "fits my skin," "doesn't irritate."

2. **Avoid statistics and guarantees**: No "9 out of 10," "reduces in X days," "all-day," "100%," or comparative claims ("better than," "more effective").

3. **Talent authenticity**: Ensure the woman appears genuinely relaxed and natural—not performing or overselling. If she seems scripted or stiff, it undermines UGC credibility.

4. **Lighting control**: Bright enough to see product and skin texture clearly, but warm enough to feel home-like. Avoid clinical fluorescent or harsh shadows on face.

5. **Product visibility**: Serum bottle must be identifiable and the label readable in at least one shot. No blurred or obscured product.

6. **Adherence to 25 seconds**: Do not exceed. Tight edit. Cut any non-essential dialogue or pauses.

7. **Mobile-first framing**: Entire composition safe for 9:16 aspect ratio. Center critical elements. Avoid text or action in the safe-zone edges.

8. **No external links or CTAs to websites**: If a call-to-action is needed, use only "Available on [brand name]" or general brand mention. Per customer instruction, no external website reference.
