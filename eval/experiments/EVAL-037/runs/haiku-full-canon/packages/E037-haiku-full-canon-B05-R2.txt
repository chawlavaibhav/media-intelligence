FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

---

**DELIVERABLE**
Six-shot, 40-second cinematic scene. Two actors, one café table, 16:9 format. Dialogue-driven revelation and confrontation. No camera movement beyond motivated pans/reframing.

---

**OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**
Produce a closed two-person dialogue scene in which spatial relationships remain legible, emotional stakes escalate through restrained performance and editing rhythm, and cinematic framing (not technical coverage) drives viewer engagement. The reveal of deception occurs early; the scene focuses on the aftermath and tension of the confrontation itself.

---

**CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA**
A café conversation becomes a private crisis. Two people sit across from each other; one has just learned a truth. The scene prioritizes the psychology of the moment—careful eye contact, pauses, the weight of what's unsaid—over dramatic gestures. Camera positions establish and then respect the table boundary; we see both people's faces within frames that acknowledge the physical distance between them and the emotional closeness of the moment.

---

**MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY**
1. **Spatial anchor**: Where are they? What contains them? (Shot 1)
2. **First revelation and response**: The accusation and initial reaction (Shots 2–3)
3. **Defense and emotional break**: The accused person's attempt to explain or deflect (Shots 4–5)
4. **Stalemate and finality**: The moment neither can retreat from (Shot 6)

No exposition. Dialogue assumes the audience understands the context is betrayal.

---

**VISUAL_SYSTEM**
- **Colour**: Warm, muted café interior. Soft natural or tungsten light. Slight color desaturation to emphasize restraint and seriousness.
- **Depth**: Shallow depth of field in close shots (favoring the speaker). Wider depth in establishing frames.
- **Framing logic**: Head-on or near-profile angles from both sides of the table. Close shots isolate faces. Medium shots show posture and hands (which carry tension in restraint).
- **Lighting balance**: Even but not flat. One side slightly favored by available light to suggest time of day and mood.

---

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**

**Story premise for dialogue:**
- Character A (MAYA): "You told them about the money. You said you wouldn't."
- Character B (JAMES): "I had to. It was worse if I didn't."
- MAYA: "You made a promise to me, not to them."
- JAMES: "I know what I did."
- MAYA: "Do you? Because it doesn't sound like it."
- JAMES: [pause] "What do you want me to say?"
- MAYA: "I want you to say you understand what you've taken from me."

**Shot List (in order):**

| Shot | Framing | Purpose | Duration | Camera Direction |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Wide, from café entrance angle | Establish the space. Two figures at small table. Afternoon or early evening light. Background softly blurred. | 3–4 sec | Static. Frames both people, table between them, neutral ground. |
| 2 | Medium-close, favoring MAYA (left side of table, camera from her 3/4 view) | MAYA speaks the accusation. We read her face clearly. JAMES visible in soft focus across table. | 4–5 sec | Static. MAYA slightly off-center. Depth emphasizes her proximity to camera and emotional directness. |
| 3 | Medium-close, favoring JAMES (right side, camera from his 3/4 view) | JAMES hears and absorbs. Subtle facial shift from denial to resignation. MAYA out of focus in background. | 3–4 sec | Static. Mirror angle to Shot 2. His silence is the content. |
| 4 | Close-up, JAMES | JAMES speaks his defense. Camera tight enough to catch the micro-hesitations and the effort of restraint. | 4–5 sec | Static. Isolates his face. No escape into the wider scene. |
| 5 | Medium two-shot, both visible, slight reframe from Shot 1 angle | MAYA leans back slightly. JAMES remains. The table, their hands, the space between them. This is the spatial checkpoint—we confirm we know where each sits. | 4–5 sec | Slow pan or slight reframe to include both fully in frame. Emphasizes the separateness within closeness. |
| 6 | Close, MAYA's face (slight push-in or static) | MAYA's final line. Her expression doesn't change much. The weight is internal. Hold slightly into silence. | 4–5 sec | Minimal push-in (0.5 second) or static. Ends on her decision to stop speaking. Fade to black or cut hard. |

**Approximate total: 26–28 seconds dialogue + 12–14 seconds of breath/pause = ~40 seconds.**

Soft-focus background with blurred other tables and window. Shallow depth of field. Neutral framing, equal balance. 16:9, cinematic lighting. Mood: intimate but tense. No action, static composition."

**Shot 2 – Medium-Close, MAYA Speaking (3/4 view from her left):**
"Close-up of female face, three-quarter angle, from the left side of table. She is mid-sentence, lips moving or just closed, eyes direct. Serious, controlled expression. Soft warm light on her face. Male figure visible out of focus across small table in background. Café setting, subtle. 16:9, shallow depth of field. Cinematic, restrained emotion."

**Shot 3 – Medium-Close, JAMES Reacting (3/4 view from his right):**
"Close-up of male face, three-quarter angle, from the right side of table. He has just heard words. Subtle change in expression—jaw slightly tighter, eyes searching. Moment of processing. Female visible blurred in soft focus across table in background. Warm café light. 16:9, shallow depth. Still, restrained. Cinematic tension."

**Shot 4 – Close-Up, JAMES Speaking:**
"Tight close-up of male face, front-facing or slight angle. He is speaking a low response, eyes down briefly then up. Faint crease in brow. Warm overhead/3-quarter light. No background visible. 16:9. Isolated. Cinematic intimacy. Every small movement of his face is visible. Mood: resigned, struggling to explain."

**Shot 5 – Medium Two-Shot, Both Visible, Reframed Angle:**
"Two people seated at café table, both in frame, medium distance. He leans forward slightly or holds still; she has drawn back a fraction. Hands visible on table edge. Neutral warm light. Sharp focus on both faces. 16:9. Clear spatial relationship—table between them, distance measurable. Cinematic. Pause in conversation."

**Shot 6 – Close, MAYA Final Moment:**
"Tight close-up of female face, front-facing or slight angle, almost no background. She has just stopped speaking or is about to speak a final quiet line. Eyes still, slight tension in jaw. No movement or very subtle micro-expression. Warm light. 16:9, cinematic restraint. End frame holds 1–2 seconds in near silence or with ambient café sound. Fade to black or cut."

**DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS**
- **Shot order**: Fixed. Sequence is 1–2–3–4–5–6 (no deviation).
- **Dialogue**: Specific lines provided (or substitute with equivalent intensity/rhythm; do not paraphrase).
- **Spatial logic**: Shots 2 and 3 must mirror each other (opposite sides of table). Shot 5 must re-establish both in frame to anchor spatial understanding.
- **Duration per shot**: Dialogue length determines timing; no shot shorter than 3 seconds or longer than 5 seconds (except final hold, which is ~2 seconds of silence).
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9, locked.
- **Frame rate**: 24 fps (cinematic standard).

---

**AUDIO_AND_EDIT**
- **Dialogue recording**: Capture cleanly; minimal room tone. No music until end credits or fade.
- **Ambient sound**: Café ambience (subtle clinking, distant voices) present but recessed. Foreground dialogue always intelligible.
- **Editing rhythm**: Cut on line end or micro-pause. No crossfade; hard cuts preferred. Final cut to black or fade over 1 second into silence or end tone.
- **Silence**: Approximately 2–3 seconds of silence at the end before fade. Emphasizes the weight of the moment.

---

**FAILURE_PREVENTION**
- **Spatial confusion**: Always re-establish positions in Shot 5. If viewer cannot answer "Who sits on which side?" the scene has failed. Confirm before final output.
- **Over-acting**: Dialogue is tense but restrained. Micro-expressions only. No jaw-clenching pantomime or heavy sighs. Coach actors toward internal struggle.
- **Inadequate framing**: Avoid profile-only or extreme angles that hide faces. All shots must show at least partial frontal face for emotional reading.
- **Pacing**: Do not allow pauses to become awkward silence (except final 2 sec). Dialogue should flow naturally even when restrained.
- **Lighting continuity**: Slight jump in color or brightness between shots will break intimacy. Lock down lighting temp and key/fill ratio.
- **Audio bleed**: If multiple takes, ensure dialogue is intelligible in final edit. No mumbling.
