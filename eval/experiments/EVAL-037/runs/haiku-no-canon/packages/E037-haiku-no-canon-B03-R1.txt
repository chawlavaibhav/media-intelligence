FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

---

**DELIVERABLE**
Single premium 4:5 (vertical) advertising photograph for point-of-sale, digital, and social deployment. Product-focused still image with aspirational but accessible tone.

---

**OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**
Establish a mosambi sparkling drink as a premium, contemporary choice for urban Indian adults (22–35) by positioning the product as the sophisticated, refreshing alternative to mass-market soft drinks. The image must convey quality, freshness, and cultural relevance without relying on decorative Indian visual clichés or nostalgic framing.

---

**CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA**
Minimalist product hero shot: a clear, elegant glass of the sparkling drink (golden-amber liquid, visible carbonation, condensation on glass) positioned at center-to-upper-frame, accompanied by one halved mosambi fruit and one whole fruit, arranged with intentional negative space and clean, contemporary styling. The composition conveys refinement and natural authenticity without artifice.

---

**MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY**
1. **Primary:** The drink itself—clear, inviting, sophisticated.  
2. **Secondary:** Mosambi as visible proof of ingredient (cut fruit shows interior); reinforces "natural" positioning.  
3. **Tertiary:** Aesthetic cues (condensation, carbonation, premium glassware) signal freshness, quality, coldness.  
4. **Absent:** Text, decorative elements, cultural signifiers, external context.

---

**VISUAL_SYSTEM**

**Color Palette:**
- Drink: natural golden-amber (mosambi juice + water, no artificial saturation)
- Fruit: pale lime-green skin, pale cream interior (cut face)
- Background: soft off-white or warm concrete gray (neutral, non-distracting, premium)
- Highlight accents: cool silver-blue (condensation, carbonation bubbles catch light)

**Lighting:**
- Key light: soft, directional (60° angle) to model the glass, emphasize condensation, and create subtle shadow depth
- Fill: gentle, to maintain premium brightness without flattening
- No harsh shadows; contemporary and clean aesthetic

**Composition:**
- 4:5 framing (vertical, slightly top-heavy)
- Glass positioned center-right or center
- Mosambi halves and whole positioned lower-left or asymmetrically to balance negative space
- Depth of field: glass and near fruit in sharp focus; background and far elements softly diffused
- No visual clutter; every element purposeful

**Styling:**
- Glass: contemporary, simple geometry; no ornament; appears cold (condensation beads visible, slight frost ring at base)
- Mosambi fruit: freshly cut (no browning); positioned to show both exterior and interior
- Surface: seamless, minimal (white or warm gray, possibly with subtle texture)

---

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**

1. **Set & Environment:** Seamless white or warm concrete-gray backdrop; minimal reflector bounce for fill; cool-toned side light for glass definition.

2. **Product Preparation:**
   - Fill contemporary glass with actual mosambi juice mixed with clear sparkling water (golden-amber appearance, visible carbonation)
   - Chill glass; create condensation by chilling glass exterior or misting with fine spray
   - Prepare one mosambi: cut in half horizontally to show interior; keep one whole fruit with stem

3. **Positioning:**
   - Glass at frame center or center-right, positioned at 2/3 height
   - Halved fruit lower-left; whole fruit nestled nearby with natural spacing
   - Ensure glass catches light to emphasize condensation and carbonation

4. **Photography:**
   - Shoot tethered; bracket exposure to ensure detail in highlights and juice color
   - Depth of field: f/4–f/5.6 to keep glass sharp with soft background
   - Capture at least 5–10 angles to provide director choice

5. **Post-Processing:**
   - Enhance condensation visibility (subtle digital detail work if required)
   - Color grade: warm but not saturated; maintain natural mosambi color
   - Ensure carbonation bubbles visible in liquid (light refraction enhancement if needed)
   - Clean, precise; no vintage or retro processing
   - Deliver at 4:5 with minimal 10% safe margin for text overlay

**FAILURE_PREVENTION**

1. **Avoid overly saturated amber/orange** – reads cheap; maintain natural mosambi tone
2. **Avoid visible carbonation that looks artificial** – ensure bubbles are captured in-camera or enhanced subtly
3. **Avoid background that competes** – keep neutral and soft; test with diffuser/white bounce
4. **Avoid fruit that looks wilted or oxidized** – work quickly after cutting; refresh fruit 15 min before shoot
5. **Avoid heavy shadows or dramatic contrast** – contemporary premium aesthetic is clean and bright, not moody
6. **Avoid ornamental glassware** – glass must be simple and modern, not decorative
7. **Avoid secondary products or visual clutter** – only glass, fruit, surface
8. **Test condensation durability** – bring ice packs or dry ice nearby (not in shot) to maintain frost during shoot
