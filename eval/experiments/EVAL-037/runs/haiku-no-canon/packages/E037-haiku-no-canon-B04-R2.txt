# FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

## DELIVERABLE
Vertical UGC video (9:16), 25 seconds, performance-optimized for social conversion. Single Indian woman talent speaks directly to camera with natural product demonstration. Suitable for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok.

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create credible, non-clinical creator content that educates oily-skin consumers on the core product benefit (fragrance-free 2% salicylic acid serum) and generates purchase intent without medical claims or false efficacy statements. Tone prioritizes authenticity over polish.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
Woman with oily/blemish-prone skin documents her honest experience with a lightweight, fragrance-free serum—addressing the practical problem that many actives irritate or feel heavy. Single message: this serum works for oily skin without fragrance interference or texture burden.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. **Primary**: Fragrance-free + effective for oily, blemish-prone skin
2. **Secondary**: Lightweight, non-irritating application
3. **Tertiary**: Direct product availability/purchase link (verbal CTA)

Avoid: medical/clinical language, percentage emphasis, skin-transformation promises.

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM
- **Setting**: Well-lit bathroom or bedroom; natural daylight preferred (windows/soft window light). Minimal styling—talent in casual loungewear or light top.
- **Background**: Clean, uncluttered (plain wall or neutral corner). No competing visual noise.
- **Framing**: 9:16 vertical, talent's face center-frame for dialogue shots; product centered in demo shots.
- **Color tone**: Warm, natural daylight. Slight desaturated finish acceptable if it reads as "authentic creator phone content."
- **Product visibility**: Packaging clearly legible in at least one shot; applicator/texture visible in application demo.

---

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- **Lighting**: Single diffused window light or 1–2 soft LED panels (avoid harsh shadows on face).
- **Audio**: Lapel or omnidirectional mic clipped to talent; minimal ambient noise. Record in quiet space.
- **Camera**: Smartphone or mirrorless camera in 9:16 vertical; gimbal stabilization optional but recommended for walking/movement shots.
- **Talent direction**: Conversational, direct eye contact with camera. Natural hand gestures. Speak at normal conversational pace (not rushed).
- **Duration per shot**: See shot-level breakdown below.

---

## GENERATION_PROMPTS

### **Shot 1 – Intro/Problem Statement (0–4 sec)**
**Prompt**: Close-up of Indian woman (face, shoulders, centered in frame) in vertical 9:16. Soft natural daylight from left. She looks directly at camera, relaxed expression. She gestures lightly to her face. Background is a clean, neutral interior (bedroom or bathroom wall, out of focus). Phone/handheld camera aesthetic. No makeup heavy enough to look styled. She is speaking conversationally.

**Talent dialogue**: "My skin gets oily super fast, and most serums just make it worse. They're either too heavy or they smell so strong I can't even stand it."

---

### **Shot 2 – Product Reveal (4–8 sec)**
**Prompt**: Medium close-up, 9:16 vertical. Same lighting. Talent holds the skincare serum bottle/container directly toward camera, bottle label clearly visible. She rotates it slightly so packaging and product name read on screen. Her face is partially visible, expression engaged. Clean background. Natural hand movement, no overly polished gesturing.

**Talent dialogue**: "That's why I switched to [Brand Name]. It's fragrance-free, and it's got two percent salicylic acid—which actually works for my skin without all the extra stuff."

---

### **Shot 3 – Application Demo (8–18 sec)**
**Prompt**: Two-part shot, 9:16 vertical, soft natural daylight. 

*Part A (0–5 sec within shot)*: Close-up of her hands. She dispenses 2–3 drops of clear serum onto fingertips. Serum texture is visible—liquid, lightweight. She gently pats and massages it into clean facial skin (forehead, cheeks, chin area visible in frame). Movements are calm, deliberate, not rushed. Her face is partially visible as she applies.

*Part B (5–10 sec within shot)*: Slight pull-back to show her face post-application. She touches her face lightly, nods, makes eye contact with camera. Natural expression—not exaggerated satisfaction.

**Talent dialogue**: "I use it morning and night. It sinks in instantly—no sticky residue, no fragrance to bother me, and my skin actually feels clearer. It's that simple."

---

### **Shot 4 – Benefit/CTA (18–25 sec)**
**Prompt**: Back to close-up, 9:16 vertical, same lighting setup. Talent framed from shoulders up, looking directly at camera. Confident, natural expression. She may tap her chin or gesture subtly to her skin.

**Talent dialogue**: "If you've got oily skin and you're tired of heavy serums, this is worth trying. Grab it [on our website / link in bio] and see how your skin feels."

## DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- **Deterministic**: Product packaging (must be the actual brand bottle), talent's face (must be Indian woman, natural appearance), application technique (must show real serum on real skin), spoken dialogue (scripted, exact wording as provided above to control messaging).
- **Generated/Variable**: Exact lighting conditions, minor framing adjustments, talent's micro-expressions (within conversational tone), background blur intensity.

---

## AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- **Audio strategy**: Direct, clear voice recording. Conversational tone, no background music (UGC authenticity). Optional: subtle ambient room tone underneath to avoid dead silence.
- **Edit approach**: 
  - Linear cut (no jump cuts within shots; use shot transitions only).
  - Transitions: Simple cross-dissolve or hard cut (no wipes/effects).
  - Color correction: Slight warm shift if daylight is cool; ensure product packaging and skin tone are natural and recognizable.
  - No text overlays or graphics (maintains UGC aesthetic); consider only a simple end-frame with product name/CTA if necessary for platform compliance.
- **Audio level**: Dialogue -6dB to -3dB, peaks at -1dB. No compression artifacts; natural voice dynamics preserved.

---

## FAILURE_PREVENTION
1. **Messaging drift**: Pre-approve exact dialogue with compliance team before shoot. Do not improvise claims.
2. **Product legibility**: Test packaging readability on vertical 9:16 frame; ensure logo/name is legible even on small phone screens.
3. **Skin/application realism**: Talent must have actual oily/blemish-prone skin or makeup that reads authentically. Avoid flawless, heavily-filtered skin (undermines credibility).
4. **Audio clarity**: Use lav mic; check for rustling, wind, or room hum during recording. Do not over-compress dialogue.
5. **Lighting consistency**: All shots lit similarly (same direction/temperature); avoid sudden brightness shifts between shots that suggest reshoots or artificial editing.
6. **Pacing**: Time each shot strictly to ensure 25-sec total. If dialogue takes longer, shorten shot duration or trim dialogue (prioritize message hierarchy).
7. **Vertical framing errors**: Ensure no critical visual information is in safe-frame margins. Test composition on actual 9:16 smartphone before final delivery.
