**VISUAL_SYSTEM**

- **Positioning**: Watch on wrist at 2–4 o'clock angle (three-quarter view, dial face toward camera)  
- **Framing**: Dial occupies 60–70% of frame; wrist/strap extends naturally to lower frame edge (4:5 crop)  
- **Lighting**: Single soft directional key light from upper left at ~45° angle, positioned to catch dial obliquely (reveals sunburst) and strike sapphire dome (shows curvature). Minimal fill from opposite side (~25% intensity).  
- **Background**: Neutral warm gray, slightly lighter than midtone, evenly lit and featureless  
- **Depth of field**: Shallow; dial and case remain sharp, strap/wrist soften gradually  
- **Camera angle**: Slight downward tilt (10–15°) to emphasize sapphire dome profile  

---

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**

1. **Setup**  
   - Position watch on wrist (male or neutral-proportioned female wrist to emphasize 38mm scale)  
   - Angle at three-quarter orientation with dial facing camera  
   - Stabilize wrist for sharp focus on dial plane  

2. **Lighting**  
   - Key light: soft directional source (5600K, diffused), positioned upper left 45°, distance adjusted to catch sunburst detail without flare  
   - Fill light: low-intensity source opposite side (reduce to 20–30% of key) to preserve depth  
   - Background: evenly lit neutral gray (ensure no shadows on background behind case)  

3. **Camera/Framing**  
   - Close-up macro or equivalent focal length  
   - 4:5 aspect ratio (minimum 1000×1250px; preferred 2000×2500px for e-commerce)  
   - Focus point: center of dial, slightly forward of sapphire dome surface  
   - Aperture: f/4–f/8 for controlled depth of field (sharp dial, soft strap)  

4. **Post-Production**  
   - **Color**: Deep blue sunburst dial (saturated, neither navy nor purple; warm undertone to leather strap)  
   - **Contrast**: Moderate boost to reveal dial radial pattern without crushing shadows  
   - **Clarity/Texture**: Enhance fine details (baton markers, hand edges, sapphire reflection)  
   - **Warmth**: Slight warm color lift (~200K) to bring out leather richness  
   - **Vignette**: None, or extremely subtle (preserve full frame legibility for e-commerce)  
   - **Sharpening**: Dial and case only; preserve softness in strap/background
