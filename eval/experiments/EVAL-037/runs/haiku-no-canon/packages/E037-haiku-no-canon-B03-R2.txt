FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

**DELIVERABLE**
Single premium advertising image, 4:5 aspect ratio (vertical), photorealistic style, RGB color space, minimum 2400×3000 px resolution. Hero product image for social/digital deployment.

---

**OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION**
Deliver a product-centric advertising image that presents a new mosambi sparkling drink to urban Indian consumers (22–35) as a sophisticated, refreshing category player—distinctly contemporary and premium, not mass-market or heritage-focused. Product must occupy visual hierarchy dominantly. No lifestyle narrative, celebrity, or cultural stereotyping.

---

**CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA**
A single, elegantly composed glass of the sparkling mosambi drink positioned as the visual hero in a minimal, contemporary urban setting. The drink's golden-yellow clarity, visible effervescence, and fresh mosambi garnish communicate dual identity (fruit + fizz). Refined lighting, clean surfaces, and absence of clutter signal premium positioning. The image works as a standalone product shot for premium beverage advertising.

---

**MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY**
1. **Primary**: The drink itself—its color, clarity, and carbonation
2. **Secondary**: Mosambi fruit authenticity—via color accent and optional fresh slice/segment
3. **Tertiary**: Premium/contemporary aesthetic—via setting, lighting, surfaces
4. **Absent**: Narrative, people, brand logo, external context

---

**VISUAL_SYSTEM**

**Composition:**
- Vertical 4:5 frame with drink occupying central to slightly off-center position (~55% of visual weight)
- Drink glass/vessel: clean glassware (standard 300–350 ml tumbler or premium drinking glass), positioned at slight angle for visual interest
- Mosambi fruit element: 1 fresh mosambi slice or whole fruit, positioned adjacent (bottom-right or top-left), soft focus or secondary focus
- Negative space: 40–50% of frame, uncluttered

**Color Palette:**
- Drink liquid: bright, translucent golden-yellow (mosambi's natural color)
- Carbonation: visible bubble trails and effervescence in liquid
- Background/Surface: neutral (off-white, soft gray, pale concrete, or matte light surface)—no color competition
- Mosambi fruit: saturated, warm yellow-green

**Lighting:**
- Key light: soft directional light (appears to come from upper-left or front) creating subtle shadows and depth
- Reflections: gentle highlights on glass rim and beverage surface
- Backlight (optional subtle): to enhance translucency and bubble visibility
- Mood: clean, bright, contemporary—not warm/golden-hour, not dark/moody

**Surface/Setting:**
- Minimal environmental context: clean table, marble slab, matte concrete, or neutral backdrop
- No clutter, pattern, or decoration
- Modern, urban aesthetic (not rustic, not ornamental)

---

**PRODUCTION_RECIPE**

1. **Setup**: Prepare a clean, neutral surface (white or light gray). Use professional beverage photography lighting (3-point or 2-point setup with softbox key light).

2. **Beverage Preparation**: 
   - Fill glass with clear sparkling water
   - Add mosambi juice (approximately 40–60% juice, 40–60% sparkling water blend) to achieve golden-yellow transparency
   - Add 3–5 ice cubes or chill beverage to create authentic carbonation bubble appearance
   - Ensure bubbles are visible but not violent; allow 2–3 minutes post-pour for stable state

3. **Garnish**: Place 1 fresh mosambi slice (thin-cut, seeds visible) adjacent to glass, or position 1 whole mosambi nearby, slightly out of focus

4. **Framing**: Compose in 4:5 ratio; position drink slightly off-center (rule-of-thirds or golden ratio) for sophistication; ensure glass is in sharp focus, fruit secondary focus

5. **Lighting Pass**: Adjust key light for:
   - Liquid translucency and internal bubble visibility
   - Subtle rim highlights on glassware
   - Soft shadow for dimension (not harsh)
   - No lens flare or blown highlights

6. **Final Capture**: Shoot 15–20 variations (slight angle shifts, zoom, mosambi position) to ensure hero shot is captured

**FAILURE_PREVENTION**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Drink looks murky or cheap | Pre-test juice concentration; ensure sparkling water is truly carbonated; use clear glassware; light from front/side to show translucency |
| Bubbles are invisible | Allow 2–3 min post-pour; use macro/close lighting to highlight carbonation; ensure adequate bubbles at shoot time |
| Mosambi fruit looks wilted | Use fresh fruit purchased same day; keep refrigerated until use; cut immediately before shoot |
| Composition feels cluttered | Remove all non-essential elements; use 40–50% negative space; position fruit in secondary focus, not competing with drink |
| Image feels clichéd or "Indian" | Avoid: saffron/orange colors, ornamental surfaces, spices, marigolds, fabric patterns. Use: neutral surfaces, contemporary lighting, no cultural shorthand |
| Product doesn't appear premium | Use professional lighting (no flat/dull lighting); ensure glass is clean and gleaming; include subtle ice condensation; avoid plastic cups or cheap glassware |
| 4:5 aspect ratio not maintained | Explicitly crop/frame all shots to 4:5 before final export; verify aspect ratio in generation settings and post-production |
