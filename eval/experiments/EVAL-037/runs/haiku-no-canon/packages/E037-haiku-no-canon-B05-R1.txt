FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

---

## DELIVERABLE
A 16:9 cinematic video scene, ~40 seconds, depicting a tense café confrontation between two people across a table. The discovery of deception unfolds through exactly 6 shots with clear spatial orientation maintained throughout.

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a self-contained dramatic moment—a moment of accusation and evasion—that relies on intimate framing, spatial clarity, and restrained emotional performance rather than dialogue volume or elaborate cinematography. The viewer must always know where each person sits relative to the other.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
Two people at a small café table. Person A has found evidence of Person B's deception. The confrontation is glacial and quiet. The tension lives in the space between them, in held glances, and in what remains unspoken. Each shot is positioned to either widen the perceived distance (establishing Person A's separateness and accusation) or tighten it (forcing Person B's discomfort). No music. Only ambient café sound and dialogue.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. **Establishment of location and spatial relationship** (Shot 1)
2. **Person A's accusation and emotional state** (Shot 2)
3. **Person B's guilt/defensiveness and response** (Shot 3)
4. **The uncomfortable shared space—moment of reckoning** (Shot 4)
5. **Person A's deeper reaction/pain to the answer** (Shot 5)
6. **The unresolved moment—what happens next is ambiguous** (Shot 6)

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM

**Framing principle:** Two-person coverage using:
- One wide/medium establishing shot showing both figures and table
- Medium shots (waist-up) on each person for dialogue, angled slightly toward each other to show spatial relationship
- One intervening wide shot to "reset" spatial understanding and show the gulf between them
- Close-up registers only on moments of maximum emotional intensity

**Spatial anchors:** The table divides the frame. Coffee cups, hands on the table, and the line of sight between them should be consistent and visible.

**Lighting:** Soft, naturalistic café light (diffused daylight from windows or warm interior). No dramatic shadows. Clarity and intimacy matter more than stylization.

**Depth of field:** Wide in establishing and mid-scene wide shots (to show space). Shallow on close-ups (to isolate emotional reaction).

**Color:** Warm, slightly desaturated. Café palette: beige, ochre, dark wood. Restrained—nothing that competes with performance.

---

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Setting:** Small café table, two chairs opposite each other, coffee cups and small items on table. Neutral background (other tables, counter, soft out-of-focus café interior). Daytime, window light preferred.

**Performance note:** Both actors play restraint. No raised voices. Movements are minimal—hands, eye contact, subtle shifts in posture. Person A is controlled but hurt. Person B is defensive but not hostile.

**Timing allocation:**
- Shot 1: 6 seconds
- Shot 2: 6 seconds
- Shot 3: 6 seconds
- Shot 4: 5 seconds
- Shot 5: 8 seconds
- Shot 6: 9 seconds
- **Total: 40 seconds**

## GENERATION_PROMPTS

### SHOT 1 (Establishing, ~6 sec)
**Purpose:** Show both people, the table, spatial relationship. Person A is speaking; Person B listens, tense.

**Prompt:**
"Wide shot across a small café table, two people sitting opposite each other in daylight. Person A (left side of frame), dark-haired, sits forward slightly, hands on the table, speaking with controlled intensity. Person B (right side) sits back, shoulders tensed, avoiding eye contact. A coffee cup and phone sit on the wood table between them. Warm café interior behind them, soft out-of-focus. 16:9. Cinematic, shallow depth of field in background, sharp focus on both figures. Tense but quiet moment."

**Dialogue over shot:**
Person A: "I found the emails."

---

### SHOT 2 (Medium close-up, Person A, ~6 sec)
**Purpose:** Register Person A's controlled accusation and the hurt underneath. Tighter framing emphasizes isolation.

**Prompt:**
"Medium shot from Person B's perspective, framing Person A (waist-up) from across the table. Person A's jaw is set, eyes direct and searching. The background shows the café interior and the edge of Person B's shoulder (showing spatial relationship). Shallow depth of field. Afternoon light on Person A's face, warm and clear. 16:9. Cinematic lighting, slight tension in the expression."

**Dialogue over shot:**
Person A: "I was going through the drawer, and I found everything."

---

### SHOT 3 (Medium close-up, Person B, ~6 sec)
**Purpose:** Show Person B's defensive reaction, guilt, closed-off body language.

**Prompt:**
"Medium shot from Person A's perspective, framing Person B (waist-up) across the table. Person B's expression shifts—caught off-guard, defensive. Eyes dart down to the table, then back up. Hands grip the edge of the table or fold. The background shows the café and the edge of Person A's shoulder. Shallow depth of field. Same warm afternoon light. 16:9. Cinematic, the background slightly out of focus but spatially continuous with Shot 2."

**Dialogue over shot:**
Person B: "I was going to tell you. I just... the timing was never right."

---

### SHOT 4 (Wide, mid-conversation, ~5 sec)
**Purpose:** Reset spatial understanding. Show the distance between them, reinforce the gulf. This is the "pulling back" moment.

**Prompt:**
"Medium-wide shot of both people across the table, similar to Shot 1 but with slightly different camera position—shot from a slight angle that shows the full table and both figures more clearly. Both are visible; the silence between them feels physical. Person A has leaned back slightly. Person B's posture is closed. The café around them is warm and blurred. 16:9. Cinematic framing, both figures in focus, the space between them is the subject."

**No dialogue—ambient café sound (faint chatter, cups).**

---

### SHOT 5 (Close-up, Person A, ~8 sec)
**Purpose:** Deepest emotional register. Person A's hurt and realization that this was deliberate. Longest dialogue beat, minimal movement.

**Prompt:**
"Close-up of Person A's face, framed from across the table (slightly higher angle, as if from Person B's eye line). Person A's controlled expression cracks slightly—eyes glisten, jaw tightens. The person looks down briefly, then back at Person B with a mix of pain and betrayal. Soft café light, shallow depth of field, the background is completely blurred. 16:9. Cinematic, intimate, the performance is quiet and internal."

**Dialogue over shot:**
Person A: "That's what makes it worse. You chose not to tell me."

---

### SHOT 6 (Wide or medium-wide, resolution, ~9 sec)
**Purpose:** The unresolved moment. Neither person speaks. The scene ends in the space between them. Ambiguous, cinematic, leaves the viewer in the discomfort.

**Prompt:**
"Medium-wide shot of both figures across the table, camera at a slight angle (different from Shots 1 and 4 to avoid repetition, but maintaining spatial clarity). Person A has straightened, looking down at the table or their hands. Person B is still, looking at Person A or away. The café exists around them, warm and indifferent. Silence. A few seconds pass. Person A reaches for the coffee cup or phone—a small gesture of disengagement or resignation. 16:9. Cinematic, soft light, both in focus, the moment stretches. The scene ends."

**No dialogue. Only ambient café sound. Moment fades to black or holds on the image.**

## DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS

**Props (must be present):**
- Small café table, wood or light-colored
- Two chairs opposite each other
- Two coffee cups (at least one visible on table)
- A phone (visible in Shot 1, referenced in dialogue)

**Location continuity (must be consistent across all 6 shots):**
- Same table, same two chairs, same spatial relationship
- The background (café interior) should be recognizably the same venue
- Window light or café ambient light should be consistent in color temperature and direction

**Spatial anchors (must be maintained):**
- Person A always on same side of table across all shots
- Person B always on opposite side
- The table surface and its contents (coffee cups, phone) should occupy consistent positions
- Eyelines and sightlines should be consistent with camera positions

**Performance consistency:**
- Person A's clothing, hair, and overall appearance must be identical across all 6 shots
- Person B's appearance must be consistent
- Emotional progression: Person A moves from controlled to slightly fractured to resigned. Person B moves from caught off-guard to defensive to stillness.

*Shot 2:*

*Shot 3:*

- No music.
- No sound effects beyond natural café environment.

**Edit approach:**
- Straight cuts between shots. No dissolves or transitions.
- Shot timing is as specified. Dialogue and silence are precise beats.
- The final shot holds in silence for 2-3 seconds after the last line, then fades to black over 1 second.
- Total duration: 40 seconds.

## FAILURE_PREVENTION

**Spatial continuity failure:** Generate each shot with explicit instruction that the same table, same two people, and same relative positions must be visible. Include a spatial anchor phrase in each prompt (e.g., "from across the table," "the edge of Person B's shoulder visible," "the table surface visible").

**Eyeline failure:** Ensure that in each close-up or medium shot, the direction of gaze is consistent with the camera position. If Person A is looking at Person B in Shot 5, the camera should be positioned as if from Person B's vantage point (slightly lower, across the table).

**Dialogue timing mismatch:** Generate shots with explicit duration markers. Ensure that dialogue delivery is paced to fit the shot duration and that pauses in dialogue align with the silence in Shot 4.

**Tonal failure (scene becomes slapstick or too melodramatic):** Emphasize in performance notes that restraint is key. Specify "controlled," "quiet," "minimal movement," "internal emotional register" in every prompt. Avoid prompts that suggest shouting, dramatic hand gestures, or overacting.

**Cinematic vs. mechanical coverage:** Do not use rigid shot-reverse-shot at identical angles. Vary camera height and angle between shots (especially between Shots 2 and 3, and between establishing shots 1, 4, and 6) to create visual interest without elaborate movement.

**Temporal inconsistency (people age, light shifts drastically, clothing changes):** Keep lighting warm and consistent across all shots. Specify "afternoon café light" or "daytime window light" in every prompt. Lock costume, hair, and appearance descriptions into the performer descriptions.

**Background inconsistency (café looks different in different shots):** Every prompt should include a descriptor of the café interior as visible in that shot's background. Reference the same elements (other tables, counter, windows) so the AI understands it's the same space.

✓ **~40 seconds total duration:** 6+6+6+5+8+9 = 40 seconds.
✓ **Two people at café table:** Core setup in every shot prompt.
