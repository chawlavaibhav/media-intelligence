### FAILURE_PREVENTION

**Risk 1: App screenshots look too generic or outdated.**
- *Mitigation:* Use current RentOK app screenshots only. Verify with RentOK team before production. Do not use generic PG-management-app UI; must be recognizably RentOK.

**Risk 2: Chaos section feels too exaggerated or comical, losing credibility.**
- *Mitigation:* Keep real. Use authentic PG/hostel owner footage. Real notebooks, real spreadsheets, real WhatsApp. Avoid theatrical acting. Tone: sympathetic, not mocking.

**Risk 3: Transition feels abrupt or confusing.**
- *Mitigation:* Use a clear visual cue (swipe, fade, or dissolve) that signals "system change." Music shift reinforces transition.

**Risk 4: Solution section moves too fast; features unclear.**
- *Mitigation:* Each feature (dashboard, complaints, KYC, automation) gets 1.5–2 seconds and explicit visual labeling (e.g., "Complaint Management" text label on screen).

**Risk 5: Voiceover (if included) sounds corporate/stiff.**
- *Mitigation:* Brief, conversational script. Natural cadence. Low-key delivery. Record multiple takes; select warmest, most relatable read.

**Risk 6: CTA is weak or unclear.**
- *Mitigation:* Explicit buttons ("Download App," "Get Free Demo"). Clear branding. Offer a clear next action—no ambiguity.

**Risk 7: Video doesn't feel "Indian" enough—risks appearing like generic SaaS.**
- *Mitigation:* Set in authentic PG/hostel office, not minimalist co-working space. Include Hindi-English code-switching in WhatsApp messages. Use non-Western music. Avoid Americana design language.
