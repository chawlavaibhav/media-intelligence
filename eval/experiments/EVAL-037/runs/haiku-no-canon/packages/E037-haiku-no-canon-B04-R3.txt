# FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

---

## DELIVERABLE
9:16 vertical UGC-style video, ~25 seconds, single-take or 2–3 seamless cuts. Appears shot on smartphone or mirrorless with natural light. One Indian woman, mid-20s to early 30s, speaks directly to camera while handling product. Suitable for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok.

---

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Produce credible creator content that demonstrates a 2% salicylic acid serum for oily skin, establish one clear, honest reason to purchase (lightweight + oil control without stripping), avoid clinical language, and build trust through authentic, relatable communication and visible product interaction.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
**"This serum actually works for my oily skin without ruining it."**

A real-seeming creator shows the product in hand, talks briefly about her oily/blemish struggle, applies/demonstrates the serum's texture and absorption, and closes with one specific, non-medical observation: it controls oil and stays lightweight throughout the day. No scripts about clinical efficacy—only personal observation.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY

**Primary (Lead):** "My skin gets oily fast, and most treatments either strip it or do nothing."

**Secondary (Middle):** "This serum is fragrance-free and lightweight—I can feel the difference in how it sits on my skin."

**Tertiary (Close):** "It keeps my skin matte without that tight, uncomfortable feeling."

**Unspoken subtext:** Suited for Indian climate/skin; practical; not a clinical product presentation.

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM

**Lighting:** Soft natural daylight from one side (window or diffused outdoor light). Minimal shadows on face. Warm, non-clinical.

**Camera:** Handheld or tripod-mounted; slight imperfection/micro-movements acceptable (reinforces UGC authenticity).

**Framing:** Mostly close-up (shoulders/head), product clearly visible in hand when introduced.

**Color:** Natural skin tone, minimal color grading. Product bottle should read clearly.

**Background:** Neutral (bedroom corner, bathroom mirror, soft-focus white/beige wall). No branded signage or studio cues.

**Product interaction:** Hand pours a small amount onto fingertip, taps serum gently onto forehead/cheek, spreads with fingertips, camera shows absorption/shine fade over 2–3 seconds.

---

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE

1. **Setup**: Single location (natural light preferred). Tripod + phone or mirrorless camera.
2. **Talent direction**: Speak naturally, no memorized script—brief talking points only. Aim for one uninterrupted take or 2–3 stitched takes.
3. **Product handling**: Open bottle, dispense, apply visibly. Show absorption in real time (no time-lapse—maintains authenticity).
4. **Edit style**: Minimal cuts. Possible one subtle cut after product application (jump to face close-up or to hand). No effects, transitions, or music beds that sound "branded."
5. **Audio**: Direct voice (minimal background noise). Optional subtle ambient (subtle birdsong/room tone if edited in, not loud).

## DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS

- **Product bottle**: Use actual bottle or photo-real mockup with finalized label design. Must be consistent across shots.
- **Talent**: Cast and brief before shoot. Provide talking points only (see below).
- **Lighting setup**: Physical—daylight from one direction, no flash.
- **Location**: Scout and lock beforehand.
- **Audio**: Record live dialogue; no voiceover.

**Talent Talking Points (Not a Script—Conversational Prompts):**
1. *Opening*: "Hey. So my skin gets oily really fast, especially here [gesture to T-zone]. Most serums either don't help or they leave my skin feeling tight and uncomfortable."
2. *After product intro*: "This is [brand name]. It's fragrance-free, which actually matters because a lot of products irritate my skin when I'm breaking out."
3. *After application*: "You can see how lightweight it is. It sinks in super quickly. I don't get that slick feeling—just control, all day."
4. *Close*: "Honestly, it's the one serum that feels like it was made for oily skin that actually wants to stay healthy."

---

## AUDIO_AND_EDIT

- **Dialogue**: Record live on set. Minimal background noise (interior ambient acceptable).
- **Music**: Optional low-volume ambient or none. If music, subtle lo-fi or modern indie—no electronic dance/upbeat commercial sound.
- **Sound design**: Natural foley (serum dispense, light tap on skin) can be added in post if not captured well in production. Keep subtle.
- **Edit style**: One or two invisible cuts maximum. No transitions, no text overlays (or minimal watermark only). Final video should feel like one continuous moment captured on someone's phone.
- **Color grade**: Minimal. Keep natural warmth and skin tones truthful.

---

## FAILURE_PREVENTION

1. **Claim creep**: Review final dialogue for any words like "acne-fighting," "clears," "medical-grade," "clinical results." Remove. Replace with "keeps skin clear," "reduces breakouts," or omit entirely.
2. **Over-production**: If final video looks like a commercial (studio lighting, perfect hair/makeup, scripted delivery), re-shoot. UGC should show real creator authenticity.
3. **Product visibility**: Ensure bottle label/name is legible in all shots. Don't obscure with shadows or poor angles.
4. **Application realism**: Serum should actually absorb or appear to absorb in real time. Avoid time-lapse or unrealistic quick-drying.
5. **Talent authenticity**: If talent sounds robotic or reads from cards, re-direct to conversational tone or re-shoot.
6. **Aspect ratio**: Confirm all shots are true 9:16 throughout; no pillarboxing or center-framed widescreen mistakes.
7. **Duration**: Target 23–27 seconds. If under 20 or over 30, edit to tighten.

---

## HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK

✓ **No clinical statistics or medical claims**: Talent speaks only to personal experience ("works for me," "keeps my skin clear"). No mention of percentages, study results, or clinical efficacy.

✓ **No invented guarantees**: No "100% results," "guaranteed," or "dermatologist-proven" language.

✓ **9:16 format**: All shots framed for vertical mobile viewing.

✓ **One woman, direct to camera**: Single talent, primary communication mode is face-to-face speech.

✓ **Product demonstration**: Serum visible, opened, applied, absorbed on skin in one continuous shot.

✓ **One purchase reason**: "Lightweight + oil control without stripping/discomfort" is the single, repeatable core message.

✓ **Visually controlled, commercially usable**: Natural but intentional lighting, clean backgrounds, professional-grade camera/audio. Not a low-res random phone capture.

✓ **Creator authenticity, not TV commercial**: No studio gloss, no voiceover, no music bed underneath. Handheld feel with professional execution.

✓ **No external websites referenced**: None needed. Brand name and product visible in-video only.

✓ **~25 seconds**: Shot list and pacing designed for 23–27 sec final cut.
