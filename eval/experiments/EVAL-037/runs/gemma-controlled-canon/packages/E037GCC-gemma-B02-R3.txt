FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The goal is to communicate aggressive, low-cost pricing (₹9/₹99) for an AI media API without degrading the brand's positioning as a "serious AI infrastructure" company. The "festive" element must be a sophisticated atmospheric layer rather than a literal retail theme. The tension to resolve is: "High-value/Low-cost" (Premium Styling + Discount Price).

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Architectural Celebration." The design will use a "Tech-Noir" palette (deep matte charcoal and obsidian) contrasted with a single, high-end accent color (Champagne Gold) used in a minimalist, geometric way. Instead of festive clip-art, we use a single, ultra-thin, gold-vector geometric motif inspired by a mandala but rendered as a technical schematic. The pricing is treated as a core architectural element of the page, utilizing massive, clean typography that feels like a luxury watch ad or a fintech dashboard.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. THE HOOK (Primary): The prices—₹9 and ₹99—presented as the dominant visual anchors.
2. THE PRODUCT (Secondary): "Image Generation" and "Video Generation" paired with their respective prices.
3. THE BRAND POSITIONING (Tertiary): "Enterprise AI Infrastructure for Indian Businesses."
4. THE UTILITY (Footer): "Prepaid Rupee Wallet | Domestic GST Invoices" (directly from website).
5. BRAND SIGNATURE: aight logo.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Obsidian Black (#0A0A0A), Matte Charcoal (#1A1A1A), and Champagne Gold (#D4AF37).
- Typography: 
    - Prices: An oversized, heavy-weight Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter Bold or Montserrat) with tight tracking.
    - Labels: A refined, medium-weight Sans-Serif with wide tracking for a "premium" feel.
    - Body: A clean, mono-spaced font to evoke "API/Infrastructure" coding aesthetics.
- Layout: A strict centered grid. Extreme negative space around the central pricing block to prevent a "crowded" retail look.
- Festive Element: A single, hairline-thin gold geometric circle/mandala centered behind the text, partially cropped by the edges of the frame, appearing more like a technical diagram than a decoration.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Background: Deep gradient from Obsidian to Matte Charcoal.
2. Central Motif: Place a gold-line vector mandala/geometric star; reduce opacity to 30% so it acts as a subtle backdrop.
3. Typography Layer:
    - Center-top: aight logo in white.
    - Center: "₹9" (Massive, Gold) followed by "IMAGE" (Smaller, White, Wide Tracking) below it.
    - Center: "₹99" (Massive, Gold) followed by "VIDEO" (Smaller, White, Wide Tracking) below it.
    - Bottom: A horizontal divider line in gold, followed by the brand positioning and GST/Wallet utility text in a small, white mono-spaced font.

GENERATION_PROMPTS

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid "Sale" or "Discount" language: Use the prices as facts, not offers.
- Avoid saturated reds/yellows: Stick to Champagne Gold to maintain the "Infrastructure" feel.
- Avoid clutter: If the generation adds too many "festive" ornaments, use a negative prompt for "ribbons, confetti, lanterns, fireworks."
