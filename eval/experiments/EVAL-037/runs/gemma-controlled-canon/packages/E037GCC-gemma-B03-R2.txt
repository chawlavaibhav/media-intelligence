FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Position a mosambi sparkling drink as a sophisticated, modern urban beverage for 22–35 year olds. The goal is to shift the perception of mosambi from "traditional street juice" to "premium sparkling refreshment" by using a contemporary Indian architectural aesthetic, avoiding ethnic clichés, and emphasizing high-end product photography.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The New Gold Standard." The image features the drink as a sculptural object set against the backdrop of modern Indian minimalism (terrazzo and hard sunlight), framing the familiar flavor of mosambi through a lens of luxury and urbanity.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero: The chilled, sparkling drink (Immediate recognition of refreshment and quality).
2. Flavor Cue: The pale gold hue of the liquid and a minimalist slice of mosambi (Familiarity).
3. Brand Positioning: The contemporary, architectural setting (Sophistication and urban identity).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Pale Mosambi Gold, Polished Concrete Grey, Crisp White, and a deep Botanical Green accent.
- Materials: Polished grey terrazzo, chilled glass, condensation droplets, crystalline ice.
- Lighting: High-contrast "Hard Sunlight." Strong directional light creating sharp, architectural shadows to emphasize the "sparkle" of the carbonation and the texture of the condensation.
- Composition: 4:5 Vertical. Low-angle shot. The bottle is the central hero, slightly offset to create a dynamic, modern balance.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Setting: A minimalist outdoor ledge of polished grey terrazzo in a sun-drenched urban space.
- Product Styling: A sleek, minimalist glass bottle with a translucent label. The bottle must be "sweating" with heavy, realistic condensation. Inside, micro-bubbles are visible, clinging to the glass.
- Props: One thin, translucent crescent slice of fresh mosambi resting on the terrazzo next to the bottle, and a single, clear, square ice cube with a slight melt.
- Background: A blurred, bright white architectural wall with a sharp geometric shadow cutting across the frame, suggesting a modern building.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Generation Prompt:
"A premium high-end advertising photograph of a sparkling mosambi drink in a sleek minimalist glass bottle, 4:5 aspect ratio. The bottle is the central hero, covered in realistic heavy condensation droplets. The liquid inside is a pale, translucent golden-yellow with visible carbonation bubbles. The bottle is placed on a polished grey terrazzo ledge. Next to the bottle lies a single, thin, fresh slice of mosambi and one crystalline square ice cube. Lighting is hard, bright, directional afternoon sunlight creating sharp, architectural geometric shadows on a bright white background wall. Hyper-realistic, 8k, commercial beverage photography, shot on 85mm lens, f/8 for deep clarity on product, minimal urban Indian luxury aesthetic, crisp textures, refreshing, sophisticated."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Product Label: Must be added post-generation to ensure brand-accurate typography and logo placement.
- Color Grade: Final polish to ensure the "Mosambi Gold" is pale and sophisticated, not neon or overly orange.

N/A (Static Image).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Anti-Cliché Filter: No marigolds, no paisley patterns, no rustic wooden tables, no vibrant orange saturation.
- Anti-Cheap Filter: No plastic bottles, no overly saturated "soda" colors, no generic tropical leaf backgrounds.
- Hero Check: Ensure the background is blurred enough to keep the focus entirely on the bottle's texture and the liquid's color.
