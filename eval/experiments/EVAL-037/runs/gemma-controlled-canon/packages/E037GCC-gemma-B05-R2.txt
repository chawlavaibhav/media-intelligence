FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
40-second cinematic scene (16:9) consisting of exactly six shots.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension dialogue between two characters (A and B) at a café table. Character A has uncovered a secret held by Character B. The scene emphasizes psychological pressure through static framing and strict spatial continuity.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." Use a rigid adherence to the 180-degree rule to create a feeling of entrapment. The tension is derived from the lack of movement—both physical and camera—forcing the viewer to focus on micro-expressions and the heavy silence between lines.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Relationship: Fixed opposition (A on left, B on right).
2. Emotional State: A's cold certainty vs. B's crumbling composure.
3. The Catalyst: A physical object (the "evidence") acting as the focal point between them.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Lighting: Low-key, directional light from a window, creating soft shadows on faces.
- Palette: Muted tones (slate grey, deep browns, cream) to avoid distracting from the performances.
- Framing: Strict adherence to the 180-degree rule. A is always frame-left looking right; B is always frame-right looking left.
- Depth of Field: Shallow (f/2.8 or similar) to isolate characters from the café background.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Set: Small round bistro table, two chairs, minimal background clutter.
- Props: Two coffee cups, one small handwritten note/document.
- Casting: Two actors capable of conveying intensity through eyes and breath rather than voice.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1: [0-7s] Medium wide shot, 16:9. A quiet, moody café. Person A (left) and Person B (right) sit opposite each other at a small round table. Person A slowly places a small piece of paper on the table between them. Cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, static camera.

Shot 2: [7-13s] Medium close-up, 16:9. Person A, frame left, looking right. Expression is calm and cold. Static shot. Soft window light hitting one side of the face. "I found it."

Shot 3: [13-21s] Medium close-up, 16:9. Person B, frame right, looking left. Person B looks down at the paper, then slowly looks up at Person A. Eyes wide, restrained panic. Static shot.

Shot 4: [21-28s] Close-up, 16:9. Person A, frame left, looking right. Focus on the eyes. A slight, knowing tilt of the head. Static shot. "Why did you think I wouldn't?"

Shot 5: [28-34s] Close-up, 16:9. Person B, frame right, looking left. Mouth tight, jaw clenched. A slight swallow. Static shot. "It's not what it looks like."

Shot 6: [34-40s] Medium two-shot, 16:9. Camera shifted 45 degrees from Shot 1 but remaining on the same side of the 180-degree axis. Person A (left) and Person B (right) locked in a stare across the table. The paper remains between them. Static shot. Fade to black.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (Strict).
- Shot Count: Exactly 6 (Strict).
- Camera Movement: Zero (Static frames).

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Sound Design: Ambient café noise (distant clink of porcelain, low hum of conversation) heavily attenuated. Focus on "near-field" sounds: the slide of the paper on the table, the breath of the actors.
- Edit: Hard cuts between shots. No transitions except for a final fade to black.
- Pacing: Slow. Allow the silence in Shot 3 and Shot 6 to build the tension.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Prevent "Jump Cuts": Ensure the camera position for Shot 6 is at least 30 degrees offset from Shot 1.
- Prevent "Crossing the Line": Ensure A is always on the left and B is always on the right to maintain spatial clarity.
- Prevent "Over-acting": Direct actors to keep dialogue at a near-whisper; tension must be visual, not auditory.
