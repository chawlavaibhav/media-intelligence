FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium advertising image (4:5 aspect ratio).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end visual for a Mosambi (sweet lime) sparkling drink targeting urban Indian millennials/Gen Z (22–35). The image must move away from traditional "ethnic" markers and instead use a globalized, minimalist luxury aesthetic to signal "contemporary sophistication" while using material cues (color, fruit) to maintain the familiarity of mosambi.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Architecture of Refreshment." 
The product is treated as a piece of industrial design. Instead of a rustic or "natural" setting, the drink is placed in a hyper-minimalist, high-contrast studio environment. The "premium" feel is achieved through a muted, sophisticated color palette and the precise rendering of physical states: the tension of a water droplet, the glow of carbonation, and the translucency of a perfectly sliced fruit.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRODUCT HEROISM: The bottle is the unmistakable central focus.
2. REFRESHMENT: Immediate sensory triggers (condensation, effervescence, ice-cold temperature).
3. FLAVOR CUE: Sophisticated representation of mosambi (color and a curated fruit element).
4. BRAND POSITIONING: Premium/Urban (signaled through lighting and minimalist composition).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Pale Chartreuse/Champagne (the drink), Deep Slate Grey/Matte Charcoal (background), Crisp Silver/White (highlights and bubbles).
- Lighting: "Describing Highlights." Strong, narrow vertical light strips on the bottle's edges to define its silhouette. Soft, diffused backlighting to create a glow within the pale yellow liquid (subsurface scattering).
- Texture: Macro-detail on glass condensation (micro-beads), sharp carbonation bubbles, and the matte texture of a stone-like background.
- Composition: Centered, slightly low-angle shot to give the product "stature." A single, thin, translucent disk of mosambi floating or resting geometrically beside the bottle.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Setup: Studio product photography. 
- Focal Length: 85mm or 100mm macro to eliminate distortion and compress the depth of field.
- Styling: 
    - Bottle chilled to the dew point for natural condensation.
    - Liquid carbonated immediately before the shot for active bubble plumes.
    - Mosambi slice cut into a precise, thin architectural circle (no rustic wedges).
- Background: A seamless, matte charcoal concrete or slate surface to provide a moody, urban contrast to the bright liquid.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Generation Prompt:

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To avoid "cheap soft drink" look: Eliminate bright, saturated primary colors (no neon yellows or bright greens). Avoid generic "splash" effects; prefer "effervescence" and "condensation."
- To avoid "clichéd Indian" look: No paisley, no saffron, no rustic wooden tables, no traditional textiles. Use urban materials (concrete, slate, glass).
- To ensure "Hero" status: Ensure the background is a neutral, receding color (slate) so the pale yellow bottle pops forward.
