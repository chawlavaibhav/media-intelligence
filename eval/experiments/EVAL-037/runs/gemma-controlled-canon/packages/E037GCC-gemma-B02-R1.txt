FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The goal is to communicate aggressive, low-cost pricing (₹9 for images, ₹99 for video) for a high-end AI infrastructure product. The primary challenge is the "Price-Perception Paradox": low prices usually signal "cheap/discount," but the brand must signal "premium/industrial." The solution is to treat the pricing as a "technical specification" rather than a "sale offer," using the visual language of high-end fintech and architectural blueprints.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Architecture of Efficiency." Instead of a festive "sale" layout, the poster will use a rigid, Swiss-style typographic grid. The low prices will be presented as precise data points within a high-end technical framework. Festive elements will be reduced to a single, sophisticated accent color (Champagne Gold) and a minimalist geometric motif, ensuring the "serious infrastructure" vibe remains dominant.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRIMARY HOOK: The Pricing (₹9 / ₹99) — Presented as the core "spec" of the offering.
2. BRAND IDENTITY: Aight (The "Counter" for Indian AI).
3. VALUE PROP: "Production AI. Billed in Rupees." (Directly leveraging the website's "AI, billed right" positioning).
4. TRUST SIGNALS: "One domestic GST invoice. No forex. No surprises."
5. CALL TO ACTION: getaight.ai

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Deep Matte Charcoal (#1A1A1A) background; "Infrastructure White" (#F5F5F5) for primary text; "Champagne Gold" (#D4AF37) for currency symbols and festive accents.
- Typography: High-contrast Neo-Grotesque Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or Helvetica Now). 
    - Bold/Black weights for prices.
    - Medium/Regular weights for labels.
    - Light/Condensed weights for technical footnotes.
- Grid: A visible, thin-line architectural grid (0.5pt lines) in dark grey, dividing the poster into precise modules.
- Festive Element: A single, abstract geometric gold line forming a minimalist "Diya" or "Lotus" shape in the top-right corner, resembling a technical logo rather than an illustration.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Background: Solid deep charcoal.
2. Layer 1: Apply a subtle 8-column architectural grid in #2A2A2A.
3. Layer 2: Position the brand name "Aight" in the top-left module in white, small-caps, wide tracking.
4. Layer 3: Center-align the pricing blocks. 
    - Block A: "IMAGE GENERATION" (Light weight, white) $\rightarrow$ "₹9" (Extra Bold, Champagne Gold).
    - Block B: "VIDEO GENERATION" (Light weight, white) $\rightarrow$ "₹99" (Extra Bold, Champagne Gold).
5. Layer 4: Place the headline "Production AI. Billed in Rupees." beneath the prices in a medium weight, clean white.
6. Layer 5: Footer section separated by a thin gold line, containing "One domestic GST invoice. No forex." and the URL.
7. Layer 6: Add the minimalist gold geometric festive icon in the top-right.
