FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end visual for a sparkling mosambi drink that appeals to the "New India" urban professional (22–35). The image must pivot away from traditional "ethnic" markers and instead use a language of sophisticated minimalism—blending global premium beverage aesthetics with subtle, contemporary Indian materiality.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Architecture of Refreshment." Treat the product not as a soft drink, but as a design object. The composition will use high-contrast, architectural lighting and a palette of muted mineral tones to make the vibrant, natural yellow-green of the mosambi "pop" as the singular focal point.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero: The bottle (form, clarity, carbonation).
2. Refreshment: Fine condensation beads and sparkling bubbles.
3. Flavor Identity: The organic color of the drink and a precision-styled mosambi slice.
4. Brand Positioning: Sophisticated, urban, contemporary (conveyed through lighting and surface texture).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Charcoal grey, muted slate, and polished stone neutrals, contrasted against the vivid citrus yellow-green of the drink.
- Materials: Polished grey sandstone (Jodhpur stone style) for the base; heavy, clear flint glass for the bottle; matte-textured paper for the label.
- Lighting: Single-source, directional "morning gallery" light. Sharp, clean shadows and high-specular highlights on the glass to create depth and a "premium" feel.
- Composition: Centered but dynamic; 4:5 vertical crop with a low-angle perspective to give the bottle presence and authority.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Subject: A sleek, minimalist glass bottle filled with a pale, sparkling yellow-green liquid.
- Styling: 
    - The bottle should have a light, uniform mist of condensation.
    - A single, perfect, translucent slice of mosambi (sweet lime) leans against the base of the bottle.
    - The liquid must show active, tiny carbonation bubbles clinging to the inner glass.
- Surface: A slab of matte, polished dark grey sandstone with a subtle natural grain.
- Background: A soft-focus, neutral grey architectural wall, maintaining a clean, uncluttered space.
- Camera: Macro-lens feel, shallow depth of field focusing sharply on the label and the condensation beads.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Professional product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A premium minimalist glass bottle of sparkling mosambi drink as the hero. The liquid is a vibrant, translucent pale yellow-green with fine carbonation bubbles. The bottle is chilled with a delicate layer of condensation beads. It sits on a polished dark grey Jodhpur sandstone slab. A single, precision-cut slice of fresh mosambi fruit leans elegantly against the bottle. Lighting is sharp, directional sunlight from the side, creating high-contrast specular highlights on the glass and a deep, clean shadow. Background is a blurred, neutral charcoal architectural concrete wall. Hyper-realistic, 8k, sophisticated urban aesthetic, luxury beverage advertising, high-end materiality, muted mineral tones, vivid citrus accent.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid any ornamental patterns, mandalas, or vibrant multi-colored backgrounds to prevent "clichéd" Indian shorthand.
- Ensure the bottle is not surrounded by "splashes" or floating fruit, which often characterizes cheap soft drink ads.
- Maintain a strict muted palette for everything except the product to ensure the product remains the unmistakable hero.
