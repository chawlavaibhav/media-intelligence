FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
A 40-second cinematic scene (16:9) featuring two people at a café table in a tense, restrained confrontation.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The scene must convey a power shift caused by the discovery of a secret. Spatial clarity is paramount, requiring strict adherence to the 180-degree rule to ensure the viewer always knows the physical orientation of the characters. The "cinematic" quality is achieved through varied framing and a strategic insert rather than camera movement or "mechanical" (simple A-B) shot-reverse-shot patterns.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." The tension is carried not by the words, but by the proximity and the unseen "object" of betrayal. The visual progression moves from the shared space (two-shot) to the internal isolation (tight close-ups), punctuated by a tactile detail (insert) that anchors the conflict in reality.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Orientation (Who is where)
2. Emotional Subtext (Tension, restrained anger, guilt)
3. The Catalyst (The concealed item)
4. The Resolution (The final realization/judgment)

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Desaturated tones, cool shadows, with a single warm accent (e.g., the café lighting or the object).
- Lighting: Soft side-lighting to create depth and subtle shadows on faces, emphasizing the "hidden" nature of the secret.
- Composition: Strict 180-degree axis. Use of "dirty" frames (shoulders/hair in foreground) to maintain connection between subjects.
- Depth of Field: Shallow (f/2.8 or equivalent) to isolate characters from the café background.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Set: Quiet corner of a café, small circular table.
- Cast: Two actors capable of micro-expression acting.
- Camera: Static setups. No pans, tilts, or zooms.
- Pace: Slow, deliberate cuts. Allow silence to breathe.

GENERATION_PROMPTS

Shot 1 (0-6s): Medium Two-Shot.
Prompt: Cinematic medium shot, side profile of a man and woman sitting opposite each other at a small round marble café table. Natural soft side-lighting. 16:9 aspect ratio. Shallow depth of field, blurred café background. Static camera. Tense atmosphere, neutral expressions. 8k, photorealistic, film grain.

Shot 2 (6-13s): Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) on Person A.
Prompt: Cinematic over-the-shoulder shot, looking past the shoulder of Person B toward Person A. Person A is speaking with a calm, cold expression. Tight framing. Soft lighting on face, deep shadows in the background. Static camera. 16:9, photorealistic, film grain.

Shot 3 (13-20s): Tight Close-up on Person B.
Prompt: Cinematic tight close-up of Person B's face. Eyes reflecting guilt and restraint. Slight tension in the jaw. Soft side-lighting. Maintaining screen direction (facing toward Person A). Static camera. 16:9, photorealistic, high detail, film grain.

Shot 4 (20-25s): Extreme Close-up (Insert).
Prompt: Cinematic extreme close-up of a hand on a marble table slowly sliding a small, folded piece of paper/photograph toward the center. Soft morning light. Tactile texture of the paper. Static camera. 16:9, photorealistic, macro photography.

Shot 5 (25-32s): Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) on Person B.
Prompt: Cinematic over-the-shoulder shot, looking past the shoulder of Person A toward Person B. Person B is attempting to speak, expression pained but restrained. Tight framing. Static camera. 16:9, photorealistic, film grain.

Shot 6 (32-40s): Tight Close-up on Person A.
Prompt: Cinematic tight close-up of Person A's face. A long, silent beat of disappointment. Eyes fixed on Person B. Cold, soft lighting. Static camera. 16:9, photorealistic, high detail, film grain.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9.
- Shot Count: Exactly 6.
- Camera Movement: Zero (all static).
- Axis: Constant 180-degree alignment.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Ambient café noise (distant clinking, muffled chatter) lowered in volume to create an "acoustic vacuum" around the table. Dialogue is hushed, near-whisper.
- Edit: Hard cuts. No transitions. Pacing follows the emotional beats: Establishment -> Question -> Reaction -> Evidence -> Denial -> Finality.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid "crossing the line": Ensure Person A is always screen-right and Person B is always screen-left (or vice versa) across all shots to prevent spatial disorientation.
- Avoid movement: Explicitly prompt "static camera" to prevent AI-generated pans/zooms.
- Avoid shouting: Direct audio/performance cues to "restrained" and "hushed."
