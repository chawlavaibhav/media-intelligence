FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 premium promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a B2B promotional asset for aight's media API during the Indian festive season. The challenge is to communicate low price points (₹9/₹99) while maintaining the brand identity of a "serious AI infrastructure company." This requires avoiding retail aesthetics (bright colors, burst shapes, "SALE" language) in favor of a high-end, architectural, and minimalist design where the value is presented as a technical specification rather than a discount.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Infrastructure of Celebration."
The design will treat the festive element not as a theme, but as a lighting condition. Instead of using literal festive imagery (lamps, crackers), the poster will use a "Cinematic Dark Mode" aesthetic: a deep, matte obsidian background illuminated by a single, sophisticated gold-saffron light leak or geometric glow. This creates a "premium" atmosphere. The pricing is presented in a rigid, technical grid, framing the costs as "unit rates" for infrastructure, ensuring the low price feels like an efficiency gain rather than a budget clearance.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary Hook: "FESTIVE SCALING" (Positioned as a technical objective).
2. Key Rates: "IMAGE_GEN: ₹9" and "VIDEO_GEN: ₹99" (The largest typographic elements, presented as data points).
3. Brand Identity: Aight Logo (Clean, minimal placement).
4. Trust Signals: "Prepaid Rupees. Domestic GST. No Surprises." (Direct pull from aight's core value proposition to signal seriousness).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
*   Color Palette: Matte Obsidian (#0A0A0A), Deep Saffron Gold (#FFD700 / #C5A059) for lighting and accents, Stark White (#FFFFFF) for primary typography.
*   Typography: A heavy, architectural Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or Helvetica Now) for prices to convey stability; a monospaced font (e.g., JetBrains Mono) for the "GEN" labels to evoke API/Code environments.
*   Composition: An asymmetric Swiss-style grid. Large areas of negative space to evoke luxury. The text is aligned to a strict left-margin, with the prices acting as the visual anchor.
*   Lighting: A soft, atmospheric volumetric glow emanating from the top-right corner, casting a gold gradient across the obsidian surface, suggesting a festive "diya" or light source without showing the object itself.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Background: Generate a deep charcoal/black void with a sophisticated gold volumetric light leak from the upper right.
2. Type Layer 1: Place the "Aight" logo in the top left (small, white, high tracking).
3. Type Layer 2: Centered-left, bold headline "FESTIVE SCALING" in all-caps, white.
4. Type Layer 3 (The Rates): Create two distinct rows.
    - Row 1: `IMAGE_GEN` (Mono, Gold) → `₹9` (Bold Sans, White, Giant).
    - Row 2: `VIDEO_GEN` (Mono, Gold) → `₹99` (Bold Sans, White, Giant).
5. Type Layer 4: Footer text in a smaller, clean sans-serif: "Prepaid rupees. Domestic GST. No surprises."
6. Final Pass: Apply a subtle film grain and a slight chromatic aberration to the edges of the white text to give it a high-end digital render feel.
