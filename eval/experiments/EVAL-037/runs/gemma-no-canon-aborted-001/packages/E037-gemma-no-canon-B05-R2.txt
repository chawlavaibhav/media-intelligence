FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic short video scene (40 seconds, 16:9).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, emotionally restrained confrontation between two characters in a café. The focus is on subtext, spatial clarity, and atmospheric cinematography rather than action.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." Use a static, 180-degree axis to maintain perfect spatial orientation. Emphasize the emotional gap between the characters through shallow depth of field and a cold, rainy exterior contrasting with the warm but sterile café interior.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Relationship (Who is where).
2. Emotional Tension (Restrained anger vs. guarded guilt).
3. The Evidence (The physical catalyst for the conflict).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
* Color Palette: Desaturated teals and ambers; cool natural light from a window, warm tungsten interior highlights.
* Lighting: Side-lit "Rembrandt" lighting to create shadows on faces, emphasizing the "hidden" nature of the secret.
* Cinematography: Static camera, shallow depth of field (f/2.8), strictly adhering to the 180-degree rule.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
* Environment: Quiet corner of a modern café, rain-streaked window in background.
* Characters: Character A (Accuser - poised, cold); Character B (Concealer - anxious, avoiding eye contact).
* Prop: A single piece of paper/photograph on the table.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1: (5s) [Wide Shot] Cinematic side profile of two people sitting opposite each other at a small café table. Rain-streaked window in the background. Soft bokeh. Static camera. High-end film stock, 16:9.
Shot 2: (8s) [Over-the-shoulder Shot] Looking over Character B's shoulder at Character A. Character A is speaking calmly, eyes fixed on B, expression stern and disappointed. Static camera, shallow depth of field.
Shot 3: (8s) [Over-the-shoulder Shot] Looking over Character A's shoulder at Character B. Character B is looking down at the table, shifting slightly, expression of guilt and apprehension. Static camera, shallow depth of field.
Shot 4: (5s) [Extreme Close-up] Top-down view of a photograph on the wooden table. A hand slowly slides the photo toward the center of the table. Static camera.
Shot 5: (7s) [Close-up] Character A's face. Tight framing. A slow blink, jaw tight, maintaining intense eye contact. No shouting, just a heavy, silent gaze. Static camera.
Shot 6: (7s) [Medium Two-Shot] Side profile of both characters. They sit in heavy silence, the physical space between them feeling vast. Rain continues on the window. Static camera.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
* 180-degree axis: Character A always on the left side of the frame (or consistently oriented), Character B always on the right.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
* Audio: Ambient café noise (muffled espresso machine, distant chatter) mixed with the rhythmic sound of rain against glass. Dialogue is whispered/low-volume.
* Edit: Hard cuts between shots. No transitions. Pacing follows the duration listed in the prompts.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
* To prevent "mechanical" feel: Ensure shots emphasize emotional reactions (micro-expressions) rather than just "talking heads."
* To prevent spatial confusion: Maintain a strict line of action; never cross the axis between the two characters.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
* Two people opposite each other? Yes.
* Tense/restrained dialogue? Yes.
* Clear spatial relationship? Yes (Strict 180-degree rule).
* Exactly six shots? Yes.
* No elaborate camera movement? Yes (All static).
* Duration ~40 seconds? Yes (5+8+8+5+7+7 = 40s).
* Aspect ratio 16:9? Yes.
* No external websites? Yes.
