FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 premium promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The goal is to drive adoption of Aight's image and video generation APIs among Indian businesses during the festive peak. The critical tension is presenting aggressive, low-cost pricing (₹9/₹99) without degrading the brand's identity as a serious, enterprise-grade AI infrastructure provider. The design must pivot from "Discount Flyer" to "High-End Technical Specification."

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Architecture of Celebration." 
Instead of traditional festive clutter, the poster will treat the price points as precise technical specifications. We will use a "Swiss-Tech" aesthetic: a rigid typographic grid, a dark luxury palette, and a single, hyper-realistic 3D abstract object that blends a traditional Diya (lamp) with futuristic AI hardware.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRIMARY HOOK (Visual Dominance): The Prices (₹9 | ₹99).
2. PRODUCT CATEGORY: Image Generation | Video Generation.
3. VALUE PROPOSITION: Enterprise API. Domestic GST. Hard-capped billing.
4. BRAND IDENTITY: aight (The AI Production Engine).
5. CALL TO ACTION: getaight.ai

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Matte Charcoal (#121212), Burnished Gold (#C5A059), and Stark White (#FFFFFF).
- Typography: 
    - Prices: Ultra-bold, heavy Grotesque Sans-serif (e.g., Inter Tight or Helvetica Now) to feel architectural.
    - Technical Specs: Precision Monospace (e.g., JetBrains Mono) to signal "API/Code."
- Layout: Symmetrical vertical stack with heavy use of negative space.
- Imagery: A single center-piece 3D render.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Background: Deep matte charcoal with a subtle radial vignette to draw focus to the center.
2. Center-piece: An abstract 3D "Digital Diya"—a minimalist gold geometric vessel with a floating, glowing white plasma orb instead of a flame.
3. Typography Placement: 
    - Top: Small, centered monospace text: "FESTIVE SCALE EDITION // 2024"
    - Middle-Upper: The price "₹9" in massive gold typography, with "IMAGE GENERATION" in white monospace directly beneath it.
    - Middle-Lower: The price "₹99" in massive gold typography, with "VIDEO GENERATION" in white monospace directly beneath it.
    - Bottom: A thin gold horizontal divider line, followed by the brand lockup: "aight" (bold) and "The AI Production Engine" (light).
    - Footer: "getaight.ai" centered at the absolute bottom.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Image Generation Prompt:
"A high-end, hyper-realistic 3D studio render of a minimalist, futuristic Diya lamp. The lamp is made of brushed burnished gold geometric plates with sharp architectural edges. Instead of a flame, a small, intensely glowing white plasma sphere floats 2cm above the wick, casting a soft, sophisticated light. Background is a seamless, void-like matte charcoal grey with subtle studio lighting. 8k resolution, cinematic lighting, industrial design aesthetic, shot on 85mm lens, extremely clean composition, luxury tech product photography."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- All text and typography must be added via graphic design software (not generated by AI) to ensure precision, correct currency symbols (₹), and brand alignment.
- The gold horizontal divider line and the layout grid are deterministic.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
N/A (Static Poster).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- No "Sale" or "Offer" tags: These trigger the "discount flyer" association.
- No bright reds or yellows: These clash with the "serious infrastructure" objective.
- No generic festive stock imagery (fireworks, sweets): These dilute the premium AI positioning.
- Ensure the ₹ symbol is the correct Indian Rupee glyph, not a generic 'R'.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Premium promotional poster? Yes.
- Indian businesses/Festive season? Yes (via Digital Diya and pricing).
- Image ₹9 / Video ₹99? Yes.
- Infrastructure feel vs. Retail flyer? Yes (via Swiss-Tech aesthetic).
- Typography-driven hierarchy? Yes.
- aight brand info used? Yes (AI Production Engine/API focus).
- 4:5 Ratio? Yes.
