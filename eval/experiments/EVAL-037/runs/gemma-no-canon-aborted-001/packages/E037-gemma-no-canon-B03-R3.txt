FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 static advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Position a mosambi sparkling drink as a sophisticated, modern beverage for the urban Indian millennial/Gen Z demographic. The goal is to elevate a traditional flavor (mosambi) into a "lifestyle" product, distancing it from mass-market sodas and avoid cultural stereotypes.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Urban Luminance." A high-end product shot utilizing a minimalist, architectural setting. The product is placed on a raw concrete or honed limestone plinth, illuminated by sharp, natural sunlight creating a high-contrast interplay of light and shadow, evoking a sophisticated afternoon in a modern Indian metropolitan apartment or gallery.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero: The bottle/glass (central focus, sharp detail).
2. Sensory Appeal: Condensation and effervescence (immediate "refreshment" cue).
3. Ingredient Origin: A single, perfectly sliced piece of fresh mosambi (flavor identification).
4. Brand Positioning: Minimalist environment (sophistication/urbanity).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Pale citrus yellow, sage green, cool concrete grey, and crisp white.
- Lighting: Hard directional sunlight (mimicking a window) with soft fill to maintain detail in shadows.
- Composition: Center-weighted, low-angle shot to give the product stature.
- Texture: Contrast between the matte texture of concrete, the glossy glass, and the glistening water droplets.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Subject: A sleek, minimalist glass bottle containing a pale yellow sparkling liquid.
- Set: A neutral-toned, architectural concrete pedestal against a plain, off-white gallery wall.
- Props: A single, thin, translucent slice of mosambi leaning against the base of the bottle.
- Effect: Heavy condensation (beading) on the exterior of the glass. Visible carbonation bubbles rising within the liquid.
- Camera: Macro lens, f/2.8 for shallow depth of field, focusing sharply on the brand area/condensation.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Commercial product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A premium sleek glass bottle of sparkling mosambi drink standing on a minimalist raw concrete plinth. The liquid is a pale, luminous citrus yellow with visible effervescent bubbles. The bottle is covered in crisp, cold condensation droplets. A single, thin, perfectly circular slice of fresh sweet lime (mosambi) rests elegantly at the base. Background is a clean, soft-white architectural wall. Lighting is hard, direct afternoon sunlight creating a sharp, diagonal shadow across the scene. High contrast, sophisticated urban aesthetic, 8k resolution, shot on Phase One XF, macro detail, depth of field, neutral color grading, luxury beverage advertising.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid "Indian" clichés: No marigolds, no paisley, no bright orange/pink fabrics, no traditional motifs.
- Avoid "Cheap" cues: No plastic bottles, no overly saturated "fruit punch" colors, no splash-art chaos.
- Ensure the mosambi looks like a sweet lime (pale yellow/green), not a bright yellow lemon.
