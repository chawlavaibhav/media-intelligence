FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
9:16 Vertical UGC Video (approx. 25 seconds)

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-conversion, authentic-feeling creator video that positions a 2% salicylic acid serum as the solution for oily/blemish-prone skin. The goal is "relatable authority"—looking like a trusted recommendation from a peer rather than a corporate advertisement.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Mid-Day Reset": A relatable Indian woman shares her secret for managing oily skin and congestion, focusing on the "fragrance-free" and "pore-clearing" aspects as the primary purchase driver.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Hook: The relatable struggle of oily/blemish-prone skin.
2. Solution: Introduction of the 2% Salicylic Acid Serum.
3. Key Differentiator: Fragrance-free (safe for sensitive/reactive oily skin).
4. Action/Benefit: Clears pores and controls excess oil.
5. CTA: Direct prompt to purchase.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Lighting: Natural daylight (window light) to ensure skin texture is visible but flattering.
- Setting: Modern Indian home bathroom or vanity area; clean, minimalist background.
- Talent: Indian woman (25-35), natural skin (minimal makeup), relatable "girl-next-door" styling.
- Editing: Fast-paced jump cuts, native-style social media text overlays (TikTok/Instagram style), no cinematic color grading.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Frame: 9:16 vertical.
- Camera: High-quality smartphone (4K, 30fps).
- Audio: Direct-to-mic voiceover or clear on-camera dialogue with minimal ambient noise.
- Pace: Fast, rhythmic cuts to maintain engagement.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (0-3s): [Medium Close-up] Indian woman looking into the camera with a slightly frustrated expression, touching her T-zone/forehead. Natural lighting. Text overlay: "Oily skin struggles? 🙄"
Shot 2 (3-8s): [Close-up] Talent holding the serum bottle clearly toward the camera. She is smiling and speaking. Text overlay: "Fragrance-free 2% Salicylic Acid ✨"
Shot 3 (8-18s): [Close-up/Macro] Montage: 1. Dropper placing 3 drops of clear serum on the cheek. 2. Talent gently massaging the serum into the skin using upward motions. 3. Close-up of the serum absorbing into the skin. Text overlay: "Clears pores + controls oil"
Shot 4 (18-25s): [Medium Close-up] Talent with a fresh, matte glow, holding the product next to her face, nodding confidently to the camera. Text overlay: "Your skin will thank you. Shop below! 👇"

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Product Packaging: Minimalist white bottle with clean black sans-serif typography stating "2% Salicylic Acid Serum" and "Fragrance-Free".
- Text Overlays: Use "Classic" or "Typewriter" font styles common to IG/TikTok.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Natural speaking voice of the talent. Background music: Low-volume, upbeat, lo-fi hip hop beat.
- Edits: Jump cuts between shots to remove pauses.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid "clinical" white backgrounds to prevent looking like a TVC.
- Ensure the talent does not use words like "cure," "treat," or "heal" to avoid medical claims.
- Ensure the product label is legible and not blurred during the demo.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- 9:16 Ratio: Yes.
- Indian Woman: Yes.
- Fragrance-free 2% Salicylic Acid: Yes.
- No clinical stats/medical claims: Yes.
- Duration ~25s: Yes.
- Direct-to-camera/UGC feel: Yes.

KNOWLEDGE_AND_WEBSITE_USE
None.
