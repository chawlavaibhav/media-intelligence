FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-conversion promotional asset for Aight’s media API targeting Indian businesses. The goal is to communicate aggressive festive pricing (₹9/image, ₹99/video) without compromising the brand's positioning as a serious, enterprise-grade AI infrastructure provider. The design must avoid "retail flyer" tropes (no starbursts, no loud "SALE" banners) and instead use architectural typography and minimalist luxury to signal quality.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Architectural Festivity." The design treats the prices not as discounts, but as "infrastructure specifications." By placing high-precision typography against a dark, cinematic void with a single, hyper-realistic AI-generated festive object, the poster proves the product's capability while maintaining a premium, B2B aesthetic.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRIMARY: The Price Points (₹9 and ₹99) — The immediate hook.
2. SECONDARY: The Asset Type (Image and Video) — The value delivery.
3. TERTIARY: The Context ("Scale the Celebration" / Festive Infrastructure) — The reason for the offer.
4. QUATERNARY: Brand Trust (getaight.ai / GST Invoice / No Forex) — The professional reassurance.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Obsidian Black (#0A0A0A) background, Slate Grey accents, and a single "Champagne Gold" (#D4AF37) glow for festive warmth.
- Typography: Heavy contrast. Ultra-bold, wide-kerning sans-serif for the numbers; light, geometric, all-caps sans-serif for the labels.
- Imagery: A single, center-aligned, hyper-realistic 3D iridescent glass Diya or a geometric metallic lotus. It must look like a "benchmark" image—perfect lighting, ray-traced reflections—to showcase the API's power.
- Layout: Swiss-style grid. Symmetric and spacious.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. GENERATE: Create a high-fidelity 3D asset of a modern, abstract festive symbol in a dark void.
2. COMPOSITE: Place the asset in the center of a 4:5 canvas.
3. TYPOGRAPHY OVERLAY: 
    - Top Center: "SCALE THE CELEBRATION" (Small, tracked out).
    - Mid Center (above asset): "₹9 / IMAGE" (Large, bold).
    - Mid Center (below asset): "₹99 / VIDEO" (Large, bold).
    - Bottom: Brand logo and "getaight.ai" with the value prop "Prepaid. GST Invoice. No Forex."

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Image Generation Prompt:
"A hyper-realistic, cinematic 3D render of a minimalist, abstract Diya lamp made of iridescent frosted glass and polished gold chrome. The object is floating in a deep obsidian void. Single source of soft, warm golden light from above creating sharp ray-traced reflections and a subtle caustic glow on the surface. 8k resolution, octane render, architectural photography, extreme detail, luxury product aesthetic, minimal composition, deep blacks, 4:5 aspect ratio."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Text: All text must be added via graphic design software (not generated) to ensure perfect spelling and alignment.
- Layout: Precise alignment of the ₹9 and ₹99 elements to create a vertical axis of symmetry.
- Logo: Official Aight brand mark in white/silver.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
N/A (Static Image).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid: Red colors, "Offer" text, cluttered backgrounds, or generic stock photos of festivals.
- Ensure: The currency symbol (₹) is clearly legible and the spacing between the number and the label is wide enough to feel "designed" rather than "typed."

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Premium feel? Yes (Dark mode/Swiss design).
- Prices immediate? Yes (Central hierarchy).
- Festive season? Yes (Subtle gold glow/Diya asset).
- 4:5 Ratio? Yes.
- Only aight.ai info? Yes.

KNOWLEDGE_AND_WEBSITE_USE
- getaight.ai: Used for brand positioning ("Where Indian businesses buy AI"), operational USPs (GST Invoice, No Forex, Prepaid), and product categories (Image, Video).
