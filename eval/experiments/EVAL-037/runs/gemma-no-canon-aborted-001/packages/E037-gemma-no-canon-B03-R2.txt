FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end visual for a Mosambi sparkling drink that appeals to young urban Indians (22–35). The image must balance the traditional familiarity of sweet lime with a modern, sophisticated "premium" aesthetic, avoiding cultural stereotypes and the "mass-market soda" look.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Modernist Refreshment." The product is presented as a design object on a minimalist, architectural plinth. The focus is on the interplay of light, crisp textures (condensation), and a muted, sophisticated color palette that elevates the fruit from a street-side juice to a premium beverage.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero (The Bottle): Central focus, visual dominance.
2. Refreshment Cues: Hyper-realistic condensation and sparkling bubbles.
3. Ingredient Origin: A single, artfully placed slice of Mosambi.
4. Brand Vibe: Minimalist, urban, and upscale environment.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Sage green, muted citrus yellow, honed travertine beige, and crisp white.
- Lighting: Hard, direct sunlight creating sharp, high-contrast shadows (modernist architectural lighting).
- Texture: Matte stone vs. glossy glass and glistening water droplets.
- Composition: Center-weighted, slightly low angle to give the product stature.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Subject: A sleek, clear glass bottle with a minimalist label containing a pale yellow sparkling liquid.
- Setting: The bottle sits on a block of honed light-beige travertine stone against a clean, off-white matte wall.
- Detail: Intense condensation on the glass; a single, thin, translucent slice of Mosambi resting beside the base of the bottle.
- Style: High-end commercial product photography, 8k resolution, macro detail on water droplets.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
High-end commercial product photography of a premium sparkling Mosambi drink in a sleek, minimalist clear glass bottle. The bottle is the central hero, covered in crisp, glistening condensation droplets. The bottle sits on a modern, honed beige travertine stone plinth. Beside the bottle lies a single, perfectly sliced, translucent wedge of sweet lime (mosambi) with tiny water droplets on its surface. Background is a clean, minimalist off-white matte wall. Lighting is hard, direct bright sunlight creating sharp, architectural shadows. Color palette of sage green, pale citrus yellow, and warm beige. 8k resolution, shot on 85mm lens, f/8 for deep focus, hyper-realistic textures, sophisticated urban aesthetic, 4:5 aspect ratio.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Post-production: Color grade to ensure the yellow is "citrus-sophisticated" rather than "neon-soda."
- Framing: Strict 4:5 crop.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
N/A (Static Image)

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- No bright, saturated oranges or reds (avoids "cheap soda" look).
- No paisley, mandalas, or traditional Indian patterns (avoids clichés).
- No people/hands in shot (keeps focus on the product).
- No generic "juice" splashes (avoids low-budget advertising tropes).

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Premium 4:5 image? Yes.
- Mosambi & Sparkling water? Yes.
- Urban 22-35 target? Yes (via minimalist/architectural style).
- No clichéd shorthand? Yes.
- Product as hero? Yes.
- No celebrity/website? Yes.
