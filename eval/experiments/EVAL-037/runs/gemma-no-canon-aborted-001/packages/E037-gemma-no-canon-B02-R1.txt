FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 premium promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Promote Aight's media-generation API to Indian businesses during the festive season. The goal is to communicate aggressive pricing (₹9 for images, ₹99 for video) while maintaining the brand's identity as a serious, reliable AI infrastructure provider rather than a retail discount service.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Precision Festive." 
The design will eschew traditional, cluttered festive imagery in favor of "Architectural Minimalism." It will use a stark, high-contrast layout where the pricing acts as the primary visual anchor. The "festive" element will be represented not by a collage of symbols, but by a single, hyper-realistic 3D object rendered in a luxury material (brushed gold or frosted glass), bridging the gap between Indian tradition and cutting-edge AI infrastructure.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. HERO: The Prices (₹9 | ₹99) — Immediate, bold, undeniable.
2. CONTEXT: Image & Video Generation — Defining the utility.
3. VALUE PROPOSITION: "Prepaid Rupee Wallet. Domestic GST Invoice." — Establishing professional infrastructure.
4. BRANDING: Aight Logo — The seal of quality.
5. CALL TO ACTION: "The counter is open. getaight.ai" — Consistent with brand voice.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Deep Obsidian (#0A0A0A) background; Brushed Gold (#D4AF37) for accents/festive element; Stark White (#FFFFFF) for primary typography.
- Typography: Heavyweight Swiss-style Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or Helvetica Neue Bold) for pricing to convey stability and precision. Light/Regular weight for supporting text.
- Layout: A strict grid system with generous negative space. No gradients, no bevels, no "burst" stickers.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Base: Deep charcoal/black matte background.
2. Hero Element: A single, center-aligned, hyper-realistic 3D render of a traditional Diya or a Mandala fragment, but reimagined as a precision-engineered piece of gold jewelry or hardware.
3. Type Overlay: 
    - Top-center: Aight logo (White).
    - Mid-section: Two columns of text. 
        - Left: "IMAGE" (small, white) / "₹9" (huge, gold).
        - Right: "VIDEO" (small, white) / "₹99" (huge, gold).
    - Bottom-center: A horizontal line separating the prices from the infrastructure details.
    - Footer: "Prepaid Rupee Wallet • Domestic GST Invoice • Zero Surprise Bills" (small, white, tracked out).
    - Absolute Bottom: "getaight.ai"
