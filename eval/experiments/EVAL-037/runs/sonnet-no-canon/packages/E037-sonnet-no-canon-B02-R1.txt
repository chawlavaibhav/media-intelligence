FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One (1) premium festive-season promotional poster, static image, 4:5 aspect ratio (deliver at 4000×5000 px for print/digital re-use), single final composited file (no alternates).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The poster must make two exact numbers — ₹9 (image) and ₹99 (video) — instantly legible and unambiguous, because this is the entire commercial hook. At the same time it must read as an AI infrastructure product from a serious B2B platform (per getaight.ai: prepaid rupee wallet, hard caps, one domestic GST invoice, GPT/Claude/Gemini + India-first voice, founder-level support) — not a festive-sale flyer with starbursts and confetti. Festivity should be atmospheric and premium (light, warmth, prosperity), never discount-signage. Typography and hierarchy carry the message; imagery is restrained and supportive.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Festive Ledger." Aight's own brand language is built around a counter/invoice metaphor — "prepaid rupees," "one domestic GST invoice," "the merchant's job." The poster visualises the two prices as two clean ledger line-items on a dark, gold-foil invoice card, gently lit by a single diya — as if this is literally the line on the bill Indian businesses will actually pay this festive season. This ties the seasonal cue directly to the brand's real proof point (transparent, single, domestic billing) rather than to generic Diwali decoration.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Hero (largest, top-weighted visually within the ledger card): the two price lines —
   "IMAGE  ₹9" / "VIDEO  ₹99"
2. Brand lockup: "aight ." — small, top of frame, quietly confident
3. Positioning line (site-sourced): "Where Indian businesses buy AI."
4. Seasonal cue (subordinate, one line): "This festive season."
5. Proof/credibility strip (small, below ledger): "GPT · Claude · Gemini · India-first voice — prepaid rupees, one GST invoice."
6. CTA (bottom, clear but modest): "Talk to us · getaight.ai"

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Format: vertical 4:5, generous margins, strict grid — nothing touches edges.
- Palette: near-black charcoal ground (#121110); brass/gold foil (#C9A227) for rules, logo, numerals accents; warm amber diya glow (#FF8A3D at low opacity) as the only "festive colour"; ivory/parchment (#EDE6D8) for body copy. No red/orange discount-tag colours, no confetti, no fireworks, no percentage-off badges, no starbursts.
- Typography: two-family system.
  - Numerals/prices: a refined serif or slab with tabular oldstyle figures, set very large, letter-spaced tightly, treated like ledger/invoice figures (echoes "invoice line," not "sale tag").
  - Labels, tagline, CTA: a clean geometric/grotesk sans, small size, wide tracking, matching the minimal tech-site tone.
- Composition:
  - Top 15%: logotype "aight ." + tagline, small, centered or left-aligned.
  - Middle 55%: the ledger card — a thin gold-rule rectangle on charcoal, two rows separated by a hairline gold rule: row 1 "IMAGE — ₹9", row 2 "VIDEO — ₹99", each with a tiny qualifier beneath ("per generation"). One small diya glow motif sits quietly at one corner of the card, its light falling across the gold rule.
  - Lower 20%: proof strip + seasonal line, both small, then CTA at the very bottom, set apart by whitespace.
  - Bottom 10%: clear breathing margin, no clutter.
- Mood reference: annual-report cover meets premium fintech meets a single lit diya on a dark wood counter at night — restrained luxury, not a bazaar poster.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Two-layer build for accuracy and premium finish:
1. AI-generated atmospheric base layer: dark textured background (fine linen/paper-grain charcoal), a single soft diya glow, faint brass hairline rule graphics, deep vignette — generated with an explicit reserved negative-space grid so no AI-rendered text appears anywhere in this layer.
2. Deterministic typography overlay (see next section): logo, tagline, seasonal line, both price lines, qualifiers, proof strip and CTA are set as real vector/type layers and composited on top, guaranteeing ₹9 and ₹99 render pixel-perfect, correctly spelled, and consistently kerned — critical since generative models frequently corrupt currency symbols and numerals.
Final export: flatten to single 4:5 image, sRGB, print-safe contrast checked (gold-on-charcoal minimum contrast verified for both digital and print reproduction).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Risk: AI renders garbled/incorrect currency symbols or numerals → mitigated by keeping all text out of the generative layer and applying exact vector type afterward.
- Risk: poster reads as discount retail flyer → mitigated by strict palette lock (no red/orange sale colors, no starbursts/badges/percent-off graphics), and by anchoring festivity to a single diya + warm glow rather than decorative clutter.
- Risk: price hierarchy lost among festive imagery → mitigated by making the ledger card and its two numerals the single largest visual element on the canvas; all festive/brand elements are smaller and quieter.
- Risk: brand inaccuracy → all claims (tagline, models, prepaid/GST language) sourced verbatim/paraphrased only from the getaight.ai snapshot, nothing invented.
- Risk: illegibility at thumbnail size → verified via large numeral scale, strong gold/charcoal contrast, generous negative space around the ledger card.
