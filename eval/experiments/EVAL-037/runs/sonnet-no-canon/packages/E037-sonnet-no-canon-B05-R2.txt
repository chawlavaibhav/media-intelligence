FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
A single continuous short film scene, 16:9, ~40 seconds, six shots, depicting a restrained confrontation between two people at a café table after one discovers the other concealed something important. No shouting; tension carried through stillness, silence, and controlled dialogue. No external site or reference material used.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer wants an emotionally charged but quiet two-hander that reads as directed cinema, not mechanical shot/reverse-shot coverage. Key non-negotiables: exactly six shots, ~40s runtime, 16:9, minimal camera movement, and unbroken spatial clarity (viewer always knows who is where relative to the other). "No external website" is treated as a constraint that no outside sourcing/reference is required — this is a self-contained fictional scene needing no research.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
A worn photograph on the table is the physical proof of what was hidden. The scene never explains the backstory in dialogue — it lets the object, the stillness, and two controlled faces carry the meaning. Classic scene grammar (master → OTS/singles → insert → return to master) is used deliberately, not as generic coverage, so the geography and escalation feel authored rather than assembled.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial/relational fact first: two people, a table, an object between them (the master shot).
2. Evidence: the concealed item is real and undeniable (insert shot).
3. Verbal confrontation: brief, restrained exchange — accusation, deflection, refusal to excuse.
4. Emotional residue: silence, held gaze, no resolution (final shot).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 throughout.
- Fixed 180-degree line: Person A (the discoverer) is always frame-left in wides/OTS; Person B (the concealer) is always frame-right. This never flips.
- Palette: warm, slightly desaturated café interior — cream walls, brass fixtures, soft afternoon window light from camera-left, low contrast, muted browns/ambers.
- Lensing: master shot ~35mm (deeper focus, both bodies + table + object legible); singles/OTS ~50–85mm (shallow depth, isolating each face); insert macro-equivalent (~90mm, very shallow, object tack-sharp).
- Camera movement: locked-off tripod for all shots except one almost imperceptible slow push-in on the final shot (under 5% frame-scale change) to add a last beat of pressure without calling attention to itself. No pans, no handheld, no whip cuts.
- Continuity anchors: same wardrobe, hairstyle, table setting (two coffee cups, one folded napkin, the photograph), and window light direction across all six shots so the eye never has to re-orient.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Six shots, total ≈40s, cut on action/breath rather than on a rhythm-driven beat.

1. MASTER / ESTABLISHING — 8s
2. MEDIUM TWO-SHOT, OTS favoring Person A (B's shoulder in foreground-right, A visible frame-left) — 6s
3. SINGLE, Person B — 6s
4. SINGLE, Person A — 6s
5. INSERT, the photograph on the table — 4s
6. RETURN TO WIDE/MEDIUM TWO-SHOT, held, slow push-in — 10s

Dialogue distribution (quiet, restrained, no raised voices, long micro-pauses):
- Shot 2: A: "How long have you had this."
- Shot 3: B: (silence, breath, looks down) then quietly: "It wasn't the right moment."
- Shot 4: A: "You knew. This whole time." … "There's no right moment for this."
- Shot 5: no dialogue — silence, ambient only.
- Shot 6: no dialogue — B looks down at the table, A holds the stare, scene ends on held silence.

"A small café table by a window, warm muted afternoon light from the left, two coffee cups, a folded napkin, a worn photograph face-down near the center. Two adults seated opposite each other, one frame-left (mid-30s, dark cardigan, tense composed posture), one frame-right (mid-30s, pale shirt, guarded posture). Naturalistic, cinematic color grade, shallow ambient café background, soft bokeh."

"Static wide shot, 35mm equivalent, café interior, warm afternoon window light from camera-left. DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- A single locked reference image (the anchor prompt above) is generated first and used to seed/condition all six shots so wardrobe, faces, table props, and light direction remain consistent.
- The photograph prop and its screen position (center table, slightly toward B's side, face-down) is fixed as a non-negotiable continuity asset across shots 1, 2, 5, 6.
- A uniform color-grade LUT (warm/muted, low contrast) is applied in post across all six generated clips to guarantee visual consistency regardless of per-shot generation variance.
- Exact shot count (6) and per-shot durations are treated as fixed editorial timecodes, not generative suggestions.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Ambient bed: low café room tone (distant murmur, occasional cup clink) throughout, mixed low so dialogue reads as intimate/near.
- Score: sparse, minor-key solo piano or cello, entering subtly under shot 3, staying under threshold of "score," never swelling — restraint mirrors the performances.
- Dialogue mix: close, dry, slightly intimate perspective; no reverb suggesting the wider room.
- Cuts: on breath/blink transitions rather than on dialogue peaks, per shot order 1→2→3→4→5→6. Shot 6 held longest deliberately to let silence do the final work.
- No non-diegetic sound effects, no music sting at the end — cut to black on ambient tone only.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Risk: crossing the 180-degree line → Mitigation: A is explicitly locked frame-left, B frame-right in every prompt, including the OTS and inserts, with no shot description that could invert this.
- Risk: character/appearance drift between generated shots → Mitigation: single locked reference anchor image conditions all six shots; wardrobe/hair described identically in every prompt.
- Risk: dialogue timing not fitting the specified duration → Mitigation: lines are short, single-beat, with built-in pause instructions matching the 6–8s shot lengths; editor trims silence, not dialogue.
- Risk: photograph prop inconsistency (position/appearance changes) → Mitigation: prop position and description fixed identically across shots 1, 2, 5, 6.
- Risk: shots reading as "shouted" or over-performed → Mitigation: every dialogue prompt explicitly specifies quiet, controlled, restrained delivery and prohibits raised volume.
- Risk: exceeding six shots or adding camera flourishes during execution → Mitigation: shot count and "no camera movement except single slow push in shot 6" are stated as hard constraints in the recipe, not suggestions.
