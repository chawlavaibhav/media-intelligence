FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One vertical (9:16) video advertisement, ~30 seconds, for RentOK, targeting owners/operators of hostels and PGs (paying-guest accommodations) across India. Single edit, no alternate cuts.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
RentOK's own site frames its value as operational relief for PG/hostel/flat owners: "Save time. Work less. Earn more," with concrete mechanisms — automatic rent/due collection ("5 days max"), WhatsApp-based reminders sent from the owner's own number, digital KYC, complaint management, floor-plan/room status, a "24/7 Digital Accountant," and trust proof (15,000+ properties, 4.9★/675 reviews, ₹100 Cr+ collected). The customer wants this pain made "painfully recognisable" before the product resolves it, executed with contemporary, credible, Indian craft — not a translated Silicon Valley SaaS explainer. That means: real PG/hostel textures (steel almirahs, ledger registers, common corridors, tiffins, ceiling fans), Hinglish speech and on-screen text, WhatsApp as a literal visual motif (since RentOK's own differentiator is WhatsApp-native operations), and an unglamorous, slightly documentary tone rather than glossy corporate motion-graphics.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Chaos → Control," told through one owner's ordinary evening. The ad opens inside the exact operational mess the brief describes — a buzzing phone, a torn ledger, a tenant dodging rent, a WhatsApp thread nobody can follow — escalating until it's overwhelming. A single deliberate tap on a phone becomes the hinge: the same owner, same room, same evening, but now the chaos has been replaced by one calm screen doing the work. The ad never leaves the PG; it never becomes a boardroom or a stock-photo office. RentOK is positioned as the thing that was missing from the room all along, not an outside system imposed on it.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary (felt, 0–14s): Running a PG/hostel on WhatsApp + notebooks + spreadsheets is exhausting and leaky — rent-chasing, KYC paperwork, unresolved complaints, "jhikjhik."
2. Primary (resolved, 15–26s): RentOK turns that into one app — automatic rent collection, WhatsApp reminders sent from the owner's own number, one-tap digital KYC, tracked complaints, real-time room status.
3. Proof (24–29s): 15,000+ property owners, 4.9★ rating, ₹100 Cr+ collected — stated verbatim from RentOK's own site, not invented.
4. Call to action (28–30s): Free demo / RentOK logo lock-up.
Everything above must also read with sound off, since feed video is frequently watched muted — captions and key numbers are burned in, not audio-only.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Setting: a modest, real-feeling Indian PG/hostel — corridor with numbered rooms, common area with steel cots/lockers, small reception table with a thick paper register, ceiling fan, tube light. Avoid polished startup-office aesthetics entirely.
- Cast: one PG/hostel owner, Indian, 35–50, in ordinary daily wear (kurta or half-sleeve shirt), not a suited executive. One or two tenants glimpsed, naturalistic, not model-styled.
- Palette: Act 1 (chaos) — dim, slightly warm/cluttered, handheld, desaturated; Act 2 (resolution) — same location, same light source, but tidier and calmer, slightly higher contrast and brighter whites on the phone screen, so the shift is felt physically, not just narrated.
- Typography/on-screen text: Hinglish colloquial phrasing echoing RentOK's own site voice (e.g., "jhikjhik," "2026 mein bhi register?"), bold, high-contrast captions burned in throughout — because a vertical feed ad is frequently viewed muted and small, on-screen text must carry the message independently of audio (this also protects the brand statistics, which must appear as legible text, not only spoken).
- Brand device: RentOK's own product screens (rent-reminder-via-WhatsApp flow, KYC approval, complaint tracker, floor-plan view) appear as clean, legible UI inserts — these are composited in post from real product captures/motion graphics, not left to a generative model to hallucinate (see DETERMINISTIC section).
- Motion grammar: fast, slightly jittery handheld cuts in the chaos section (0.5–1.5s per shot) versus slower, steadier, single-take-feeling shots in the resolution section — pace itself dramatizes "control."

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Generate/shoot the six live-action beats below as a photoreal, vertical, continuous-feeling sequence (same actor, same location, matched wardrobe/lighting across chaos and calm halves).
2. Separately produce or record the RentOK app-UI inserts and all on-screen text/captions/stat cards as post-production overlays (motion graphics or real screen capture) — do not rely on the generative model to render legible app UI, Devanagari/Hinglish text, or WhatsApp iconography.
3. Edit: chaos montage (0:00–0:14) → hinge cut on tap (0:14–0:16) → calm/resolution montage with UI overlays (0:16–0:26) → proof stats + logo + CTA card (0:26–0:30).
4. Grade: desaturated/warm for chaos, clean/brighter-whites for resolution, consistent skin tones throughout so the cut reads as "same day, same person," not two different ads stitched together.
5. Sound design and burned-in captions applied in the same edit pass, timed to each cut (see AUDIO_AND_EDIT).

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot order, purpose, and approximate duration below; each is an executable prompt for a photorealistic, vertical (9:16) video generation model. Character and location descriptions are repeated verbatim across shots to preserve continuity.

SHOT 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:02, ~2s)
Purpose: seize attention in the first two seconds with instantly recognisable chaos.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, extreme close-up on a smartphone screen lying on a cluttered wooden desk in a modest Indian hostel reception area. The screen is flooded with overlapping WhatsApp notification pop-ups in Hindi-English mixed text, phone buzzing and vibrating slightly on the desk, warm dim tube-light ambience, shallow depth of field, handheld micro-shake, fast documentary realism, no text rendering required (captions added in post)."

SHOT 2 — PROBLEM: THE LEDGER (0:02–0:05, ~3s)
Purpose: establish the owner and the analogue-chaos of manual rent tracking.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, medium shot of an Indian PG/hostel owner, male, mid-40s, tired expression, wearing a plain half-sleeve shirt, sitting at a small reception desk piled with a thick paper rent register, loose photocopied ID papers, and cash notes. He flips pages of the register searching for an entry while his phone rings beside him. Modest Indian hostel interior, ceiling fan visible, warm practical lighting, handheld camera, realistic skin tones and textures."

SHOT 3 — PROBLEM: CHASING RENT (0:05–0:09, ~4s)
Purpose: dramatize the "running after tenants" pain point explicitly named in the brief.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, same PG/hostel owner from prior shot walking down a narrow hostel corridor with numbered room doors, knocking on a door. A young tenant opens it, shrugs casually and says something dismissive, closing the door again. Owner's shoulders sag with frustration. Real Indian hostel corridor with steel lockers and drying clothes visible, natural daylight through a corridor window, fast-paced handheld camera, documentary realism."

SHOT 4 — PROBLEM: PEAK OVERWHELM (0:09–0:13, ~4s)
Purpose: rapid montage compressing multiple pain points (KYC papers, complaints, move-out disputes) into a single overwhelmed beat.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, fast rapid-cut montage (four quick flashes, roughly half a second each) inside a modest Indian PG: (1) loose paper KYC/ID forms scattering off a desk, (2) a handwritten complaint slip pinned crookedly to a corkboard among other curling papers, (3) two people gesturing in a mild dispute over a room deposit near a cupboard, (4) the same tired PG owner from earlier rubbing his temples at the same cluttered desk, phone still buzzing. Warm, slightly desaturated grade, handheld urgency throughout."

SHOT 5 — HINGE / TRANSITION (0:13–0:16, ~3s)
Purpose: the turning point; deliberate calm action replaces reactive chaos.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, close-up on the same PG owner's hand deliberately and calmly tapping his phone screen once, in the same reception desk setting as before but now slightly tidied. The ambient sound of buzzing notifications should feel like it stops (handled in post-audio). Lighting shifts subtly brighter and cleaner than the preceding chaos shots. Slow, steady camera move, minimal shake, calm deliberate pacing, same actor, same wardrobe, same location for continuity."

SHOT 6 — RESOLUTION: CALM OWNER (0:16–0:20, ~4s)
Purpose: show the emotional payoff — the same person, same place, now unburdened.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, medium shot of the same Indian PG/hostel owner now relaxed, sitting at the same reception desk which is visibly tidier, holding a cup of chai in one hand and his phone in the other, calm relaxed posture, faint content smile, warm natural light, steady camera, same wardrobe and setting as prior shots for visual continuity, no UI or text rendering required (app screen composited in post)."

SHOT 7 — RESOLUTION: SYSTEM AT WORK MONTAGE (0:20–0:25, ~5s)
Purpose: quick, confident beats showing the owner using the (post-composited) product functions with ease.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, three-part quick montage of the same relaxed PG owner: (1) glancing at his phone with a satisfied nod, (2) tapping the phone screen once decisively while seated in the common area near steel cots, (3) walking calmly down the same hostel corridor from earlier, now relaxed, greeting a tenant with a casual smile instead of confrontation. Warm-to-neutral clean grade, steady handheld, confident unhurried pacing, same actor and locations throughout, screens left simple/blank for post-production UI overlay."

SHOT 8 — BRAND CLOSE (0:25–0:30, ~5s)
Purpose: brand lock-up, proof stats, and call to action.
Prompt: "Vertical 9:16 photorealistic video, the same PG owner sitting calmly at the tidied reception desk, looking up toward camera with a warm, confident, unforced smile, holding his phone loosely, soft natural light, steady static-feeling camera, same wardrobe/location for continuity, screen and surrounding space left clean and uncluttered for post-production logo, stat cards, and call-to-action text overlay."

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Feature-accuracy risk: every claim in VO/captions is restricted to what RentOK's own site states (automatic rent/due collection "5 days max," WhatsApp reminders from the owner's own number, digital KYC, complaint management, floor plan, 24/7 digital accountant, 15,000+ properties, 4.9★/675 reviews, ₹100 Cr+ collection). Nothing is invented.
- Generic-SaaS-look risk: explicitly bans polished office/boardroom/stock-photo aesthetics; all footage is grounded in real PG/hostel textures and Hinglish speech per the brief's "not a generic Silicon Valley explainer" instruction.
- Muted-viewing risk: message and stats are carried by burned-in text, not audio alone.
- UI/text rendering risk: no reliance on the generative model for legible app UI or brand wordmarks — these are composited from real assets.
- Overload risk: limited to three product beats (rent collection, KYC, complaints) rather than listing all nine site features, keeping the 30-second ad legible rather than a feature-dump.
- Continuity risk: identical actor, wardrobe, and location descriptions are repeated verbatim across all shot prompts to prevent a generative model from drifting the character or setting between chaos and resolution halves.
- Representation risk: PG owner and tenants are depicted as ordinary, dignified, non-stereotyped individuals in a real domestic-commercial setting, avoiding caricature.
