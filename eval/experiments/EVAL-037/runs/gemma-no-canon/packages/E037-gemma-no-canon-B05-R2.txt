FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic video scene (40 seconds, 16:9).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, restrained confrontation between two individuals at a café. The visual storytelling must prioritize spatial orientation and psychological pressure over camera movement, using a minimalist six-shot structure to convey betrayal and discovery.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Weight of Silence." Use static, meticulously composed frames and a cold color palette to mirror the emotional distance between the characters, focusing on micro-expressions rather than overt action.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Relationship (Establishing who is where).
2. The Accusation (The catalyst).
3. The Reaction (The emotional weight).
4. The Evidence (The object of concealment).
5. The Disappointment (The emotional peak).
6. The Stalemate (The unresolved tension).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Muted teals, slate greys, and warm amber highlights from café lamps.
- Lighting: Soft side-lighting from a large window to create depth and shadow on faces.
- Composition: Strict adherence to the 180-degree rule. Use of Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) shots to lock the spatial axis.
- Camera: Static tripod shots. No pans, tilts, or zooms.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot Count: 6
- Average Shot Duration: 6.6 seconds.
- Frame Rate: 24fps for cinematic motion blur.
- Editing: Slow, rhythmic cuts. No flashy transitions.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (5s): [Wide Shot] A quiet, modern café with minimalist decor. A man and a woman sit opposite each other at a small round marble table. Soft window light. Cinematic lighting, 16:9, static shot.
Shot 2 (7s): [OTS Shot - Looking at Person A] Over the shoulder of Person B. Person A has a cold, steady gaze, lips slightly parted as if speaking calmly. Tense atmosphere, shallow depth of field, 16:9, static shot.
Shot 3 (7s): [OTS Shot - Looking at Person B] Over the shoulder of Person A. Person B is frozen, eyes widening slightly, avoiding direct eye contact. Subtle nervousness. Shallow depth of field, 16:9, static shot.
Shot 4 (6s): [Close-up/Insert] A hand slowly slides a crumpled piece of paper across the white marble tabletop. High detail on texture, soft bokeh background, 16:9, static shot.
Shot 5 (8s): [Close-up] Person A’s face. A slight, slow blink and a subtle shake of the head. Expression of profound disappointment. Cinematic skin textures, moody lighting, 16:9, static shot.
Shot 6 (7s): [Medium Two-shot] Side profile of both characters sitting opposite each other. A wide gap of silence between them. The background café blur. Symmetric composition, 16:9, static shot.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (Hard constraint).
- Shot Count: 6 (Hard constraint).
- Movement: Zero camera movement (Static).

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Sound Design: Ambient café noise (distant clinking of cups, low chatter) that slowly fades into a ringing silence by Shot 6.
- Dialogue: Low-volume, whispered delivery. No shouting.
- Music: A single, sustained low-frequency synth drone that increases slightly in volume.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Spatial Drift: To prevent the viewer from losing orientation, Shot 1 establishes the axis, and Shots 2 and 3 use OTS framing to maintain that axis. Shot 6 confirms the position.
- Pacing: The 40-second duration is strictly divided to avoid "mechanical" feeling; the duration of the close-ups (Shot 5) allows the emotion to land.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Six shots? Yes.
- Opposite each other? Yes.
- Tense/restrained? Yes.
- Spatial clarity? Yes.
- No elaborate movement? Yes.
- Duration ~40s? Yes.
- Aspect Ratio 16:9? Yes.
- No external website? Yes.
