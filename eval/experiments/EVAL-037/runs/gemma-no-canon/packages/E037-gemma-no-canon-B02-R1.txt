FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 premium promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end B2B advertisement for aight's media-generation API. The goal is to communicate a low-cost festive offer (₹9/image, ₹99/video) while reinforcing a brand image of "Serious AI Infrastructure." The tension between "discount price" and "premium product" is resolved by using architectural typography and an abstract, generative visual rather than retail-style sales graphics.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Festive Grid." The design treats the pricing not as a "sale" but as a "unit cost" within a high-performance system. The visual is a sophisticated blend of Indian festive geometry (Mandala-inspired) and generative AI art (fluid, digital, holographic), signifying that traditional creativity is now powered by industrial-grade infrastructure.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary Focal Point: The pricing (₹9 | ₹99) in oversized, bold typography.
2. Secondary Focal Point: The categories (Image Generation | Video Generation).
3. Tertiary: The brand identity (aight logo) and the festive context ("Scaling Festive Creativity").
4. Footnote: The technical value prop ("API access. Domestic GST. Prepaid Rupee Wallet.").

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Obsidian Black background, Champagne Gold accents, and stark White text.
- Typography: Heavyweight Swiss-style Sans-Serif for numbers (Impact/Inter Bold) and a clean Monospaced font for technical details to evoke "API/Code."
- Layout: A strict grid-based composition. Use of white space (dark space) to create a luxury feel.
- Imagery: A centered, abstract 3D generative object—a holographic, shimmering sphere or torus that subtly forms a geometric Mandala pattern, representing the intersection of AI and Indian culture.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Background: Solid #0A0A0A (Obsidian).
2. Centerpiece: Generate an abstract 3D render of a generative gold silk-like structure forming a complex symmetric mandala.
3. Typography Layer:
    - Top center: "Scaling Festive Creativity" (Small, tracked-out uppercase, Gold).
    - Middle section: Two distinct columns. 
        - Left: "IMAGE" (Mono) / "₹9" (Bold Sans)
        - Right: "VIDEO" (Mono) / "₹99" (Bold Sans)
    - Bottom section: "aight" logo centered.
    - Footer: Small print regarding GST and API access in a mono-font.
