FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Convert Indian B2B clients during the festive season by leveraging aggressive pricing (₹9/₹99) while maintaining a "serious AI infrastructure" brand image. The design must avoid "discount retail" tropes and instead use the aesthetic of a high-end technology firm to signal reliability and scale.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Digital Opulence." The visual will feature a single, hyper-realistic 3D object—a traditional Indian Diya (oil lamp)—rendered as a "digital twin." One half of the lamp is polished traditional brass; the other half dissolves into a precise, glowing gold holographic wireframe/voxel grid. This serves as a direct product demonstration of the API's generative capabilities while subtly nodding to the festive season.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRIMARY HOOK: The Price Points (₹9 / ₹99) - Largest elements, centered or logically split.
2. PRODUCT CATEGORY: Image Generation / Video Generation - Directly linked to the prices.
3. BRAND IDENTITY: aight (logo/wordmark) - Positioned as the authoritative provider.
4. CONTEXTUAL TAGLINE: "Festive Infrastructure for Indian Businesses."
5. UTILITY PROPOSITION: "Prepaid Rupees. Domestic GST. Production Ready."

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Obsidian Black background, Champagne Gold accents (for the wireframe and key text), and Stark White (for supporting copy).
- Typography: High-contrast Swiss-style layout. Heavy weight sans-serif for prices; light/medium weight for descriptors.
- Composition: Centered focal point (the Diya) with a rigid, mathematical grid for the typography to evoke "infrastructure."

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Generate the hero asset (the hybrid Diya) using the provided generative prompt.
2. Post-process the image to ensure a deep black background for seamless integration.
3. Overlay typography using a strict grid system:
   - Top: aight wordmark (Small, centered).
   - Middle: The hybrid Diya asset.
   - Lower Middle: Large-scale pricing: "₹9" (Left) | "₹99" (Right).
   - Under Prices: "Image Generation" | "Video Generation".
   - Bottom: "Festive Infrastructure for Indian Businesses" followed by the utility proposition in a smaller, clean font.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Image Prompt:
"A hyper-realistic, macro 3D render of a single traditional Indian Diya lamp centered on a pure obsidian black background. The lamp is split vertically: the left side is crafted from polished, antique brushed brass with a soft warm flame; the right side is a glowing, translucent champagne-gold holographic wireframe consisting of precise digital voxels and geometric nodes. Cinematic studio lighting, 8k resolution, octane render, industrial design aesthetic, sharp focus, minimal composition, metallic textures, high contrast between the organic flame and the digital grid."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Logo: aight official wordmark.
- Typography: Inter or Helvetica Now (Bold for pricing, Regular for body).
- Layout: A structured 2-column grid for the pricing section.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
N/A (Static Poster).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- No "Sale" or "Offer" badges.
- No generic festive patterns (mandala, sparkles, confetti).
- No bright red or yellow background colors.
- Avoid "stock photo" looks; the asset must look like a high-end 3D render.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- One 4:5 poster? Yes.
- Festive season context? Yes (Diya motif).
- Indian businesses? Yes (Rupee symbol, GST mention).
- Prices ₹9 and ₹99 clear? Yes.
- Serious AI infra vibe? Yes (Obsidian/Gold palette, Grid layout).
- Only used getaight.ai? Yes.

KNOWLEDGE_AND_WEBSITE_USE
- getaight.ai: Used for brand positioning ("Where Indian businesses buy AI") and utility markers (Prepaid rupees, Domestic GST).
