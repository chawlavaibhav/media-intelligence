FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One 4:5 promotional poster.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Promote aight's image and video generation pricing (₹9 / ₹99) to Indian businesses during the festive season. The design must balance "festive appeal" with "enterprise infrastructure" credibility, avoiding the look of a budget retail sale by relying on high-end typography and a minimalist layout.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Technical Celebration." The poster uses a high-contrast, dark-mode aesthetic (infrastructure vibe) punctuated by a single, hyper-premium AI-generated visual that blends traditional Indian festive symbols with futuristic material science (glass, gold, liquid metal). Pricing is presented as a technical specification rather than a discount, treating the cost as a "unit price" for a high-performance tool.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. PRIMARY (The Offer): Image ₹9 | Video ₹99 (Largest visual weight).
2. SECONDARY (The Context): "Festive Scale for Indian Enterprises."
3. TERTIARY (Brand Trust): aight logo + "Prepaid. GST Invoice. No Forex."
4. CALL TO ACTION: "getaight.ai"

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A) background; Champagne Gold (#D4AF37) for accents and key pricing; Crisp White for secondary text.
- Typography: Bold, wide-set Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or Helvetica Now) for pricing to evoke a "tech-spec" feel. Light-weight tracking for sub-headers.
- Composition: Centered layout. The top 60% is a high-fidelity AI visual; the bottom 40% is a clean, structured grid containing the pricing and brand credentials.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Generate the "Hero Visual" using the prompt provided below.
2. Place the image in the top 60% of the 4:5 canvas, utilizing a soft gradient fade (Charcoal) at the bottom edge to merge into the text area.
3. In the bottom section, create a two-column grid.
   - Left Col: "IMAGE" (White, small) $\rightarrow$ "₹9" (Gold, massive).
   - Right Col: "VIDEO" (White, small) $\rightarrow$ "₹99" (Gold, massive).
4. Place the aight logo centered at the very top or bottom.
5. Add the trust markers ("Prepaid. GST Invoice. No Forex.") in a small, mono-spaced font at the bottom edge, separated by mid-dots.
