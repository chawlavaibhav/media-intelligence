FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
9:16 Vertical UGC Video (~25 seconds).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Produce a high-conversion, "authentic" social media ad for a 2% Salicylic Acid serum. The goal is to establish trust through a believable creator persona and a "no-nonsense" approach to oily/blemish-prone skin, avoiding "over-produced" commercial tropes.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Skin-Minimalist’s Secret": A relatable Indian woman shares her streamlined routine, highlighting the serum as the essential tool for controlling oil and blemishes without the irritation of added fragrances.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary Hook: Relatability regarding oily/blemish-prone skin.
2. Key Product Feature: 2% Salicylic Acid + Fragrance-Free.
3. Core Purchase Reason: Effective blemish control without unnecessary skin irritants.
4. Call to Action: Direct prompt to purchase.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Setting: Bright, naturally lit modern Indian bathroom or bedroom vanity.
- Talent: Indian woman (25-35), natural makeup/clean skin, relatable "girl-next-door" appearance.
- Color Palette: Clean whites, soft pastels, and the brand's product packaging.
- Cinematography: Handheld feel but stable; mix of talking-head and tight B-roll (texture/application).
- Text Overlays: Simple, sans-serif "TikTok/Reel style" captions for key points.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Format: 9:16.
- Total Duration: 25 seconds.
- Pace: Fast cuts every 3-5 seconds to maintain engagement.
- Lighting: Soft natural window light to emphasize skin texture without masking it.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (0-4s): [Talking Head] Mid-shot of a relatable Indian woman in her late 20s, natural skin, standing in a bright bathroom. She looks at the camera with a knowing smile. 
Prompt: "9:16 UGC style video, Indian woman speaking to camera, natural lighting, bright bathroom background, authentic expression, high resolution, handheld camera movement."

Shot 2 (4-10s): [B-Roll/Product] Close-up of the serum bottle being held. The bottle is minimalist. She opens the dropper.
Prompt: "9:16 extreme close-up, hand holding a minimalist skincare serum bottle, dropper extracting a clear liquid drop, soft focus bathroom background, natural daylight, commercial product cinematography."

Shot 3 (10-18s): [Demo] Close-up of the woman applying 2-3 drops of clear serum to her cheek and forehead, gently patting it in.
Prompt: "9:16 close-up, Indian woman applying clear skincare serum to her face, gentle patting motion, glowing but natural skin texture, soft daylight, authentic UGC feel."

Shot 4 (18-25s): [Talking Head] Return to mid-shot. She holds the product next to her face, nodding confidently.
Prompt: "9:16 UGC style video, Indian woman speaking to camera, holding skincare bottle, smiling, natural lighting, bright bathroom, authentic and credible expression."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Text Overlays: 
    - 0-4s: "Oily skin struggle? 🫠"
    - 10-15s: "2% Salicylic Acid | Fragrance-Free ✨"
    - 20-25s: "Link in Bio to Shop"
- Branding: Fictional logo applied to the bottle in post-production.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Natural voiceover (Indian English accent, conversational, warm) + low-volume, upbeat, lo-fi background track.
- Script:
    - (0-4s): "If you're dealing with constant oil and breakouts, listen up."
    - (4-12s): "I've switched to this 2% Salicylic Acid serum. It's a game-changer for my blemish-prone skin."
    - (12-20s): "The best part? It's completely fragrance-free, so no irritation, just results."
    - (20-25s): "Seriously, your skin will thank you. Grab yours at the link below."

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid "perfect" studio lighting; ensure it looks like a smartphone recording.
- Ensure the talent does not claim the product "cures" acne (keep it to "control" and "blemish-prone skin").
- No heavy skin-smoothing filters to maintain "credible creator" status.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- 9:16 aspect ratio? Yes.
- Indian woman? Yes.
- 2% Salicylic Acid mentioned? Yes.
- Fragrance-free mentioned? Yes.
- One purchase reason? Yes (effectiveness without irritation).
- No medical claims/stats? Yes.
- Duration ~25s? Yes.
- UGC style? Yes.
