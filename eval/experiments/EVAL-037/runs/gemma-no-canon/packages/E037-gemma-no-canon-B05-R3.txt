FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic short scene (Video), 16:9 aspect ratio, ~40 seconds.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension interpersonal conflict between two characters in a cafe setting. The core conflict is a revelation of deceit. The execution must prioritize spatial clarity and atmospheric tension over dynamic movement, utilizing a restrained performance style.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Heavy Silence." The scene relies on the "negative space" between the two characters. By using static shots and focusing on micro-expressions, the cinematic feel is derived from the psychological pressure rather than camera flourishes.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Orientation (Who is where)
2. The Catalyst (The concealed object)
3. The Emotional Weight (Restrained betrayal/guilt)
4. The Standoff (Unresolved tension)

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Muted tones, cool blues and greys with warm amber highlights from café lighting.
- Lighting: Soft, directional window light creating slight shadows on faces (Rembrandt lighting).
- Depth of Field: Shallow (f/2.8) to blur the background and isolate the characters.
- Camera: Static tripod shots. No pans, tilts, or zooms.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Total Shots: 6
- Total Duration: 40 Seconds
- Framing: Alternating between establishing wide, medium close-ups, and an insert.
- Pacing: Slow cuts to emphasize the awkwardness and tension.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
- Shot 1 (6s): [Wide Shot] A cinematic wide shot of a minimalist modern café. A woman and a man sit opposite each other at a small round marble table. Soft natural light from a large window. They are motionless, staring at each other. 16:9, static camera, 4k, highly detailed.
- Shot 2 (7s): [Medium Close-Up] Character A (Woman). Her expression is cold and disappointed. She is looking directly across the table. Minimal blinking. Soft window light on one side of her face. 16:9, static camera, shallow depth of field.
- Shot 3 (7s): [Medium Close-Up] Character B (Man). He looks away, eyes shifting nervously, mouth tight. He is leaning slightly back in his chair. 16:9, static camera, shallow depth of field.
- Shot 4 (5s): [Insert/Close-Up] A close-up of the table surface between them. A folded piece of handwritten paper lies open, revealing a shocking detail. A coffee cup sits nearby, steam barely rising. 16:9, static camera.
- Shot 5 (8s): [Medium Close-Up] Character A (Woman). She speaks a single sentence with a steady, low voice. Her eyes remain locked on Character B. 16:9, static camera.
- Shot 6 (7s): [Over-the-Shoulder Shot] From behind Character A’s shoulder, looking at Character B. Character B finally looks up and meets her gaze. The shot lingers on the silence. 16:9, static camera.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Consistent Character Design: Character A (Woman, charcoal blazer, hair in a low bun); Character B (Man, navy blue sweater, short groomed beard).
- Consistent Environment: White marble table, two white ceramic coffee cups, minimalist grey cafe interior.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Low-level ambient café noise (distant clinking of spoons, muffled chatter). Dialogue is recorded "dry" and close-mic'd to feel intimate and oppressive. No background music.
- Edit: Hard cuts between shots. No transitions.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Spatial Logic: The use of the Shot 1 (Wide) and Shot 6 (OTS) ensures the viewer never loses track of the characters' positions.
- Tone Control: Prompts explicitly specify "motionless," "low voice," and "minimal blinking" to prevent the AI from generating exaggerated movements or shouting.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- Two people opposite each other? Yes.
- Concealment discovered? Yes (Shot 4).
- Tense/Restrained/No shouting? Yes.
- Spatial relationship clear? Yes (Wide + OTS).
- Exactly six shots? Yes.
- No elaborate camera movement? Yes (All static).
- Duration ~40s? Yes (6+7+7+5+8+7 = 40).
- Aspect ratio 16:9? Yes.
