9:16 Performance UGC Video (~25 seconds)

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-converting, authentic-feeling video ad for a 2% Salicylic Acid serum targeting Indian adults with oily/blemish-prone skin. The goal is to establish trust through a "creator recommendation" style, focusing on the functional benefit of pore management and the safety of a fragrance-free formula.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The No-Nonsense Essential": A relatable Indian creator shares her "secret" for managing oily skin, positioning the serum as a reliable tool rather than a miracle cure.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Hook: The struggle with midday oil/breakouts.
2. Solution: Introduction of the 2% Salicylic Acid Serum.
3. Key Purchase Reason: Effective pore-clearing without the irritation of added fragrances.
4. Action: Call to purchase.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Talent: Indian woman (25-35), natural skin texture (no heavy beauty filters), minimal makeup.
- Setting: Bright, naturally lit bathroom or vanity area.
- Lighting: Soft window light to mimic authentic UGC.
- Camera: Handheld movement, slight shakes, direct-to-lens eye contact.
- Wardrobe: Neutral casual wear (e.g., a white cotton t-shirt or lounge set).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot 1: Talking head hook. (3s)
- Shot 2: B-roll close-up of product bottle and serum droplet on finger. (5s)
- Shot 3: Application shot; serum being patted onto the T-zone. (7s)
- Shot 4: Talking head; explaining the fragrance-free benefit. (6s)
- Shot 5: Final shot holding product next to face with a smile. (4s)
