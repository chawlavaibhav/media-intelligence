FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 static advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end visual for a Mosambi (Sweet Lime) sparkling drink that pivots away from "mass-market soda" and "ethnic stereotypes." The goal is to position the product as a sophisticated, adult beverage for the urban Indian millennial/Gen Z demographic, blending global luxury aesthetics with a subtle, contemporary Indian identity.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Modern Refreshment." A minimalist, high-contrast composition featuring the product on a polished, light-colored Indian stone surface (e.g., white Makrana marble or light Kota stone). The scene utilizes "architectural lighting"—sharp shadows and bright highlights—to evoke a sun-drenched, high-end urban apartment. The freshness is communicated through hyper-realistic condensation and a single, artfully placed slice of mosambi.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero (The Bottle): Central focus, unmistakable silhouette.
2. Sensorial Cues: Condensation and bubbles (Refreshing/Sparkling).
3. Flavor Signal: A fresh slice of mosambi (Natural/Authentic).
4. Brand Vibe: Minimalist setting (Sophisticated/Premium).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Pale citrus yellow, crisp white, cool slate grey, and soft gold sunlight.
- Texture: Polished stone, chilled glass, effervescent bubbles, matte citrus rind.
- Lighting: High-contrast natural light (simulating a window with slats), creating a play of light and shadow.
- Composition: 4:5 aspect ratio, center-weighted product, clean negative space.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Environment: A minimalist, modern interior. Surface is a polished white marble slab.
2. Product Styling: A sleek, slim-neck glass bottle with a minimal, modern label. The bottle must be chilled with a heavy coat of realistic condensation beads.
3. Accents: One perfectly circular, thin slice of mosambi resting beside the bottle; a few clear, geometric ice cubes.
4. Lighting: Direct, warm sunlight coming from a 45-degree angle, casting a distinct, clean shadow of the bottle.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Final Generation Prompt:
"A professional high-end commercial product photograph of a premium sparkling mosambi drink in a sleek, minimalist glass bottle. The bottle is the central hero, covered in crisp, hyper-realistic condensation droplets. It sits on a polished white Makrana marble surface in a sun-drenched, modern urban interior. Beside the bottle is a single, fresh, thin translucent slice of sweet lime (mosambi) and two clear crystalline ice cubes. Lighting is high-contrast natural sunlight filtering through a window, creating sharp, sophisticated architectural shadows. Color palette of pale citrus yellow, bright white, and soft gold. 8k resolution, shot on 85mm lens, f/8, commercial studio quality, minimalist composition, no people, no text, ultra-realistic textures, sophisticated atmosphere."

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To avoid "cheap soda" look: Ensure the bottle is glass, not plastic; avoid neon colors; keep the background minimalist.
- To avoid "clichéd Indian" look: No ornate patterns, no traditional fabrics, no saturated "festival" colors. Focus on modern Indian materials (marble) and urban minimalism.
- To ensure product hero status: Keep the background uncluttered and use a shallow depth of field to keep the bottle in sharp focus.
