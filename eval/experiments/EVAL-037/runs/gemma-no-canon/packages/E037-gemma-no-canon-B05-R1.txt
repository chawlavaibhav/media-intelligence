FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
Cinematic video scene (40 seconds, 16:9).

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
A high-tension, dialogue-driven encounter between two characters at a café. The focus is on psychological pressure and spatial clarity, utilizing a restrained visual style to emphasize the emotional weight of a revealed secret.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Heavy Silence." Use a static, formal composition with a muted color palette and strategic over-the-shoulder (OTS) framing to maintain a constant sense of opposition and claustrophobia despite the public setting.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Spatial Relationship (Who is where).
2. Emotional Subtext (Tension/Betrayal).
3. The Evidence (The object of concealment).
4. The Reaction (Restraint vs. Guilt).

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Steely blues, charcoal, and warm amber accent lighting from café lamps.
- Lighting: High contrast, side-lit faces to create "chiaroscuro" shadows, suggesting duality and secrets.
- Setting: Urban café, rainy window background to isolate the characters from the world.
- Framing: Static shots, shallow depth of field to blur the background.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Shot Count: 6.
- Movement: Zero camera movement (static tripod).
- Editing: Slow, rhythmic cuts based on dialogue beats.
- Duration: ~40 seconds total.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1: (5s)
Prompt: Cinematic wide shot, 16:9. Two people sitting opposite each other at a small round marble café table. Rain blurring the window in the background. Muted colors, moody lighting. Static camera. Profile view showing both characters.

Shot 2: (8s)
Prompt: Cinematic over-the-shoulder shot from behind Person B, focusing on Person A. Person A has a cold, steady expression, speaking in a low tone. Shallow depth of field. Static camera. 16:9.

Shot 3: (8s)
Prompt: Cinematic over-the-shoulder shot from behind Person A, focusing on Person B. Person B looks unsettled, avoiding eye contact, lips tight. Shallow depth of field. Static camera. 16:9.

Shot 4: (5s)
Prompt: Cinematic extreme close-up, top-down angle. A single weathered envelope and a photograph lying on a marble table between two coffee cups. Static camera. High detail on paper texture. 16:9.

Shot 5: (7s)
Prompt: Cinematic tight close-up on Person A's face. Eyes are piercing and disappointed. Minimal facial movement. Side-lighting creating deep shadows. Static camera. 16:9.

Shot 6: (7s)
Prompt: Cinematic medium wide shot from a side angle. Both characters visible in frame, staring at each other in a heavy, tense silence. The distance between them feels vast. Static camera. 16:9.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Aspect Ratio: Fixed 16:9.
- Frame Rate: 24fps for cinematic motion blur.
- Continuity: Constant wardrobe (Person A: Dark blazer; Person B: Grey knit sweater) and constant prop (Marble table, two coffee cups).

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Dialogue: Low-volume, whispered delivery. Long pauses between lines.
- Sound Design: Ambient café noise (distant clinking of porcelain, muffled rain on glass) lowered in mix to prioritize dialogue.
- Music: A single, low-frequency drone or a minimal cello note holding throughout the scene.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Spatial Clarity: Using OTS shots (2 and 3) ensures the viewer never loses track of the seating arrangement.
- Tension: Prohibit any shouting or sudden movements in the prompt logic to maintain "restrained" atmosphere.
- Cinematic Quality: Explicitly banning camera movement to avoid "mechanical" panning/zooming and focusing on composition.
