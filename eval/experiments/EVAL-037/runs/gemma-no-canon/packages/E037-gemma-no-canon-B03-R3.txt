FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-end commercial visual for a sparkling mosambi drink that appeals to urban Indian millennials/Gen Z. The image must pivot away from traditional "ethnic" tropes toward a "Modern Indian Minimalism" aesthetic—positioning the beverage as a sophisticated, healthy, and refreshing alternative to mass-market sodas.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The Elevated Refreshment." A minimalist, studio-lit composition featuring the product on a tactile, contemporary architectural surface. The focus is on materiality: the cold condensation on the glass, the effervescence of the sparkling water, and the natural, pale yellow-green hue of the mosambi.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero (The bottle/glass)
2. Sensory Cues (Condensation, bubbles, ice)
3. Flavor Identity (A single, elegant slice of mosambi)
4. Brand Mood (Sophisticated, clean, airy)

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: Pale citrine, muted sage, cream, and light grey.
- Lighting: High-key, diffused natural side-lighting (simulating a bright urban apartment or modern café).
- Textures: Honed travertine stone, frosted glass, crisp water droplets.
- Composition: Centered, slightly low-angle to give the product presence.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Background: A slab of light-grey honed travertine or pale sandstone.
2. Product: A sleek, clear glass bottle with a minimalist, matte-finish label.
3. Pairing: A modern high-ball glass filled with the sparkling drink and clear, square-cut ice cubes.
4. Garnish: A single, thin, translucent circular slice of mosambi resting against the inner wall of the glass.
5. Detail: Fine mist/condensation covering the bottle and glass to signal extreme cold.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
High-end commercial product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A sleek glass bottle of sparkling mosambi drink and a matching high-ball glass with ice and a single thin slice of sweet lime. The bottle is the central hero. Set on a minimalist light-grey honed travertine stone surface. Soft, diffused natural sunlight from the side creating elegant caustics and soft shadows. Hyper-realistic condensation droplets on the glass. The liquid is a pale, translucent yellow-green with fine, active carbonation bubbles. Background is a blurred, airy, neutral-toned modern interior. 8k resolution, cinematic lighting, sophisticated, clean composition, architectural minimalism. No text, no people.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Post-production: Color grade to ensure the yellow-green of the mosambi looks natural and appetizing, not neon.
- Layout: Ensure the bottle is positioned in the center-right third to allow for potential copy placement in the upper-left.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
N/A (Static Image)

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid "Indian" clichés: No marigolds, no paisley, no ornate gold, no street-market backgrounds.
- Avoid "Cheap" look: No exaggerated liquid splashes, no saturated neon colors, no plastic bottles.
- Quality Control: Ensure the ice looks clear (not cloudy) and the bubbles are fine (champagne-like) rather than large soda bubbles.

- 4:5 Ratio: Yes

- Mosambi/Sparkling Water: Yes
- No Clichés: Yes
- Product as Hero: Yes
- No Celebrity: Yes
- No Website: Yes
