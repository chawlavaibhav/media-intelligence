FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One premium 4:5 advertising image.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Position a new Mosambi sparkling drink as a sophisticated, adult beverage for urban Indian Millennials/Gen Z. The visual goal is to move away from "mass-market soda" or "traditional ethnic" aesthetics toward a "modernist wellness/premium" aesthetic.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"Architectural Freshness." A high-end studio still life featuring the product on a minimalist stone plinth. The image relies on the contrast between the organic textures of the mosambi fruit, the kinetic energy of carbonation, and the stillness of a contemporary, minimalist urban environment.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Product Hero (The Bottle): Immediate identification of the brand and liquid.
2. Refreshment (Condensation/Bubbles): Immediate sensory trigger of "cold and sparkling."
3. Flavor (Mosambi): Visual cue of the fresh fruit without being rustic.
4. Premium Positioning (Materials/Lighting): High-end lifestyle association.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Color Palette: Pale Citrus Yellow, Muted Sage Green, Matte Cream/Travertine, and Crisp White.
- Lighting: "Golden hour" directional sunlight filtered through a window, creating soft-edged, architectural shadows.
- Textures: Honed matte stone, frosted glass, micro-beads of condensation, and the porous skin of a fresh mosambi.
- Composition: Centralized product placement, slightly low angle to give the bottle "stature."

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Setting: A minimalist studio setup with a light-colored travertine or limestone slab.
2. Hero: A sleek, tall glass bottle with a minimalist, white-label design. The bottle is chilled to produce a fine mist of condensation.
3. Accents: One perfectly sliced wheel of Mosambi leaning against the base of the bottle; a few scattered, crystalline water droplets on the stone surface.
4. Environment: A neutral, soft-grey backdrop that suggests a high-end urban apartment or gallery space.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Commercial product photography, 4:5 aspect ratio. A premium sleek glass bottle of sparkling Mosambi drink centered on a honed light-beige travertine stone plinth. The bottle is covered in fine, realistic condensation droplets. Beside the bottle, a single, crisp, translucent slice of fresh mosambi (sweet lime) showing detailed citrus pulp. High-end minimalist aesthetic. Lighting is soft, directional natural sunlight coming from the side, creating elegant architectural shadows. Background is a blurred, neutral-toned contemporary urban interior. Colors: pale citrus yellow, muted sage, cream, and clear glass. Hyper-realistic, 8k resolution, shot on 85mm lens, f/4.0 for slight depth of field, clean composition, sophisticated mood, no text on bottle, no people.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Logo/Label: To be composited in post-production for brand precision.
- Brand Colors: Specific hex codes for the label should be applied during the final graphic design layer.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
N/A (Static Image).

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- To avoid "cheap soda" look: Avoid bright, saturated primary colors; avoid plastic bottles; avoid exaggerated "splashes" or flying fruit.
- To avoid "clichéd Indian" look: Avoid gold filigree, paisley patterns, bright saffron/orange, or rustic wooden tables. Use stone and minimalist architecture instead.
- To ensure "Product Hero": Ensure the bottle occupies the central vertical axis and is the sharpest point of focus.

- No clichéd shorthand? Yes.
- Product is hero? Yes.
