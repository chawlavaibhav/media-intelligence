FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
9:16 Vertical Video (UGC Style), ~25 seconds.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a high-conversion, "credible creator" style ad for a 2% Salicylic Acid serum. The goal is to move away from "corporate polish" toward "peer recommendation," focusing on the product's suitability for oily, sensitive skin (fragrance-free) and its active ingredient.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"The No-Nonsense Glow Up." A relatable Indian woman shares her "secret" for managing oil and blemishes using a product that doesn't irritate her skin with scents, positioning the serum as a practical, effective tool rather than a luxury miracle.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Hook: Relatability (Oily/blemish-prone skin struggle).
2. Solution: Introduction of the 2% Salicylic Acid Serum.
3. Key Differentiator: Fragrance-free (safe for sensitive/reactive oily skin).
4. Proof: Visual application and skin texture.
5. Action: Clear call to purchase.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Lighting: Natural daylight (simulated window light).
- Setting: Clean, modern Indian home bathroom or vanity area.
- Talent: Indian woman (25-35), natural look, minimal makeup, visible but healthy skin texture (no heavy blurring filters).
- Camera: Handheld 9:16 movement, slight organic shakes, direct-to-lens eye contact.
- Color Palette: Neutral whites, soft greens/blues, skin tones.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
- Framing: Medium close-up (talking head) alternating with extreme close-ups (product texture).
- Pace: Fast cuts, rhythmic delivery.
- Styling: Casual home wear (e.g., a simple cotton tee or robe).

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Shot 1 (0-5s): 
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 UGC video. A relatable young Indian woman in her late 20s speaking directly to the camera in a bright, naturally lit bathroom. She has a friendly, authentic expression and is gesturing to her forehead and cheeks. Natural skin texture, no heavy makeup. High-quality smartphone camera aesthetic.
Purpose: The Hook.
Duration: 5s.

Shot 2 (5-10s): 
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 extreme close-up. A glass dropper bottle of clear facial serum being held by a hand. The label is clean and minimalist. The dropper releases one clear, viscous drop of serum onto a fingertip. Soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field.
Purpose: Product Introduction.
Duration: 5s.

Shot 3 (10-20s): 
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 medium close-up. The Indian woman gently patting the clear serum into her cheek and forehead. She is smiling slightly, looking into a mirror (off-camera). The skin looks hydrated and fresh, not greasy. Authentic home environment.
Purpose: Demonstration/Benefit.
Duration: 10s.

Shot 4 (20-25s): 
Prompt: Vertical 9:16 talking head. The woman holding the product next to her face, giving a knowing nod and a smile to the camera. Bright, airy background.
Purpose: CTA/Closing.
Duration: 5s.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Text Overlays: 
    - 0-5s: "Oily skin struggles? 😫"
    - 5-10s: "2% Salicylic Acid"
    - 10-20s: "Fragrance-Free = No Irritation ✨"
    - 20-25s: "Shop Now [Brand Name]"
- Brand Logo: Small, unobtrusive logo in top-right corner throughout.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- Audio: Natural voiceover (recorded as a "voice memo" style for authenticity).
- BGM: Low-volume, upbeat, lo-fi chill beat.
- SFX: Subtle "pop" sound when text overlays appear.
- Edit: Jump cuts between talking head and B-roll to maintain energy.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Avoid: Over-smoothed "beauty filters" that make skin look plastic (destroys UGC credibility).
- Avoid: Studio lighting (too professional/commercial).
- Avoid: Claims like "Cures acne instantly" or "100% guaranteed results."
- Avoid: Heavy makeup on talent.

HARD_CONSTRAINT_CHECK
- 9:16 Ratio: Yes.
- Indian Woman: Yes.
- Fragrance-free/2% Salicylic Acid: Yes.
- No clinical/medical claims: Yes.
- UGC Style: Yes.
- Duration ~25s: Yes.

None.
