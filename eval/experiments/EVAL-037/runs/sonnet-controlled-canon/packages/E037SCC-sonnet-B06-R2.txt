FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One (1) still image, 4:5 vertical aspect ratio (e.g. 3200×4000 px minimum for e-commerce zoom-in), for use as the primary hero image on a premium product detail page. Single final composited/generated frame — no alternates.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer needs a commercially deployable hero image for a fictional premium mechanical watch, the Aster Meridian 38, that must simultaneously (a) show the entire product accurately enough to serve as the primary purchase-decision image, (b) read as "premium/craftsmanship" through material and light rendering rather than through graphic device, and (c) hold every stated product fact rigidly — case size/finish, dial color and finish, marker/hand style, absence of date window, crystal profile, strap material and color. The brief explicitly rejects the generic "floating watch on white void" CGI cliché unless the concept demands it — it doesn't here, since a grounded, photographically-lit studio shot communicates material truth and desirability more credibly than a floating render.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
A single, still-life studio product shot: the Aster Meridian 38 rests at a confident three-quarter angle on a dark, softly grained walnut surface, photographed as if with a macro-capable lens under classic tabletop product lighting. The watch is not floating, not on white seamless, not styled with props competing for attention — the product is the hero (a deliberate, simple presentation choice rather than a "creative" gimmick), and craftsmanship is communicated the way real premium watch photography communicates it: through controlled reflection behavior on the brushed case, a clean single highlight arc on the domed sapphire crystal that does not obscure the dial, and directional light that reveals the dial's radial sunburst brushing.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Primary read (first 1 second): "premium mechanical watch, whole object, unmistakably well made" — silhouette, case-to-dial-to-strap relationship, sunburst blue color.
2. Secondary read: dial craftsmanship — baton markers, dauphine hands, sunburst texture, clean absence of date window (a deliberate quiet-dial signal of design discipline).
3. Tertiary read: material cues — brushed steel grain on the case, dome curvature and clarity of the sapphire crystal, grain and stitching-free simplicity of the leather strap.
No text, logo, or price is placed in the image itself; this is a pure product hero image for a template that will carry a separate text layer.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Angle: elevated three-quarter view, dial tilted ~15–20° toward camera so the crystal dome's curvature and all hour markers remain legible without foreshortening a marker into invisibility.
- Framing: watch occupies roughly 65–70% of frame width, positioned slightly off-center (right-of-center) per a rule-of-thirds placement, strap trailing toward the lower-left foreground with a soft natural loop for depth.
- Surface: dark walnut wood, fine visible grain, matte-satin sheen (not glossy) — gives warmth and premium context without competing with the steel/blue palette.
- Background: seamless graduated charcoal-to-near-black backdrop, softly darkening toward the edges (subtle vignette) to keep all visual weight on the product.
- Focus: full end-to-end sharpness across the watch (case, dial, crystal surface, near strap coils) — appropriate for e-commerce accuracy — with only the far background/surface falling off very slightly softer.
- Color palette: deep sunburst blue (dial) + brushed steel neutral grey (case) + dark umber brown (strap) + warm dark wood + near-black background. No competing color accents.

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Lighting (grounded in classic metal/product-lighting logic, applied cautiously since source is HOLD-status but directionally sound):
- Key: one large soft source (large softbox equivalent) camera-left/above, sized and positioned so its reflection family covers the brushed case at a controlled mid-grey-to-bright register — bright enough to read as polished/brushed steel, not blown to a flat white card.
- Fill: broad, low-contrast fill camera-right to open shadow detail on the case's far side and the strap, keeping the tonal spread narrow enough that detail survives on all visible faces of the product (a product with detail on every visible side needs a narrower highlight-to-shadow range than a plain geometric object).
- Edge/definition: a soft dark gobo positioned to place one controlled dark reflective arc along the case bezel and crystal edge, giving the metal a visible "edge" against the light background rather than letting it merge into a flat gradient.
- Crystal: light angled so the sapphire dome shows one soft, curved specular highlight in the upper third of the dial only — never centered over the markers or hands, so the sunburst texture and all indices stay fully legible beneath it.
- Dial: light raked slightly off-axis to catch the sunburst dial's radial brushing so the texture is visible as texture, not just as flat blue.
- Strap: soft, slightly lower-key light so the dark brown leather reads with visible grain rather than going to solid black.
- Camera/lens equivalent: macro-capable ~90–100mm equivalent, narrow aperture equivalent (f/8–f/11 look) for full product sharpness, tripod-locked framing.
- Color grade: neutral premium grade — protect true blue-grey neutrality in the steel (no color cast), keep the dial blue rich but not oversaturated, warm the wood slightly, crush background toward near-black without clipping to pure 0.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Dial over-complication: generation must not invent a seconds hand, sub-dial, date window, or brand wordmark not specified by the customer — re-check final frame against the fixed fact list before sign-off.
- Crystal blowout: verify the specular highlight on the dome does not cross over any hour marker or hand; if it does, regenerate/retouch to relocate the highlight.
- Case geometry distortion: verify the case reads as a true circle from the chosen angle (elliptical compression is acceptable only as much as the tilt angle justifies) and that brushing texture direction is consistent across the case, not patchy or mismatched between lugs.
- Flat/lifeless metal: if the case reads as flat mid-grey with no highlight structure, the "family of angles" light placement has failed — increase key light size/proximity until a controlled bright register appears, without clipping to white.
- Dial legibility loss: if sunburst texture or markers disappear into overexposed blue or underexposed shadow, narrow the lighting ratio — a product with detail on every visible face needs a narrower tonal range than a plain object.
- Strap color drift: confirm strap renders as dark brown with visible grain, not black or auburn/tan.
- Background competing for attention: confirm backdrop stays a smooth, low-detail gradient with no texture or object that could split attention from the product.
