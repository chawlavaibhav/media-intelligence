FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One vertical video ad, 9:16, ~30 seconds, for hostel/PG owners in India. Single edited master file (MP4), captioned (on-screen text baked in + separate SRT for platform auto-caption), Hindi-English (Hinglish) VO with English text overlays.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer wants recognition, not education: owners must see their own daily mess (WhatsApp threads, notebooks, spreadsheets) reflected back accurately enough to feel "that's literally me," then experience relief when one system replaces it. The explicit creative risk to avoid is looking like a polished, placeless B2B SaaS explainer (isometric icons, sterile actors, corporate voiceover). The ad must read as shot in India, for Indian PG/hostel owners, using real textures of that business (steel almirahs, handwritten ledgers, rooftop water tanks, Hinglish speech, cash, Aadhar cards) — credible first, "clean tech" second.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
"One day, two systems." Follow one PG/hostel owner through the exact chaos named in the brief — rent chasing, tenant records, KYC paperwork, a move-out, a complaint, compliance — building to a visible breaking point, then a hard visual/audio snap-cut into the same day re-run through RentOk: every one of those five threads resolved in a tap. The structural device is direct 1:1 payoff — each chaos beat in Act 1 gets its exact mirror-resolution in Act 2, so the improvement is legible, not generic. RentOk's own site language ("Why rent like the 1990s when you're in 2026?") validates that this old-vs-new frame is on-brand, not invented.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Recognition (0–8s): "This is my exact daily mess" — rent follow-ups, tenant papers, KYC, complaints, notebooks/spreadsheets/WhatsApp scattered.
2. Tension peak (8–11s): the cost of the mess — exhaustion, missed follow-up, a specific dropped ball (an overdue rent, an unresolved complaint).
3. Turn (11–13s): one system replaces all of it — the single strongest branding beat.
4. Proof-by-feature (13–24s): rent collection automated, KYC digital, complaints + move-outs tracked — each stated as a concrete, tangible outcome (time/money/certainty), per Indian SME buyers' known preference for tangible cost-benefit claims over aspirational imagery.
5. Confidence + credibility proof (24–27s): calmer owner, plus real scale numbers from rentok.com (15,000+ properties, 4.9★/675 reviews).
6. CTA (27–30s): brand lockup + "Get Free Demo" + rentok.com.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Look: documentary-realist, handheld, natural available light (mixed tungsten/daylight Indian interiors), not studio-clean. Slightly desaturated, warm-neutral grade for chaos section; grade opens up (more contrast, cooler clarity, slightly brighter whites) once RentOk appears — a graded "before/after" rather than a set-dressed one.
- Location: a real-feeling PG/hostel — reception desk with steel cupboard, wall calendar, tube light, rooftop water tank visible from balcony, common corridor with room numbers.
- Character: one Indian owner (40s, everyday clothing — half-sleeve shirt, no corporate blazer), a couple of tenant background actors (20s, students/working professionals — realistic PG tenant demographic).
- UI inserts: real phone-in-hand inserts of the RentOk app screen (rent reminders, KYC verification tick, complaint ticket, floor-plan room status) — treated as clean deterministic graphic inserts, not asked of the generative model to "imagine" UI.
- Typography: bold sans, high-contrast white-on-dark stamps for chaos-phase words (RENT / RECORDS / KYC / COMPLAINTS / COMPLIANCE), switching to RentOk's own clean app-style type once the turn happens. All safe-zone respecting for 9:16 (captions and CTA within middle-safe 80%).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
Shoot-and-composite hybrid: live-action (or live-action-style generative) narrative shots for the human/environment beats, deterministic screen-recorded/UI-graphic inserts for all app footage and all on-screen numerals/stats, motion-graphics text overlays laid in post (not generated in-shot). Grade shifts from warm/flat to punchy/contrast at the turn cut (shot 4). Sound design and VO mixed separately and married in edit.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
(Shot-level, in order; each is an executable prompt for the video generation step. Total ≈30s across 9 shots.)

Shot 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:03, 3s)
Purpose: instant recognition of the chaos.
Prompt: "Extreme close-up, handheld, slightly shaky: a middle-aged Indian man's hand flips through a worn, ink-stained notebook ledger full of crossed-out rent entries, while a smartphone next to it buzzes with a stack of overlapping WhatsApp notification pops (blurred generic chat bubbles, no real logos). Warm tungsten light, slightly desaturated grade, shallow depth of field. Ambient sound: overlapping muffled voices, notification pings, a ceiling fan. No dialogue yet."

Shot 2 — ESCALATION (0:03–0:08, 5s)
Purpose: pile on the five named pain points fast.
Prompt: "Fast-cut handheld montage, real PG/hostel interior: (a) owner walking a corridor with phone pressed to ear, saying in Hindi-accented English/Hinglish 'Bhaiya, rent kal tak bhej dena...'; (b) a tenant handing over a folded Aadhar card and cash at a reception desk; (c) a wall calendar with move-out dates scribbled and crossed out; (d) a laptop screen with a messy spreadsheet, red overdue cells; (e) another tenant saying off-camera 'Uncle, complaint diya tha, koi reply nahi aaya.' Documentary energy, quick cuts on action, natural available light, rising ambient noise of a busy PG."

Shot 3 — BREAKING POINT (0:08–0:11, 3s)
Purpose: emotional low point, sets up the turn.
Prompt: "Medium shot, static camera: the same owner sits down heavily at his desk, exhales, rubs his temple, notebook falls open on the desk revealing messy crossed-out ledger lines. Sound drops to near-silence except a slow ceiling fan and a distant phone ring. Text stamps on screen, bold white sans, one word per beat: RENT — RECORDS — KYC — COMPLAINTS — COMPLIANCE, each landing with a soft thud sound."

Shot 4 — THE TURN (0:11–0:13, 2s)
Purpose: hard tonal snap from chaos to clean system.
Prompt: "Whip-pan or match-cut transition: the owner's thumb taps his phone screen; hard cut to a crisp, bright close-up of the RentOk app home screen lighting up (clean UI, real app screenshot composited in post, not generated). Music cuts from tense low drone to a confident upbeat rhythm on this frame. A single clean UI chime sound on the cut."

Shot 5 — SOLUTION: RENT (0:13–0:16, 3s)
Purpose: resolve pain point 1 (rent collection).
Prompt: "Close-up of phone in hand: owner taps 'Send Bulk WhatsApp Reminder' inside the RentOk app; a list of tenant names shows payment status ticking from red 'Due' to green 'Paid' in quick succession. Bright, clean, high-key light — contrast with earlier warm chaos grade. Text overlay: 'Rent collected automatically.' Confident upbeat music continues."

Shot 6 — SOLUTION: KYC (0:16–0:19, 3s)
Prompt: "Close-up: owner holds phone camera over a tenant's ID card (details generically blurred/fictional, no real personal data), app shows a scanning animation resolving to a green 'Verified' checkmark with the tenant's photo and basic details populating a clean digital form. Shot 7 — SOLUTION: COMPLAINTS & MOVE-OUT (0:19–0:23, 4s)

Text overlay: 'Complaints and move-outs, tracked.' Same bright, orderly visual language as prior solution shots."

Shot 8 — RESOLUTION / CALM (0:23–0:27, 4s)
Purpose: emotional payoff, credibility.

Text overlay: 'One app for it all.'"

Shot 9 — LOGO/CTA (0:27–0:30, 3s)
Purpose: brand lockup and action.
Prompt: "Clean static end card, RentOk logo centered on white/light background, tagline beneath: 'Save time. Work less. Earn more.' Below it, proof line: 'Trusted by 15,000+ properties · 4.9★ (675 reviews).' Call to action button-style text: 'Get Free Demo — rentok.com'. Simple upbeat musical resolve, UI chime echo fades out."

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- All RentOk app UI screens (Shots 4–7) and the end-card stats/logo (Shot 9): sourced from real product screenshots/brand assets and composited in post as deterministic motion graphics — not left to the generative model to invent, since generated UI text/logos are unreliable and any invented numbers or feature claims would misrepresent the real product.
- Proof numbers (15,000+ properties, 4.9★, 675 reviews, "Save time. Work less. Earn more.") — pulled verbatim from rentok.com; typeset deterministically, not generated as in-scene text.
- Any tenant ID/Aadhar card visuals must use entirely fictional placeholder data, generated or typeset deterministically, never real personal data patterns.
- On-screen Hindi/Hinglish text and captions: typeset in post for spelling accuracy, not generated in-shot (generative video frequently garbles Devanagari/Hinglish text).

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
- VO/dialogue: Hinglish, colloquial, spoken by a native-sounding Indian voice actor matching the owner's on-screen register (not a corporate narrator). Sample lines: "Bhaiya, rent kal tak bhej dena..." (chaos) → post-turn line "Ab sab kuch ek jagah." → closing line "RentOk. Kaam kam, kamai zyada."
- Music: contemporary Indian-inflected track — tabla/percussion layered under a modern beat; tense/sparse under chaos (Shots 1–3), hard cut to upbeat confident version at the turn (Shot 4 onward).
- SFX: notification pings, overlapping voice murmur, page flips, phone ring in chaos section; single clean UI chime on the turn; ambient chai-cup clink and rooftop wind in resolution.
- Edit pace: fast cuts (0.5–1.5s) through chaos section to feel overwhelming; slightly longer, calmer holds (1.5–2s) in solution/resolution section to feel orderly — the cutting rhythm itself dramatizes "chaos vs system."
- Captions burned in for sound-off viewing (majority of vertical feed consumption), safe-zone respecting.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Risk: generative video renders illegible/garbled app UI or Devanagari text → mitigated by compositing real UI and typeset text in post (see Deterministic section).
- Risk: result reads as generic global SaaS ad → mitigated by specific Indian production detail (steel almirah, rooftop water tanks, chai tumbler, Hinglish dialogue, real PG visual clutter) and by avoiding corporate-actor styling, stock "diverse team in blazers" tropes, and isometric icon graphics.
- Risk: chaos section feels cartoonish/mocking rather than recognisable → mitigated by grounding every chaos beat in the customer's own named pain points (rent, records, KYC, move-outs, complaints, compliance) rather than invented generic mess.
- Risk: privacy/legal exposure from showing realistic ID documents → mitigated by mandating fictional placeholder data on any ID/KYC visual.
- Risk: pacing loses viewers before the turn on a sound-off vertical feed → mitigated by strong visual word-stamps (Shot 3) and burned-in captions throughout, so the hook and message survive without audio.
- Risk: overclaiming features not on the website (e.g., implying insurance/zero-deposit or legal services not requested by the brief) → mitigated by restricting feature claims strictly to the five pain points named in the brief and only the matching rentok.com-confirmed features (Autopay/reminders, Digital KYC/verification, Complaint Management, room status/move-out tracking).
