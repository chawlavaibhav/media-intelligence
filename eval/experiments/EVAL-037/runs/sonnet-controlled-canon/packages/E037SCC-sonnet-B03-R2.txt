FINAL_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE

DELIVERABLE
One static advertising image, 4:5 vertical (recommend 1080×1350 px minimum, deliverable at print-safe resolution ≥3000×3750 px), single hero product shot. No video, no celebrity, no external website reference.

OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
The customer needs a launch hero image for a new Indian mosambi-sparkling-water drink targeted at urban 22–35 year-olds. The image must do three things simultaneously: (1) make the bottle the unmistakable visual subject, (2) read as premium/editorial rather than mass-market soda, and (3) feel "culturally contemporary" — recognisably rooted in mosambi without leaning on stock Indian iconography (no marigolds, saris, henna, elephants, Taj Mahal, filmy colour grading). The win condition is a shopper/scroller instantly identifying (a) what it is, (b) that it's premium, (c) that it's citrus-fresh and familiar, in under a second, with the product legible even at small thumbnail size.

CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
A single slender glass bottle, mid-condensation, shot in soft editorial daylight against a warm, minimal, matte backdrop (terracotta/clay tone), with real mosambi (sweet lime) — a halved fruit and a couple of wedges — as the only supporting props, plus visible fine carbonation inside the liquid. Sophistication comes from restraint: one product, one fruit, one accent colour, generous negative space, quiet premium typography — not from cultural motifs or busy soda-commercial energy. The fruit does the "familiarity" work; the styling, light and materials do the "sophistication and contemporary" work.

MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. Bottle (hero, tack-sharp, center-weighted, largest single mass in frame) — communicates category + premium cue instantly.
2. Mosambi fruit (halved + wedge, adjacent, softly out of primary focal plane) — communicates flavour/familiarity, answers "what is this" in one glance.
3. Condensation + visible bubbles — communicates "sparkling," "cold," "refreshing" without any text.
4. Brand wordmark + small abstracted citrus-slice mark on label (part of the product itself, not a floating graphic) — identity carried by the product, per the make-the-product-the-hero principle rather than by a separate graphic device.
5. Optional minimal tagline in bottom-third clear space (e.g., "Mosambi. Sparkling. Elevated.") — smallest, lowest priority, easily dropped if it competes with the bottle.
No other claims, badges, bursts, or percentage callouts — those read as cheap soft-drink shorthand and are deliberately excluded.

VISUAL_SYSTEM
- Palette: warm terracotta/clay background, muted sage-green accent (leaf), brass/copper bottle cap, translucent pale citrus-gold liquid. No neon greens, no primary-blue soda cues.
- Materials: matte stone/clay surface + glass + brass — a tactile, non-plastic material story that reads premium.
- Light: single large soft key (softbox or bounced daylight) positioned high and to one side, sized roughly 10–20× the bottle diameter, angled so its reflection on the glass produces one large, dimmed highlight rather than a small hot specular spot. A large-but-dim highlight reads as considered surface/texture; a small bright highlight reads as cheap and distracting — this governs the entire lighting decision.
- Background treatment for the glass edge: since a round glass/liquid subject acts as a lens and sees a wider field than the camera, the backdrop must be large enough (or the camera positioned close enough) to fill what the liquid itself reflects, otherwise a dark ring or a stray hot rectangle will appear inside the bottle — construction plan below controls for this.
- Composition: bottle placed on a lower-third power point, vertical emphasis suits 4:5; ample clear space above/beside for label legibility and optional tagline; camera near eye-level with a very slight downward tilt to show the liquid's surface/bubbles.
- No clichéd Indian iconography anywhere in frame. Cultural specificity is carried entirely by the real fruit and by a quiet, non-stereotyped colour/material palette — this deliberately trades "instant ethnic legibility" for premium universality, a known trade-off (heavy cultural signage reads as more "authentic" but also as more generic/dated shorthand; a fruit-led, material-led approach reads as premium and still unambiguously mosambi).

PRODUCTION_RECIPE
1. Hero plate: bottle centered slightly left of vertical axis, camera at ~eye level, macro-capable framing, shallow-to-medium depth of field (bottle fully sharp, background falls off softly).
2. Liquid/carbonation: capture or render fine, evenly distributed bubbles rising, pale gold-green liquid, filled to a level that shows both liquid and a visible pour/fizz cue near the neck.
3. Condensation: fine, realistic water beading concentrated on the lower two-thirds of the bottle, a few controlled rivulets — not an all-over frosted look (frosted-all-over reads as cheap/canned soda).
4. Props: one mosambi half (cut face toward camera, pith and segments visible) resting near the base, one or two wedges leaning on the bottle; optionally a single small mint or curry-leaf sprig for a fresh green accent — kept minimal, not decorative clutter.
5. Backdrop: warm terracotta seamless or textured clay surface, large enough in frame (or camera close enough) to fill the field of view the liquid itself reflects, avoiding a visible dark ring or hot studio reflection inside the glass.
6. Label/wordmark: designed and finalized as a deterministic vector asset (see below), composited onto the bottle with correct wrap/perspective — not left to the generative pass to invent.

GENERATION_PROMPTS
Single final image-generation prompt (base plate, product/scene only — label to be composited deterministically afterward):

"Premium editorial product photograph, 4:5 vertical composition. A single slender clear glass bottle of pale gold-green sparkling citrus drink, centered slightly left of frame, fine carbonation bubbles visible rising inside the liquid, condensation droplets beading on the lower two-thirds of the glass with one or two controlled rivulets. Brass-copper metal cap. Bottle stands on a warm matte terracotta clay surface. Beside it, one halved mosambi (sweet lime) showing its cut segments and pith, plus one mosambi wedge leaning against the bottle base, and a single small fresh green leaf sprig for accent. Soft, large, high-angled daylight-style key light from upper left producing one large soft dimmed highlight along the glass, gentle fill, warm neutral tones throughout — terracotta, brass, pale citrus gold, muted sage green. Shallow depth of field, bottle in crisp sharp focus, background softly defocused with no clutter. No text, no logo, no people, no cultural props or ornament, no bright primary colors, no plastic or neon soda aesthetic. Clean generous negative space above and to the right of the bottle for later graphic overlay. Photoreal, high-end beverage advertising style, natural material textures, matte finish, restrained and sophisticated mood."

Construction plan:
- Layer 1 (background): terracotta backdrop, out-of-focus, large enough to avoid reflection artifacts in the glass/liquid.
- Layer 2 (hero): bottle with liquid/carbonation/condensation, sharp focus, centered lower-third.
- Layer 3 (props): mosambi half + wedge + leaf, arranged not to overlap or obscure the bottle's label zone.
- Layer 4 (deterministic overlay, post-generation): label graphic, brand wordmark, small abstracted citrus-slice mark, optional tagline — vector-precise, composited and perspective-matched to the bottle in a compositing/retouch pass.
- Layer 5 (finishing): color grade to lock warm/neutral palette, dial down any specular hot spots that emerged too small/bright, confirm bubble/condensation realism, confirm product retains full legibility at thumbnail scale.

DETERMINISTIC_OR_NON_GENERATIVE_ELEMENTS
- Final label design, wordmark, logotype/mark, and any on-pack legal or tagline text: produced as a fixed vector/graphic asset by a designer and composited onto the bottle in post — not generated freeform, to guarantee spelling, kerning and brand-mark accuracy.
- Brand color values (terracotta, brass, sage) locked to exact hex/Pantone references from brand guidelines, applied in the grading/compositing pass.
- Any nutritional/regulatory text (if required for the market) added as deterministic type in post, not generated.

AUDIO_AND_EDIT
Not applicable — this is a single static image deliverable with no audio or edit timeline.

FAILURE_PREVENTION
- Reject any output where the bottle is not the largest, sharpest, most central mass in frame — hierarchy is non-negotiable.
- Reject small, bright, hard-edged specular highlights on the glass (reads as cheap/soda); require one large, dimmer highlight area instead.
- Reject an all-over "frosted" condensation look; keep it localized and realistic.
- Reject any generated Indian cultural iconography (textiles, marigolds, elephants, monuments, festival colour palettes) — flag and remove if it appears.
- Reject any generated on-bottle text/logo attempt from the model itself; always replace with the deterministic label asset.
- Reject oversaturated, neon, or "soft-drink commercial" color grading; hold to the warm/matte palette.
- Check the glass/liquid area for a visible dark ring or a hot rectangular studio reflection; if present, treat the background/camera framing rather than trying to relight the highlight.
- Verify legibility and hero clarity at reduced (mobile thumbnail) size before final sign-off.
