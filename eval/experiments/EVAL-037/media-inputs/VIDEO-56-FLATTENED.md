I am RentOK. Create a commercially strong vertical video ad for owners who run hostels and PGs in India. Managing rent collection, tenant records, KYC, move-outs, complaints and compliance through WhatsApp, notebooks or scattered spreadsheets creates leakages and missed follow-ups. ### B04
Create a 9:16 performance-oriented UGC video for a fictional Indian D2C skincare brand. The product is a fragrance-free 2% salicylic-acid facial serum for adults with oily, blemish-prone skin. One believable Indian woman should speak directly to camera, naturally demonstrate the product and communicate one clear purchase reason. It should feel like credible creator content rather than a polished television commercial, while remaining visually controlled and commercially usable. ### B05

The dialogue is tense but restrained; nobody shouts. The viewer must always understand where each person is in relation to the other. Use exactly six shots. Avoid elaborate camera movement. The scene should feel cinematic rather than mechanically covered. Target final duration: about 40 seconds. Aspect ratio: 16:9. No external website.

# CONTEST V01 — B01

Use frozen brief B01 from the top of this file.

## PACKAGE A

## DELIVERABLE
A 30-second vertical video ad (9:16 aspect ratio) that viscerally shows the chaos of managing hostels/PGs through scattered tools, then reveals RentOK as the unified solution. Targeted at Indian property owners. Contemporary, credible tone—no Silicon Valley pastiche.

---

### OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Position RentOK not as a feature-rich app, but as the antidote to daily operational friction (rent follow-ups, tenant records, KYC, complaints, move-outs). The pain is real and present for the target audience. The relief should feel earned and practical, not aspirational.

---

### CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
**"Chaos to Clarity in 30 Seconds"**

Open with rapid-fire scenes of an overwhelmed PG/hostel owner drowning in operational fragmentation—WhatsApp notifications piling up, scattered notebook pages, spreadsheet confusion, tenant complaints going unresolved. Then cut sharply to the same scenario resolved inside RentOK: one screen, organized, automatic, peaceful. The contrast is the message.

---

### MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. **Primary:** RentOK consolidates all the scattered chaos into one system.
2. **Secondary:** Specific pain points solved (rent collection, tenant records, KYC, complaints, move-outs, compliance).
3. **Tertiary:** It's built for Indian property owners; it's practical, not theoretical.
4. **Call-to-Action:** Simple—"Download RentOK" or "Get free demo."

---

### VISUAL_SYSTEM
- **Aesthetic:** Hand-held, documentary-real footage mixed with screen recordings of the app. Avoid overly polished or sterile UI showcases.
- **Color language:** Warm, lived-in early scenes (yellowed notebooks, cluttered desks, phone notifications); cool, organized digital clarity in the RentOK scenes.
- **Typography:** Simple, Indian-vernacular-friendly sans-serif. No heavy design flourishes.
- **On-screen elements:** Real WhatsApp conversations, notebook scribbles, spreadsheet rows—then the equivalent data organized in RentOK.

---

### PRODUCTION_RECIPE

**Duration: 30 seconds (9:16 vertical)**

**Structure:**
- Seconds 0–12: Chaos (pain identification)
- Seconds 12–24: Solution (RentOK clarity)
- Seconds 24–30: CTA + branding

**Shots in order:**

| Shot | Content | Duration | Purpose |
|------|---------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Close-up: Phone screen flooded with WhatsApp notifications—tenant messages mixed with personal texts, payment reminders, complaints stacked unanswered. | 2 sec | Establish immediate pain: information overload. |
| 2 | Wide: PG owner at desk, surrounded by notebooks, pen in hand, squinting at scribbled tenant details. | 2 sec | Tactile chaos—analog overwhelm. |
| 3 | Close-up: Spreadsheet on laptop, multiple sheets, data scattered, columns misaligned, rent amounts inconsistent. | 2 sec | Digital chaos—no coherent system. |
| 4 | Quick cuts (1 sec each, 3 total): Tenant lodging a complaint verbally, owner frantically searching notes, late rent payment missed. | 3 sec | Consequences of disorganization: compliance failure, revenue leak, tenant dissatisfaction. |
| 5 | Transition: Phone/laptop screen "dissolves" or "swipes" to RentOK app home screen. | 1 sec | Visual break—system change. |
| 6 | Screen recording: RentOK dashboard showing tenant list, occupancy status, rent collection status, auto-payments scheduled. | 2 sec | Solution: Everything in one place, organized. |
| 7 | Screen recording: Complaint section—one tap to log, assign, track resolution. | 1.5 sec | Solution: Complaints managed seamlessly. |
| 8 | Screen recording: Tenant KYC section with document verification, move-out checklist visible. | 1.5 sec | Solution: Compliance handled. |
| 9 | Screen recording: WhatsApp integration—automated reminders being sent, payment links auto-generated. | 1.5 sec | Solution: Automation replaces manual follow-up. |
| 10 | Wide: Owner at same desk, now calm, phone in hand, reading RentOK dashboard. Sunlight brighter. | 1.5 sec | Result: Calm, in control. |
| 11 | Text overlay + app icon + CTA: "RentOK. Rent management made simple. Get free demo." + buttons. | 2 sec | Close: branding + action. |

**Total: 30 seconds**

**Risk 6: CTA is weak or unclear.**
- *Mitigation:* Explicit buttons ("Download App," "Get Free Demo"). Clear branding. Offer a clear next action—no ambiguity.

**Risk 7: Video doesn't feel "Indian" enough—risks appearing like generic SaaS.**
- *Mitigation:* Set in authentic PG/hostel office, not minimalist co-working space. Include Hindi-English code-switching in WhatsApp messages. Use non-Western music. Avoid Americana design language.

## OBJECTIVE_INTERPRETATION
Create a self-contained dramatic moment—a moment of accusation and evasion—that relies on intimate framing, spatial clarity, and restrained emotional performance rather than dialogue volume or elaborate cinematography. The viewer must always know where each person sits relative to the other.

---

## CORE_CREATIVE_IDEA
Two people at a small café table. Person A has found evidence of Person B's deception. The confrontation is glacial and quiet. The tension lives in the space between them, in held glances, and in what remains unspoken. Each shot is positioned to either widen the perceived distance (establishing Person A's separateness and accusation) or tighten it (forcing Person B's discomfort). No music. Only ambient café sound and dialogue.

---

## MESSAGE_AND_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY
1. **Establishment of location and spatial relationship** (Shot 1)
2. **Person A's accusation and emotional state** (Shot 2)
3. **Person B's guilt/defensiveness and response** (Shot 3)
4. **The uncomfortable shared space—moment of reckoning** (Shot 4)
5. **Person A's deeper reaction/pain to the answer** (Shot 5)
6. **The unresolved moment—what happens next is ambiguous** (Shot 6)

---

## VISUAL_SYSTEM
**Framing principle:** Two-person coverage using:
- One wide/medium establishing shot showing both figures and table
- Medium shots (waist-up) on each person for dialogue, angled slightly toward each other to show spatial relationship
- One intervening wide shot to "reset" spatial understanding and show the gulf between them
- Close-up registers only on moments of maximum emotional intensity

**Spatial anchors:** The table divides the frame. Coffee cups, hands on the table, and the line of sight between them should be consistent and visible.

**Lighting:** Soft, naturalistic café light (diffused daylight from windows or warm interior). No dramatic shadows. Clarity and intimacy matter more than stylization.

**Depth of field:** Wide in establishing and mid-scene wide shots (to show space). Shallow on close-ups (to isolate emotional reaction).

**Color:** Warm, slightly desaturated. Café palette: beige, ochre, dark wood. Restrained—nothing that competes with performance.

---

## PRODUCTION_RECIPE
**Setting:** Small café table, two chairs opposite each other, coffee cups and small items on table. Neutral background (other tables, counter, soft out-of-focus café interior). Daytime, window light preferred.

**Performance note:** Both actors play restraint. No raised voices. Movements are minimal—hands, eye contact, subtle shifts in posture. Person A is controlled but hurt. Person B is defensive but not hostile.

**Timing allocation:**
- Shot 1: 6 seconds
- Shot 2: 6 seconds
- Shot 3: 6 seconds
- Shot 4: 5 seconds
- Shot 5: 8 seconds
- Shot 6: 9 seconds
- **Total: 40 seconds**

**Prompt:**

Both are visible; the silence between them feels physical. Person A has leaned back slightly. Person B's posture is closed. The café around them is warm and blurred. 16:9. Cinematic framing, both figures in focus, the space between them is the subject."

**Purpose:** Deepest emotional register. Person A's hurt and realization that this was deliberate. Longest dialogue beat, minimal movement.

**Prompt:**

The person looks down briefly, then back at Person B with a mix of pain and betrayal. Soft café light, shallow depth of field, the background is completely blurred. 16:9. Cinematic, intimate, the performance is quiet and internal."

**Dialogue over shot:**

### SHOT 6 (Wide or medium-wide, resolution, ~9 sec)
**Purpose:** The unresolved moment. Neither person speaks. The scene ends in the space between them. Ambiguous, cinematic, leaves the viewer in the discomfort.

**Prompt:**
"Medium-wide shot of both figures across the table, camera at a slight angle (different from Shots 1 and 4 to avoid repetition, but maintaining spatial clarity). Person A has straightened, looking down at the table or their hands. Person B is still, looking at Person A or away. The café exists around them, warm and indifferent. Silence. A few seconds pass. Person A reaches for the coffee cup or phone—a small gesture of disengagement or resignation. 16:9. Cinematic, soft light, both in focus, the moment stretches. The scene ends."

**No dialogue. Only ambient café sound. Moment fades to black or holds on the image.**

**Spatial anchors (must be maintained):**
- Person A always on same side of table across all shots

**Performance consistency:**
- Person A's clothing, hair, and overall appearance must be identical across all 6 shots
- Person B's appearance must be consistent
- Emotional progression: Person A moves from controlled to slightly fractured to resigned. Person B moves from caught off-guard to defensive to stillness.

## FAILURE_PREVENTION
**Spatial continuity failure:** Generate each shot with explicit instruction that the same table, same two people, and same relative positions must be visible. Include a spatial anchor phrase in each prompt (e.g., "from across the table," "the edge of Person B's shoulder visible," "the table surface visible").

**Eyeline failure:** Ensure that in each close-up or medium shot, the direction of gaze is consistent with the camera position. If Person A is looking at Person B in Shot 5, the camera should be positioned as if from Person B's vantage point (slightly lower, across the table).

**Dialogue timing mismatch:** Generate shots with explicit duration markers. Ensure that dialogue delivery is paced to fit the shot duration and that pauses in dialogue align with the silence in Shot 4.

**Tonal failure (scene becomes slapstick or too melodramatic):** Emphasize in performance notes that restraint is key. Specify "controlled," "quiet," "minimal movement," "internal emotional register" in every prompt. Avoid prompts that suggest shouting, dramatic hand gestures, or overacting.

**Cinematic vs. mechanical coverage:** Do not use rigid shot-reverse-shot at identical angles. Vary camera height and angle between shots (especially between Shots 2 and 3, and between establishing shots 1, 4, and 6) to create visual interest without elaborate movement.

**Temporal inconsistency (people age, light shifts drastically, clothing changes):** Keep lighting warm and consistent across all shots. Specify "afternoon café light" or "daytime window light" in every prompt. Lock costume, hair, and appearance descriptions into the performer descriptions.

**Background inconsistency (café looks different in different shots):** Every prompt should include a descriptor of the café interior as visible in that shot's background. Reference the same elements (other tables, counter, windows) so the AI understands it's the same space.
