# EVAL-004 Controller stop decision — 24 Aug 2026

**Status:** STOPPED by Controller before protocol completion.

## Decision

EVAL-004 will not continue to the planned second independent blind reader pass.

One complete 54-item Reader-A pass was collected as an exploratory pilot. A second person informally looked at the material, but did **not** complete the frozen EVAL-004 independent blind transcription protocol. That informal review is therefore not Reader B and must not be used as such.

The Controller judged the photographed-signage pack to be too basic / low-value relative to the intended downstream problem and chose not to spend further human time completing the two-reader calibration.

## Consequences

- EVAL-004 is **not completed** under its approved protocol.
- Reader A must **not** be promoted to strict ground truth by itself.
- No two-reader exact-agreement reference exists.
- No checker may be qualified, disqualified, ranked, or entered into the Capability Registry from this run.
- No claim about checker accuracy, false-pass rate, or Hindi-reading qualification may be made from this run.
- The Reader-A pass may be retained only as **exploratory pilot evidence** about item difficulty / task design.
- No additional human time, API/model spend, generator work, BSTD use, or Marathi subset is authorized by this decision.

## Design lesson

The pilot suggests that a real-world signage reading screen built mostly from basic words may be a weak proxy for the failure state that matters commercially: a multimodal checker silently normalizing or autocorrecting subtly malformed **generated** Hindi text.

If Eval work resumes, the next Controller decision should be whether to redesign the qualification battery around harder, controlled failure cases that better match generated-text errors (for example matra substitutions, character confusions, omissions, transpositions, malformed glyphs, and plausible-but-wrong words), rather than automatically completing the current two-reader signage protocol.

This note supersedes the active-execution posture of EVAL-004 but does not erase the earlier readiness work or the pilot response artifact.
