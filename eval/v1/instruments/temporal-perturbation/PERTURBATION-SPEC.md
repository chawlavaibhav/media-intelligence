## 1. What problem this solves

The Eval stream's founding result is that **a capability number without its instrument is not a
measurement**. Before any checker's output may be written into the Capability Registry, that
checker must have passed a qualification protocol for the exact judgement it is being asked to
make.

For the temporal/video family, the judgements are things like *"does the person stay the same
person for the whole clip"* and *"does the on-screen text stay the same text"*. None of those can
be answered by looking at one frame, which is why this family cannot be replaced by running an
image checker over sampled frames.

Qualifying such a checker normally needs footage that already contains those faults, plus somebody
to say where they are. Both are expensive, and the second one is itself uncertain.

**The alternative used here:** take clean footage and inject a known fault at a known moment. The
truth is then the transformation itself. Nothing is annotated, nothing is generated by a paid
model, and the answer is exact rather than someone's opinion.

The frozen planning documents already chose this route. `EVALUATOR-AND-MATERIAL-STAGE-MAP.yaml`
calls it the *cheapest large unblock available*: nine capabilities from twelve clean clips and
zero human labels. This task builds the machinery that route needs.

## 3. The unit of material

A clip's scientific identity here is **not** an mp4 file. Two ffmpeg builds can encode identical
pixels into different bytes, so an mp4's hash proves nothing durable.

Instead a clip is an **ordered sequence of decoded frames plus an integer frame rate**, stored as
lossless PNGs, and fingerprinted by a hash over the ordered per-frame pixel hashes. That
fingerprint is stable across machines, operating systems and encoder versions.

An mp4 can be exported afterwards for checkers that need a playable file. The export records its
own hash and states plainly that its bytes are **not** promised to be reproducible. The frame
sequence is the truth; the mp4 is a convenience.

### Ingest of real footage

Real clips arrive at whatever rate the camera chose — 23.976, 29.97, variable. Fractional and
variable rates make *"the defect is at frame 41"* ambiguous, and ambiguity is exactly what a
truth-by-construction pack cannot afford. So ingest resamples each clip to one declared integer
frame rate before anything is injected.

That step is deterministic, and both the exact ffmpeg command and the ffmpeg version are recorded.
It becomes a **stated condition** of any qualification earned on the pack.

Two hashes are recorded per real clip, and they mean different things:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `source_file_sha256` | the delivered file, exactly as Resources handed it over |
| `content_hash` | the normalised frame sequence the perturbation actually acts on |

## 4. The thirteen perturbations

Each one is deterministic: integer arithmetic, and any randomness seeded from a hash of the source
clip plus the parameters, so a rebuild reproduces the same bytes.

| # | Perturbation | What it creates | Frozen capability it supplies truth for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `frame_freeze` | picture stalls for a known run | `motion_action_quality`, `technical_visual_integrity` |
| 2 | `frame_duplication` | a moving run replays; clip gets longer | `motion_action_quality`, `technical_visual_integrity` |
| 3 | `frame_drop` | time jumps; clip gets shorter | `motion_action_quality`, `technical_visual_integrity` |
| 4 | `frame_reversal` | motion reverses and returns | `motion_action_quality`, `action_adherence`* |
| 5 | `segment_reordering` | visible state runs backwards | `sequence_state_continuity`, `action_adherence`* |
| 6 | `shot_horizontal_flip` | screen direction violated across a cut | `multi_shot_spatial_continuity` |
| 7 | `midclip_horizontal_flip` | orientation breaks with no cut | `multi_shot_spatial_continuity`, `technical_visual_integrity` |
| 8 | `identity_splice` | a different person mid-shot | `person_stability_in_clip` |
| 9 | `product_region_substitution` | the product morphs from frame *k* | `product_stability_in_clip` |
| 10 | `text_region_mutation` | the text surface changes from frame *k* | `text_logo_stability_in_clip` |
| 11 | `text_glyph_substitution` | the text becomes a different string | `text_logo_stability_in_clip` |
| 12 | `framing_discontinuity` | the framing jumps; subject unchanged | `camera_framing_fidelity`* |
| 13 | `technical_corruption` | block breakup in a known interval | `technical_visual_integrity` |

`*` = **negative direction only.** See §7.

**Freeze is not duplication.** A freeze repeats one frame in place and keeps the clip length.
Duplication replays a moving run and makes the clip longer, so the motion stutters rather than
stopping. They are different failures and are scored as separate types — the frozen gate forbids
merging them into one average.

**Text mutation is not glyph substitution.** Glyph substitution re-renders the region with a
different string and records both strings, but it is only available on clips whose text we drew
ourselves. Real footage uses `text_region_mutation`, which alters the pixels and records *"the text
surface provably changed at frame k"*. Both give known truth; only one gives string-level truth.

### Regions are geometry, not labels

Three perturbations act on a rectangle. A bounding box says **where to look**; it never asserts
that anything is wrong, so it is not a defect label and needs no annotator. Where Resources
declares a box, `region_source: declared` is recorded. Where none is declared, ingest defaults one
from frame geometry and records `region_source: geometric_default`, so the weaker basis is visible
in the manifest rather than hidden.

## 5. Controls

**Clean positive controls.** Every base clip is also entered untouched, with
`defect_present: false`. Recall alone cannot expose a detector that flags everything; the
false-alarm rate on untouched clips is the other half of the frozen gate.

**Corrupt negative controls.** Seven artifacts that must **refuse to load**: a truncated frame, a
broken chunk CRC, a missing frame, a frame whose pixels were swapped without updating the sidecar,
a clip declaring zero frames, a frame that is not an image, and an unparseable sidecar. Each must
raise, the run must stop, and the clip must never be scored and never counted as clean. **If any
of them loads, the pack build aborts.**

**Builder control.** A deliberately inert transformation must be refused. A fixture whose injected
defect changed nothing would score every checker as a miss and silently corrupt the recall figure.

This follows rule 6 of the frozen master spec: negative-control fixtures previously exposed three
real defects in this project's own harness, including a run that raised integrity errors and still
exited successfully. None was visible from reading the code.

### When a perturbation cannot be injected

If a transformation genuinely changes nothing on a particular clip — most often a defaulted region
that turns out to be featureless, so there is no text there to break — the fixture is **not**
written. The gap is recorded in `skipped` with the reason, the capability's coverage count drops,
and `coverage_warnings` reports any capability left with fewer than two independent clips. Silence
about a missing perturbation would read later as coverage that never existed.

## 6. The gate, and why no verdict is issued

The frozen family-4 gate has three measures, all of which the scoring harness computes:

1. **Detection recall, per perturbation type, never averaged.** A checker that catches freezes and
   misses text mutation is not "80% accurate"; it is blind to the failure that costs the most.
2. **False-positive rate on the untouched clean clips.**
3. **Localisation** — did it point near the frame where the defect was injected? Detection without
   localisation cannot drive repair. No frame tolerance is applied, because none exists in the
   frozen contracts; the harness reports whether the reported interval overlaps the injected one,
   and the distribution of gaps in frames.

**No numeric pass mark exists.** Nothing in the frozen contracts says what recall is good enough
for this family. So `qualify_temporal.py` returns `gate_verdict: undetermined` and cannot return
status `qualified` — it raises if it ever tries. Promotion is a Controller decision against an
approved pass mark, and `validate_package.py` fails the package if a threshold constant appears in
the code.

### Three counting rules the harness enforces

- **Frames from one clip are ONE trial.** Every fixture carries
  `independent_opportunity_id` = its base clip. Recall is reported over fixtures *and* over
  independent clips, and the reference statistic is computed over clips.
- **The sample rate is part of the measurement.** A defect between two sampled frames is invisible.
  Every detection record must state `sampled_frames`; a record without it invalidates the run.
  Each fixture also records how many samples must land inside the affected interval before the
  defect is observable in principle — 2 for stalls and jumps, where the evidence *is* the
  relationship between two samples; 1 for content substitutions. That is a derivation from what
  each defect physically is, not a pass mark.
- **An incomplete run is `unmeasurable`, not a score.** A fixture with no detection record is
  neither a miss nor a pass. Unknown fixture ids and duplicate records are refused outright.

### Gate and diagnosis are stored apart

Whether the checker said pass or fail (routing needs this) is kept separate from whether it said
*what* broke (repair needs this). The project has already recorded a checker with a perfect gate
and an incomplete diagnosis; the two may never share one number.

### The reference statistic is a sizing calculation

A Clopper–Pearson upper bound is computed over independent clips and always carries
`independence_status: NOT ESTABLISHED`. It is never the checker's real-world error rate: a checker
blind to one defect type is blind to it on every clip carrying that type, so errors here are
correlated *by construction*.

## 7. What this cannot establish — stated plainly

**Two capabilities get only the negative direction.**

- `action_adherence` — reversing or reordering a run provably destroys the action. It does not
  establish that an untouched clip performs a *requested* action, because no transformation can
  create a request. The positive direction needs a brief, which is Stage A material.
- `camera_framing_fidelity` — a known framing *break* is constructible. Whether a clip honours a
  requested camera move is not, for the same reason.

Proving a checker notices a destroyed action is real evidence and worth having. It is not the
whole capability, and this document does not let anyone read it as such.

**Human adjudication is not removed.** Four of the nine capabilities are marked
`model_based_plus_human` with required adjudication in the frozen map. Injected truth gives
known-defect recall; it does not repeal that requirement.

**Stand-in material qualifies nothing.** The frozen family-4 condition is real footage. A
qualification does not transfer from constructed clips to real ones, and the harness enforces this
by returning `unmeasurable` whenever the pack is not built purely from supplied clips.

**Zero instruments are qualified today, and this task does not change that.**

## 8. What becomes runnable when the twelve clips land

"Runnable" means the protocol can execute and produce per-type recall, false-alarm and
localisation numbers. It does **not** mean any checker passes.

| Capability | Runnable | Via | Residual condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `motion_action_quality` | yes | freeze, duplication, drop, reversal | motion load recorded per clip, as the contract requires |
| `person_stability_in_clip` | yes | identity splice | donor must match geometry and fps; ingest normalisation makes this hold within one delivered pack |
| `product_stability_in_clip` | yes | product region substitution | needs a product region; a defaulted box is a weaker basis and is recorded as such |
| `text_logo_stability_in_clip` | yes | text region mutation | pixel-level truth on real footage, not string-level |
| `multi_shot_spatial_continuity` | yes | shot flip, mid-clip flip | shot flip needs the ≥2 clips with cuts already required of the pack |
| `sequence_state_continuity` | yes | segment reordering | frozen map still requires human adjudication |
| `technical_visual_integrity` | yes | corruption, freeze, duplication, drop, flip | frozen map still requires human adjudication |
| `action_adherence` | partial | reversal, reordering | negative direction only |
| `camera_framing_fidelity` | partial | framing discontinuity | negative direction only |

**Seven of nine fully; two in the negative direction only.**
