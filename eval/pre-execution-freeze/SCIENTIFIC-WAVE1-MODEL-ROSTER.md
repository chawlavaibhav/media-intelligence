## Rows are slots, not model names

A row is defined by its **question** and the characteristics a model must have to answer it. A named candidate is the current best guess at an *instrument* for that question.

**Why this matters:** if EVAL-010 finds a candidate unavailable, misidentified or mispriced, the **slot survives** and an equivalent fills it. Naming models as the unit of science would let a sourcing failure silently delete an empirical question — exactly the coupling the independence rule forbids.

**Every model identity, version and price below is UNRESOLVED and is EVAL-010's to verify.**

## How EVAL-008 was challenged

### CHALLENGE-01 — ~99% Hindi/Bengali character-accuracy claim (attributed to OpenAI, gpt-image-2)

- **Verification attempted:** Direct fetch of the vendor announcement page (EGRESS_BLOCKED) plus a targeted web search for primary evidence and evaluation methodology.
- **Result:** `NO_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_FOUND`
- **What the search returned:** Reseller product pages, a Medium post and marketing summaries. The search itself concluded the results were "primarily marketing claims and general descriptions rather than detailed peer-reviewed primary source documentation of the evaluation methodology".
- **Disposition:** NOT LOAD-BEARING. It is not used as an admission reason anywhere in this roster. INSTEAD IT IS CONVERTED INTO THE HYPOTHESIS THE BENCHMARK TESTS: slot IMG-01 exists to find out whether frontier generated Devanagari is viable at all, and this claim is the null it will falsify or support. A marketing number becomes a testable prediction rather than a reason to believe.

### CHALLENGE-02 — Leaderboard/Elo evidence (gpt-image-2 T2I arena #1 ~1370; reve-2.1 #2 ~1306)

- **Disposition:** INSUFFICIENT ALONE, per Controller. Arena Elo measures aggregate human preference on community prompts - HPSv2 states preference comparison is "only meaningful for images generated by the same prompt". It says nothing about exact Devanagari, brand-mark geometry or campaign consistency, which are our actual constraints. Elo is used here ONLY as a weak tie-break BETWEEN candidates already justified by a question, never to create a row.

### CHALLENGE-03 — Vendor geography / training lineage as a differentiator (H_lineage_acceptance)

- **Disposition:** DEMOTED. 'Different national/vendor lineage' is not a concrete failure hypothesis. It was RE-SPECIFIED into something testable and kept only in that form: whether a model trained predominantly on non-Indian commercial imagery produces Indian-market people, products and typography that reviewers accept. That is a measurable acceptance question; 'made elsewhere' is not. Row IMG-04 carries the re-specified version.

### CHALLENGE-04 — H_clean_rights (marey-realism, licensed-data-only training)

- **Disposition:** DEMOTED OUT OF WAVE 1. Rights-clean training is a procurement/legal property, not a capability our benchmark measures. It would change a purchasing decision, not a production design. Recorded as a Controller legal question, not an Eval row.

### CHALLENGE-05 — H_conversational_loop (gemini-omni-flash)

- **Disposition:** DEMOTED TO RESERVE. Multi-turn is explicitly recognised by the Controller as real but NOT solved, with no request-history schema frozen and no block on Wave 1. Testing a conversational loop before the request grammar exists would measure against a moving target.

### CHALLENGE-06 — 27 EVAL-008 entries carrying no hypothesis at all

- **Disposition:** NOT ADMITTED. Superseded versions and unhypothesised entries were excluded by the admission rule automatically - a row with no question cannot earn a slot. This is the rule doing its job rather than a judgement about those models.

### CHALLENGE-07 — Composite-route arms treated as model rows

- **Disposition:** RESTRUCTURED. 'Generate image without type, then composite type locally' and 'TTS + lip-sync instead of native AV' are WORKFLOW TOPOLOGY arms, not models. Treating them as roster rows would have inflated the roster and hidden the fact that the comparison is between ROUTES. They are modelled as topology comparisons that reuse admitted slots (see topology_arms).

**Why it changes production:** THE highest-value question in Wave 1. - **Workflow mode:** `t2i`

If the generalist fails and the specialist succeeds, production routes text through a specialist step - a materially different design. If both fail, the composite topology is forced. They cannot substitute for each other.

## Workflow topology comparisons — routes, not roster rows

### TOPO-01 — Hindi dialogue video with a visible speaker

- **Arm A:** native_audio_video (uses VID-01)
- **Arm B:** silent video + TTS + lip-sync (uses VID-01, AUD-01, AUD-03)
- **Why compared:** Materially different cost, failure surface and applicable capability set. In arm A sync is a property of ONE trial; in arm B it is a property of a transform over our own inputs, which makes part of it deterministically checkable. This is a PLANNER decision, not a customer request.
- **Eval's position:** Eval measures both routes. Eval does not choose.

### TOPO-02 — Static ad with exact Devanagari headline

- **Arm A:** type generated by the image model (uses IMG-01, IMG-02)
- **Arm B:** image generated without type + type composited locally (uses IMG-03)
- **Why compared:** If arm A fails, arm B is the product. Arm B's compositing step creates artifacts WITHOUT provider trials, per the approved topology.
- **Eval's position:** Measures both; the choice is the Planner's.
