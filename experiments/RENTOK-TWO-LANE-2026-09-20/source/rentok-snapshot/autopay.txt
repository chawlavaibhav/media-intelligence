Common Questions
Questions Property Owners Ask
Honest answers to your concerns about Autopay
What if tenant refuses to enable Autopay?
You can make Autopay mandatory in your rental agreement. In our experience, 98% of tenants enable it willingly because it is convenient for them too.
What if tenant's bank account has no money on payment date?
Autopay automatically retries for 3 consecutive days. You get instant notifications for failures, so you only follow up with specific tenants.
Is this legal? Will banks allow auto-debit for rent?
Yes. It uses the same NPCI infrastructure used for SIP, insurance premiums, EMIs, and credit card bills.
What if tenant leaves mid-month? Do I still pay for them?
No. You disable their Autopay in one click. You are charged only for active users.
Do I need a special bank account or merchant account?
No special account needed. It works with regular savings accounts and most Indian banks.
What if I want to stop using Autopay? Is there a contract?
You can cancel anytime with no lock-in and no penalty.
